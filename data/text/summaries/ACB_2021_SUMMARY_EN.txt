### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Fiscal year 2021 marked a robust outperformance for Asia Commercial Joint Stock Company (ACB), with its share price appreciating by +51.08% to close at VND 13,960 per share (reaching a 52-week high of VND 15,450 and a low of VND 7,930), alongside an average matched liquidity of 8,250,153 shares per session. The core financial driver behind this valuation re-rating was superior earnings performance, with pre-tax profit reaching approximately VND 12 trillion (up 25% YoY), propelling the Return on Equity (ROE) to a top-tier market level of 23.90%. 

ACB’s core growth catalyst stems from its 2019–2024 retail banking strategy, which prioritizes the affluent retail and Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise (SME) segments to achieve a targeted top-line growth of 20% per annum. The business model's structural shift toward a higher non-interest income contribution was heavily catalyzed by bancassurance operations, driven by an exclusive partnership with Sun Life Vietnam. Digitalization velocity and technological adoption—including eKYC implementation, the upgrade of the retail Mobile App, online loan disbursements via ACB Online, and the rollout of the ACB Business App for corporate clients—have yielded operational efficiencies, optimized headcount, and accelerated market share acquisition. Furthermore, the scaling of subsidiaries, notably ACB Securities (ACBS), has consolidated an integrated competitive advantage for the Group.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

ACB maintains a robust capital structure and abundant liquidity profile, mitigating cash flow risks through the optimization of its earning-asset portfolio and interbank collateralized transactions. Within its borrowing and collateral structure, the bank adheres to stringent credit covenants:
* **VND 5,511.2 billion** in term deposits placed at other credit institutions (CIs) are utilized as collateral for **VND 5,465 billion** in deposits received from other CIs.
* Subsidiary ACBS utilizes **VND 1,435.21 billion** of term deposit balances at other CIs as collateral for borrowings amounting to **VND 1,351.43 billion**.
* Regarding the Held-to-Maturity (HTM) Government securities portfolio, ACB has pledged **VND 10,582.875 billion** as collateral for borrowings of **VND 10,185.801 billion** from other CIs; pledged **VND 1,102.562 billion** against deposits from other CIs valued at **VND 940 billion**; and pledged **VND 2,172.327 billion** to secure the issuance of valuable papers worth **VND 2,177.016 billion**.

This funding structure reflects a high capacity to leverage high-quality liquid assets (HQLAs)—particularly sovereign bonds—to mobilize short-term funding at an optimized cost of capital, thereby dampening interest rate volatility risks and systemic liquidity pressure.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

ACB preserves asset quality within a highly secure threshold, reporting a non-performing loan (NPL) ratio of just **0.77%**. Regarding provisioning, the bank demonstrated exceptional conservatism by fully provisioning 100% of the supplementary specific reserves for Covid-19 restructured loans pursuant to Circular 03. Specifically, the 2021 specific provision balance incorporates **VND 2,319.425 billion** in supplementary provisions under Circular 03, of which **VND 2,308.667 billion** represents additional provisioning allocated to Group 1 loans (Standard Loans). 

Addressing legacy receivables from prior periods, ACB has provisioned 100% (**VND 131.905 billion**) of the outstanding receivables due from two entities among the six corporate affiliates linked to a former executive, thereby neutralizing asset impairment risks. The bank also reclassified **VND 196.29 billion** of Available-for-Sale (AFS) equity securities to other long-term investments. Internal control oversight was reinforced as the Internal Audit Department executed audits across 80 branches and 11 critical risk management special topics (including capital adequacy ratios under Circular 13, real estate collateral valuation, and credit risk). 

Post-balance sheet, in early 2022, the Board of Directors appointed a new Chief Executive Officer—an internal stalwart with tenure dating back to 1996—to ensure execution continuity for the 2019–2024 strategy. The financial targets for 2022 are proactive: an 11% increase in total assets (reaching VND 588,187 billion), an 11% rise in customer deposits, a 10% expansion in gross loans (reaching VND 398,299 billion, subject to State Bank of Vietnam approval adjustments), consolidated pre-tax profit of approximately VND 15,018 billion, and an NPL ratio maintained below 2%.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

* **Environmental (E):** ACB explicitly formalizes its environmental stewardship through recurring budgetary allocations. The bank scaled up its "Gần Lại O" campaign to curb plastic and paper waste by instituting the internal "O-Branch" award for branches demonstrating exemplary local environmental compliance, positioning itself as the pioneering Vietnamese bank to adopt this environmental emulation mechanism.
* **Social (S):** ACB prioritizes human capital development via succession pipeline training and personnel rotation between headquarters and branches to maximize sales velocity. Human resources policies guarantee gender equality in recruitment, competitive compensation packages, and targeted workforce welfare during the Covid-19 pandemic, earning ACB recognition as one of HR Asia’s “Best Companies to Work for in Asia” for three consecutive years.
* **Governance (G):** The governance architecture operates under strict operational independence. In 2021, the Supervisory Board convened 5 voting sessions to oversee regulatory compliance with SBV capital adequacy ratios and NPL resolution. Transparency is underscored by the absence of any ACB equity transactions executed by members of the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, or the Executive Board, alongside zero transactions or contracts executed with internal shareholders during the year.
