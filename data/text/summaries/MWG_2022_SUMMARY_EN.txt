### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Throughout 2022, Mobile World Investment Corporation (MWG) navigated a challenging macroeconomic backdrop characterized by softening aggregate demand, an uptick in consumer thriftiness, and escalating corporate overheads. Equity price performance mirrored these operational pressures, contracting by 36.50% over the year as the stock traded from an open of VND 64,440 to close at VND 40,920 (registering a 52-week high of VND 77,200 and a low of VND 33,620, accompanied by a mean daily trading volume of 2,610,310 shares). 

To adapt, MWG sustained its omnichannel O2O strategic focus, leveraging its proprietary technology infrastructure to drive customer retention and expand online market share. Core retail chains prioritized same-store sales growth (SSSG) optimization and stringent opex control over aggressive footprint expansion. Specifically, the Bach Hoa Xanh (BHX) grocery chain targeted basket-size enhancement, higher shopping frequency, and increased foot traffic, alongside supply chain and warehouse reconfiguration to minimize shrinkage and fresh-produce write-offs, thereby bolstering gross margins. Regarding nascent "seed" retail concepts—such as An Khang (pharmacy), AVAKids, and the EraBlue joint venture in Indonesia (which inaugurated its first 5 stores in late 2022)—management executed a selective rollout strategy, pausing blanket expansion to protect operating cash flow and sustaining only cash-flow-positive doors to curb losses and maximize operational efficiency.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Amid heightened financial market volatility and a tightening monetary cycle, MWG maintained rigorous working capital and cash-flow governance to preserve liquidity and balance-sheet integrity. Per the financial statement footnotes, the group utilized short-term unsecured bank borrowings carrying floating interest rates to fund working capital requirements, with maturities spanning from January 6, 2023, to September 15, 2023. Management prioritized deleveraging wherever feasible to rationalize financial expenses, mitigate exposure to benchmark interest rate fluctuations, and safeguard operating cash flows, thereby establishing a robust financial buffer against macroeconomic headwinds.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
MWG’s operational and financial risk management framework remained robust throughout 2022. Inventory management showed low risk profiles across all retail banners, characterized by stable inventory turnover days and a year-end contraction driven by cautious consumer spending. Substandard and obsolete inventory was maintained at minimal levels, with adequate provisions fully recognized. Regarding credit and counterparty risk—encompassing consumer finance partners and import advance payments—diligent monitoring confirmed no requirement for doubtful debt provisions, with all receivables, cash, and merchandise successfully recovered. The internal audit and control systems reported no material financial anomalies, significant legal disputes, or off-balance-sheet guarantees arising after December 31, 2022, that would exert a material adverse effect on the group's financial position.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
MWG demonstrated a steadfast commitment to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles by systematically embedding sustainable development frameworks across all organizational tiers, from the Board of Directors to operational staff:
- **Environmental (E):** The company executed tangible initiatives aimed at environmental stewardship, including resource conservation (electricity and water), the reduction of hazardous packaging, greenhouse gas emissions mitigation, and the promotion of circular economy practices throughout its operational footprint.
- **Social (S):** Empowering a workforce exceeding 70,000 employees, MWG prioritized workplace safety by strictly enforcing occupational health and safety protocols alongside fire prevention standards, while maintaining comprehensive property and labor insurance coverage for all personnel. The group also reinforced a corporate culture rooted in core values and operational integrity.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adhered to statutory regulations and the corporate Charter, underpinned by rigorous oversight from the Board of Directors, the Audit Committee, and independent external auditors. MWG sustained high standards of disclosure transparency, comprehensive risk management, and the progressive alignment of its sustainability practices with international benchmarks.
