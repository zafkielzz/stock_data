1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, against the backdrop of a global economic slowdown driven by monetary tightening and geopolitical uncertainties, compounded by domestic headwinds including below-target GDP growth and sluggish recovery in the real estate and corporate bond markets, Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - VCB) firmly reinforced its industry-leading position in operating efficiency and asset quality. The bank’s core growth momentum was propelled by disciplined credit expansion and rigorous asset quality control. As of December 31, 2023, VCB’s total assets surpassed VND 1.84 trillion, with gross credit outstanding reaching approximately VND 1.28 trillion, representing a 10.8% expansion compared to the end of 2022. Pre-tax profit established a new historical high of roughly VND 41,244 billion (equivalent to VND 41.2 trillion), up approximately 10% YoY, maintaining its undisputed #1 ranking industry-wide in profitability scale. Return on Average Equity (ROAE) remained robust at approximately 21.99%. VCB shares traded within an opening price of VND 44,780 and a closing price of VND 52,930, yielding an annual total return of +18.20% (with a 52-week high of VND 61,770 and a low of VND 44,780; average matched trading volume reached 1,004,196 shares per session).

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2023, Vietcombank focused on reinforcing its financial capacity and expanding its charter capital to meet capital adequacy standards and support long-term strategic initiatives. Specifically, in September 2023, the bank completed a stock dividend issuance funded from retained earnings after tax for 2019 and 2020 at an issuance ratio of approximately 18.1% of charter capital at the time of issuance. This raised VCB’s charter capital to VND 55,891 billion and pushed its market capitalization above USD 20 billion. Regarding regulatory prudential metrics, the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR, inclusive of corporate bonds) and the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) were stringently managed in compliance with limits, thresholds, and risk appetite frameworks mandated by the Board of Directors and the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV). The bank is actively progressing subsequent stock dividend plans alongside preparations for a private placement of shares to further fortify its capital base moving forward.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vietcombank maintained a conservative operating stance, delivering top-tier asset quality management across the broader market. As of year-end 2023, the bank’s internal non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was tightly contained at 0.99%—the lowest among large-scale credit institutions. Notably, the NPL coverage ratio stood at approximately 227%, ranking among the highest in the sector and providing an exceptionally robust loss-absorption buffer against potential credit risks amid macroeconomic volatility. Risk governance was further institutionalized through the active oversight of the Risk Management Committee (which convened 04 regular meetings during the year), advising the Board of Directors on the formulation and approval of risk policies, risk appetite, and mitigation strategies covering credit risk, market risk, and operational risk.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vietcombank has established a core strategic objective to become the leading green bank in ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance). The bank prioritizes the expansion of green credit, steering toward a sustainable green banking model serving the community. In 2023, VCB was selected as one of the 20 constituent stocks in the Vietnam Sustainability Index (VNSI) basket published by the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE), underscoring its adherence to and transparent reporting on sustainable development standards.
- **Social (S):** The bank remains deeply committed to public welfare initiatives and community development. Since 2012, Vietcombank has allocated over VND 3,300 billion to support disadvantaged populations in remote, border, and island regions. Furthermore, VCB has acted as a pioneer in executing debt restructuring and fee/interest rate reductions to assist distressed clients, alongside optimizing its organizational structure, streamlining human resources, and fostering workforce quality.
- **Governance (G):** Vietcombank’s corporate governance adheres to high transparency standards, underpinned by a comprehensively reinforced management apparatus (comprising the Board of Directors, Executive Board, Supervisory Board, and specialized committees such as the Risk Management Committee and Personnel Committee). Investor Relations (IR) activities were professionalized to international standards, highlighted by 4 quarterly earnings release webcasts, approximately 75 one-on-one/group meetings, participation in 17 investor events across major financial hubs (Singapore, Hong Kong, Tokyo, Dubai, etc.), and engagement with over 600 institutional investors. The bank also ensured accurate and timely disclosure of over 130 periodic and ad-hoc items, strictly complying with securities regulations.
