Below is a standardized qualitative analysis summary based on the 2023 Annual Report of Vietnam International Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VIB) and system-wide data:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, VIB sustained robust growth momentum driven by its strategic focus on the retail banking segment and superior digitalization capabilities. Profit Before Tax (PBT) reached VND 10,704 billion, recording a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 48% per annum over the past 7 years, alongside a Return on Equity (ROE) of 25%. The core growth driver stemmed from the Retail Banking (RB) Division, which delivered a PBT of VND 5,540 billion (up 8% year-on-year). Retail credit portfolio expanded by 8%, accounting for 85% of the bank's total outstanding loans, within which mortgage lending grew by 8% and business loans surged by 28%. VIB maintained its leading market position in auto loans and credit cards (surpassing 710,000 cards in circulation, with total card spending reaching USD 4 billion, marking an expansion of over 8 times within 6 years). The Corporate Banking Division also recorded impressive outstanding loan growth of 56% year-on-year, while maintaining tight control over the Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio at a low 0.7%. Meanwhile, the Treasury and Foreign Exchange Division continued to play a pivotal role in optimizing the capital structure, ensuring liquidity, and providing FX and interest rate hedging products, with foreign exchange trading volume ranking among the top 5 in the market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
VIB maintains a resilient balance sheet structure through the diversification of domestic and international funding sources. In 2023, the bank successfully executed multiple large-scale international borrowings, highlighted by a USD 100 million 5-year direct loan from the International Finance Corporation (IFC) and a USD 280 million 3-year syndicated loan arranged by three mandated lead arrangers—United Overseas Bank Limited, Maybank Securities Pte. Ltd., and Mashrek Bank Psc—alongside 14 other international banks. This stands as the largest syndicated loan, with VIB being the sole bank in Vietnam to successfully access long-term funding at competitive costs amid a tightening international market environment. The funding structure is rigorously managed in accordance with Basel III standards (maintaining optimal LDR and NSFR levels), ensuring a high proportion of liquid assets and a low cost of capital. Stock price performance during the year recorded an opening price of VND 8,710 and a closing price of VND 11,570 (+32.84% fluctuation), trading within a range of VND 8,710 to VND 12,980 with an average matching volume of 4,487,954 shares/session, reflecting strong investor confidence in the bank's financial health and capital management capabilities.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
VIB applies a stringent risk management framework aligned with international standards (Advanced Basel II and Basel III), closely monitoring credit quality to minimize potential losses. The bank-wide NPL ratio was safely contained at 2.20% as of December 31, 2023. The bank proactively set aside full provisions while executing debt repayment rescheduling, debt classification retention, and fee/interest waivers for customers in strict compliance with Circular 02/2023/TT-NHNN. Regarding key risk exposures such as the corporate bond investment portfolio, real estate business lending, and high-risk sectors, loan proportions were maintained at the lowest levels in the market. The Basel II Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 11.73%, remaining amongst the industry leaders. The bank recorded no major legal disputes, litigation, or material abnormal financial events occurring after the financial year-end of December 31, 2023, that would adversely impact business operations.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
VIB strongly integrates Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards into its comprehensive business strategy:
- **Environment (E):** The bank intensifies control and mitigation of environmental impacts through energy optimization, solid waste management, and wastewater treatment across its business units. VIB promotes the comprehensive digitalization of operational processes (such as Green PIN services and paperless credit approval processes - ACL), thereby minimizing paper and printer ink consumption while prioritizing financing for green and sustainable development sectors in alignment with Government and SBV directives.
- **Social (S):** VIB focuses on human capital development with a high-quality workforce of over 12,253 employees, where female staff account for 53%. The bank ensures a democratic and transparent working environment, maintains comprehensive welfare policies, regular health check-ups, and organizes numerous specialized training programs (spanning hundreds of thousands of training hours). VIB has also actively contributed tens of billions of VND to social security, healthcare, education, and community support initiatives nationwide.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly complies with best international practices (IFC, GBCG) and legal regulations. The Board of Directors comprises 05 members with a balanced composition, ensuring independence and rigorous oversight via specialized subcommittees (Personnel Committee, Risk Management Committee). The internal control system operates under an independent 3-line-of-defense model, alongside transparent financial disclosures and investor relations adhering to international standards.
