Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative financial report summary for Dat Xanh Group (DXG) for the fiscal year 2022:

**1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Fiscal year 2022 marked a highly challenging period for the real estate market in general and Dat Xanh Group (DXG) in particular, which was directly reflected in the equity price action: the stock plummeted 63.61% from an opening price of VND 23,880 to close at VND 8,690, trading within a wide range of VND 32,330 (high) to VND 5,410 (low), while maintaining a high average liquidity of 10,833,117 shares per session. Core business operations were severely impacted by tightened real estate credit, protracted project legal procedures, and a sharp contraction in market purchasing power. Traditional growth drivers from land-bank development and secondary trading experienced a significant stall. The real estate brokerage segment—a core strength that historically contributed a substantial revenue share—faced immense pressure as broader market liquidity froze, forcing numerous brokerage offices to downsize or temporarily suspend operations to optimize operating costs and navigate adverse financial market conditions.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Amid tight regulatory control over the corporate bond market and constrained bank credit flows in 2022, Dat Xanh’s capital structure and liquidity pressures faced stringent tests. The Group’s debt structure comprises short- and long-term borrowings from major domestic commercial banks alongside alternative capital-raising channels. The continuous escalation of market interest rates in the second half of 2022 directly drove up financial expenses on floating-rate debt. Capital structure risks were heavily concentrated in short-term refinancing pressures, as cash inflows from sales and product handovers across projects slowed down due to legal bottlenecks and cautious homebuyer sentiment, posing a major challenge in balancing timely principal and interest debt service.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS**
Financial pressures in 2022 forced Dat Xanh to maintain an exceptionally conservative approach to balance-sheet risk management. The company reviewed and provisioned for inventory write-downs on slow-moving projects, while reassessing short- and long-term receivables to record provisions for bad debts from partners, customers, and investments facing liquidity constraints. Off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments related to the financial obligations of homebuyers across group-developed projects also harbor the risk of materializing into actual debt obligations if the market remains sluggish. Legal and macroeconomic shocks during the year necessitated continuous portfolio restructuring by management, including extending the deployment timeline of non-essential projects to preserve financial resources.

**4. ESG EXECUTION**
- **Environmental (E):** Dat Xanh has progressively integrated sustainable development criteria into its real estate value chain. The Group emphasizes researching and applying eco-friendly building materials, optimizing design to reduce energy consumption across residential projects, and systematically monitoring greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) across office operations and projects, aligned with a strategic roadmap toward future Net-Zero emissions.
- **Social (S):** Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) across construction sites is rigorously managed through compliance with national technical standards. Human resource policies prioritize gender equality, fair promotional opportunities, and professional capacity-building training programs for employees. Employee welfare, social insurance, and healthcare benefits were stably maintained even amidst a highly volatile economic environment.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance adheres to current regulatory frameworks applicable to large-scale public companies. The Board of Directors maintains relative independence in formulating strategic direction and supervising the Executive Board's operations. Financial statements comply with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) under the independent audit of leading auditing firms, ensuring transparency of disclosures for shareholders and the market.
