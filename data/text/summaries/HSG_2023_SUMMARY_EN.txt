### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

The 2022–2023 financial year (FY) was characterized by an exceptionally hostile macro environment for both the global and Vietnamese steel industries, exacerbated by persistent high inflation, geopolitical conflicts disrupting supply chains, and a sharp contraction in construction activity and infrastructure investment. Amidst these headwinds, Hoa Sen Group (HSG) generated over VND 31,650 billion in revenue and net income of over VND 30 billion. Despite significant margin compression compared to the industry's peak years, HSG’s share price posted an impressive growth of +101.46% (opening at VND 8,230/share and closing at VND 16,580/share, reaching a peak of VND 17,380/share) alongside robust liquidity, with average trading volume reaching 14,072,216 shares per session. This price action reflects strong market expectations of a cyclical bottoming and subsequent recovery for the galvanized steel value chain.

The core driver underpinning HSG’s operating cash flow generation was a flexible dual-channel distribution strategy. In the export segment, HSG reinforced its leadership by capturing nearly half of the industry's total galvanized steel export volume, serving over 87 countries and territories, which effectively mitigated domestic demand shocks. Domestically, medium-term growth momentum was supported by product diversification and the optimization of the internal distribution network, marked by the launch of the 114th outlet under the Hoa Sen Home building materials and interior decoration supermarket system. Rigorous adherence to procurement policies and disciplined inventory turnover management were decisive factors enabling the company to navigate the volatility of global steel prices.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

HSG’s capital structure reflects the characteristic profile of a large-scale manufacturing and trading enterprise with intensive working capital requirements. Short-term liabilities constitute the dominant share of total debt, primarily funding inventory and short-term accounts receivable. To meet its financing needs, HSG predominantly utilizes short-term credit facilities from major commercial banks to support working capital.

Within a volatile financial market, the primary risks to HSG’s capital structure stem from foreign exchange rate fluctuations and the sensitivity of interest expenses on floating-rate debt. However, management has proactively mitigated cash flow risk by capitalizing on low-interest-rate environments across the banking system to restructure the debt portfolio and minimize financial expenses. The Group's strategic roadmap for the subsequent period prioritizes tightening financial leverage and optimizing operating cash flow to progressively reduce reliance on bank borrowings, thereby shielding profit margins against potential interest rate shocks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

HSG’s accounting policies record inventory conservatively at the lower of cost and net realizable value (NRV) using the weighted average method. Given the high volatility of global steel prices, inventory write-down provisions represent a material risk directly impacting the Group's cost of goods sold. Additionally, accounts receivable are strictly managed through allowances for doubtful accounts based on historical cost to mitigate credit risk from small-scale retail agents amid a frozen construction market.

Regarding long-term investments, HSG records investments in subsidiaries at cost on its separate financial statements, recognizing impairment provisions when investees incur losses while rigorously monitoring capital recoverability. Subsequent to the close of the financial year (September 30, 2023), the Group prioritized comprehensive operational restructuring, consolidating a lean governance model and transitioning towards the specialization of independent business units to deploy resources for a new growth cycle.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

**Environment (E):** HSG achieved a major milestone in its green production roadmap, with Hoa Sen Steel products officially receiving ISO 14064-1:2018 certification (Greenhouse Gas Inventory) and ISO 14067:2018 certification (Product Carbon Footprint). This serves as a core competitive advantage, enabling HSG to clear stringent technical barriers (such as the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism - CBAM) in premium export markets including Europe and the US. The integration of advanced engineering technologies into manufacturing has also driven energy efficiency, reduced raw material wastage, and promoted circular economy models across its plants.

**Social (S):** The company maintains a steadfast commitment to occupational health and safety (OHS) standards across all manufacturing facilities and distribution networks. Human resource policies emphasize workforce rejuvenation through strategic partnerships with prestigious universities to cultivate the next generation of leadership. Concurrently, the Group continuously deploys professional training programs and robust welfare policies to secure employee retention amidst industry-wide challenges.

**Governance (G):** HSG’s corporate governance framework is aligned with best practices under the direct supervision of the Board of Directors and specialized committees, including the Audit Committee led by Independent Board Members (namely Mr. Nguyen Van Luan and Mr. Dinh Viet Duy). The participation of independent members ensures transparency and objectivity in internal control and the approval of related-party transactions. HSG’s sustainability report is meticulously prepared in accordance with global GRI standards and Vietnam’s Corporate Sustainability Index (CSI), reaffirming the highest standard of information transparency for shareholders and investors.
