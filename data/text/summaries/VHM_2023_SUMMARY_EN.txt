### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2023, Vinhomes JSC (VHM) shares experienced a drawdown of -10.00%, opening at VND 23,110 and closing at VND 20,800, with an intraday 52-week trading range of VND 17,690 to VND 31,870 and an average daily trading volume of 3,925,692 shares. Vinhomes' core growth catalyst continues to stem from the successful execution and operation of large-scale townships based on an "all-in-one" ecosystem model, epitomized by the rollout of Ocean City (spanning nearly 1,200 hectares with an estimated population of 200,000), which integrates three mega-urban developments in eastern Hanoi (Vinhomes Ocean Park 1, 2, and 3). The company emphasizes a strategy of creating four-season tourism, cultural, and entertainment destinations—such as the inauguration of the Kingdom of Light Square and the Grand World complex, which attracted nearly 160,000 visitors in late December 2023—complemented by synchronized green transportation infrastructure, including the 24/7 Vinbus electric bus network. Furthermore, the implementation of smart technology applications (such as the Vinhomes Resident app and Smart City Version 2.0 at Ocean Park 2 & 3) and the elevation of living standards via community club formations have firmly reinforced product desirability and sustained its market-leading position as Vietnam's premier property developer. This is underscored by prestigious accolades from BCI Asia Awards, the Asia Pacific Enterprise Awards (APEA), and Brand Finance, which valued the brand at USD 1.74 billion.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinhomes’ capital structure and liquidity profile throughout 2023 were characterized by large-scale financial transactions executed through partner lending channels and strategic investment cooperation agreements. Specifically, short-term corporate partner loans carried interest rates ranging from 7.0% to 11.0% p.a., with principal maturities predominantly concentrated in 2024. Notable transactions include a corporate partner loan of VND 2,892 billion (maturing between January and August 2024, carrying an interest rate of 7.0%–11.0% p.a., secured by shares and equity contributions), a VND 1,230 billion loan (maturing in August 2024 at 11.0% p.a.), and a VND 568.3 billion individual loan (maturing in December 2024 at 11.0% p.a., collateralized by listed equities within the Group). Cash flow pressures and capital structuring were also reflected in investment and business cooperation advances, including advances exceeding profit-sharing thresholds that accrue interest at 12.0% p.a., alongside strategic capital contributions such as VND 13,571 billion extended to inter-group entities for real estate development, VND 7,300 billion in earnest deposits, and VND 2,790 billion disbursed under construction investment cooperation agreements.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding asset risk and provisioning, Vinhomes reported a carrying value of VND 23,285 billion in inventories pledged as collateral with commercial banks to secure credit facilities for the Company, its subsidiaries, and related partners. Inventories are predominantly weighted toward land use rights, site clearance costs, and construction work-in-progress across flagship developments, including the Dream City Ecological Urban Area, the Dai An Project, Vinhomes Grand Park, Vinhomes Ocean Park, and Vinhomes Smart City. Property, plant, and equipment with a residual book value of VND 5,581 billion were similarly encumbered as loan collateral. Additionally, long-term investment and contingent risks stem from substantial advance deposits allocated for future investment purposes, including VND 12,000 billion deposited with partners to execute real estate project cooperation contracts and VND 10,646 billion deposited with external counterparties. Non-performing loan risk management and provision provisioning are rigorously maintained through continuous recoverability assessments of corporate and individual partners, alongside receivables originating from construction services, property management, and related-party transactions executed within the governance framework approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders and the Board of Directors.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vinhomes steadfastly operationalizes its sustainable development vision in alignment with Vietnam's national Net Zero trajectory. The company focuses on developing green urban developments with optimal construction densities, open architectural layouts designed to maximize natural lighting and ventilation, and a "two-in-one" green mobility infrastructure comprising electric buses, cars, and motorcycles to mitigate carbon emissions and noise pollution. Notably, in 2023, Vinhomes supported Vingroup’s "For a Green Future" fund by gifting 1,000 VinFast electric vehicles to apartment and villa purchasers. This was coupled with a series of "Green Living" initiatives—such as "Waste for Gifts," Earth Hour, Vinhomes Go Green, Vinhomes Run4Green, and Green Sunday—to proliferate sustainable lifestyle adoption and environmental stewardship.
- **Social (S):** Vinhomes’ social strategy centers on a human-centric approach, continuously elevating living standards and fostering community cohesion via the launch of two community clubs (the "Green – Civilized – Elite Living Club" and the "Joyful – Healthy Living Club") in January 2024. The company also prioritizes the development of a comprehensive educational ecosystem, highlighted by a strategic partnership with the Korea Global School (KGS) to establish two international multi-level school campuses at Ocean City (Hanoi) and Vinhomes Grand Park (HCMC), thereby delivering premium educational and residential welfare environments for residents.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance at Vinhomes is executed transparently under a rigorous oversight framework comprising the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, and the Supervisory Board. The company maintains a robust internal audit mechanism and strictly adheres to periodic and ad-hoc disclosure regulations on the securities market. Governance practices consistently comply with prevailing Vietnamese laws and international best practices, safeguarding the holistic interests of shareholders, investors, and stakeholders alike.
