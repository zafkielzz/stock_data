### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, the global oil and gas sector experienced a positive recovery, with Brent crude prices surging by up to 50% driven by global economic momentum (global GDP growth reaching 5.6%) and OPEC's production restraint policies. However, the Southeast Asian market continued to face fierce competitive pressures due to high oversupply in the jack-up rig fleet (as of late July 2021, only 33 out of 60 jack-up rigs were contracted, yielding a utilization rate of approximately 55%), while the domestic market in Vietnam was characterized by short-term and limited drilling programs. For PetroVietnam Drilling and Well Services Corporation (PVD), compressed revenue and profitability resulted in depressed Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA), though the Corporation successfully preserved key strategic bright spots. The primary growth catalyst was the successful completion of the investment and integration of a new Drilling Equipment Set (DES) onto the PV DRILLING V hull, alongside its triumphant redeployment to execute a long-term drilling campaign for Brunei Shell Petroleum Company Sdn Bhd (BSP) in Brunei starting January 2022. Furthermore, the well-technical services segment maintained its market-share leadership, and PVD successfully deployed three drilling rigs overseas for long-term campaigns, reaffirming its competitive capabilities in the international market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PVD’s financial structure maintained a sound and prudent profile in 2021, with total assets closing at VND 20,761 billion (down a marginal 0.5% YoY), of which current and non-current assets accounted for 26% and 74%, respectively. Total liabilities at year-end stood at approximately VND 6,930 billion, up 1.7% due to borrowings incurred for the DES investment, while the total debt-to-total assets ratio remained stable at approximately 33%. Liquidity metrics exceeded safety thresholds comfortably, with the current ratio registering at 1.79x and the quick ratio at 1.54x. Total outstanding debt at the end of 2021 reached VND 3,954 billion (representing 57% of total liabilities), stemming primarily from capital expenditures for the PV DRILLING V and PV DRILLING VI rigs, as well as the newbuild DES. Specifically: the loan for the PV DRILLING V rig via PVD Deepwater amounted to VND 382 billion ($16.87 million); the financing for the PV DRILLING VI rig via PVD Overseas stood at VND 3,026 billion ($133.66 million, having been successfully restructured in 2018 with a 7-year principal repayment extension from 2023–2030); and the DES newbuild loan via PVD Tech reached nearly VND 546 billion ($24.11 million, with an additional $14.35 million disbursed during the year). Total interest expense incurred for the year decreased slightly to VND 109 billion, with the interest coverage ratio standing at a robust 1.57x, establishing a solid cash flow foundation to service debt obligations from new projects.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding risk management, PVD reversed inventory write-downs amounting to VND 8,591,714,420 following a reassessment of inventory condition and economic utility (over 84% of inventory value comprises rig consumables, with an average inventory turnover of nearly 5x/year). In terms of credit risk, concerning the contract for the provision of the PV DRILLING III rig for the Apsara field in Cambodia, which concluded in February 2021, the parent company KrisEnergy Limited filed for liquidation in the Cayman Islands Court in June 2021, with Borrelli Walsh Ltd appointed as receiver. To maintain financial conservatism and prudence, the Board of Management resolved to provision 30% against the total outstanding receivables from KrisEnergy Apsara as of December 31, 2021, while actively cooperating with diplomatic agencies and partners to recover the debt. Regarding collateral, PVD pledged tangible fixed assets—specifically the PV DRILLING V and PV DRILLING VI rigs with a net book value of VND 6,713,260,910,000—as security for bank borrowings.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PV Drilling places strong emphasis on its Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality (HSEQ) commitments, maintaining an ISO 14001-certified Environmental Management System, rigorously monitoring emissions, and optimizing energy consumption across drilling operations.
- **Social (S):** The Corporation prioritizes occupational safety, notably ensuring safe working conditions amidst complex Covid-19 developments and recognizing the dedication and resilience of personnel who worked continuously onboard the PV DRILLING V rig from April 2021 to January 2022 without rotational crew changes due to travel restrictions. Additionally, PVD maintains competitive welfare policies, invests in high-quality human capital development, and actively engages in community social welfare initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** PVD operates a streamlined, standardized parent-subsidiary governance model aligned with international best practices, clearly delineating authorities among the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and the Board of Management. The Corporation leverages advanced management platforms such as Oracle ERP (Phases I-II-III), Maximo, ISO 31000, and RCM, while proactively executing a comprehensive digital transformation roadmap in accordance with Vietnam National Oil and Gas Group’s (PVN) strategy through 2025, with a vision toward 2030, aimed at optimizing cost structures and enhancing operational efficiency.
