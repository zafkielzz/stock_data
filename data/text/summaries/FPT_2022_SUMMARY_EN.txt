1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING:
In FY2022, FPT Corporation delivered robust financial performance despite macro headwinds and global economic volatility, posting consolidated revenue of VND 44,010 billion (+23.4% YoY) and profit before tax (PBT) of VND 7,662 billion (+20.9% YoY), translating to EPS of VND 4,429 (+22.4% YoY). The core growth engine was the Technology sector, which accounted for 59% of total revenue and 44% of consolidated PBT. Within this division, Offshore IT Services experienced stellar momentum, with revenue surging 30.1% YoY, led by the US market (+50%) and the APAC region (+35.7%), alongside a solid recovery in Japan (+16% in VND terms and +30.3% in JPY terms). New overseas contract sales surpassed the USD 1 billion milestone for the first time, underpinned by 31 large-scale projects valued at over USD 5 million and 204 projects exceeding USD 1 million.

Additionally, Digital Transformation (DX) revenue reached VND 7,349 billion, representing 39% of offshore IT revenue. The "Made by FPT" proprietary product ecosystem generated VND 1,150 billion in revenue (+54.3% YoY), highlighted by the launch of medical IoT microchip lines and the commercialization of the akaBot platform. The Telecommunications segment maintained steady growth driven by the expansion of FPT Play and FPT Cloud, while the Education segment cemented its mega-scale status with 108,100 converted learners, scaling campus infrastructure across Hai Phong, Bac Ninh, Bac Giang, and Ha Nam.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES:
FPT’s capital structure reflects prudent liquidity management and disciplined financial risk oversight. The Group maintains a substantial allocation of short-term bank deposits (ranging from 3 to 12 months) and long-term deposits (>12 months), establishing a robust liquidity buffer to support operating cash flows. The debt and financial lease portfolio comprises short- and long-term borrowings with maturities spanning under 3 years, 5 years, and exceeding 5 years. Foreign currency-denominated debt is primarily concentrated in USD and JPY.

To mitigate foreign exchange volatility and floating interest rate risks on foreign currency borrowings, FPT employs a proactive hedging strategy utilizing derivative financial instruments, including currency swaps and forward exchange contracts. Furthermore, the Group benefits from a natural hedge through substantial foreign currency inflows from software export contracts, effectively minimizing cash flow imbalances and interest expense pressures amid a volatile global interest rate environment.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS:
In receivables management, FPT maintains a diversified client base, with short-term accounts receivable exceeding 2% of total receivables involving key counterparties such as LG Electronics, Cox Automotive Corporate Services, Vietcombank, and the Bangladesh National Board of Revenue. The Group executes provisioning for doubtful debts and inventory write-downs in strict compliance with accounting standards for overdue receivables or work-in-progress production costs carrying impairment risks.

The Supervisory Board confirmed that no abnormal self-serving transactions occurred during FY2022 between the Group and related parties of the Board of Directors (BOD), Executive Management, or Supervisory Board. The enterprise recorded no material legal disputes or contingent liabilities arising from off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments that could exert a material adverse effect on its financial position. Subsequent to the balance sheet date of December 31, 2022, no extraordinary financial events transpired that would alter the substance of the figures reported in the published Financial Statements.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION:
- Environmental (E): FPT accelerated its green transition by scaling the "Made by FPT" ecosystem, optimizing cloud infrastructure (FPT Cloud), and deploying process automation (akaBot), thereby assisting thousands of corporate clients (including Petrolimex, Ba Huan, and Thien Long) in curbing energy consumption and paper waste.
- Social (S): The Group allocated over VND 171 billion to CSR initiatives, directly supporting 47,777 beneficiaries. A notable social highlight was the operation of Hope School, which provides upbringing and education for 200 orphans impacted by Covid-19. FPT fostered "Great Place to Work"-certified environments across Japan, the US, and Singapore while upgrading human capital capabilities through the issuance of over 8,700 new international certifications during the year.
- Governance (G): The Board of Directors and Executive Management operated transparently under the stringent supervision of the Supervisory Board (maintaining 100% attendance across 4 BOD meetings during the year). Internal governance systems were upgraded utilizing real-time data integration via the FPT Data Lake project. The enterprise strictly adheres to its Code of Conduct and Professional Ethics, earning recognition among the Top 50 Sustainable Development Companies in 2022.
