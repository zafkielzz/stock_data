**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2024, Vinhomes recorded robust operational performance that surpassed budgetary targets, underpinned by disciplined commercial execution and accelerated property handovers across major township developments. Growth was primarily driven by the effective monetization of mega-projects, most notably Vinhomes Royal Island (Vu Yen, Hai Phong), spanning 877 hectares with a total investment outlay of USD 2.4 billion. The project registered a record-breaking ~2,000 reservation bookings within just one month of launch, alongside over 500 commercial townhouses and shophouses handed over within six months of commercialization. Additionally, Vinhomes Global Gate (Dong Anh, Hanoi) served as a critical growth catalyst, anchored by its strategic positioning and the integration of the National Exhibition Center. High-profile events—such as the 8Wonder Winter 2024 music festival, the "Call Me By Fire" mega music concert at Ocean City, and recurring activities at Grand World—significantly enhanced regional real estate capital values. In terms of market reach and distribution channels, the digital transformation strategy, spearheaded by the launch of the Vinhomes Market online transaction platform, captured over 8.2 million visits (a 258% surge during the initial two-month period), effectively optimizing the sales pipeline. Regarding equity market performance, ticker VHM exhibited price volatility over the year, opening at VND 20,850 and closing at VND 19,260 (representing an annual contraction of 7.63%), with an intraday peak of VND 23,380 and a trough of VND 16,370, alongside an average matched trading volume of 9,559,926 shares per session.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
During 2024, Vinhomes executed the largest treasury share buyback in the history of the Vietnamese equity market, successfully acquiring 247 million VHM shares (representing an aggregate consideration of approximately VND 11 trillion) between October 23 and November 21, 2024. This transaction reflects a proactive capital management strategy designed to optimize the capital structure, reduce the number of shares outstanding, and consequently accrete existing shareholders' ownership stake while enhancing key financial metrics, notably basic earnings per share (EPS). Regarding short- and long-term liquidity management, the Company maintained term bank deposits yielding interest rates ranging from 2.5% to 6.0% p.a. (down from 3.5% to 7.6% p.a. in the previous year). Cash and cash equivalents comprised VND-denominated deposits held with credit institutions carrying original maturities of 1 to 3 months, earning interest between 1.9% and 4.75% p.a., alongside VND 1,105.5 billion in restricted cash earmarked for operational activities. In its lending portfolio, Vinhomes recorded short-term corporate and individual partner loans carrying interest rates from 7.5% to 12% p.a., alongside long-term loans extended to three corporate counterparties totaling VND 10,893 billion maturing in July 2026, with coupon rates ranging from 11% to 12% p.a.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
Vinhomes’ risk management framework is demonstrated through rigorous oversight of inventories, non-performing assets, and off-balance-sheet financial commitments. Inventories remained substantial, heavily concentrated in flagship developments including Vinhomes Ocean Park 2, Vinhomes Ocean Park 3, Vinhomes Grand Park, Vinhomes Smart City, Vinhomes Golden Avenue, and the Cam Ranh Bay coastal urban area. Notably, VND 13,017 billion of inventory book value was pledged as collateral to secure credit facilities extended to the Company, its subsidiaries, and corporate partners. With respect to receivables and advances, Vinhomes maintained site-clearance advances and investment-cooperation deposits with various partners and related parties (such as a VND 12,196 billion capital contribution to an affiliated entity within the Group for real estate development, and VND 18,334 billion in profit-sharing receivables due from Vingroup). Non-performing assets comprised overdue or impaired receivables, advances, deposits, and loans extended to corporate and individual counterparties. Furthermore, as of December 31, 2024, the enterprise recorded maintenance funds for completed apartments and villas, comprising VND 1,761 billion in apartment maintenance funds transferred to the respective Building Management Boards, while villa maintenance funds remained under the direct administration of the Company and its subsidiaries.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** Vinhomes emphasizes the integration of sustainable urban development practices aligned with environmental stewardship. A notable milestone was the execution of a comprehensive Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Japan’s Mitsubishi Corporation, focusing on energy transition, research into energy-optimization solutions to curb $CO_2$ emissions, infrastructure-integrated urban development, and the cultivation of a low-carbon society.
- **Social (S):** The company prioritizes enhancing community well-being and resident experience by integrating Digital Mapping and upgrading the Vinhomes Resident application into a Super App to seamlessly address daily lifestyle requirements. Additionally, Vinhomes reinforced its market leadership through prestigious accolades, including "Vietnam National Brand" and "Best Residential Real Estate Developer in Vietnam 2024" awarded by Euromoney, acknowledging its continuous drive to elevate product and service quality for society.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance is anchored by a commitment to optimize and streamline operational workflows, thereby maximizing return on investment and sharpening competitive positioning. Vinhomes places strong emphasis on cultivating corporate culture—particularly in leadership development and onboarding training—while upholding transactional transparency via digital platforms such as Vinhomes Market, thereby consolidating investor confidence in the company’s long-term compounding growth potential.
