Below is the qualitative equity research summary for Saigon Thuong Tin Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Sacombank – ticker: STB), based on the disclosures in the 2021 Annual Report and verified financial data:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
In 2021, despite unprecedented challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic, the stock market and trading activity of STB shares recorded a strong breakout. STB's share price performance witnessed an impressive growth of +84.75%, moving from an opening price of VND 17,050 to a closing price of VND 31,500 (with a peak of VND 33,900 and a trough of VND 15,500). Average matched trading volume achieved high liquidity at 26,982,506 shares per session, reflecting robust institutional and retail cash flow interest in the bank's restructuring narrative and core growth potential. Core growth drivers during the year stemmed from accelerating a comprehensive digital transformation strategy, optimizing the credit portfolio toward priority sectors, scaling up retail banking services, and effectively managing operating expenses to cushion provisioning pressures stemming from the restructuring scheme.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As a specialized credit institution operating in the banking sector, Sacombank's funding structure relies on customer deposits, the issuance of valuable papers, and shareholders' equity, rather than traditional commercial borrowings typical of manufacturing enterprises. The bank maintained its Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR) in compliance with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) regulations while tightly managing its cost of funds (COF) through the flexible adjustment of its funding mix between Current Account Savings Accounts (CASA) and term deposits, amid a low-interest-rate environment maintained to support the economy. Capital structure risks and liquidity pressures were managed through rigorous liquidity risk governance limits, ensuring the capacity to meet credit demand and systemic payment obligations.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Credit risk management and provisioning remained the focal point of legacy asset resolution strategies under Sacombank's post-merger restructuring scheme. The bank maintained a prudent non-performing loan (NPL) coverage ratio to absorb potential shocks from the NPL portfolio and foreclosed assets awaiting resolution. Off-balance-sheet commitments (including letters of credit, guarantees, and loan commitments) were managed within approved risk limit frameworks. Regarding legal and compliance aspects, the 2021 Annual Report was officially and transparently published in a timely manner as prescribed on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE), the Hanoi Stock Exchange (HNX), and the State Securities Commission (SSC) on April 18, 2022, confirming the absence of any irregular legal events or material occurrences that would impact the bank's going-concern status subsequent to the financial year-end.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** Sacombank has progressively integrated environmental and climate risk factors into its credit appraisal process for investment projects. The bank prioritizes capital allocation toward green, energy-efficient, and sustainable development sectors, while promoting a paperless office environment through the robust digitization of internal operational workflows and customer transactions to minimize its carbon footprint.
- **Social (S):** The bank emphasizes building a professional working environment, ensuring occupational health and safety (OHS) standards, and fully implementing welfare and insurance policies for its workforce. Regular training programs are deployed to enhance the professional competencies and digital skill sets of employees. Additionally, Sacombank upholds its corporate social responsibility through community initiatives, social welfare programs, and support for disadvantaged groups nationwide.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to Vietnamese legal regulations applicable to listed credit institutions. The Board of Directors maintains independence and exercises rigorous oversight over the Executive Board's operations. Annual financial statements are audited by reputable independent auditing firms, ensuring transparency, accuracy, and compliance with stock market disclosure standards.
