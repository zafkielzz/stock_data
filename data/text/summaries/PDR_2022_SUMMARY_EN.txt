### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2022, Phat Dat Real Estate Development Corporation (PDR) navigated the most challenging operating environment in the Vietnamese real estate sector for over a decade, absorbing severe macro shocks and uncontrollable variables that rendered initial contingency plans obsolete. Nevertheless, through intense focus and agile operational adaptation, PDR successfully secured a resilient year-end close, generating a profit before tax (PBT) of VND 1,482 billion and a profit after tax (PAT) of PAT of VND 1,161 billion, closely approaching its revised guidance. Total assets expanded by 11.15% YoY from VND 20,552 billion to VND 22,843 billion; total equity grew by 13.7% YoY from VND 8,145 billion to VND 9,261 billion; and charter capital increased by 36.3% YoY to VND 6,716 billion. PDR’s core business momentum centered on highly absorbable asset segments—underpinned by multidimensional assessments of consumer absorption capacity, localized demand, and execution readiness—highlighted by the Cadia Quy Nhon project in Binh Dinh and other key developments in emerging, high-growth markets.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Amid a tightening financial liquidity landscape, PDR proactively conducted a comprehensive balance sheet review, deploying optimal financial mechanisms to safeguard liquidity. The company maintained robust liquidity profiles, fully honoring all principal and coupon payments for debt facilities and corporate bonds due within the period—either on schedule or ahead of maturity. Regarding capital structure, the strengthening of total equity (reaching VND 9,261 billion, up 13.7% YoY) and charter capital (reaching VND 6,716 billion, up 36.3% YoY) significantly reinforced internal balance sheet fundamentals. Despite acute cash flow pressures driven by systemic volatility, PDR’s prudent and flexible resource-allocation strategy successfully insulated the firm from systemic solvency risks, ensuring sufficient financial flexibility to absorb subsequent market shocks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
PDR maintained rigorous Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) practices by integrating strategic oversight with internal control frameworks aligned with industry best practices. To ensure financial transparency, the company engaged an independent auditor from the Big 5 accounting firms. During the fiscal year, PDR systematically restructured short-term advances for equity and project acquisitions (such as the Song Han - Da Nang project, Bien Xanh project, and Phuoc Hoa - Vung Tau project) alongside long-term receivables originating from investment cooperation agreements (such as the Vung Bau - Phuoc Quoc project and the Thu Duc ICD logistics hub). All related-party transactions and debt obligations were rigorously monitored and approved to safeguard the best interests of the company and its shareholders.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PDR integrated stringent environmental and social compliance mandates into its procurement criteria for contractors and suppliers, emphasizing sustainable development benchmarks throughout project lifecycles to balance economic growth with ecosystem preservation across host municipalities.
- **Social (S):** The Board of Directors strictly enforced comprehensive welfare, occupational health and safety, and human capital development programs, while fostering employee engagement in corporate governance. PDR’s professional working environment was validated by prestigious accolades, including the Top 100 Best Places to Work in Vietnam, Top 10 Best Workplaces in the Real Estate Industry, and Top 50 Most Attractive Employers.
- **Governance (G):** PDR adopted 09 core corporate governance principles benchmarked against best practices from the State Securities Commission (SSC) and the International Finance Corporation (IFC, August 2019). The Board of Directors exhibits a high degree of independence, with over two-thirds of its members being non-executive and at least one-third serving as independent directors, while the roles of Chairman and Chief Executive Officer are strictly segregated. The company established an Audit Committee under the Board—comprising independent and non-executive members—alongside an Internal Audit Department operating under institutional standards. PDR enforces a zero-tolerance anti-corruption and anti-bribery policy, instating a corporate Code of Conduct as its ethical foundation while ensuring transparent, timely disclosure of material information to domestic and international shareholders and investors.
