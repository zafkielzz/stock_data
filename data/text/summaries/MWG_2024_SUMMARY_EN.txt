Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative and financial summary based on the 2024 Annual Report of Mobile World Investment Corporation (MWG):

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2024, MWG posted a robust business recovery amid domestic purchasing power remaining flat or increasing slightly, driven by a comprehensive restructuring strategy centered on the philosophy of "Reducing Quantity - Enhancing Quality." Consolidated net revenue reached VND 134,341 billion (fulfilling 107% of the plan and increasing by 13.6% YoY compared to 2023), with the online channel contributing VND 9,600 billion (accounting for 7%). Profit after tax broke out impressively to VND 3,733 billion, surging 21-fold compared to the low base of 2023 and achieving 156% of the annual target.

The core momentum stemmed from the TGDĐ and ĐMX chains, which achieved nearly VND 89,500 billion in revenue, maintaining market share and profit margins through store network optimization and effective cost control. Notably, the Bach Hoa Xanh (BHX) chain reached a historical turning point with revenue growing by 30% to over VND 41,000 billion (accounting for 30.6% of the revenue mix), officially turning a full-year profit at the company level after two years of restructuring. In addition, the Erablue consumer electronics retail chain in Indonesia recorded more than a 4-fold increase in revenue, operating 87 stores and beginning to turn a profit from Q3 2024. Regarding the product category mix, mobile phones and personal devices accounted for 35.1%, consumer electronics accounted for 27.8%, food & FMCG accounted for 30.6%, with the remainder coming from other product lines.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As of December 31, 2024, MWG’s total assets reached VND 70,438 billion, up 17.2% year-to-date. Total liabilities stood at VND 42,316 billion, predominantly comprising short-term debt with short-term borrowings reaching VND 27,300 billion (up significantly from VND 19,128 billion at the beginning of the year), while long-term debt was fully paid off to VND 0. Equity stood at VND 28,122 billion, up 20.4% on the back of higher retained earnings.

Interest expense for the year was recorded at VND 1,137 billion, down from VND 1,447 billion in the previous year, thanks to proactive monitoring of interest rate fluctuations and cash flow optimization. Regarding credit relationships, MWG maintained flexible credit lines with major domestic and international banks; notably, in December 2024, its subsidiary Mobile World Corporation signed a Sustainable Commercial Credit Facility Agreement with BNP Paribas to secure capital for business operations and the development of emission-reduction initiatives.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
MWG maintains a stringent risk management policy for inventory and receivables. At the end of 2024, inventory value stood at VND 22,668 billion, for which the company set aside an inventory write-down provision of VND 423 billion to prudently reflect real values and hedge against slow-moving inventory risks across retail chains. Short-term trade receivables were tightly controlled at VND 241 billion out of total short-term assets of VND 65,836 billion.

Regarding legal matters and commitments, the company recorded no material litigation disputes affecting operations. Inefficient subsidiaries such as MWG (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, 4K Farm, and Toan Tin Logistics are undergoing dissolution or restructuring procedures to streamline operations and concentrate resources on core business segments. The consolidated financial statements audited by Ernst & Young Vietnam recorded no qualified opinions or abnormal events arising after December 31, 2024.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** MWG aggressively deployed sustainable development initiatives such as installing solar energy systems across more than 600 stores and equipping IoT systems at over 1,700 stores to automate operations, optimize energy consumption, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2). The company also promoted a circular economy through plastic waste collection and recycling programs, exemplified by the "Recycle Old Toothbrushes - Build a Green Future" campaign in collaboration with Colgate at the Bach Hoa Xanh chain.
- **Social (S):** With a large workforce, MWG prioritizes compensation policies, capacity-building training, and absolute occupational health and safety (OHS) compliance across its supermarket and warehouse systems. During the year, the company contributed VND 5 billion through the Vietnam Fatherland Front Committees of various provinces and cities to support residents in overcoming the aftermath of Typhoon Yagi, while rolling out practical charity programs such as "Tet Sharing - Millions of Warm Meals" and "Prosperous Tet - Sending Peace of Mind."
- **Governance (G):** The company maintains a transparent governance structure with a 9-member Board of Directors, including 3 independent members (accounting for 30%), ensuring objectivity and a clear separation between the roles of the Chairman and the CEO. Independent audits are conducted by Ernst & Young Vietnam. MWG strictly complies with information disclosure regulations, prepares Sustainability Reports in accordance with international GRI standards, and aims to apply advanced governance standards under the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) and G20/OECD principles.
