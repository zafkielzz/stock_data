Below is the professional institutional equity research translation of the financial summary for Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
In 2023, DGC reported consolidated net revenue of VND 9,748 billion (-32.0% YoY) and net profit after tax of VND 3,241 billion (-46.3% YoY). The contraction was primarily driven by unfavorable market conditions and fierce competition from Chinese products, which triggered a sharp decline in average selling prices for key items such as yellow phosphorus, compounded by double-digit declines in global demand. Nevertheless, several product lines recorded notable positive growth, including 85% $H_3PO_4$ up 43.5%, various phosphate fertilizers up 2.9%, and detergent products up 48.9%. Regarding market mix, export revenue accounted for a dominant 78.1% share (VND 7,609 billion, down 35.6% YoY), while domestic revenue reached VND 2,139 billion, representing 21.9% of total revenue. Maintaining a large export share denominated in foreign currency provides the Group with a robust USD inflow, mitigating foreign exchange risks in procurement and daily operations.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DGC maintains a highly sound capital structure, with total consolidated assets reaching VND 15,535.9 billion as of year-end 2023. Total liabilities represented a modest 22.5% of total capital at VND 3,509.9 billion (comprising VND 3,492.9 billion in short-term debt and a negligible VND 16 billion in long-term debt). Short-term borrowings primarily consist of VND and USD credit lines to supplement working capital with major commercial banks, including Vietcombank (the largest facility at VND 830 billion), BIDV (VND 895 billion), Indovina, Shinhan Bank, and HSBC. Short-term borrowing rates are pegged at competitive levels, ranging from 1.9% to 3.6% per annum depending on the currency. With a low debt-to-equity ratio (0.29x) and trillions of VND in cash and short-term deposits, DGC faces no material cash flow pressure or liquidity risk.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
DGC’s provisioning policy is executed prudently and aligns closely with underlying business realities. As of December 31, 2023, the Group recorded short-term doubtful debt provisions of VND 4.93 billion and inventory write-down provisions of VND 212.4 million against a total inventory value of VND 855.1 billion. Regarding off-balance-sheet obligations, the Group maintains long-term land lease commitments with minimum future payments exceeding VND 432 billion, alongside unexecuted capital commitments for factories, machinery, and equipment valued at over VND 631.9 billion. Notably, financial obligations related to the environmental restoration and rehabilitation of the Apatite ore mine at Mining Area 25 are estimated at over VND 8 billion (subject to periodic escrow deposits). Regarding subsequent events, in January and February 2024, the Group completed the full payment of the remaining dividend balance as of December 31, 2023, to existing shareholders utilizing retained earnings.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DGC strictly complies with environmental protection laws, recording no major environmental incidents in 2023. The Group focuses on technological investments to recover 100% of waste gas emissions from its phosphorus plant—thereby reducing direct electricity consumption—while also capturing waste heat. Furthermore, internal wastewater treatment systems achieve a reuse rate of 90–95%, optimizing water resources and minimizing external discharges.
- **Social (S):** Total headcount across the Group stood at 2,528 employees, with a stable average monthly salary of VND 22 million per employee. Labor policies strictly adhere to the Labor Code, ensuring comprehensive social and health insurance benefits, regular health check-ups, and occupational safety gear. The company also allocates tens of billions of VND to cultural, athletic, tourism, and social welfare programs, while maintaining a multi-billion VND budget for local community sponsorships and charitable contributions.
- **Governance (G):** DGC’s corporate governance apparatus operates under the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), Board of Directors (BoD), Supervisory Board, and Board of Management. Transparency is upheld through timely information disclosure, the retention of reputable independent auditors (such as PwC) for financial statement audits, and adherence to public company governance regulations and Vietnamese securities laws.
