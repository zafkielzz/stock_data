Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative and financial analysis summary for Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC), based on its 2022 Annual Report and financial statements:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2022 marked a breakthrough operational period for DGC, with consolidated net revenue reaching VND 14,444.1 billion, representing a robust year-on-year growth of 51.2%. Profit after tax attributable to shareholders of the parent company reached VND 5,565.0 billion, more than 2.3x higher than in 2021 and exceeding the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) target by 72%. Core growth drivers stemmed from maintaining maximum operational capacity across all plants, alongside fully capitalizing on a favorable global market cycle for key product lines such as yellow phosphorus, phosphoric acid, and various fertilizer products. According to geographical segment reporting, exports served as the overwhelming revenue driver, contributing VND 11,826.4 billion (equivalent to 81.9% of total consolidated revenue), while the domestic market accounted for VND 2,617.7 billion (18.1%). The substantial export revenue share, coupled with a robust USD cash reserve held in foreign currency accounts, enabled DGC to optimize operational efficiency and establish a natural hedge against foreign exchange risks on USD-denominated debt.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
As of December 31, 2022, DGC’s financial structure maintained a high level of safety, with total assets expanding by 57.3% to VND 13,405.2 billion and equity growing by 71.1% to VND 10,833.7 billion. The total liabilities-to-total-capital ratio remained low at 19.2%. Regarding the debt profile, total short-term borrowings were recorded at VND 2,571.4 billion, while long-term debt stood at zero. Short-term loans were primarily utilized to supplement working capital and finance collateralized assets (secured by term deposits and inventories), backed by major strategic banking partners including Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - Hanoi Branch, Transaction Office), Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (ACB - Hoang Cau Branch), Maritime Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MSB), and Shinhan Bank Vietnam Limited. Short-term borrowing rates were competitive, ranging from 3.0% to 3.7% per annum for both VND and USD-denominated loans, exerting negligible financial pressure on operating cash flows.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Provisioning practices were executed conservatively by DGC in strict compliance with accounting standards. For inventories, the carrying value stood at VND 999.9 billion with no provision for obsolescence or price decline recorded, supported by sound inventory management and a rapid turnover rate (inventory turnover reached 6.4x). For receivables, bad debt risks were tightly controlled via stringent commercial credit policies applied to both third parties and related parties. Regarding contingent liabilities, the Group recognized long-term environmental rehabilitation and restoration obligations for the Apatit ore mine at Mining Area 25 under Decision No. 1057/QĐ-BTNMT (with total estimated costs of approximately VND 8.0 billion fully provisioned and contributed to the Lao Cai Provincial Environmental Protection Fund). Furthermore, the financial statements recorded no adverse legal disputes or material extraordinary financial events occurring after the accounting period ended December 31, 2022.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DGC emphasizes mitigating environmental impacts through substantial investments in exhaust gas and wastewater treatment systems (with billions of VND invested in new projects in 2022). The Group applies circular economy solutions, such as recovering waste heat from phosphorus furnaces to reduce direct electricity consumption, achieving a 90–95% water recycling rate across its plants, and systematically planting greenery across industrial park facilities to advance its sustainable development goals.
- **Social (S):** Total headcount reached 2,305 employees, with average monthly compensation maintained at a high level of VND 23 million per employee. DGC strictly adheres to occupational health and safety (OHS) regulations, provides comprehensive insurance coverages and periodic health check-ups, and maintains robust employee welfare, housing assistance, subsidies, and regular skills training programs. Additionally, the Group contributed over VND 6.7 billion to social welfare, charity, education, and local community initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance operates transparently under the structure of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), Board of Directors (BoD), Supervisory Board, and Board of Management (General Director and deputies). The BoD comprises members with deep expertise in the chemical industry, ensuring full compliance with the Law on Enterprises and the Law on Securities. Consolidated financial statements were independently audited by PwC (Vietnam) Company Limited – Hanoi Branch, ensuring fairness, accuracy, and full compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS).
