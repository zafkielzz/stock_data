Below is a standard qualitative analysis summary based on the 2022 Annual Report and verified actual figures of Vietnam International Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VIB):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, VIB posted outstanding business results with profit before tax reaching VND 10,581 billion, up 32.1% YoY, marking its sixth consecutive year of robust growth and its third consecutive year of maintaining a Return on Equity (ROE) of 30%—leading the listed banking sector. The core driver stemmed from the Retail Banking Division, which delivered a profit exceeding VND 7,000 billion (up 40% YoY). Within this segment, revenue grew by 27%, while effective cost control helped improve the Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR) from 41% to 39%. 

Retail lending reached nearly VND 211,000 billion, accounting for 90% of total bank-wide credit outstanding (up 18%), leading market share in auto loans and ranking in the top 2 industry-wide in bancassurance with Annual Premium Equivalent (APE) reaching VND 1,868 billion. VIB's credit card segment achieved a breakthrough, with the number of cards in circulation reaching nearly 616,000 and total card spending surging over 85% to nearly VND 75,000 billion, capturing a 33% market share of Mastercard in Vietnam. This was driven by a 100% digitalized process strategy and the application of "Embedded Finance" technology. Meanwhile, the Corporate Banking Division recorded a 16% increase in total income (net fee income up 71%), and the Treasury & Foreign Exchange Division continued to cement its market-leading position in Government bond and FX swap trading volumes.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWING & INTEREST RATES
VIB maintained a robust balance sheet with charter capital increasing to VND 21,077 billion (up 35.7%). Total mobilized funds reached VND 231,899 billion, with customer deposits and valuable papers managed flexibly to optimize the cost of funds amid volatile market interest rates. The bank proactively diversified its international medium- and long-term funding sources, highlighted by the successful drawdown of a USD 150 million 5-year facility from the International Finance Corporation (IFC) and the mobilization of over USD 1 billion in short-term trade finance operations (such as a 2x increase in Usance Payable At Sight Letters of Credit - LC UPAS).

Liquidity safety ratios were strictly controlled in compliance with Basel II and Basel III standards: the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) stood at 75.63%; the ratio of short-term funds used for medium- and long-term loans was 30.11% (well below the State Bank of Vietnam's cap of 37%); the average liquid asset ratio was 16.9%; and the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 12.77% – 12.8%, placing VIB among the top tier in the industry.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Credit risk management and asset quality were executed stringently based on advanced quantitative models (Application scorecard, Behaviour scorecard) and a high proportion of lending backed by fully-legalized real estate collateral. The Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio was contained at 1.79% as of December 31, 2022. VIB maintained a very low proportion of corporate bond and real estate lending relative to total outstanding credit to mitigate systemic risks.

Regarding stock market volatility, VIB shares experienced general market downward pressure in 2022, opening at VND 16,330 and closing at VND 8,730 (down 46.54%). The stock traded within a 52-week range of VND 7,440 to VND 16,690, with average matched trading volume reaching 2,367,337 shares/session. No material legal disputes or abnormal financial events occurred post-balance-sheet date that would adversely affect the bank's status as a going concern.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** VIB strictly adheres to environmental protection regulations, optimizes water and energy resource consumption across its business locations, and promotes the digitalization of internal processes and customer interactions to reduce paper and ink consumption. The bank enforces fuel consumption norms for transport vehicles meeting Euro 4 emission standards and selects eco-friendly air conditioning equipment.
- **Social (S):** The bank ensures a democratic, transparent, and safe working environment for over 10,200 employees (with female staff accounting for approximately 50% of the total headcount and holding 55.2% of management positions at Head Office). VIB maintains competitive compensation and welfare policies, comprehensive capability development training programs via the VIBLearning platform (over 333,857 training hours in 2022), and actively participates in social security, educational sponsorship, and community activities.
- **Governance (G):** VIB applies advanced corporate governance models in line with international standards. Its Board of Directors comprises 5 members (including 1 independent member who also serves as the Chair of the Human Resources Committee), ensuring transparency and a clear separation between governance, executive management, and control functions (the 3-lines-of-defense model under Circular 13/2018/TT-NHNN). Independent audits are conducted by top-tier reputable auditing firms, ensuring full compliance with the principles of the State Securities Commission (SSC), the IFC, and global sustainable development standards.
