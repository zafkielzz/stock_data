### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2021, SSI Securities Corporation posted record-breaking financial results, with net revenue surging 72% YoY to VND 7,786 billion and profit before tax (PBT) jumping 116% YoY to VND 3,365 billion, exceeding the annual target by 60%. This core growth was catalyzed by the unprecedented equity market boom in Vietnam: the VN-Index peaked at 1,500.81 points, average trading liquidity expanded 252% YoY to VND 26.6 trillion per session, and 1.5 million new retail accounts were registered. This outperformance was broadly driven by all key business segments, including Securities Brokerage, Principal Investments, Treasury and Financial Services, Investment Banking, and Asset Management. The firm proactively scaled up its human capital and technological infrastructure to seamlessly absorb surging market liquidity while effectively capturing tactical business opportunities to maximize top-line growth. On the macroeconomic front, despite supply chain disruptions stemming from the pandemic, nationwide export turnover still reached $336.3 billion (up 19% YoY) alongside a trade surplus of $4.1 billion, reinforcing a stable macroeconomic foundation for the financial sector.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
SSI's capital structure underwent a notable structural shift driven by scale expansion to support core operations. Most notably, the entirety of the 1,150 million convertible bonds issued on February 9, 2018 (carrying a 4.0% coupon rate and an initial discount rate of 7.76%) were converted by bondholders into 46,859,491 ordinary shares in March 2021 at a conversion price of VND 24,541 per share. Additionally, SSI maintained short-term working capital credit facilities with major commercial banks, including Vietcombank, BIDV, VietinBank, and other financial institutions. These borrowings are collateralized by FVTPL financial assets (comprising 5.9 million shares with a par value of VND 59 billion, 4,380 unlisted corporate bonds with a par value of VND 780 billion, and certificates of deposit with a par value of VND 6,100 billion) alongside term deposits and certificates of deposit with maturities under one year totaling VND 7,310 billion. Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks were well-mitigated, underpinned by ample systemic liquidity from the banking sector and historically low interest rate environments throughout 2021.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
SSI maintained a prudent risk management framework governing its financial asset portfolio and receivables. The company recorded provisions and reversals for impairment losses on margin lending, doubtful debt allowances for corporate services (such as receivables from Phuc Bao Minh Trading and Construction Co., Ltd., Nghe An General Friendship Hospital JSC, and Dabaco Group Vietnam), and provisions for Available-for-Sale (AFS) financial assets. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, SSI recorded pledged financial assets for margin lending at a par value of VND 15,375.8 billion (with a substantially higher fair value) as of December 31, 2021, alongside VND 441.785 billion in restricted cash/deposits held as collateral for structured warrants issued by the company. Major post-balance-sheet events primarily revolved around the completion of bonus share issuances, rights offerings to existing shareholders, and a 10% cash dividend payout to strengthen the firm's financial capacity.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Operating as a securities and financial services institution, SSI focuses on optimizing operational efficiencies and digitalizing trading workflows to reduce paper consumption and energy usage, while strategically steering investment capital toward sustainable asset portfolios.
- **Social (S):** SSI oversees a workforce of over 1,300 employees backed by competitive, equitable, and comprehensive compensation frameworks, ensuring workforce stability throughout the pandemic. The company demonstrated robust corporate citizenship by allocating nearly 80% of its annual community budget to Covid-19 relief efforts, supporting frontline healthcare workers and vulnerable communities.
- **Governance (G):** The firm strictly complies with public corporate governance regulations, ensuring transparency via comprehensive bilingual (Vietnamese-English) disclosures. Shareholder rights are fully protected on a non-discriminatory basis for both domestic and foreign investors, under the rigorous oversight of the Board of Directors, the Audit Committee, and the Internal Control Department.
