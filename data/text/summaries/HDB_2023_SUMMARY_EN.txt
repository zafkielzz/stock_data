### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In FY2023, HDBank delivered a record profit before tax (PBT) of VND 13,017 billion (+26.8% YoY), fulfilling its annual target driven by robust balance sheet expansion and superior operational efficiency. Total assets crossed the threshold of VND 602 trillion (+44.7% YoY), underpinned by stellar credit growth of 31.8% YoY (exceeding VND 353 trillion). Core operational momentum was spearheaded by two main pillars: deep market penetration into tier-2 urban areas and agri-rural regions, alongside strong digital banking tailwinds.

Digital transformation initiatives served as a key productivity engine, driving a 107% YoY surge in digital users and an 88% YoY increase in digital transaction volume. Crucially, the penetration rate of retail digital transactions rose from 77% in 2022 to 94% in 2023, marking the first year where digital channels outperformed traditional brick-and-mortar branches in acquiring new customers. Concurrently, ecosystem monetization via the "Partnership Banking" model and cross-selling through the SkyJoy customer base generated solid synergies across retail and supply chain financing segments. In the equity market, HDB stock performance reflected robust business fundamentals with strong liquidity (average matched volume of 4,299,449 shares/session), delivering an impressive share price gain of +52.74% (opening at VND 8,210 and closing at its year-high of VND 12,540), elevating market capitalization to VND 58,719 billion (+46.4% YoY).

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

HDBank’s capital structure expanded significantly, with total capital mobilization exceeding VND 536 trillion, representing a 46.5% YoY increase over FY2022. To optimize the cost of funds (CoF) amid interest rate volatility, the bank decisively executed its targeted CASA program to raise demand deposit ratios and mitigate funding cost pressures. Regarding liability structure and interbank liquidity, placement and lending activities with other credit institutions (CIs) were prudently maintained, predominantly allocated to standard (Group 1) assets.

The bank also scaled up foreign currency UPAS L/C (Usance Payable at Sight Letter of Credit) refinancing facilities. Under this structure, HDBank acts as the funding provider for importing clients, while the issuing bank assumes full liability to reimburse principal, interest, and fees at maturity, thereby hedging direct credit risk exposure. Looking ahead to FY2024, HDBank aims to further diversify its long-term funding mix via planned issuances of Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital instruments to bolster its Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and support long-term credit growth.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Amid sector-wide asset quality pressures (with the system-wide NPL ratio reaching 4.95% at year-end FY2023), HDBank maintained tight asset quality control, reporting standalone and consolidated NPL ratios of 1.5% and 1.8%, respectively. The bank maintained a conservative provisioning policy across customer loans and interbank exposures to preserve a robust loss-absorption buffer.

HDBank emerged as a pioneer among domestic commercial banks in fully implementing Basel III Reforms risk management standards, enhancing its structural resilience against market shocks. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, contingent liability risks from guarantees and L/C issuances were tightly managed via rigorous limit approval frameworks. Notably, the bank approved detailed plans to acquire an equity stake in a securities firm to convert it into a subsidiary/affiliate pursuant to AGM Resolutions, pending regulatory approval. HDBank is also proactively mobilizing resources to participate in the Government and State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) mandate for restructuring distressed credit institutions.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** HDBank is committed to supporting the Government’s Net-Zero by 2050 mandate through green credit expansion, circular economy financing, and funding for renewable energy and clean agriculture projects. The bank collaborated closely with major Development Finance Institutions (DFIs) including IFC, DEG, Proparco, and Leapfrog to upgrade its Environmental and Social Risk Management (ESMS) framework, embedding green metrics into its credit underwriting process.
*   **Social (S):** The bank actively advanced financial inclusion across remote and rural regions. A key corporate social responsibility milestone was the strategic partnership with Fulbright University Vietnam, committing matching funding equivalent to USD 20 million for campus development and high-quality education. Internally, the bank prioritized employee welfare, fostering a professional and equitable working environment.
*   **Governance (G):** HDBank was awarded "Board of the Year" by the Vietnam Institute of Directors (VIOD), reflecting its high corporate governance and transparency standards. The bank strictly adheres to the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS). Its governance structure maintains a clear separation of roles, with the Chairman of the Board of Directors (Mr. Kim Byoungho) distinct from the Chief Executive Officer (Mr. Pham Quoc Thanh). During the year, the Board conducted 114 written solicitations to facilitate timely decision-making. The governance framework fully complies with information disclosure and related-party transaction regulations, maintaining its position within HoSE’s Vietnam Sustainability Index (VNSI).
