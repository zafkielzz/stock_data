### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In FY2024, FPT Corporation sustained its robust top and bottom-line expansion, posting a 19.4% revenue increase and a 20.3% growth in profit before tax (PBT), translating to a 22.0% rise in earnings per share (EPS). This outperformance propelled the Group's market capitalization past the USD 9 billion threshold. Growth was predominantly catalyzed by the Technology sector, which generated a 24.4% revenue increase, accounting for 62.2% of consolidated revenue and 47.2% of consolidated PBT. The division underscored its strengthening international competitiveness by securing landmark mega-contracts, notably a USD 225 million project in the US, a USD 115 million contract in Germany, and a USD 110 million engagement in Singapore. The Digital Transformation (DX) segment remained a key structural driver, maintaining a top-line growth trajectory of 40–45% per annum.

Anchoring its strategic pivot around the five pillars of "AI, Semiconductor, Automotive, Digital, and Green," FPT advanced its semiconductor value chain by fulfilling orders for 70 million chips, launching 10 proprietary chip designs, and partnering with NVIDIA to establish two AI Factories in Vietnam and Japan. Within automotive technology, the Group deepened collaborations with Tier-1 suppliers on digital cockpit and instrument cluster/HUD solutions. Beyond technology, the Telecommunications segment emerged as a bright spot, spearheaded by its Data Center business, which surged nearly 50% year-on-year on the back of aggressive Wi-Fi 6 infrastructure deployment. The Education segment scaled its footprint to 152,000 enrolled students across more than 30 provinces and municipalities. Concurrently, cross-border M&A activity was intensified to capture broader market share, highlighted by the strategic acquisitions of AOSIS Consulting SARL (France) and NAC Co., Ltd. (Japan).

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

FPT’s capital structure heavily utilizes unsecured bank borrowings to optimize the weighted average cost of capital (WACC) while funding working capital requirements. Short-term debt, deployed primarily for liquidity management, carries floating and fixed interest rates ranging from 1.04% to 6.60% per annum (compared to 0.00% to 8.00% p.a. in FY2023). Long-term debt, utilized to finance capital expenditures and the acquisition of fixed assets, bears interest rates between 0.28% and 6.90% p.a. (versus 0.00% to 6.75% p.a. in FY2023).

Cash flow pressures and foreign exchange (FX) risk exposures denominated in foreign currencies (USD, JPY, EUR) are actively managed via derivative financial instruments, including cross-currency swaps and forward contracts. Furthermore, the Group maintains a natural operational hedge via substantial foreign currency inflows generated from USD- and JPY-denominated software export contracts. Capital expenditure (CapEx) during the year was heavily skewed toward strategic infrastructure, including the AI Factory, the District 9 Data Center, F-Town 3, Hoa Lac 3, and the FPT University campus in Da Nang.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

FPT maintains a conservative provisioning policy, recognizing strict allowances for inventories and doubtful short- and long-term trade receivables generated through normal course of business operations. Trade receivables exhibit moderate concentration risk, with key institutional accounts—namely Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (VCB), LG Electronics, and PETRONAS Digital Sdn Bhd—each accounting for over 2% of total receivables. Regarding short-term prepayments to suppliers, exposures exceeding 10% are concentrated within two counterparties: Moore Law Consultancies LLC and The Forest City.

Financial statements indicate that the Group maintains sound financial risk controls. Off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments and material legal disputes do not pose any adverse financial implications. During the year, the Group finalized the fair value allocation for prior-year business combination transactions in strict compliance with applicable accounting standards. No anomalous financial events occurred post-December 31, 2024, that would materially alter the interpretation of the period-end financial figures.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

- **Environmental (E):** FPT formally committed to a pioneering Net-Zero roadmap by 2040 through its participation in the Vietnam Low Emission Energy Program II (V-LEEP II). The Group has scaled its comprehensive carbon management service offerings (including carbon auditing, reporting, and offsets) to enterprise clients, while retaining its position among the top 20 constituents of the HoSE VNSI (Vietnam Sustainability Index).
- **Social (S):** The Group enforces the international ISO 45001:2018 occupational health and safety standard and has consistently earned "Best Places to Work" accolades across the US, Japan, Germany, Singapore, and the Philippines. FPT prioritizes human capital development, targeting AI skill enablement for 500,000 workers and 50,000 AI engineers by 2030. Social responsibility initiatives during the period included post-Typhoon Yagi educational reconstruction efforts, financial relief for 1,400 affected employee households, and ongoing sponsorship of long-term philanthropic projects such as Hope School (Truong Hy Vong).
- **Governance (G):** The Group instituted corporate guidelines on "democratic centralized leadership" alongside an expanded ESG policy framework, achieving a 97.5% training completion rate among its workforce. Executive leadership serves as founding members of the High-Level Council for the Vietnam ESG Forum. FPT strictly adheres to best-practice disclosure standards, consistently ranking in the Top 3 large-cap enterprises for excellence in Investor Relations (IR).
