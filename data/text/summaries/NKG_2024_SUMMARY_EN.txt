**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2024, Nam Kim Steel Joint Stock Company (NKG) posted resilient operational results amidst ongoing macroeconomic volatility and cyclical fluctuations in the global steel sector. Total revenue reached VND 20,707 billion, achieving 98.6% of the full-year target, while profit before tax (PBT) came in at VND 558 billion, outperforming the target at 132.8%. Notably, net profit after tax (NPAT) exhibited an impressive year-over-year surge of 285.8%, reaching VND 453 billion. The core growth driver stemmed from the successful capitalization of the domestic market recovery—supported by Vietnam Steel Association (VSA) data indicating a 26.9% and 5.5% expansion in industry-wide sales volumes for galvanized sheet and steel pipe, respectively—coupled with an agile export strategy across key geographies. NKG successfully maintained its position as one of the top three domestic market-share leaders in galvanized steel, underpinned by competitive advantages in product differentiation, compelling pricing strategies, and an extensive distribution network spanning major international markets, including the US, Mexico, Australia, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

- **Environmental (E):** The "green steel" transition remains a pivotal global and sectoral strategic imperative aimed at substantially abating carbon emissions. Advanced technologies shaping the industry's future include electrochemical processes powered by renewable energy, green hydrogen utilization (which curtails carbon emissions by up to 95% compared to conventional methodologies), and proactive compliance initiatives addressing the EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) to safeguard international competitiveness.

Management’s strategic vision centers on commercial agility, preparing for the safe, high-quality, and cost-efficient execution of the Nam Kim Phu My Steel Plant Project, fully aligned with sustainable development standards.
