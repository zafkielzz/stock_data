Below is the qualitative equity research summary for Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (ACB) based on its 2024 Annual Report:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2024, ACB delivered positive earnings growth, underpinned by a flexible strategy and effective risk management amid a volatile macroeconomic environment. Group profit before tax reached VND 21,006 billion, up 5% YoY and achieving 95% of the annual target. Total revenue rose 2.3% to VND 33,515 billion, driven by an 11.4% increase in net interest income (NII) to VND 27,795 billion. Conversely, non-interest income declined 27% to VND 5,720 billion, pressured by an unfavorable investment and securities market. This was cushioned by the retail segment, which reinforced its role as a core driver: individual customer credit grew by 15.8%, individual deposit mobilization expanded by 12% (reaching approximately 230 thousand customers with a payment turnover of VND 23 trillion), and the CASA ratio improved to 23.3% of total deposits. The corporate banking division also reported a 25% increase in credit outstandings, most notably a striking 150% YoY growth in the FDI client segment. Performance across subsidiaries (ACBS, ACBA, ACBL, and ACBC) continued to contribute positively to the Group’s overall efficiency, with ACBS doubling its profit and ACBL achieving 29% growth in finance lease outstandings.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
ACB’s capital structure was maintained at a sound and robust level to comply with stringent Basel III standards and State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) regulations. As of year-end 2024, charter capital stood at VND 44,667 billion (up from VND 38,841 billion in 2023), with total equity reaching VND 83,462 billion (up 18%). The consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 11.82%, well above the 8% regulatory minimum, against total risk-weighted assets (RWAs) of VND 675,593 billion. Regarding funding mix, total customer deposits reached VND 537 trillion (up 11.3%), with the Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio improving to 23.3%. The bank proactively balanced its assets and liabilities, applying flexible market interest rates to mitigate margin compression (NIM at 3.62%), while keeping the short-term funding for medium- and long-term loans ratio at 18.78% (comfortably below the 30% regulatory cap) to ensure stable liquidity and ease short-term cash flow pressures.

### 3. PROVISIONING, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Amid industry-wide upward pressure on non-performing loans (NPLs), ACB successfully preserved top-tier credit quality within the domestic banking sector. The NPL ratio stood at 1.49% in 2024 (inching up from 1.21% in 2023, yet remaining below the 2% target ceiling). Total loans in Groups 3–5 (NPLs) amounted to VND 8,650 billion. Credit risk provisioning expenses for 2024 were VND (1,606) billion, down VND 198 billion YoY, with an NPL coverage ratio of 78%. Provisions for long-term investment depreciation and doubtful debts recorded a net reversal of VND 17 billion. Regarding legal matters and off-balance-sheet commitments, the overdue guarantee ratio over total guarantees was 0.28%. Operational, market, and compliance risk management (including AML controls, Basel III implementation, and preparations for the Internal Ratings-Based [IRB] approach) were closely monitored. No material extraordinary financial events occurred between December 31, 2024, and the reporting date.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
ACB maintained a strong commitment to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards:
- **Environmental (E):** The bank established a Sustainable Finance Framework and became the first bank in Vietnam to publish a standalone ESG report. Total greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions in 2024 reached 32,656.9 metric tons of $CO_2$-eq (with Scope 2 accounting for 83.1% and Scope 1 for 11.9%), translating to an emission intensity of 2.4 tons/employee/year. ACB advanced energy-saving initiatives and comprehensive digital transformation (reducing paper consumption by 42% via e-forms and Green CSR initiatives), with green credit outstandings reaching hundreds of billions of VND to support the Net-Zero 2050 target.
- **Social (S):** Total headcount at ACB reached 13,290 employees by the end of 2024 (female employees accounting for 66%). The bank maintained robust occupational health and safety (OHS) policies, reporting zero major workplace accidents. Average employee income reached VND 487 million/year (up from VND 441 million in 2023), supported by a comprehensive welfare system and large-scale training programs (834,570 training hours, equivalent to 64 hours/employee/year). ACB also contributed approximately VND 107 billion to community development and local social welfare initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance aligns with international standards, featuring independent Board of Directors members to ensure objectivity in executive oversight. Independent audits were conducted by KPMG with an unqualified (clean) audit opinion. The bank strictly complies with disclosure regulations, GRI standards, and the sustainable development orientations set by regulatory authorities.
