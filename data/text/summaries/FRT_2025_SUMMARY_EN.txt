### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2025 marked a robust operational breakout for FPT Retail (FRT) against the backdrop of Vietnam's positive macroeconomic recovery, with GDP growth reaching 8.02%. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 51,083 billion, representing a year-on-year (YoY) top-line expansion of 27% and achieving 106% of the annual target. Consolidated profit before tax (PBT) surged by an impressive 131% YoY to VND 1,219 billion, fulfilling 135% of the guidance. The company-wide gross profit margin was sustained at 19.92%, serving as the springboard for a Return on Equity (ROE) of 7.54% and a Return on Assets (ROA) of 1.03%. The efficacy of the omnichannel strategy was clearly demonstrated by online revenue hitting VND 9,344 billion, up 30% YoY.

FRT’s core growth drivers stemmed from the synchronized recovery and breakthrough of both strategic business segments:
*   **ICT Retail Segment (FPT Shop):** Recorded a pivotal turnaround following a comprehensive restructuring, contributing VND 16,809 billion in revenue (up 11% YoY) and officially returning to profitability with VND 82 billion in PBT. The chain optimized its operational efficiency across a footprint of 623 stores by converting over 130 points of sale into the "FPT Shop Điện Máy" (Consumer Electronics) format to diversify revenue streams. High-end product launches yielded stellar outcomes, with double-digit sales growth for the iPhone 17 Series and a 40% YoY increase for the Galaxy Z series. Additionally, the FPT Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) achieved an 80% surge in subscribers, crossing the milestone of 720,000 users driven by pioneering the integration of authentic SIM activation via the VNeID national population database.
*   **Healthcare Segment (FPT Long Châu):** Remained the primary core growth catalyst, contributing 68% to total consolidated revenue at VND 34,501 billion (a 36% YoY increase). The chain rapidly expanded its service network to 2,640 locations, comprising 2,417 pharmacies (474 newly opened stores) and 223 vaccination centers (97 newly opened centers). Long Châu’s leadership position was firmly reinforced through breakthrough technological applications, becoming the first pharmacy chain to enable citizens to purchase medications online via the VNeID identification application.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Given the characteristics of a large-scale retail model and the aggressive store rollout strategy of the Long Châu chain, FRT’s capital structure exhibits a relatively high degree of financial leverage. Financial metrics indicate a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 381.51%.

As detailed financial statement notes were not provided in this reporting period, specific figures regarding short-term versus long-term debt composition, the allocation ratio between fixed and floating interest rates, and the identities of primary credit-providing banking partners are currently undisclosed. From an analytical perspective, maintaining a D/E leverage ratio approaching 3.8x reflects significant cash flow pressure stemming from financing costs. Despite a sharp improvement in earnings capacity—exemplified by a 131% increase in PBT—FRT must rigorously manage capital structure risks and liquidity profiles to ensure that operating cash flows are sufficient to service short-term debt obligations arising from inventory financing and working capital requirements at newly opened outlets.

Detailed information regarding provisions for inventory obsolescence, allowances for doubtful accounts, off-balance sheet guarantee commitments, material legal disputes, or financial events occurring after December 31, 2025, was absent from management’s current operational reports.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

FPT Retail’s sustainable development strategy is deeply integrated into its core business operations under the guiding ethos of "Pioneering Technology - Elevating Health," aiming to balance stakeholder interests:
*   **Environmental (E):** FRT achieved preliminary recognition in standardizing sustainable development criteria, earning the Committed Badge from the reputable rating agency EcoVadis. Although detailed quantitative metrics regarding Scope 1/2/3 greenhouse gas emissions and the Net-Zero roadmap have not been disclosed, the company is actively fostering the digital economy by optimizing paperless workflows, integrating public services via VNeID, and deploying technological solutions to conserve energy and optimize operational resources across the entire retail network.
*   **Social (S):** The enterprise underscored its proactive community engagement through the 5th iteration of the "Long Châu Sẻ Chia" (Long Châu Sharing) program, delivering 7 tons of medicines and medical supplies to flood-affected regions and sponsoring 500,000 doses of measles vaccines for the Ministry of Health. Internally, adhering to "People as the Foundation," FRT is committed to cultivating a safe, equitable working environment, prioritizing professional capability training, and securing optimal welfare policies for frontline store personnel.
*   **Governance (G):** FRT’s corporate governance framework is operated through a robust structure comprising the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and a professional risk management system. The company is committed to upholding integrity and transparency in information disclosure to build steadfast shareholder confidence, aligning with modern governance standards to generate sustainable, long-term value.
