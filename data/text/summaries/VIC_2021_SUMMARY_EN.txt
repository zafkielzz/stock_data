### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2021, Vingroup's strategic pivot toward the Technology–Industrial sector was aggressively reinforced, anchored by VinFast as its core strategic driver amidst the group’s proactive divestment from several non-core verticals. The primary growth catalyst stems from VinFast’s milestone globalization push, highlighted by the official debut of its EV brand at the 2021 Los Angeles Auto Show and CES 2022, alongside the announcement of its strategic phase-out of internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicle manufacturing by late 2022 to transition entirely to pure battery electric vehicles (BEVs).

In both domestic and international markets, EV product lines demonstrated robust demand, registering over 24,000 global pre-orders for the flagship electric SUVs VF 8 and VF 9 within 48 hours of launch, alongside nearly 4,000 deposits for the VF e34 within 12 hours of its domestic rollout. The rollout of the inaugural VF e34 batch on December 25, 2021, marked a historic milestone, placing Vietnam on the global map of clean-energy automotive manufacturing markets. Furthermore, the technology ecosystem continued to expand with the ground-breaking of the VinES battery manufacturing plant in Ha Tinh (Phase 1 total capex of VND 4 trillion), VinBigData’s pilot testing of Level 4 autonomous electric vehicles and human genomic decoding, and VinAI’s installation of the NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD supercomputer to power artificial intelligence research.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vingroup’s capital structure and liquidity profile in FY2021 were characterized by large-scale capital-raising activities via domestic and international credit and bond channels to finance flagship projects such as VinFast and Vinpearl. Short-term investments held to maturity predominantly comprised VND-denominated term deposits with tenors ranging from over 3 months to 1 year, yielding interest rates between 3.0% p.a. and 6.5% p.a. (compared to 2.0% p.a. to 7.1% p.a. in FY2020), a portion of which represents apartment maintenance fees awaiting handover to the Building Management Board.

Long-term investments held to maturity comprised 8-to-10-year bank bonds, bearing a floating coupon rate calculated as a benchmark rate plus (+) a spread of 0.8% p.a. to 1.2% p.a. Regarding lending activities, the group maintained corporate and individual partner loans maturing in 2022, of which VND 6,916 billion carried an interest rate of 9.5% p.a. and VND 7,410 billion bore an interest rate of 9.0% p.a., both secured by listed/unlisted equities and other collateral agreements. Cash flow pressure was tightly managed through credit facilities and intra-group financial guarantee operations.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vingroup’s credit and asset risk management framework in FY2021 was reflected in its conservative provisioning policies. Specifically, the group recognized provisions of VND 92.7 billion for irrecoverable short-term advance payments to suppliers (up significantly from VND 41.3 billion in FY2020). The group’s non-performing loans (NPLs)—comprising trade receivables, supplier advances, and overdue principal and interest on loans—were closely monitored, with recoverable amounts subjected to regular periodic impairment assessments.

Regarding guarantee commitments and contingent liabilities, the group incurred notable guarantee obligations during the year, including payment guarantees for international bonds issued by Vinpearl JSC, alongside payment guarantees for corporate bond tranches and credit facilities issued/incurred by VinFast Trading and Production LLC in FY2021. Collateral assets securing debt obligations included investment properties with a residual carrying value of VND 3,600 billion pledged with commercial banks.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vingroup demonstrated a robust commitment to sustainable development goals by steering its entire automotive product lineup exclusively toward pure EVs starting in late 2022, developing the VinBus smart electric bus network, and investing in clean energy technology (the VinES battery plant). The group prioritizes resource conservation and emissions management through the issuance of a Sustainable Financing Framework and the mobilization of ESG-linked capital in international markets.
- **Social (S):** The group regards its workforce as its most valuable asset, maintaining a professional, humane work environment and delivering exceptional welfare policies. Within the community, Vingroup left a distinct mark through flagship corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, including Vinschool’s digital education program and the 2021 Edurun charity run, which attracted 30,000 participants and raised VND 2.2 billion to construct schools for children in remote highland areas. Notably, starting in 2022, Vingroup reduced its direct contribution rate to the Thien Tam Fund from 90% to 10%, while the Chairman and employees continue their direct involvement to ensure a long-term equilibrium of interests between shareholders and stakeholders.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to legal frameworks and international best practices, bolstered by the active participation of independent Board members (namely Mr. Adil Ahmad, Mr. Chin Michael Jaewuk, and Mr. Ronaldo Dy–Liacco Ibasco). Independent audits, internal audit functions, and rigorous risk management systems operate cohesively to guarantee maximum transparency in disclosure and investor relations.
