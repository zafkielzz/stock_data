### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2023, Vingroup maintained its focused development strategy centered on three core pillars: Technology-Industry, Commerce-Services, and Philanthropy. The group's primary growth drivers stemmed from VinFast’s aggressive pivot toward a pure electric vehicle (EV) business model, alongside the stable earnings contributions from Vinhomes' mega-developments and the broader ecosystem spanning hospitality, retail, education, and healthcare.

Within the Technology-Industry segment, VinFast achieved strategic milestones by completing its EV line-up across the A to E segments (VF 5, VF 6, VF e34, VF 7, VF 8, VF 9), facilitating domestic deliveries, and scaling up international exports. Its landmark public listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (USA) under the ticker symbol "VFS", alongside the groundbreaking of its North Carolina plant (USA), initiation of its India manufacturing facility, and the launch of dealerships in Indonesia, underscored its burgeoning global footprint in the EV landscape. Tech subsidiaries such as VinAI maintained their position among the global top 20 artificial intelligence research firms with the rollout of the PhởGPT large language model, while VinBigdata launched "Vietnam's ChatGPT" equivalent, ViGPT, capturing over 22,000 user registrations and generating fresh technological momentum for the group.

Within the Commerce-Services segment, Vinhomes successfully handed over nearly 15,000 residential units across its mega-urban developments while inaugurating Grand World within the Ocean City cluster to enhance commercial foot traffic and vibrancy. The education vertical (Vinschool expanded to 50 campuses serving over 46,000 students), healthcare vertical (Vinmec’s strategic partnership with Cleveland Clinic), and health-tech sector (VinBrain, with its DrAid™ software receiving US FDA clearance) continued to reinforce the group’s high-value service revenue foundation.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DỢ VAY & INTEREST RATES
Vingroup’s capital structure and liquidity profile in 2023 reflected substantial capital expenditure requirements to finance VinFast’s global expansion strategy and real estate pipeline. Cash and cash equivalents as of December 31, 2023, comprised term deposits (yielding annual interest rates of 2.1% to 4.0% for tenors under 3 months, and 3.2% to 8.0% for tenors ranging from 3 months to 1 year) alongside corporate bond investments (yielding an 8.5% annual coupon). Notably, the group reported restricted and pledged cash balances (including apartment maintenance funds for real estate projects) totaling VND 354 billion within cash equivalents and VND 402 billion within bank deposits.

Regarding debt and receivables, the group maintained short- and long-term corporate and individual lending portfolios carrying interest rates ranging from 7.0% to 12.0% per annum. Cash flow and liquidity pressures necessitate rigorous management of operating cash flows, investing cash flows (driven by capital expenditures on non-current assets, lending, and equity contributions), and substantial earnest money deposits allocated for investment and project development purposes (such as deposits of VND 12,000 billion, VND 10,646 billion, and VND 9,500 billion extended to strategic partners and prospective real estate projects).

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vingroup’s risk management and provisioning framework is evidenced by specific asset encumbrances and contingent liabilities on the financial statements. Regarding inventory, as of December 31, 2023, the group held inventories with a carrying value of VND 23,435 billion pledged as collateral for corporate bank borrowings, alongside VND 402 billion pledged to secure credit facilities for select third-party partners.

Receivables, advances to suppliers (such as advance payments made to site-leveling contractors), and loans extended are rigorously monitored regarding aging profiles and recoverability to ensure adequate provisioning. Regarding capital market fluctuations and post-balance sheet events, VIC’s share price in 2023 traded within a 52-week range of a low of VND 19,350 and a high of VND 38,300, closing the year at VND 22,300 (down 17.10% year-to-date against the opening price), with an average matched trading volume of 5,073,320 shares per session. This performance reflects broader macroeconomic and market sentiment toward large-cap equities characterized by capital-intensive, long-term strategic investments.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Vingroup remains steadfast in executing its sustainable development strategy under the banner of "Creating a Green Future," seamlessly integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria across its entire business ecosystem.

- **Environmental (E):** The group acts as a domestic pioneer in driving Vietnam's green transition via its smart electric vehicle and electric bus (VinBus) manufacturing model, thereby mitigating greenhouse gas emissions and supporting the Government's net-zero carbon emissions target by 2050. The "For a Green Future" Foundation was established to advocate for sustainable lifestyles and elevate public eco-awareness. Member companies such as VinBrain actively leverage technology to eliminate physical medical film waste by digitizing diagnostic results via digital files and QR codes.
- **Social (S):** Vingroup upholds its core philosophy of treating human capital as its most valuable asset, fostering an equitable, integral corporate culture that promotes synergy. Within education and healthcare, the Vinschool network (serving over 46,000 students) and Vinmec/Vinmoke consistently elevate their standards to meet international benchmarks (such as achieving Council of International Schools - CIS accreditation), delivering comprehensive social welfare and healthcare value to the community.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance is executed in strict compliance with prevailing legal regulations, supported by transparent reporting channels from the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and the internal audit apparatus. The group maintains a professional governance framework that upholds its six core values (Credibility, Dedication, Speed, Refinement, Gratitude, and Humanity) while reinforcing a strict adherence to the rule of law across all operational facets.
