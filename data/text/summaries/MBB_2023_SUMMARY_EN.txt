### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2023, MBB registered financial performance that significantly outperformed the broader banking sector. Consolidated profit before tax (PBT) reached VND 26,306 billion, with the parent bank acting as the primary profit engine by contributing VND 24,688 billion, while subsidiaries accounted for VND 2,046 billion. Core growth was propelled by aggressive credit expansion of over 28.8% (with outstanding loans to the economy hitting VND 615 trillion), vastly outstripping the systemic average of ~13.5%. This drove total assets up by 29% year-on-year to approximately VND 950 trillion, allowing MBB to sustain industry-leading operating efficiency with a return on equity (ROE) of ~25%.

MBB’s accelerated scaling is intrinsically tied to its comprehensive digital transformation strategy. The bank onboarded 6.2 million new clients in 2023, expanding its total customer base to over 26.5 million. The share of transactions executed via digital channels remained remarkably high at over 96.7%. Alongside traditional retail banking, MBB scaled up its corporate services segment by integrating Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) solutions—connecting over 600 APIs with financial, logistics, and brokerage partners—and developing the "MB Market Place" mini-app to evolve the MBBank application into a super-app. On the equity market, MBB shares positively reflected this growth trajectory, delivering a +27.79% total return for the year and closing at VND 9,520 per share with average daily liquidity exceeding 9.04 million shares per session.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
MBB’s funding structure and liquidity profile were proactively managed amid financial market pressures concentrated in the real estate and corporate bond segments. System-wide deposit growth hovered at ~10.85%, while MBB focused on optimizing its capital structure through Tier 2 subordinated bond issuances via private placements and public offerings spanning the 2023–2024 period.

Regarding its dividend policy and charter capital, MBB’s Board of Directors (BOD) executed cash and stock dividend payouts to increase charter capital, thereby reinforcing the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and building a robust capital buffer to support credit expansion. Pressures on funding costs and liquidity were effectively mitigated by a stable low-cost current account savings account (CASA) deposit base, underpinned by an ecosystem of 26.5 million clients and a nationwide payment network.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
In credit risk management, MBB strictly complied with statutory provisioning mandates to counteract systemic asset quality degradation and rising non-performing loans (NPLs). During the period, the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) granted provisional approval for MBB to expand its international foreign exchange operations (Decision 1985/QĐ-NHNN), thereby broadening the scope of its off-balance-sheet forex business.

Fiscal year 2023 also marked a pivotal senior leadership transition: Mr. Lê Hữu Đức stepped down as Chairman of the Board of Directors, with Mr. Lưu Trung Thái elected as the new Chairman, while Mr. Phạm Như Ánh was appointed Chief Executive Officer. This generational handover was executed seamlessly, ensuring strategic continuity. Furthermore, the BOD restructured investment projects in Laos and Cambodia (transitioning the MBCambodia operating model) to optimize cross-border operational risk management.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** MBB reinforced its "Green Bank" mandate as green credit outstanding rose to VND 56,829 billion (up 22% year-on-year vs. 2022), with capital concentrated in clean energy, renewable power, and green industries. The bank digitized 90% of internal operations (advancing toward a 100% paperless target) and reaffirmed its commitment to the Government’s Net-Zero 2050 roadmap.
- **Social (S):** MBB contributed approximately VND 7,518 billion to the State Budget and allocated roughly VND 200 billion toward more than 100 social welfare initiatives. The bank deployed the synergistic CSR campaign "HiGreen," mobilizing VND 8.1 billion to transform illegal dumping sites into ecological green spaces and community playgrounds.
- **Governance (G):** MBB adheres to transparent, standard-compliant governance protocols, tightly controlling related-party transactions (specifically contracts exceeding and falling below 20% of charter capital). Remuneration for the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board was approved at 1.2% of net profit after tax pursuant to the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) Resolution. The bank maintains disclosure standards aligned with Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) sustainability reporting frameworks.
