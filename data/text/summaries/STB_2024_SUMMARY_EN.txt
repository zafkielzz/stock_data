**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Sacombank’s growth trajectory remained robust and positive throughout FY2024, marking a pivotal milestone as the bank nears the completion of its structural restructuring program. Core growth momentum was driven by the capitalization on market opportunities, anchored by the strategic pillars of "Accelerating Operations – Enhancing Efficiency through Digital Foundations" and its pioneering role in retail banking. Financial performance was highlighted by a strong breakout in consolidated profit before tax, achieving 120% of the target mandated by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders. Profitability ratios improved significantly, with Return on Assets (ROA) expanding by 0.2% and Return on Equity (ROE) increasing by 1.73%. In terms of franchise scale, the bank successfully expanded its ecosystem to 19 million clients, while service quality was underscored by a Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) score of 99.4% and a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 90%.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
As of December 31, 2024, Sacombank’s charter capital remained unchanged at VND 18,852,157 million, represented by 1,885,215,716 common shares listed on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HoSE) under the ticker STB. Regarding capital adequacy and operational solvency, the consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 10.14%, recording a 103 bps increase period-over-period. The bank has fully provisioned 100% against its entire legacy non-performing and unresolved asset portfolio, thereby fulfilling all financial obligations set forth in the post-merger Restructuring Scheme. Legacy issues pertaining to cross-ownership, equity investments exceeding regulatory limits, and treasury shares have been definitively resolved, substantially strengthening the equity capital structure and alleviating cash flow pressures stemming from historical legacy assets.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
Sacombank’s risk management framework was materially upgraded via the expanded implementation of Basel III standards and the finalization of financial statements prepared under IFRS 9. On-balance-sheet NPL ratio was tightly controlled at 2.08%. During the year, the recovery and resolution volume for non-performing loans and legacy assets reached nearly VND 10 trillion, bringing the cumulative recovery rate of post-merger legacy assets to over 81%. For the remaining unrecovered legacy portfolio, the bank has proactively provisioned 100% against credit risk. The primary remaining legal hurdle required for the bank to comprehensively finalize its Restructuring Scheme and pave the way for dividend distributions to shareholders centers on the resolution of debt secured by STB shares, which is currently being actively pursued by Executive Management and pending regulatory approvals.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** Sacombank proactively integrated global ESG standards into its sustainable development strategy. The bank accelerated its transition toward a green finance model, extending financing to environmentally friendly projects, driving digital transformation to minimize paper usage, optimizing operational energy efficiency, and fostering a circular economy across the enterprise.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritized human capital development, with total group headcount reaching 18,088 employees at year-end 2024. Robust employee welfare, training programs, and corporate culture initiatives were emphasized to ensure a professional and secure working environment, alongside an active commitment to corporate social responsibility through major social welfare programs.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance operated in alignment with advanced international standards under the rigorous oversight of the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board. The bank's reputation and governance capabilities continued to be recognized by international credit rating agencies, highlighted by Moody’s long-term credit rating of B2 (Stable Outlook) and Fitch Ratings of BB- (Stable Outlook), alongside its inclusion in the Top 50 Sustainable Development Companies in Vietnam for 2024 under the Excellence in Corporate Governance category.
