**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2021, Vietnam Dairy Products Joint Stock Company (Vinamilk) achieved consolidated net revenue exceeding VND 60 trillion for the first time, reaching VND 61,012 billion, up 2.2% YoY and achieving 98.2% of the full-year target. Core growth momentum was driven by resilient supply chain execution and distribution network optimization amid the COVID-19 pandemic, which exerted significant pressure on purchasing power. Segment-wise, domestic net revenue reached VND 51,202 billion (+0.7% YoY), underpinned by double-digit growth in the modern trade channel, the opening of 120 new "Dream Milk" stores (bringing the total to nearly 600 outlets), and a threefold expansion in the e-commerce channel. Direct export net revenue grew 10.2% YoY to VND 6,128 billion, while revenue from overseas subsidiaries rose 11% YoY to VND 3,589 billion. Key international markets—including the Middle East, the US, and Cambodia—maintained robust momentum, with two new export destinations added, expanding Vinamilk’s footprint to 57 countries. Subsidiary Moc Chau Milk (MCO) reported net revenue of VND 2,926 billion (+3.6% YoY) and net income of VND 319 billion (+13.7% YoY). However, consolidated gross profit margin contracted by 326 bps YoY to 43.1% due to surging raw material cost pressures. Consequently, consolidated net income reached VND 10,633 billion, fulfilling 94.6% of the annual plan, with a net profit margin (NPM) of 17.4% and basic EPS of VND 4,517.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Amid a favorable interest rate environment in 2021, Vinamilk leveraged its long-standing banking relationships to optimize financial leverage for operating activities. Both the debt-to-total-assets and debt-to-equity ratios recorded upward trends compared to FY2020. The company's liquidity metrics remained stable and superior to the previous year, ensuring adequate financial headroom to service upcoming debt obligations. Vinamilk continued to execute an efficient working capital management policy, improving asset turnover ratios and dynamically adjusting trade credit terms for both buyers and suppliers to mitigate global supply chain disruptions.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
Vinamilk’s financial management and internal control systems operated rigorously throughout 2021 to mitigate pandemic-induced headwinds. The company maintained disciplined inventory management despite domestic and international supply chain bottlenecks. Regarding compliance and stakeholder governance, Vinamilk strictly enforced its Code of Conduct, implemented robust related-party transaction policies, and executed a zero-tolerance policy against corruption and bribery. No conflicts of interest were identified during the fiscal year. Financial statement disclosures reflect close monitoring of cash and cash equivalents, bank deposits, short- and long-term investments, and provisioning in accordance with prevailing accounting standards (VAS).

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
*   **Environmental (E):** Vinamilk remains committed to integrating sustainability criteria into its agricultural and manufacturing value chains. Raw material sourcing and supply chain operations adhere strictly to international standards (such as GLOBALG.A.P. across its dairy farms), focusing on energy optimization, waste management, and circular economy adoption to minimize the carbon footprint across its network of 14 domestic and international farms and 17 manufacturing plants.
*   **Social (S):** The company places human capital at the core of its strategy, enforcing rigorous occupational health and safety (OHS) standards, welfare policies, and talent development programs. During the severe pandemic disruptions of 2021, Vinamilk successfully deployed safe-production models (such as the "three-on-site" arrangement), established infection-risk screening protocols, and actively fulfilled its corporate social responsibility (CSR). Initiatives included community support, collaborations with An Khang clinics and nutrition centers to provide health consultations and vaccinations, and the deployment of volunteer teams to support frontline healthcare workers.
*   **Governance (G):** Vinamilk maintains a transparent governance framework driven by the active oversight of the Board of Directors, Executive Board, Audit Committee, and Internal Audit function. The company strictly complies with disclosure regulations on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE) and implements best-practice governance standards to balance the interests of shareholders and stakeholders while aligning with global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
