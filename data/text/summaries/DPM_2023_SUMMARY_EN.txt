### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
FY2023 marked a sharp deterioration in DPM’s financial performance, with net revenue contracting by 26% and profit before tax plunging by 90% compared to the record-high peak of FY2022. The core catalyst was the rapid correction in global fertilizer markets, driven by a 64% collapse in international urea prices from their April 2022 peak and a 67% drop in potash prices, exacerbated by ongoing geopolitical friction (Russia-Ukraine, Israel-Hamas) and major exporting nations' trade restrictions. Domestically, the company faced structural oversupply, panic-selling and margin-cut sentiment in NPK, and the proliferation of low-cost substitutes (such as N21-25% and ammonium sulfate). Notably, cost-of-goods-sold pressures escalated significantly as natural gas feedstock prices—vital for urea production—surged 18% YoY to USD 10.51/MMBTU (inclusive of VAT), generating over VND 1.0tn in incremental manufacturing costs.

Despite these headwinds, core operational momentum was sustained as the Phu My Fertilizer Plant operated stably at high capacity, securing total sales volumes of 1.3 million metric tons of fertilizers and 118 thousand metric tons of chemicals. Strategically, DPM is pivoting its focus toward optimizing its chemical and petrochemical value chains (commercial NH3, UFC85, alongside R&D for H2O2, Melamine, DEF, and Soda Ash). Simultaneously, the company is prioritizing the development of organic fertilizers, smart fertilizers, and clean energy initiatives (green NH3) to mitigate structural exposure to traditional fertilizer price cycles.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DPM’s financial profile continues to exhibit a high degree of balance sheet conservatism and minimal leverage risk. As of December 31, 2023, consolidated total assets stood at VND 13.309tn, down 25% year-to-date. Total liabilities contracted sharply by 52% to VND 1.764tn. Cash and cash equivalents compressed by 40% to VND 1.242tn, primarily driven by the execution of a high-payout cash dividend for FY2022 at 70% of par value (equivalent to VND 2.739tn).

Key financial ratios underscore that DPM faces no systemic vulnerabilities regarding capital structure or debt service capacity. Both the total debt-to-total assets and total debt-to-equity ratios declined relative to FY2022; the current and quick liquidity ratios both remained robust and stable above 1.0x. The company maintains total self-sufficiency in working capital for its core operations and capital expenditure programs, remaining entirely decoupled from risky financial debt.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding inventories, DPM provisioned VND 31.2bn for inventory write-downs of finished goods and merchandise during the year due to net realizable value falling below cost, while simultaneously utilizing and reversing VND 139.2bn in provisions following the depletion of beginning-period inventory overhangs. Credit risk and provisioning are concentrated in protracted non-performing receivables exceeding three years with negligible recovery prospects: receivables linked to reciprocal guarantee obligations and ancillary costs at VNPOLY (totaling over VND 114.2bn), where the counterparty’s accumulated losses exceed equity; and entrusted investment capital with accrued interest at PVTSJSC (totaling over VND 110bn) due to severe operational degradation. Long-term investments in unlisted associates (VNPOLY, PVC Mekong, Ut Xi Aquatic Products) continued to underperform, eroding the parent company's invested capital.

Regarding contingent liabilities, DPM is actively engaging with the People's Committee of Ca Mau Province to ascertain land-use fee obligations arising from a 0.6-hectare developed roadway footprint. Regarding post-balance sheet events, the company recorded a major top-management overhaul in late March 2024, including the appointment of a new Chairman of the Board of Directors (Mr. Nguyen Xuan Hoa) and Chief Executive Officer (Mr. Phan Cong Thanh).

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
* **Environmental (E):** DPM is progressively operationalizing its decarbonization and energy-transition targets via R&D into green NH3 production, participation in green agricultural product supply chains, and the scaling of organic fertilizers. The Phu My Fertilizer Plant focuses heavily on equipment upgrades and static-asset maintenance to ensure operational safety, energy efficiency, and minimized environmental externalities.
* **Social (S):** The company maintains strong occupational health and safety standards, safeguards employee welfare, and has restructured its remuneration framework. Throughout 2023, DPM disbursed over VND 60bn toward corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, including Tet gift-distribution programs spanning over 40 provinces nationwide.
* **Governance (G):** DPM upholds a transparent governance model, fortifying its Board of Directors with independent representation (appointing Mr. Ho Quyet Thang to succeed Mr. Louis T. Nguyen). The entity has intensified internal audit functions, refined its enterprise risk management (ERM) architecture, and accelerated its digital transformation roadmap to drive operational efficiency and alignment with modern governance standards.
