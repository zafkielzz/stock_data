**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2025, Vinamilk’s consolidated net revenue reached VND 63,724 billion, representing a year-on-year (YoY) top-line growth of 3.1% and achieving 98.8% of the full-year target. Consolidated profit before tax (PBT) came in at VND 11,650 billion, edging up 0.4% YoY and fulfilling 96.3% of the annual guidance. Although cumulative metrics fell slightly short of the 100% benchmark due to the restructuring of the traditional distribution channel in Q1, quarterly performance exhibited a compelling sequential recovery from Q2 to Q4 2025. Core growth catalysts stemmed from the optimization of four strategic pillars: user experience driven by a successful brand repositioning campaign (ranked AAA+ by Brand Finance, placing it in the top 5% of global powerhouse brands and as the most valuable food brand in ASEAN); comprehensive digital transformation encompassing the ERP system, e-commerce, and AI integration in supply chain management; sustainable development; and a strong emphasis on human capital enhancement. Notably, overseas markets remained a bright spot, with export revenue accounting for 20% of total top-line and registering a 15% YoY growth, underpinned by a sustained footprint across 65 countries and compliance with stringent regulatory standards in the US, Japan, EU, and the Middle East.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
As of December 31, 2025, Vinamilk’s total short- and long-term debt stood at VND 9,457 billion, marking a 2.0% YoY increase. The debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio reached 27.15%, while the debt-to-total-assets ratio remained conservative, reflecting a proactive capital management strategy to capitalize on prevailing interest rate environments and debt financing to optimize the weighted average cost of capital (WACC). Management noted a negligible increase in financial leverage compared to the previous year, which nevertheless remains within a prudent risk threshold. Although current and quick liquidity ratios contracted slightly relative to 2024—attributable to optimized working capital management and enhanced capital efficiency—they remained comfortably above 1x, ensuring full coverage of short-term debt obligations, operational continuity, and shareholder dividend distributions. Remuneration for the Board of Directors (BOD) in 2025 totaled VND 25 billion (for 11 members), alongside BOD operational expenses of VND 6 billion.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
The inventory turnover ratio moderated slightly, reflecting a strategic decision to maintain a safe inventory buffer amid macroeconomic volatility, with no exposure to inventory write-downs, non-performing loans (NPLs), or impaired assets. The company accelerated its receivable collection cycles while prudently extending accounts payable durations to enhance operating cash flow. Regarding related-party transactions, all transactions were rigorously controlled under Policy No. 06/TTISO/12/KSNB/23, ensuring compliance with arm’s-length principles, transparency, and the best interests of shareholders under the direct oversight of the Audit Committee. Consistent with prior years, Vinamilk incurred no related-party lending or loan guarantees for members of the BOD, the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Managing Directors, or associated persons in 2025. Major corporate resolutions executed during the year—such as the divestment of the entire stake in Del Monte – Vinamilk Dairy Philippines Inc. and the termination of the Philippine venture, investments in plant expansions, and adjustments to dairy farm projects—were either fully executed by the BOD or remain strictly on schedule.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** Vinamilk reaffirmed its pioneering role in sustainable development by committing to a clear roadmap toward Net Zero by 2050. The company stands as Vietnam’s inaugural dairy enterprise with farms and manufacturing plants certified as carbon-neutral in accordance with the international PAS 2060:2014 standard, deeply integrating ESG metrics across its entire agricultural and manufacturing value chain.
- **Social (S):** The group remains dedicated to serving national nutritional needs and cultivating human capital, supporting a workforce of nearly 10,000 employees. The BOD is collaborating with an international human resources consultancy to deploy a CEO succession plan in 2025, which will extend to Managing Director positions in 2026, ensuring a seamless generational transition that harmonizes innovation with legacy corporate values.
- **Governance (G):** Vinamilk upholds exemplary corporate governance standards that surpass baseline regulatory compliance by adopting the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard, aiming for the designation of an "ASEAN Asset Class." Independence and transparency are strictly enforced: BOD members with related-party ties or conflicts of interest are strictly recused from discussions and voting procedures (e.g., performance-based bonus allocations). Investor relations (IR) operated with high transparency, featuring four quarterly newsletters and eight recurring briefings that drew nearly 750 institutional representatives, earning Vinamilk two "Best Investor Relations of 2025" awards conferred by Vietstock.
