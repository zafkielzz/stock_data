### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2024 marked a strategic transformation for Vingroup (VIC), with core growth momentum driven by two primary pillars: Technology-Industrial and Commerce-Services. Within the Technology-Industrial segment, VinFast achieved a historic milestone by emerging as Vietnam's top-selling automotive brand, delivering nearly 88,000 electric vehicles (EVs) during the year and outperforming foreign competitors. This success was propelled by a diversified pure-electric product lineup—including the VF 5 with over 32,000 units and the VF 3 with over 25,000 units, backed by over 27,000 pre-orders secured within just 66 hours of launch—and underpinned by the "Strong Vietnamese Spirit – For a Green Future" campaign. Internationally, VinFast continued to expand its footprint across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia (including Indonesia, Philippines, and India), with more than 9,500 units delivered in overseas markets.

In the Commerce-Services segment, Vinhomes maintained its market leadership with an adjusted revenue of VND 141.6 trillion, while optimizing capital expenditure on construction through design standardization and supply chain efficiencies. Concurrently, Vinpearl posted a 55% year-on-year revenue increase, welcoming 10.9 million visitors and guests (up 50%), with international overnight stays surging by 58%—outperforming the broader industry average of 40%. VinWonders also welcomed nearly 7 million visitors (up 39% year-on-year and 19% higher than pre-COVID-19 levels). The Vinmec healthcare division expanded its network to 8 facilities with the inauguration of Vinmec Smart City General Hospital, while upholding international benchmarks such as JCI accreditation and joining the Cleveland Clinic Connected program.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vingroup’s capital structure and liquidity profile in 2024 were subjected to significant pressure driven by capital expenditure requirements for global manufacturing expansion and the development of large-scale projects. As of year-end 2024, investments held-to-maturity comprised VND-denominated term deposits with maturities ranging from over 3 months to 1 year, yielding interest rates between 2.5% p.a. and 7.2% p.a. (moderating from 3.2% p.a. to 8.0% p.a. in 2023). Notably, a portion of these deposit balances includes maintenance funds for real-time estate projects pending handover to the Building Management Boards, with up to VND 1.483 trillion of deposits pledged as collateral for standby letters of credit (LCs) securing the Group's borrowings.

Regarding loan receivables, the Group maintained short-term and long-term loans extended to corporate and individual counterparties maturing in 2025 and 2026, bearing elevated interest rates ranging from 7.0% p.a. to 12.0% p.a. Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks are clearly manifested in the requirement to maintain substantial site-clearance advances for prospective projects, which are secured by subsidiary shares. Furthermore, maintaining credit lines and substantial financial commitments necessitates rigorous and continuous cash flow management by Vingroup to ensure the timely servicing of principal and interest obligations.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vingroup’s risk management and provisioning in 2024 were reflected in complex financial transactions and an asset portfolio requiring heightened surveillance. During the year, the Group acquired a 97.54% stake in Hon Tam Bien Nha Trang JSC, subsequently executing an agreement to transfer the entirety of this holding to a third party; due to the temporary nature of control, this investment was classified as trading securities. Similarly, the recoverable value from a debt purchase agreement executed with Saigon - Hanoi Commercial Joint Stock Bank (SHB) was reclassified into trading securities.

Contingent risks also stem from advances under investment and business cooperation contracts, receivables from the handover of public infrastructure to state authorities via Build-Transfer (BT) contracts, as well as project development deposits and earnest money. The Group’s non-performing loans (NPLs) are primarily concentrated in trade receivables, prepayments to suppliers, and overdue loan principal and interest. The maintenance of off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments and pledged deposits for standby LCs demands ongoing oversight to mitigate the risk of these exposures materializing into direct debt obligations.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vingroup’s core strategic orientation centers on green transformation toward achieving net-zero emissions by 2050, operationalized through pure EV manufacturing and the development of a clean mobility ecosystem. The Group and its profit-and-loss (P&L) member companies aggressively deployed circular economy initiatives and plastic waste reduction measures: single-use plastic bottles in offices were completely replaced with glass bottles and paper cups; Vinpearl piloted the transition from plastic water bottles to glass bottles (saving nearly 150,000 plastic bottles, equivalent to over 2,500 kg of plastic waste) and adopted eco-friendly materials such as bagasse straws and bamboo amenities; Vinmec rolled out campaigns to limit single-use plastics, digitalize medical imaging films, and collect 91 kg of spent batteries for safe disposal. At Vinhomes, sustainable construction materials continued to be prioritized at high utilization rates, including non-fired bricks, Acotec panels, autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC), engineered wood, and Low-E glass.
- **Social (S):** The Group prioritizes high-quality human capital development, enforcing rigorous occupational health and safety (OHS) standards while enhancing employee welfare. Prominent social initiatives include the establishment of the "For a Green Future" Fund to promote sustainable lifestyles within the community, alongside training programs and the expansion of international-standard healthcare (Vinmec) and educational systems (VinUni, Brighton College Vietnam), thereby elevating living standards and creating sustainable human value for society.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance at Vingroup strictly adheres to transparency standards underpinned by a robust internal control framework and independent audit reporting. The governance structure preserves effective oversight among the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, and the Supervisory Board, while progressively standardizing disclosure criteria in alignment with international sustainability reporting frameworks (GRI/SDGs) to safeguard the ultimate interests of shareholders and stakeholders.
