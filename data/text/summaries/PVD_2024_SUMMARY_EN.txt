Below is an in-depth qualitative analysis summary of PetroVietnam Drilling and Well Services Corporation (PVD) for the fiscal year 2024:

### 1. Business Drivers & Segment Reporting
In 2024, PVD recorded impressive business growth with total revenue reaching VND 9,566 billion, up 54%, and net profit after tax attributable to shareholders of the parent company reaching VND 698 billion, an increase of 84% compared to 2023. The core growth driver stemmed from the drilling and related services segment, where jack-up rig utilization remained very high at over 95% (specifically, PV DRILLING III and PV DRILLING VI achieved an absolute utilization rate of 99.85%). Key rigs maintained continuous operations throughout the year, with average daily charter rates increasing by approximately 20% year-on-year. Regarding geographical markets, overseas service operations continued to play a strategic role, accounting for a large proportion of the revenue structure with key markets including Malaysia, Brunei, and Indonesia. Well technical services and trading also recorded a strong recovery, benefiting from increased workloads in both domestic and international drilling campaigns.

### 2. Capital Structure, Debt & Interest Rates
PVD’s financial structure in 2024 remained at a safe level, with total assets reaching VND 16,053 billion and equity standing at VND 10,362 billion. Total liabilities were recorded at VND 5,682 billion, of which financial debt (including both short-term and long-term loans) funded major investment projects (such as the acquisition of the new jack-up rig PV DRILLING VIII and the Hydraulic Workover Unit - HWU). Bank loans are predominantly denominated in USD to hedge against foreign exchange risks. Borrowing costs are subject to international benchmark interest rates (such as SOFR/LIBOR) combined with agreed spreads. Major credit partners providing capital to PVD include prominent domestic and international banks (such as Vietcombank, MB Bank, and HSBC). Cash flow pressure and the capital structure are tightly managed through disciplined principal and interest repayment schedules, ensuring liquidity and maintaining the debt-to-equity ratio within safe boundaries.

### 3. Provisioning, Contingent Liabilities & Subsequent Events
Provisioning policies were prudently executed by PVD in strict compliance with prevailing accounting regulations. The company maintains allowances for inventory obsolescence for slow-moving or obsolete supplies and equipment, as well as provisions for overdue trade receivables to accurately reflect asset values. Regarding contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet commitments, PVD maintains short-term credit guarantee commitments and performance bond commitments to support major drilling tenders. During the year and up to the reporting date, no major legal disputes or extraordinary financial events were recorded that could materially impact the operating performance and solvency of the corporation.

### 4. ESG Execution
- **Environmental (E):** PVD is strongly committed to its sustainable development strategy and roadmap toward achieving Net-Zero emissions by 2050. The corporation strictly monitors greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, and 3), while deploying energy-saving solutions, optimizing fuel efficiency on drilling rigs, and promoting circular economy initiatives, waste management, and wastewater treatment compliant with national and international standards.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is prioritized. PVD maintains an exceptional safety record, with multiple drilling rigs achieving milestones of several consecutive years with zero Lost Time Incidents (Zero LTI). Employee welfare, remuneration (average income reaching VND 27.36 million/person/month), and professional capacity development are heavily emphasized. The corporation also actively participates in social security and community welfare programs with annual budgets amounting to tens of billions of VND.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly complies with securities regulations and the Enterprise Law. The Board of Directors (BOD) maintains a high degree of independence with the participation of independent BOD members, ensuring effective oversight. PVD applies International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), maintains a robust internal control system (under COSO and ISO standards), and consistently ranks among the top listed companies with the best corporate governance on the Vietnamese stock market.
