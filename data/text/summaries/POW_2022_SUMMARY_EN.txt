### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2022, Petrovietnam Power Corporation's (POW) operational performance was subject to cross-currents from a complex macro environment. Despite headwinds including the Covid-19 pandemic—which dampened offtake demand, resulting in an actual load growth of only 5.2% (underperforming the projected 8.2%)—alongside renewable energy capturing 12.9% of total demand and scheduled maintenance shutdowns across multiple plants (such as the Unit 1 major overhaul at Vũng Áng 1, major overhaul at Cà Mau 1, and intermediate overhaul at Nhơn Trạch 1), total power generation reached 14.2 billion kWh, achieving 102% of its full-year target. The core growth driver stemmed from safe, stable, and continuous plant operations dispatched by A0, coupled with effective spot market bidding strategies. Furthermore, aggressive cost optimization and expenditure cuts (totaling VND 3,161 billion, primarily driven by reductions in the EPC contract value for the Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 projects relative to Decision 918) provided a robust cushion, enabling financial metrics to surpass guidance. *(Note: The corporation operates primarily to serve the domestic market via the national grid; there are no specific disclosures regarding export markets).*

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
POW’s financial structure and cash flow profile in 2022 faced severe pressure from macroeconomic headwinds, as inflationary pressures drove up bank interest rates and foreign exchange volatility, thereby escalating financial expenses. The operating model—comprising multiple subsidiaries, joint ventures, and large-scale power generation capital expenditure projects—poses significant challenges for capital allocation and cash flow governance. Additional liquidity pressures stemmed from receivables collection and the stringent negotiation of Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs). Notably, substantial advance payments made under the EPC Contract executed on March 14, 2022, with the consortium of Samsung C&T and Lilama Corporation for the Nhơn Trạch 3 and Nhơn Trạch 4 projects, require rigorous monitoring of liquidity and disbursement schedules to optimize the cost of capital.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
POW maintains a prudent risk management framework regarding provisions and contingent liabilities. For long-term financial investments in underperforming service entities—such as PetroVietnam Mechatronics JSC and PetroVietnam Urban Development JSC (PVC Mekong, whose ticker PXC was suspended from trading amid substantial debt obligations and interest penalties owed to Ocean Bank)—the Corporation has fully provisioned in accordance with regulations (as of June 30, 2022, provisions stood at VND 18.5 billion against an investment book value of VND 19.2 billion). Specifically, the investment in High-Tech Concrete Investment JSC was entirely written off following its declaration of bankruptcy pursuant to Decision No. 01/2021/QĐ-TBPS dated November 29, 2021, issued by the Nhơn Trạch District People's Court, and its official dissolution on July 14, 2022, utilizing pre-established provisions. Regarding disputes and contingent liabilities, actual repair costs stemming from the technical failure of Unit 1 at Vũng Áng 1 Thermal Power Plant (occurring in September 2021) are currently under negotiation with Vietnam National Aviation Insurance Corporation / PetroVietnam Insurance (PVI) to determine compensation. Additionally, trade receivables from Vietnam National Oil and Gas Group (PVN) amounting to VND 218.49 billion—relating to the 500kV switchyard allocated to Vũng Áng 1 and amendments to the Gas Sales Agreement (GSA) for Cà Mau 1&2 effective January 1, 2022—remain in the process of final settlement among the parties.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** POW’s power plants utilize advanced technologies featuring rigorously controlled emission and wastewater treatment systems, with online data feeds linked directly to local Departments of Natural Resources and Environment (DONRE) to maintain negligible excess emissions above regulatory thresholds. Concurrently, the Corporation focuses on scaling investments in renewables oriented toward self-consumption substitution, energy efficiency, and electricity storage.
- **Social (S):** POW places strong emphasis on Occupational Health and Safety (OHS), achieving a milestone of 50 million safe man-hours in 2022. Robust protocols for disease prevention and workforce health protection remain actively enforced. The Corporation enacted a revised Parent Company Salary Regulation (effective January 1, 2021) and actively executes corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to rigorous standards, evidenced by the comprehensive overhaul of its internal regulatory framework (totaling 107 documents including the Charter, charters of governance, bylaws, and operating procedures by year-end 2022). POW stands as the first power generation corporation in Vietnam to be assigned a "BB" credit rating by Fitch Ratings for two consecutive years (2021-2022) and was named among the Top 100 Sustainable Development Enterprises, affirming the high quality of its disclosures and strategic transparency.
