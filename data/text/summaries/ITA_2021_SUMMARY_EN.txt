Below is a standard qualitative analysis summary based on the attached documents and actual data for Tan Tao Investment and Industry Corporation (ITA) for the fiscal year 2021:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Tan Tao’s core business operations continue to rely on industrial park infrastructure leasing, commercial real estate development, and support services across key industrial zones such as Tan Tao and Expanded Tan Tao in Ho Chi Minh City. Key growth drivers stem from the recovering demand for land and factory leases—driven by domestic and foreign enterprises catering to industrial production and logistics post-pandemic. Despite facing numerous headwinds from global supply chain disruptions and rising input costs, maintaining stable occupancy rates across existing industrial parks has generated steady cash inflows from upfront and annual land lease payments, complemented by infrastructure management services and utility provisions. The primary target market remains concentrated in the Southern key economic region, which benefits from favorable transport connectivity to seaport systems and vital arterial traffic routes.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Tan Tao’s capital structure maintains a balance between equity and liabilities to finance long-term infrastructure development projects. The company's short-term and long-term borrowings are primarily utilized for site clearance compensation, industrial park infrastructure construction, and working capital replenishment. Borrowing rates comprise both fixed and floating interest rates, depending on agreements with credit institutions and prevailing money market conditions. Major credit providers are predominantly domestic commercial banks with long-standing relationships with the company. Cash flow pressures necessitate that the company effectively manage debt collection from land lessees and accelerate sales and leasing progress to ensure the timely repayment of principal and interest, thereby maintaining financial safety.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Tan Tao’s risk management practices involve regular reviews of doubtful receivables from customers and partners, as well as evaluations of the net realizable value of unfinished real estate projects and inventories to make appropriate provisions in accordance with prevailing accounting regulations. Contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments are strictly monitored through internal control systems. Regarding post-balance-sheet events, market developments during the year witnessed a robust breakthrough in ITA shares, recording an annual price surge of +130.56% (opening at VND 7,200 and closing at VND 16,600, having peaked at VND 18,850). This was accompanied by vibrant liquidity, with average matched trading volume reaching 14,565,712 shares per session, reflecting positive market expectations regarding the company's recovery and growth prospects.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environment (E):** Tan Tao emphasizes compliance with environmental protection regulations across the industrial parks it manages. Waste management, centralized wastewater treatment, and the maintenance of green spaces are strictly implemented to target sustainable development, energy conservation, and the minimization of negative impacts on the surrounding ecological environment.
- **Social (S):** The enterprise maintains a stable human resources policy, ensuring rights, social insurance regimes, and occupational health and safety (OHS) for employees. Programs focused on upgrading skill sets and caring for the material and spiritual well-being of staff are prioritized to build a high-quality workforce.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance adheres to current regulations applicable to listed public companies. The Board of Directors maintains independence in strategic direction, working closely with the independent audit system to ensure transparency, public disclosure of financial information, and compliance with modern governance standards.
