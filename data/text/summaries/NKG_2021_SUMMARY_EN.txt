Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the 2021 financial report summary for Nam Kim Steel Joint Stock Company (NKG).

---

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2021 marked a period of exceptional growth for Nam Kim Steel (Tôn Nam Kim), with net revenue reaching VND 28,206.1 billion (an increase of 144.0% YoY) and net profit after tax surging 654.0% to VND 2,225.6 billion. This performance was primarily driven by robust export market momentum amid global supply chain disruptions, allowing NKG to capture an export share of 68.64% of total sales revenue. Full-year sales volume reached 703,843 tonnes (up 53.0%), with export volume hitting 3,370,482 tonnes (up 108.1%). Core business lines—including galvanized sheet, steel pipes, and color-coated sheet—reinforced their market-leading positions, supported by optimized production capacities across key manufacturing facilities (Nam Kim Sheet Plant No. 2, Long An Steel Pipe Plant, and Mỹ Phước Plant) with large aggregate design capacities. The recovery of international markets, coupled with a flexible strategic pivot in market share toward key regions including Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia, served as a solid springboard for gross margins to expand to 15.10% (compared to 7.50% in 2020).

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
NKG’s capital structure in 2021 witnessed a significant improvement in financial autonomy, underpinned by strong operating cash flows. Total short- and long-term debt decreased sharply, with short-term borrowings acting as the primary instrument for working capital financing. Interest rates for short-term loans denominated in VND and USD ranged from 4.5% to 4.8% p.a. and 2.0% to 3.5% p.a., respectively, while long-term loan interest rates ranged from 8.6% to 8.8% p.a. Major commercial banking partners providing large-scale credit facilities to NKG include Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV), Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank), and Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank). Cash flow pressure and capital structure risks were notably mitigated, with the total debt-to-total assets ratio declining to 12.2% and the interest coverage ratio improving to 11.2x, reflecting a resilient financial foundation against macroeconomic volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Provisioning policies were prudently executed by NKG to accurately reflect market risks. Regarding inventory, the company provisioned for inventory write-downs to hedge against global price volatility in hot-rolled coil (HRC) raw materials. Short-term doubtful receivables were reviewed and provisioned in accordance with prevailing accounting regulations to mitigate customer credit risk. Regarding contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet commitments, NKG maintained financial guarantee commitments and non-cancellable lease contracts commensurate with the scale of its operations. No material legal disputes or irregular financial events occurred after December 31, 2021, that could adversely impact the company’s status as a going concern.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** Nam Kim Steel places strong emphasis on investing in modern production technologies to minimize environmental impact. The company has implemented an acid regeneration system across its pickling lines (equipped with an acid recovery system that reclaims acid from micro-product waste streams, contributing to environmental protection), strictly monitors Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions, and optimizes energy efficiency to advance its long-term sustainable development goals.
- **Social (S):** The company strictly upholds occupational health and safety (OHS) standards, organizes periodic health check-ups, and provides comprehensive protective equipment to all employees. Its human resources policies ensure gender equality (maintaining an appropriate balance between female and male labor ratios), promote skill-enhancement training programs, and actively execute social welfare activities to support local communities during the COVID-19 pandemic, with total contributions to the state budget reaching VND 1,128 billion in 2021.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance operates on strict compliance with legal regulations and the company’s Charter. The Board of Directors ensures independence, backed by effective oversight from the Supervisory Board. Financial statements are independently audited by PwC Vietnam, ensuring transparency and compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) while progressively aligning with international best practices.
