Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative financial analysis summary based on the 2024 Annual Report of Duc Giang Chemicals Group Joint Stock Company (DGC):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
In 2024, DGC’s consolidated net revenue reached VND 9,864.9 billion, representing a slight increase of 1.2% YoY, while consolidated net profit after tax stood at VND 3,107 billion, down 4.4% YoY. The group's core growth driver remained its basic chemicals segment, particularly Yellow Phosphorus, which generated VND 4,252.5 billion in revenue (up 15.2% YoY) and accounted for the lion's share, supported by robust demand from the technology, electronics, and artificial intelligence sectors. Additionally, MAP fertilizers recorded a strong breakout with revenue surging 168.5% YoY to VND 1,116.8 billion; NPK grew 277.9% to VND 34 billion; and DCP feed additives reached VND 883.7 billion (up 21.1% YoY). Conversely, certain product lines experienced significant declines, such as 85% $H_3PO_4$ acid down 30.4% and WPA down 79.2% due to market fluctuations. Regarding geographical breakdown, domestic revenue posted impressive growth of 45.7% to reach VND 3,118 billion (accounting to 31.6% of total revenue), whereas export revenue (converted to VND) declined by 11.3% to VND 6,746 billion (accounting for 68.4%). Maintaining a large export proportion denominated in USD provides DGC with a plentiful foreign currency reserve, minimizing foreign exchange risks when utilizing foreign-currency borrowings for operational activities.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, LEVERAGE & INTEREST RATES
DGC’s capital structure remains highly secure and sound, with the ratio of total liabilities to total capital continuing to contract. As of December 31, 2024, consolidated total assets stood at VND 15,821.258 billion, up 1.4% year-to-date. Meanwhile, total liabilities dropped sharply by 40.5% from VND 3,563 billion to VND 2,120 billion, pulling the Short-term Debt/Total Consolidated Capital ratio down from 22.4% to 13.0%. Equity recorded a growth of 13.8% to reach VND 13,701 billion, thereby significantly improving the current ratio to 6.2x and the quick ratio to 4.9x. Backed by substantial cash and deposit balances generated from strong business performance, the group maintains minimal cash flow pressure and self-finances its matching capital requirements for major investment projects without heavy reliance on financial leverage or complex short- and long-term bank borrowings.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Throughout 2024, DGC maintained stringent risk management practices in response to macro, tax policy, and supply chain volatilities. The group recorded no material legal disputes or regulatory violations that could impact its production and business operations. Consolidated inventory value reached VND 985.030 billion (up 15.2% YoY), and short-term receivables were well-controlled (down 13.3% to VND 979.616 billion). Independent audit fees for the 2024 financial statements paid to PwC (Vietnam) amounted to VND 1.17 billion (excluding VAT). Regarding post-balance-sheet events, notable structural changes occurred in early March 2025, including the Board of Directors' decision to relieve Mr. Dao Huu Duy Anh from his position as CEO so he could assume the role of Standing Vice Chairman of the Board, while appointing Mr. Luu Bach Dat as the new CEO, thereby ensuring continuity and stability in corporate governance and executive management.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DGC focuses on optimizing its production technology in alignment with a circular economy framework. A prime example is the complete recovery of excess CO gas from phosphorus plants to be reused as thermal waste fuel, saving hundreds of billions of dong annually and directly minimizing environmental emissions. The group's manufacturing plants are located within industrial parks equipped with wastewater treatment systems capable of achieving 90-95% recycling standards, resulting in near-zero wastewater discharge into the environment. Total water consumption across the group in 2024 decreased by 5% to approximately 1.8 million $m^3$.
- **Social (S):** As of year-end 2024, total headcount across the group stood at 2,622 employees with an average salary of VND 22 million/person/month. DGC fully implements labor insurance policies, organizes regular health check-ups, and provides occupational safety and disease prevention training. Material and spiritual well-being remains a key priority through company trips and tourism activities, alongside a budget of nearly VND 32.6 billion allocated for social welfare, charity, and relief efforts supporting populations affected by natural disasters and floods (such as Typhoon Yagi).
- **Governance (G):** DGC’s governance framework strictly complies with the Enterprise Law and the company’s Charter, featuring a Board of Directors composed of highly experienced members, including an independent board member (Ms. Nguyen Thi Thu Ha), which ensures objectivity in strategic oversight. Independent audits are conducted transparently by top-tier auditing firms (PwC), and resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders and the Board of Directors consistently achieve 100% approval rates, fully complying with disclosure standards on the stock market.
