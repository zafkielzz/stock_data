The company's core growth drivers remain focused on transport infrastructure (toll collections from key BOT projects) and urban real estate development in Ho Chi Minh City. Despite the superior gross margin, Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA) for the year stood at very modest levels of 0.17% and 0.05% respectively, while trailing 12-month EPS reached VND 411.01/share. This highlights heavy pressure from financial expenses (particularly interest expenses) and operating expenses, which eroded the majority of the gross profit generated. The company has no export operations due to a business model focused entirely on the domestic technical infrastructure and public utility markets. CII’s capital structure faces heavy financial leverage pressure, with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio reaching a high of 180.41%. The pressure from a large debt scale increases annual interest expenses, placing significant strain on the company’s operating cash flow and net income. Capital raised is primarily used to finance long-term investments for large-scale infrastructure projects through long-term loans from major domestic commercial banks, combined with corporate bond issuances. The interest rate structure includes both fixed and floating rates benchmarked against market margins, directly exposing the company to macroeconomic interest rate volatility. Refinancing risks and pressure from principal and interest repayment obligations require management to continuously optimize cash flows from BOT toll stations and accelerate real estate sales progress to ensure timely liquidity.

Off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments, obligations related to project financing credit lines, and potential legal disputes during site clearance and infrastructure construction are strictly controlled through internal risk management systems and audits. Following the fiscal year-end of December 31, 2025, the company continues to closely monitor financial restructuring progress, the handling of maturing debt obligations, and macroeconomic volatilities that could impact its ability to secure funding sources for upcoming projects, ensuring strict compliance with regulatory standards on market transparency.
