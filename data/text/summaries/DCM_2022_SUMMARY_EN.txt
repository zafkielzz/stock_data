### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2022 was characterized by complex macroeconomic volatility stemming from the Russia-Ukraine geopolitical conflict and the global energy crisis. However, this backdrop paradoxically acted as a positive catalyst, driving global energy and agricultural commodity prices higher and conferring a substantial competitive advantage to fertilizer manufacturers such as Petrovietnam Ca Mau Fertilizer Joint Stock Company (DCM / PVCFC). By executing its "Flexible Adaptation - Innovation" strategy, DCM optimized operational efficiency through a balanced and agile allocation between domestic consumption and exports.

In the domestic market, the company fortified its market share across key agricultural regions, including the Mekong Delta, the Southeast, and the Central Highlands. Concurrently, export channels served as a core growth driver, with DCM successfully expanding its trading network to 14 countries, notably Cambodia, Thailand, Myanmar, India, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, and select markets in South America and Europe. This proactive international market diversification enabled DCM to sustain high sales volumes, despite downward pressure on its share price amid broader equity market corrections, which registered an annual volatility of -23.70% (opening at VND 26,840 and closing at VND 20,480 per share).

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

DCM’s capital structure exhibits a prudent balance between shareholders' equity and interest-bearing debt utilized for production and business operations. Short-term working capital requirements are primarily financed through credit facilities extended by major commercial banks, prominently Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank) – Thu Thiem Branch and Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank) – Ca Mau Branch.

Regarding long-term debt obligations, leverage is concentrated within its subsidiary, Vietnam Petroleum Packaging Joint Stock Company (PPC), to finance the Bac Lieu Petroleum Fertilizer Plant Project. The primary long-term creditors for the group are VietinBank – Bac Lieu Branch and Saigon-Hanoi Commercial Joint Stock Bank (SHB). The company faces rising cost-of-capital risks as floating interest rates on long-term loans ticked upward during the year to a range of 8.38%–12.00% p.a. (compared to 8.38%–11.00% p.a. in the prior year). Although all loans are secured by fixed assets formed from the project, elevated interest rates combined with the Federal Reserve's monetary tightening cycles continue to exert moderate pressure on the group's debt service cash flows and impending principal maturities.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

DCM adheres to a conservative financial management framework by proactively provisioning for material risk exposures. The company recorded provisions for inventory write-downs on NPK finished goods and merchandise due to cost bases exceeding net realizable values, reflecting fierce competition from imported substitute products. Regarding accounts receivable and contingent liabilities, DCM remains actively engaged with tax authorities to secure a refund for foreign contractor taxes previously paid in connection with the interest expense of the ECA credit facility (executed between Petrovietnam [PVN] and a syndicate of foreign banks comprising BNP Paribas, the Export-Import Bank of China, and Credit Agricole).

Furthermore, input cost volatility risk is underscored by DCM’s accrual of estimated gas cost payables, determined in accordance with actual gas allocation principles in Ca Mau Province pursuant to PVN’s Resolutions. These provisions are subject to subsequent adjustments upon the finalization of accounts between the parties, constituting a financial variable requiring close post-balance sheet monitoring.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** DCM remains steadfast in its mission to pioneer sustainable green agriculture by scaling up organic and safe fertilizer product lines. Amid the energy crisis, the company optimized plant operational workflows and implemented technical innovation initiatives, thereby curbing raw material loss, enhancing energy efficiency, and mitigating direct environmental emissions.
*   **Social (S):** The company is committed to stringent occupational safety standards and maintains an equitable, human-centric working environment. DCM effectively operates its Safety Bonus Fund and Welfare Fund to safeguard the material and spiritual well-being of its workforce, while placing strong emphasis on capacity-building and talent development programs. Additionally, DCM actively fulfills its corporate social responsibility through community outreach initiatives and direct technical support programs for local farmers.
*   **Governance (G):** DCM maintains a transparent governance framework featuring a strict segregation of duties between the Chairman of the Board of Directors (BOD) and the Chief Executive Officer. The BOD comprises 7 members, including 2 independent non-executive members with deep professional expertise who provide objective oversight. The internal control system functions effectively through the application of Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), specialized risk management software, and rigorous supervision by two specialized committees and the Internal Audit Department operating under the BOD.
