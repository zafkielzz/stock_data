### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2022 marked a robust post-pandemic recovery phase for Saigon Thuong Tin Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Sacombank - Ticker: STB), despite navigating various macroeconomic volatilities. Core growth drivers stemmed from the optimization of Net Interest Margins (NIM) via the rotation of the credit portfolio toward the retail and Small and Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) segments, which yield superior margins. Non-interest income (service fees, bancassurance, and foreign exchange trading) served as a critical buffer, mitigating reliance on pure lending. Accelerated digital transformation enabled the bank to reduce its Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR), thereby enhancing Return on Assets (ROA) and Return on Equity (ROE). Nevertheless, mounting cost-of-capital pressures—driven by sharp fluctuations in deposit interest rates during the second half of 2022—posed headwinds to margins, necessitating rigorous control over funding costs to safeguard earnings growth targets outlined in the restructuring plan.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
Sacombank’s capital structure in 2022 shifted toward increasing the proportion of Current Account Savings Accounts (CASA) to optimize the cost of capital. Amid an environment of rising interest rates, the bank skillfully balanced short- and long-term funding to ensure liquidity and compliance with Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR) mandated by the State Bank of Vietnam. Interbank borrowings and lines of credit from international financial institutions provided timely cash flow supplementation. Interest rate risks were managed via derivative instruments and the maturity restructuring of the investment portfolio. Nonetheless, cash flow pressures persisted amid volatility in the real estate and corporate bond markets, compelling the bank to maintain a conservative policy that prioritized loans backed by highly liquid collateral to hedge against systemic liquidity risk.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
In 2022, Sacombank continued to advance its non-performing loan (NPL) resolution efforts under the restructuring scheme. Credit risk provisioning expenses were maintained at elevated levels to reinforce the provisioning buffer against potential risks emanating from Group 2 loans and legacy debt from prior periods. Off-balance-sheet commitments (letters of guarantee, L/Cs) were closely monitored in accordance with credit risk classifications. Legally, matters concerning legacy collateral remain variables to monitor, notwithstanding the bank's substantial progress in recovering bad debts through asset liquidations. No material or extraordinary financial events occurred post-December 31, 2022, that would adversely impact the audited financial statements, underscoring the ongoing stability of post-period risk management.
