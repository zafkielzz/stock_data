Below is the qualitative analysis summary based on the 2023 Annual Report of FPT Digital Retail Joint Stock Company (FRT):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, FRT recorded a consolidated cumulative revenue of VND 31,850 billion, representing a 6% increase year-on-year. However, pressured by an intense price war in the ICT market (particularly the FPT Shop chain, which saw a 22% revenue decline) alongside year-end one-off expenses, consolidated loss before tax stood at VND 294 billion. FRT's core growth driver and primary bright spot was the Long Chau pharmacy chain, which achieved revenue of VND 15,882 billion, up 66% year-on-year and accounting for 50% of the company's total consolidated revenue. Regarding geographical markets, per segment reporting, the entire business operations of the company and its subsidiaries are concentrated exclusively within the territory of Vietnam, with no business operations or exports conducted outside of Vietnam.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
FRT’s capital structure experienced increased leverage pressures in 2023 driven by working capital funding requirements. Specifically, short-term debt balance as of December 31, 2023, reached VND 8,108 billion (sharply up from VND 5,363 billion at the beginning of the year), while long-term debt was negligible at VND 541 million. Bank borrowings are entirely unsecured with maturities of under 1 year; VND-denominated interest rates fluctuate flexibly from 1.5% to 5.3% per annum depending on individual credit agreements. Key banking partners extending the largest credit lines to FRT include DBS Bank Ltd - Ho Chi Minh City Branch, United Overseas Bank (Vietnam) Limited, and HSBC Bank (Vietnam) Limited. The decline in earnings combined with higher debt levels drove the debt-to-equity ratio up to 6.6x, creating notable pressure on cash flow and liquidity management.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
FRT maintains a conservative provisioning policy to accurately reflect financial risks. Regarding inventories, the provision for inventory obsolescence at the end of 2023 reached VND 43.22 billion (slightly up from VND 41.73 billion in 2022) due to additional provisions set aside for slow-moving and obsolete goods. For receivables, the provision for short-term doubtful debts stood at VND 7.88 billion against total overdue receivables. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, banks have issued payment guarantee letters for partners of FRT and its subsidiaries with a total guarantee balance of VND 1,273.87 billion, of which the largest share is held through Tien Phong Commercial Joint Stock Bank (TPBank) (VND 1,258.87 billion). The report records no unusual legal disputes or material events occurring after December 31, 2023, that would adversely affect the company's going concern status.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** FRT focuses on controlling environmental impacts from its technology and pharmaceutical retail operations, particularly the disposal of hazardous medical waste from its vaccine segment. The company promotes circular economy solutions such as recycling cartons and nylon bags, implements the "Smart Life" energy-saving project (automated control systems for air conditioning and lighting), and utilizes solar energy to optimize electricity consumption efficiency across major warehouses.
- **Social (S):** The company maintains a human resource development policy oriented toward workforce rejuvenation, with employees under 30 years old accounting for 77% of the total workforce, 36% of whom hold university degrees or higher. FRT strictly enforces occupational safety standards, provides comprehensive premium health insurance packages for management and their families, conducts annual health check-ups, and rolls out prominent social welfare programs such as the "Long Chau Sharing" community project (providing millions of medicine days and rice portions to disadvantaged individuals) and the "Improving Vietnamese Stature" program.
- **Governance (G):** The company strictly adheres to a modern corporate governance model with a clear separation of roles between the Chairman of the Board of Directors and the Chief Executive Officer since 2020 to ensure independence. The Board of Directors comprises 5 members (including 2 independent members actively involved in strategic decisions). The 2023 consolidated financial statements were independently audited by PwC (Vietnam) Limited, ensuring transparency and compliance with prevailing accounting standards and legal regulations.
