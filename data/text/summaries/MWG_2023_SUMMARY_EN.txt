### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2023, MWG’s financial performance faced severe headwinds from challenging macroeconomic conditions domestically and globally, characterized by a sharp contraction in consumer disposable income and consumer confidence. Propensity to spend on non-discretionary goods—specifically consumer electronics and home appliances, which serve as the core pillars generating over 70% of total revenue—softened more steeply than anticipated starting from Q4 2022. To counter this structural challenge, management deployed a critical strategic pivot at the outset of Q2 2023: prioritizing customer retention and market share expansion while trading off near-term profitability targets. For the full year, net revenue reached over VND 118 trillion, maintaining 89% of the FY2022 figure, whereas net income plummeted 96% year-over-year to VND 168 billion, missing management's initial guidance.

Notwithstanding these pressures, core operating segments demonstrated notable bright spots. Market share for consumer electronics and appliances across the thegioididong.com and Dien May Xanh chains expanded by 5% to 25%, depending on the product category. Crucially, Apple product market share surged to approximately 50% by the end of 2023 (compared to 25%-30% historically), firmly cementing MWG's market dominance. The air conditioning segment posted positive volume and revenue growth. The Bach Hoa Xanh grocery chain achieved double-digit top-line growth compared to 2022, driven by a strategic focus on fresh produce, and officially reached breakeven across all costs in December 2023 with an average revenue per store of VND 1.8 billion. Developing retail concepts such as An Khang, Avakids, and the EraBlue joint venture in Indonesia all recorded robust revenue momentum toward the latter part of the year.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2023, to navigate market volatility and safeguard liquidity, MWG proactively enforced stringent leverage controls and optimized inventory down to safe thresholds. The Group utilized unsecured short-term and long-term borrowings carrying floating interest rates to fund working capital requirements. Short-term debt maturities fell between January 2, 2024, and June 26, 2024. For long-term borrowings, the principal maturity is scheduled for September 16, 2025, denominated in USD. Maintaining a flexible capital structure, coupled with stringent cost rationalization and operational streamlining, enabled the Group to preserve a healthy financial foundation and fulfill supplier payment obligations punctually amid a highly volatile operating environment.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Amid prolonged demand weakness and shifting consumer behavior, MWG conducted a comprehensive asset portfolio review, successfully rightsizing inventory to safe levels to mitigate capital lockup risks. Amid a sweeping restructuring initiated in Q4 2023 under a " volume-reduction, quality-enhancement" mandate, the Group streamlined its store footprint and headcount, while systematically liquidating underperforming retail outlets, projects, and investments. Financial disclosures reported no anomalous contingent liabilities, substantial off-balance-sheet guarantees, or material legal disputes that would exert an adverse impact on operations subsequent to the fiscal year-end of December 31, 2023.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
MWG continues to integrate Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria into its long-term operational strategy to realize its vision of becoming Southeast Asia’s leading retail conglomerate by 2030:
- **Environmental (E):** The Group focuses on mitigating greenhouse gas emissions by scaling up renewable energy initiatives and prioritizing eco-friendly fuels to systematically advance toward its Net-Zero roadmap. MWG optimizes resource utilization through technological applications, significantly curtails environmental waste, encourages consumers to reduce single-use plastic bags, and collaborates with supply chain partners to implement circular economy principles in waste recovery and recycling.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is recognized as the Group's greatest asset. MWG champions workplace diversity, empowerment, and equitable access to learning and professional development, while elevating employee awareness through structured ESG training programs and knowledge repositories. For consumers, the Group remains committed to product quality, safety, and superior customer service. Furthermore, MWG cultivates healthy, long-term vendor partnerships and actively engages in philanthropic initiatives to give back to the community.
- **Governance (G):** MWG’s corporate governance framework strictly adheres to the Law on Enterprises, comprising the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Directors (BOD), the Directorate, and the Board of Supervision/Audit Committee reporting directly to the BOD. The Group upholds rigorous business ethics, transparency, and regulatory compliance, supported by an internal tracking system for ESG metrics to measure execution efficiency and align disclosures with best-practice standards.
