1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Amidst macroeconomic headwinds and a subdued retail landscape, Vietnam's jewelry sector faced severe structural hurdles, including raw material scarcity, softening purchasing power, and supply chain bottlenecks. Nevertheless, the domestic gold market demonstrated a positive structural shift toward greater transparency. Driven by operational resilience and strategic agility, Phu Nhuan Jewelry Joint Stock Company (PNJ) navigated these headwinds to deliver record-breaking financial performance in its 37-year operating history, posting net revenue of VND 37,823 billion (up 14.1% YoY) and net income of VND 2,113 billion (up 7.2% YoY). The core growth drivers stemmed from a steadfast commitment to transparent governance, rigorous risk management aligned with international benchmarks, targeted marketing campaigns, digital transformation, supply chain optimization, and retail operational excellence—collectively cementing PNJ’s dominant domestic market share and expanding its regional footprint in the jewelry industry.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PNJ’s short-term capital structure is maintained through commercial bank borrowings and unsecured employee loans to finance working capital requirements. Specifically, employee unsecured loans carry a 3-month tenure at a fixed interest rate of 3.5% p.a. On the banking front, PNJ maintains robust credit relationships with a diverse syndicate of major financial institutions, including VietinBank, Vietcombank, BIDV (Hoc Mon Branch), ACB, VIB, KEB Hana Bank, Shinhan Vietnam, HSBC, SCB, Woori Bank, SMBC, and CCB. These credit facilities are secured by dynamic inventories (valued at no less than covenant requirements, recording VND 2,930 billion at year-end) and a portion of tangible fixed assets, alongside uncollateralized credit lines. Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks are tightly managed through flexible short-term credit limits and efficient working capital turnover cycles.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding provisioning, PNJ maintains a conservative provisioning policy for unlisted long-term financial investments, most notably a 100% provision booked against its equity stake in Dong A Commercial Joint Stock Bank (DongA Bank), which remains under the State Bank of Vietnam’s special control with trading restrictions. For trade receivables and doubtful debts, other short-term receivables overdue from 6 months to over 3 years are duly accounted for (recording VND 31.78 billion at year-end 2024), whereas trade receivables and short-term loans (such as the loan extended to Golden Friend Joint Stock Company at an 8.5% p.a. interest rate) exhibit no overdue or non-performing risk. Notably, a significant post-balance sheet event materialized: on January 17, 2025, DongA Bank was compulsorily transferred to Ho Chi Minh City Development Joint Stock Commercial Bank (HDBank) to operate as a single-member limited liability bank 100%-owned by HDBank, thereby terminating all of PNJ’s rights and economic interests in the entity.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PNJ has strategically integrated sustainable development into its long-term corporate strategy, laying the groundwork for future access to green capital markets. The company prioritizes environmental impact mitigation, energy efficiency, and circular economy manufacturing standards, earning recognition as a Top 10 Sustainable Enterprise in the Trade-Services sector alongside various environmental accolades.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is recognized as the firm's most valuable asset. PNJ focuses on elevating employee living standards, upholding occupational health and safety (OHS) protocols, enforcing gender equality, and maintaining robust welfare and structured human resource training policies. Furthermore, the company consistently propagates humanitarian values through comprehensive social security initiatives and community engagement across local operating regions.
- **Governance (G):** PNJ operates under a transparent governance framework characterized by a highly independent Board of Directors, comprising seasoned independent directors with extensive expertise in finance, human resources, and corporate governance. Compliance oversight is rigorously executed via the Audit Committee and internal audit systems, ensuring strict adherence to public company governance standards while progressing toward international best practices.
