Below is the standard qualitative equity research summary based on the 2021 Annual Report disclosure of Ho Chi Minh City Development Joint Stock Commercial Bank (HDBank - Ticker: HDB):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
The year 2021 marked a period of dynamic operations and robust growth for HDBank amidst an economic landscape fraught with challenges from the COVID-19 pandemic. The bank's core growth drivers stemmed from the optimization of its credit portfolio, the promotion of digital banking products and services, and the effective exploitation of its retail financial ecosystem and supply chain financing. Core business operations maintained a steady growth momentum, contributing a major proportion to total operating income. Additionally, strict operating cost management helped improve the cost-to-income ratio (CIR), establishing a solid foundation for the system-wide breakthrough in pre-tax and post-tax profits for the 2021 financial year. On the stock market, HDB shares recorded a favorable performance with a full-year price growth of +59.64% (opening at VND 7,880 and closing at VND 12,580), trading within a range of VND 6,590 to VND 13,840 alongside an average matching liquidity of 4,745,362 shares/session, reflecting investors' firm confidence in the bank's long-term prospects.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As a credit institution, HDBank's capital structure is managed in compliance with strict capital adequacy ratios mandated by the State Bank of Vietnam. Mobilized capital primarily originates from corporate and retail customer deposits, combined with interbank funding and the issuance of valuable papers. The mobilization and lending interest rate structure was managed flexibly, closely tracking monetary market developments and the Government's interest rate support packages, thereby helping the bank maintain a healthy net interest margin (NIM). Credit limits and financial relationships with major partners were maintained stably, ensuring abundant liquidity to fully meet the economy's capital demands while effectively controlling interest rate and liquidity risks in accordance with Basel II standards.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
In 2021, credit risk management and risk provisioning were prudently implemented by HDBank, strictly adhering to debt classification and provisioning regulations stipulated by the State Bank of Vietnam. The bank proactively maintained a robust provisioning buffer to hedge against potential negative impacts on asset quality caused by the pandemic. Off-balance-sheet commitments, guarantees, and letters of credit (L/C) were tightly managed through a risk limit system applied to individual customers and industries. No major legal disputes, litigation, or unusual financial events were recorded after the accounting closing date of December 31, 2021, that could have a material impact on the bank's financial position.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environment (E):** HDBank actively integrates environmental and social risk factors into its credit-granting process, prioritizing capital flows toward green development sectors, high-tech agriculture, and renewable energy. The bank focuses on optimizing internal operations to conserve energy, minimize greenhouse gas emissions, and promote a paperless working culture.
- **Social (S):** The bank maintains a professional, equitable working environment, strictly ensuring occupational health and safety (OHS) standards for its employees. Competitive compensation and benefit policies, alongside regular professional development training programs, are emphasized to build high-quality human resources, while actively participating in social welfare and community-oriented activities.
- **Governance (G):** HDBank strictly complies with public company governance standards and aims toward advanced international benchmarks. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence with an experienced membership structure; independent audits, internal controls, and information transparency are comprehensively executed, closely tied to a sustainable development orientation based on the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
