Below is the professional institutional equity research translation of the financial report summary for Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (ACB):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, ACB delivered impressive business growth despite macroeconomic headwinds and monetary policy tightening. Total operating income (TOI) reached VND 28,790 billion, up 22% year-on-year (YoY). Core growth was driven by a strong strategic focus on the retail segment, a shift in funding sources toward low-cost funds, and an improving net interest margin (NIM expanding past 4% to 4%). Net interest income (NII) reached VND 23,534 billion (up 24%), while non-interest income hit VND 5,257 billion (up 14%), accounting for 18% of total revenue. Card business operations experienced robust growth of 86% compared to 2021, supported significantly by premium international credit and debit card lines. Bancassurance continued to serve as a key pillar, accounting for 55% of total net fee and commission income and generating nearly VND 2,000 billion (up 31%). Subsidiaries such as ACBS, ACBL, and ACBA maintained stable operational performance, contributing positively to the Group's overall profitability, with consolidated profit before tax (PBT) reaching VND 17,114 billion, achieving 114% of the target.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWING & INTEREST RATES
ACB's capital structure was optimized to meet stringent capital adequacy standards under Basel III and international ICAAP frameworks. By the end of 2022, consolidated total assets reached VND 607,875 billion, an increase of 15.18%. Equity stood at VND 58,439 billion (up 30%), of which charter capital accounted for VND 33,774 billion. The Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) was maintained at a high level of 12.80% (well above the regulatory minimum of 8%). Customer deposits reached VND 413,953 billion (up 8.96%), wherein term deposits represented the majority (~78%) and CASA comprised the remainder (~22%). Total customer loans reached VND 413,706 billion (up 14.31%), with retail credit constituting up to 94% of total outstanding loans. Amid volatile market interest rates and an upward trend starting in the second half of 2022, ACB proactively balanced the duration of its assets and liabilities, strictly controlling the ratio of short-term funds used for medium- and long-term loans at 19.63% (significantly lower than the 34% regulatory ceiling), thereby ensuring safe liquidity and mitigating interest rate risk.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
ACB's risk management and asset quality remained among the best-controlled in the market. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was kept low at 0.74% (well below the 1% threshold), while the loan loss reserve (LLR) coverage ratio was maintained at a high 159%, providing a robust provisioning buffer. Credit risk provisioning expenses decreased to VND 71 billion (down 98% YoY), primarily due to the reversal of provisions for Covid-19-impacted customers who had recovered their financial capacity. Off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent obligations arising from trade finance and guarantees were safely controlled within State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) limits. In 2022, there were no material legal disputes or abnormal financial events occurring after December 31 that would negatively impact the bank's operations. ACB’s share price traded within a low of VND 8,820 and a high of VND 14,700, closing the year at VND 11,060, reflecting the broader market correction phase of the Vietnamese stock market.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
ACB consistently executes its sustainable development model aligned with Environmental (E), Social (S), and Governance (G) pillars:
- **Environmental (E):** The bank proactively promoted "Green Transactions" and "Go Paperless" initiatives, saving tons of paper and reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions were optimally controlled through efficient electricity consumption management across office buildings and branch networks. ACB also implemented carbon offsetting and waste recycling programs to work toward its Net-Zero target by 2050.
- **Social (S):** ACB focuses on building a transparent, equitable, and safe working environment under the "Work:Live:Learn" standard. Remuneration, employee welfare (with average 2022 income reaching VND 417 million/person), and comprehensive healthcare programs (such as ACB Care and Eneji Station) were maintained at highly competitive levels. The proportion of employees meeting or exceeding expectations reached 90.80%. The bank also actively participated in social welfare, education, and community activities with a multi-billion VND budget.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance system strictly complies with legal regulations and OECD international standards. The Board of Directors (BOD) maintained a high level of independence, working closely with the Supervisory Board and independent auditor KPMG. Transparent information disclosure aligned with GRI standards enabled ACB to be ranked for consecutive years among enterprises with the best Investor Relations (IR) activities on the Vietnamese stock market.
