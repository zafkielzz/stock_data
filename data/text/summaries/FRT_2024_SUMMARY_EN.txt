1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Fiscal year 2024 marked a robust operational and market outperformance for FPT Digital Retail JSC (FRT). The company’s share price posted an impressive annual gain of +73.62%, closing at VND 141,400 compared to an opening price of VND 81,440, supported by an average daily trading volume of 595,202 shares. Operationally, FRT delivered consolidated cumulative revenue of VND 40,104 billion, representing a 26% year-on-year increase and achieving 108% of its full-year target. Cumulative profit before tax (PBT) achieved a breakthrough at VND 527 billion, significantly outperforming the guidance at 422% of the targeted budget.

Core growth momentum was predominantly driven by the FPT Long Châu pharmacy chain, which sustained aggressive expansion by opening 446 new pharmacies, bringing its total footprint to 1,943 outlets. Average revenue per pharmacy remained resilient and robust at VND 1.2 billion/month. The pharmacy-based immunization segment also demonstrated a major strategic leap with the inauguration of 166 new vaccination centers, expanding the total network to 126 centers across 54 provinces and cities, thereby completing the healthcare ecosystem loop. Conversely, the FPT Shop ICT retail chain underwent comprehensive restructuring amid headwinds from natural disasters (Typhoon Yagi) and intense pricing pressure. Management proactively pruned underperforming stores, reducing the retail footprint to 634 points of sale, while actively pivoting the business model by upgrading 54 stores into FPT Shop Home Appliance formats and scaling up the Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) service to over 400,000 subscribers to bolster gross margins. The online channel remained a bright spot, generating VND 7,165 billion in revenue, up 23% year-on-year.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Due to the absence of detailed Notes to the Financial Statements in the data source, specific quantitative metrics regarding short-term and long-term debt balances, fixed versus floating interest rate structures, and the roster of primary lending banking institutions for FRT were not publicly disclosed.

Nevertheless, from a qualitative analytical perspective, FRT’s capital structure exhibits clear divergent pressures. The aggressive network expansion strategy of the Long Châu chain, alongside heavy investments in high-tech medical infrastructure and vaccination services, necessitates substantial working capital and a high level of capital expenditures (CAPEX) to maintain long-term assets. Conversely, management's proactive streamlining of the FPT Shop store network—cutting underperforming outlets and accelerating receivables collection—has enabled FRT to optimize cash flow management and alleviate short-term debt burdens associated with the consumer electronics and ICT segment, which typically carries a slower inventory turnover. Cash flow pressures in the upcoming fiscal year will largely depend on Long Châu's ability to organically generate sufficient operating cash flow to fund new capital deployments.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Similar to the debt profile, the lack of detailed Notes to the Financial Statements precludes access to granular data regarding inventory obsolescence provisions and allowances for doubtful accounts.

Notwithstanding this data limitation, enterprise risk management frameworks have flagged substantial headwinds stemming from global supply chain disruptions induced by geopolitical instability in Ukraine and the Middle East, alongside climate change impacts affecting key maritime trade routes (the Panama and Suez Canals). Vietnam’s heavy import dependency for computers, electronic components, and pharmaceuticals (national imports of computers and electronic products have exceeded USD 100 billion) exposes FRT directly to the risk of surging logistics costs and prolonged delivery lead times. This indirectly heightens inventory obsolescence and near-expiry risks across both the ICT and pharmaceutical divisions, necessitating higher provisioning buffers if demand forecasting models are not sufficiently optimized.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Regarding Environmental (E) criteria, FRT actively drives comprehensive digital transformation by deploying Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Big Data analytics. Operational digitalization not only streamlines workflows but also contributes to energy conservation and lower indirect emissions by minimizing paper consumption and optimizing logistics routing.

Under Social (S) criteria, the company anchors its corporate philosophy on the tenet "People are the Foundation," committing to foster an equitable and professional work environment focused on talent cultivation and workplace safety. FRT translates corporate social responsibility into action through Long Châu’s mission of "Comprehensive Care," facilitating public access to high-quality, reasonably priced healthcare products and expanding vaccine coverage across provinces—particularly in regions with limited access to modern healthcare services.

On Governance (G), the company maintains a steadfast commitment to corporate integrity and maximal transparency to safeguard the legitimate rights of shareholders and business partners. Robust internal control and risk management systems are systematically implemented to rapidly identify and flexibly navigate macroeconomic shocks and natural disaster risks.
