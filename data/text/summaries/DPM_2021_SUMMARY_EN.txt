Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the 2021 qualitative analysis summary for PetroVietnam Fertilizer and Chemicals Corporation (DPM):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, PetroVietnam Fertilizer and Chemicals Corporation - JSC (DPM) delivered outstanding business growth, underpinned by a global fertilizer and chemical market environment that benefited from skyrocketing energy and feedstock gas prices. Core growth drivers stemmed from the production and trading of nitrogenous fertilizers (Phu My Urea) alongside specialty chemical products such as NH3, UFC85, and NPK. Global fertilizer supply shortages drove average selling prices up sharply across quarters, fully offsetting the pressure from escalating feedstock costs. While the domestic market maintained its core role with a distribution network spanning all key agricultural regions, DPM aggressively expanded its export activities, successfully capitalizing on surging international demand across Southeast Asian and broader Asian markets. This contributed a substantial share to consolidated revenue and propelled the company’s gross profit margin significantly higher compared to previous years.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DPM’s capital structure in 2021 remained highly healthy with a low financial leverage ratio, reflecting robust financial self-sufficiency and abundant operating cash flows. Short-term and long-term borrowings accounted for a minor proportion of total capital, primarily comprising local currency credit facilities dedicated to working capital requirements and periodic plant maintenance and upgrades. These loans bear competitive commercial interest rates from major domestic banks, largely structured as floating rates adjusted over short tenors; however, they pose no material pressure given the low actual outstanding debt balances. Capital structure risks and cash flow pressures were kept to a minimum, supported by a substantial buffer of cash and bank deposits accumulated from positive operating results. This enabled the company to proactively service maturing debt obligations without heavy reliance on external credit funding.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
DPM executed provisioning prudently in strict accordance with prevailing accounting standards. Inventory write-down provisions were maintained at reasonable levels, accurately reflecting the net realizable value of raw materials and finished goods amid volatile market conditions. Allowances for doubtful accounts were provisioned based on realistic assessments of recoverability from corporate clients and distribution agents, with low overdue debt ratios supported by rigorous trade credit control policies. Off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent liabilities did not record any material or abnormal items. Regarding post-balance-sheet events, on April 15, 2022, DPM officially published its audited 2021 Annual Report. Throughout the year, its share price demonstrated a strong breakout, surging from an opening price of 6,550 VND to a closing price of 18,520 VND (representing a 182.75% gain), trading within a range of 5,180 VND to 21,010 VND with an average matched liquidity of over 4 million shares per session, reflecting robust financial market interest in the company's business outlook.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DPM is committed to executing its sustainable development strategy through optimizing energy efficiency, reducing fuel and raw material consumption per ton of converted product, and rigorously monitoring greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2). Technical innovation initiatives and circular economy models have been advanced to recycle byproduct streams, minimize negative environmental impacts, and align with net-zero emission roadmaps in accordance with broader energy sector commitments.
- **Social (S):** The company maintains the highest occupational health and safety (OHS) standards across its high-risk manufacturing plants, ensuring zero severe labor accidents. Human resources policies strictly comply with labor laws, ensuring gender equality in recruitment and promotion while maintaining comprehensive welfare benefits, comprehensive health insurance, and regular skills-training programs for employees.
- **Governance (G):** DPM’s governance framework operates under high standards of transparency with independent oversight from the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board. Financial and non-financial disclosures are executed fully and punctually on the electronic information portals of the State Securities Commission and the Stock Exchange. Independent audits are conducted annually, strictly adhering to Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) while progressively orienting toward international sustainable development standards (GRI / SDGs).
