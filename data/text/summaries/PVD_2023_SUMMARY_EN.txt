### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2023, Petrovietnam Drilling and Well Service Corporation (HOSE: PVD) posted robust financial results, registering total revenue of VND 6,160 billion and net profit after tax of VND 546 billion, thereby successfully executing its strategic directive of "Capitalizing on Opportunities - Restructuring for Growth." The core growth driver stemmed from the sustained high utilization rates within the Southeast Asian jack-up rig market, compounded by the efficacy of the Corporation's international market expansion strategy. A key operational highlight during the year was the deployment of nearly all PVD-owned rigs across Tier-1 international markets, including Malaysia, Indonesia, and Brunei, which garnered multiple prestigious industry accolades. Notably, the *PV DRILLING III* set drilling records and was honored as the "Best Rig" by Petronas, while the *PV DRILLING V* was awarded "Rig of the Year" by Brunei Shell Petroleum. This strategic shift toward continuous overseas deployment was also the primary catalyst for a significant contraction in direct fuel consumption—specifically a 50.1% decline in fuel oil (FO) and an over 80% drop in diesel oil (DO)—subsequently driving a corresponding reduction in direct greenhouse gas emissions.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PV Drilling’s corporate governance and financial architecture operate under strict compliance with the 2020 Law on Enterprises, adhering to a two-tier board governance model comprising the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Directors (BOD), the Supervisory Board, and the Chief Executive Officer. Amid a macroeconomic landscape fraught with inflationary headwinds and a prolonged high-interest-rate environment throughout 2023—which exerted upward pressure on input costs, rig operating expenses, and global logistics supply chains—Management proactively exercised stringent cash flow controls and optimized operational expenditure. The Corporation's ownership and governance structure is anchored in the principles of transparency, fairness, accountability, and sustainable development to safeguard liquidity and ensure the efficient allocation of capital resources toward investments in new drilling rigs, workover equipment packages, and IT infrastructure projects for the ensuing growth cycle.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding asset-risk management, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, the Corporation reversed provisions for inventory obsolescence amounting to VND 535,622,720 (compared to a reversal of VND 11,657,688,560 in FY2022), following rigorous reassessments of inventory condition and net realizable value. Operational and legal risk controls are maintained comprehensively via established quality and environmental management systems (ISO 9001, ISO 14001) as well as the Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality (HSEQ) risk and opportunity management procedure (PVD/HSEQ/023), which undergoes mandatory annual reassessments. The Corporation reported no material abnormal financial contingencies or significant post-balance-sheet litigation arising after the close of the financial year that would exert a material adverse effect on the consolidated financial position.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PV Drilling rigorously monitors its environmental footprint via the online HSEQ reporting software and the ISO 14001:2015 management system. The Corporation has implemented pragmatic energy-efficiency initiatives, such as retrofitting fluorescent fixtures with LED lighting across its rig fleet—yielding an annualized savings of 310.7 metric tons of DO—and deploying MAESTRO software onboard the *PV DRILLING V* to optimize fuel consumption efficiency. Driven by the strategic pivot toward overseas markets and proactive energy-conservation measures, Scope 1 direct greenhouse gas emissions plummeted by 81.13% year-over-year in FY2023. Furthermore, PVD actively supports the Government’s national afforestation initiative through a five-year program focused on planting and preserving protection forests in Cần Giờ.
- **Social (S):** Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) remains a paramount priority, with safety metrics consistently outperforming International Association of Drilling Contractors (IADC) benchmarks. PVD rigorously enforces stratified periodic health screenings customized by labor cohorts, coupled with early critical illness screenings and strict adherence to pandemic prevention protocols. Corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs were expanded with a total budget exceeding VND 1 billion in FY2023, directed toward educational support for underprivileged students and community development across multiple localities.
- **Governance (G):** The Corporation implements a corporate governance framework benchmarked against both domestic best practices (the Vietnam Corporate Governance Code - VN CG Code) and international standards (OECD guidelines). The Board of Directors features clearly demarcated responsibilities concerning sustainability oversight, supported by well-structured specialized committees. Executive leadership regularly participates in professional development programs focused on corporate governance, finance, and digital transformation (such as the VIOD Director Certification Program). Information disclosure, independent audits, and internal controls are executed with a high degree of transparency to maximize and protect the legitimate rights of both domestic and international shareholders.
