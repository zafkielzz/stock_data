### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Throughout fiscal year 2023, the equity price performance of Hoang Anh Gia Lai Joint Stock Company (HAG) clearly reflected the market's recovery expectations, registering an impressive return of +44.90% (opening at VND 9,110 and closing at VND 13,200 per share), accompanied by robust liquidity averaging nearly 15 million shares matched per session.

The core operational growth drivers exhibited significant divergence across two strategic operating segments:
* **Fruit Segment (Bananas and Durians):** Acted as a robust earnings buffer following the execution of the phytosanitary Protocol between Vietnam and China, which established a transparent legal framework for official export quotas. Given the weekly volatility of selling prices to China (on a CNF basis), HAG proactively restructured its sales mix by increasing export volumes to Japan and South Korea under fixed-price contracts to mitigate market volatility risks.
* **Pork Production Segment:** Faced severe headwinds driven by a sharp decline in both global and domestic pork prices starting in July 2023, pressured by ample supply and subdued consumer purchasing power across Asia. HAG demonstrated operational flexibility by halting all new farm construction plans, focusing instead on cost optimization utilizing existing infrastructure to enhance herd quality. Through these measures, the company sustained year-on-year revenue growth in the swine segment despite a significant contraction in profit margins.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

HAG continued to operate under severely strained financial structural conditions. The enterprise remains heavily reliant on bank borrowings and floating-rate bonds, leaving financial expenses highly sensitive to interest rate fluctuations. As of year-end 2023, liquidity risk reached critical levels with short-term liabilities exceeding current assets by VND 941.87 billion, compounded by a massive accumulated deficit of VND 1,669.17 billion. Breaches of certain debt covenants alongside defaults on principal and interest payments for bonds and borrowings led the independent auditor to issue a material uncertainty regarding the Group's ability to continue as a going concern.

Furthermore, owing to capital deployment in Laos and Cambodia, the company bears dual foreign exchange risk: the sharp depreciation of the Lao Kip (LAK) degraded the translated value of foreign investments, whereas the appreciation trend of the U.S. Dollar provided a natural operational hedge for fruit export revenues. To alleviate cash flow pressures and sustain operations over the subsequent 12-month horizon, executive management is executing a remediation plan involving the restructuring of debt terms, recovery of trade receivables, liquidation of financial investments, and preparation for a private equity placement to existing shareholders to bolster capitalization.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

To uphold financial conservatism, HAG maintained provisions for doubtful trade receivables and inventory write-downs stemming from damaged or obsolete merchandise based on net realizable values as of the fiscal year-end.

Within the asset composition, short-term receivables included substantial claims due from major counterparties, namely Hoang Anh Gia Lai Hydropower Joint Stock Company and Ms. Nguyen Thi Thu Thao. Notably, a favorable financial event occurring immediately post-period-end was HAG's successful recovery of the entire VND 350 billion receivable from Ms. Nguyen Thi Thu Thao prior to the issuance of the consolidated financial statements, significantly mitigating short-term liquidity pressures. Additionally, during the year, HAG completed a pivotal M&A transaction by converting a VND 173.85 billion receivable into equity to acquire 100% ownership of Bolaven High-Tech Agriculture Company (Laos), directly expanding its cultivated agricultural landbank by 466.74 hectares in Champasak Province.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

The company has not yet published a standalone Sustainability (ESG) Report; however, core sustainability directives have been integrated into the Annual Report:
* **Environmental (E):** HAG steadfastly pursues a circular economy strategy and implements GLOBALG.A.P. standards. Agricultural by-products from livestock and cultivation are thoroughly recycled to enrich soil organic matter. The enterprise proactively addresses climate change by establishing firebreaks ahead of the dry season and dredging drainage canal networks to prevent waterlogging prior to the monsoon season. Currently, there is no established methodology for measuring greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, and 3) aligned with a Net-Zero trajectory.
* **Social (S):** Biosecurity protocols are stringently monitored within swine production to preempt epizootic risks, ensuring a secure, traceable, and wholesome food supply for consumers.
* **Governance (G):** The Supervisory Board coordinates closely with the Internal Audit Subcommittee and the independent auditor to review the internal control framework. Nonetheless, compliance with financial covenants extended to creditors remains a major governance bottleneck that must be rectified to meet modern corporate governance standards.
