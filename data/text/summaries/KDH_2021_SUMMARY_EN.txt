### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In FY2021, Khang Điền House Trading and Investment JSC (KDH) recorded an impressive equity appreciation of +90.27% (opening at VND 18,290 and closing at VND 34,800, reaching an intraday peak of VND 37,400) alongside an average trading liquidity of 2.91 million shares per session. This breakout reflects robust market conviction in the company’s landbanking strategy and project execution cadence. 

Core growth drivers stem exclusively from the domestic real estate market in Ho Chi Minh City and Thu Duc City (the company has zero export exposure). Low-rise residential developments (Verosa Park, Lucasta, The Venica) and high-rise apartment complexes (Jamila, Safira, Lovera Vista) maintained an absorption rate exceeding 90%. Furthermore, scale expansion during the year was bolstered by a subsidiary M&A transaction—acquiring a 99.9% stake in Nguyen Thu Real Estate Development JSC for a consideration of VND 419.58 billion (recognizing a bargain purchase gain). The company also expanded its aggregate operational land bank to over 500 hectares in the Southern macro-region of HCMC, including more than 100 hectares of industrial land, establishing a firm foundation for the medium-to-long-term development cycle.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Khang Điền maintained a conservative financial structure supported by flexible capital diversification throughout FY2021. The company successfully issued VND 400 billion in private placement bonds to supplement working capital requirements. Notably, financial leverage and operating cash flow were substantially underwritten by the full divestment of 19,850,640 treasury shares in September 2021 at an average execution price of VND 40,866 per share. 

Regarding liquidity management, the company maintained a substantial cash and cash equivalents position comprising short-term bank deposits yielding annual interest rates of 2.9% - 4.0% and held-to-maturity investments yielding 3.7% - 5.4% per annum. Long-term assets reflect 30 corporate bonds with a 10-year maturity carrying a floating interest rate pegged to the VietinBank Hanoi Branch reference rate plus a 0.8% annual spread. KDH's short-term cash flow pressure remains subdued owing to steady pre-sales cash inflows, despite the company's ongoing increase in advance payments for land acquisitions (such as the capital contribution transfer deposit for Phuoc Nguyen Real Estate Investment JSC pursuant to Board Resolution No. 25/2021).

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Khang Điền's asset quality remains robust. As of December 31, 2021, the company reported no overdue or impaired trade receivables or advances to suppliers, excluding items with fully allocated provisions as mandated by regulations. 

Inventories are predominantly composed of work-in-progress real estate, encompassing site clearance compensation, land leveling, and construction costs across key projects. Given the operational characteristic where project legal frameworks are fully finalized prior to commercial launches, the risk of inventory write-downs is exceptionally low. Off-balance sheet contingent liabilities and financial commitments primarily pertain to obligations regarding the completion of land-use right transfer procedures and ongoing M&A negotiations. The company recorded no material legal disputes that would impair its going-concern status subsequent to the fiscal year-end.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** KDH’s sustainability report is compiled in compliance with Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC and benchmarked against GRI standards. The company strategically focuses on developing green living spaces, optimizing biophilic architectural designs in large-scale projects. However, quantitative greenhouse gas emissions metrics (Scope 1, 2, and 3 disclosures) have not yet been detailed.
*   **Social (S):** KDH demonstrated exemplary corporate social responsibility during the Covid-19 outbreak. Across FY2020–2021, the company contributed VND 109 billion to the Vaccine Fund, medical subsidies, charity housing construction, and humanitarian cataract surgeries. Internally, 100% of customer service personnel were vaccinated and routinely screened, upholding occupational health and safety (OHS) standards and maintaining employee welfare during lockdown periods.
*   **Governance (G):** Corporate governance structures strictly adhere to the 2020 Law on Enterprises. The Board of Directors comprises five members, including two independent non-executive directors (accounting for a 40% ratio), ensuring independence and objectivity in oversight. The internal audit division operated effectively with seven meetings held during the year. The shareholding structure features participation from reputable foreign institutional funds such as VinaCapital (~12%) and Dragon Capital (~7%), enhancing information transparency and elevating corporate governance benchmarks.
