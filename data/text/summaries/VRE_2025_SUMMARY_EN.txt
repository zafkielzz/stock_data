### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2025, Vincom Retail demonstrated a robust operational recovery and growth trajectory against a more favorable macroeconomic backdrop, underpinned by a 9.2% expansion in total national retail sales of goods and consumer services. Full-year rental revenue reached VND 8,400 billion, representing a 6.6% year-on-year increase. The core growth driver stems from the Company’s portfolio of 90 shopping malls nationwide, spanning a total gross leasable area (GLA) of 1.91 million square meters. Newly leased area reached an all-time high, with the occupancy rate (including committed areas) as of December 31, 2025, improving by 2.7 percentage points year-on-year, propelled by the higher-than-expected absorption rates of newly launched retail assets. The commercial strategy centers on transitioning toward a "one-stop shoppertainment" model—integrating retail, F&B, and entertainment—positioning next-generation shopping malls as large-scale experiential destinations that act as "brand-launching pads" for both domestic and international retailers. *(Note: The enterprise focuses exclusively on the domestic market, with no export operations recorded).*

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vincom Retail maintained a conservative and sustainable capital structure in 2025, characterized by a low debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio of 10.59%. As of December 31, 2025, total borrowings and financial lease liabilities stood at VND 6,401 billion, which includes a syndicated loan facility exceeding VND 6,200 billion disbursed during the year. The debt profile comprises a USD-denominated facility (maturing in July 2028) carrying a fixed interest rate of 9.0% p.a., and a VND-denominated floating-rate loan (recording an average rate of 7.68% p.a. in 2025). Sensitivity analysis indicates that foreign exchange risk associated with the USD loan exerts a minimal impact on financial performance; specifically, with exchange rate fluctuations ranging from ±0.5% to ±1.0%, unrealized foreign exchange gains/losses would fluctuate between ±VND 21–42 billion (equivalent to approximately 0.24%–0.48% of 2025 net revenue). Overall, low financial leverage, secured liquidity, and well-matched debt maturities afford the Company ample balance sheet headroom and robust resilience against market volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Based on 2025 financial documents and corporate governance reports, Vincom Retail’s risk management and compliance practices remain transparent. During the year, the Company executed no treasury share transactions (total treasury shares maintained at 56,500,000 shares as of December 31, 2025), and no insider trading was recorded among members of the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, or major shareholders. Regarding financial and legal obligations, the reports show no abnormal provisions, major pending litigations, or material off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments that could adversely affect cash flows. Related-party transactions were executed and disclosed in strict compliance with securities regulations (with 26 ordinary transactions recorded in Note 38 of the Financial Statements). No material adjusting or non-adjusting events occurred subsequent to the fiscal year-end of December 31, 2025.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vincom Retail remains steadfast in pursuing its sustainable development strategy, focusing on the implementation of "Green Mall" initiatives to enhance operational efficiency and mitigate environmental impact. Key priorities include energy management, resource optimization, waste management, water consumption control, indoor environmental quality enhancement, and greenhouse gas emissions oversight to advance long-term environmental targets.
- **Social (S):** The Company prioritizes high-quality human capital development by attracting young talent, upgrading managerial capabilities, and fostering succession pipelines. Labor policies are executed comprehensively to guarantee occupational health and safety (OHS) standards and equal opportunities, while cultivating a "Heart-Centric Service" culture to elevate customer experience and actively create value for urban communities via tenant-support and local product programs.
- **Governance (G):** Vincom Retail’s corporate governance standards closely align with international best practices and stock market regulations. The Company maintains high transparency in disclosures, collaborates closely with its independent auditor (with 2025 audit fees amounting to VND 4,911,706,000 and non-audit fees remaining negligible at 0.75%), and integrates global reporting frameworks such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) into its strategic orientation.
