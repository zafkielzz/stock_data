Below is the qualitative financial analysis summary for Vietnam National Petroleum Group (PLX) based on publicly disclosed information and actual performance data for the fiscal year 2021:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
PLX’s business operations in 2021 were profoundly and intricately impacted by the complex developments of the COVID-19 pandemic and the unpredictable volatility of the global energy market. Nevertheless, PLX shares recorded stable full-year price movement of +0.04% (with an opening price of 45,650 VND and a closing price of 45,670 VND), trading within a range of 37,770 VND to 52,880 VND, and achieving an average matching volume of 2,003,667 shares/session. The group's core growth driver continued to stem from its core business of petroleum import, export, and domestic distribution, alongside ancillary segments such as petrochemicals, gas, asphalt, and maritime shipping. Maintaining an uninterrupted supply chain amid prolonged social distancing played a pivotal role in helping the group uphold its dominant market share domestically, despite profit margins facing severe pressure from surging global transport and logistics costs as well as fluctuating world oil prices.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PLX’s capital and debt structure in 2021 reflected a prudent financial management strategy aimed at safeguarding liquidity during the crisis period. The group maintained a reasonable leverage ratio by balancing equity and short-term borrowings to meet working capital requirements, primarily financing the import and reserve of strategic petroleum products within allocated quotas. Major credit facilities were largely arranged through leading commercial banks in Vietnam, with interest rates closely tracking money market movements. Floating interest rate risks were tightly controlled through flexible cash flow management plans and short-term debt maturity negotiation policies, preventing borrowing costs from exerting excessive pressure on the group's net cash flow from core operating activities.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
PLX’s provisioning work in 2021 was executed rigorously in compliance with prevailing accounting standards. Due to the highly volatile nature of global oil prices, the group maintained a conservative inventory write-down provisioning policy to accurately reflect the net realizable value of petroleum reserves at year-end. Regarding doubtful debts, receivables from credit-buying customers and the agency network were periodically reviewed to establish adequate provisions commensurate with collection risk levels. Off-balance-sheet commitments, contingent liabilities related to foreign exchange contracts, and credit limits for issuing letters of credit (L/C) to facilitate goods imports were controlled within safe margins. No unusual financial events or material legal disputes arising after the end of the fiscal year on December 31, 2021, were recorded that could adversely affect the enterprise's operations.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** PLX focused on implementing environmental protection initiatives by controlling greenhouse gas emissions at terminals and service stations (focusing on the measurement of direct Scope 1 and indirect Scope 2 emissions), while optimizing energy use efficiency and deploying circular economy models to minimize vaporized petroleum losses and plastic waste across the retail distribution system.
- **Social (S):** The group maintained strict Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards to ensure absolute safety regarding fire prevention, explosion control, and toxic hazards for the frontline workforce. Human resource policies ensured gender equality, emphasized technical skills training programs, and maintained comprehensive welfare and social insurance regimes for employees.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance apparatus operated under high standards of transparency, with a strong emphasis on the independence of the Board of Directors. Financial statements were independently audited by leading audit firms. Information disclosure fully complied with stock market regulations, while progressively integrating international sustainable development standards (GRI / SDGs) into the group's long-term development strategy.
