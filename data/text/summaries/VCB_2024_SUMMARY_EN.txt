### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2024, Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - VCB) continued to affirm its position as Vietnam's premier banking institution in terms of operating efficiency and asset quality, navigating a macroeconomic backdrop adversely impacted by geopolitical conflicts, persistent inflation, and trade friction risks. VCB's core growth driver stemmed from credit expansion coupled with stringent asset quality containment. Specifically, total assets surpassed VND 2.0 trillion, expanding 13% year-over-year (YoY); total gross loans reached nearly VND 1.5 trillion, marking a 14% YoY increase; and customer deposits stood at over VND 1.5 trillion, up 9% YoY. Regarding financial performance, profit before tax (PBT) reached VND 42,236 billion, representing a 2% YoY growth and sustaining its leading position across the domestic banking sector. This earnings expansion was underpinned by an effective business model pivot, diversified non-interest income streams, and the preservation of a high-tier client franchise. Furthermore, VCB’s share price appreciated by 10.01% over the year, moving from an opening price of VND 54,650 to a closing price of VND 60,120, within an intraday/historical trading range of VND 54,190 to VND 66,250, accompanied by an average daily trading volume of 1.49 million shares per session.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VCB’s funding structure and liquidity profile were proactively fortified to meet rigorous prudential standards. Throughout 2024, lending rate yields across the banking system trended downward, in alignment with monetary directives from the Government, the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV), and prevailing market dynamics. Customer deposit mobilization reached over VND 1.5 trillion (up 9% YoY), ensuring an efficient asset-liability match against a 14% credit growth rate. Core operational safety metrics—including liquidity reserve ratios, the 30-day liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) for converted foreign currencies (USD), the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), and the Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR)—strictly adhered to Circular No. 22/2019/TT-NHNN and Basel II standards, which Vietcombank pioneered well ahead of the regulatory deadline. Cash flow pressures were prudently managed via a comprehensive liquidity risk management framework overseen by the Board of Directors’ Risk Management Committee.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vietcombank’s asset quality remained industry-leading, with the non-performing loan (NPL) ratio tightly contained at 0.97%, representing a contraction compared to 2023. Notably, the NPL coverage ratio reached a robust 223%, underscoring the bank's conservative provisioning policy and substantial financial resilience against credit risk shocks. Regarding post-balance sheet events subsequent to the December 31, 2024 financial year-end, a pivotal milestone was the SBV’s issuance of Decision No. 2299/QĐ-NHNN dated October 17, 2024, mandating the compulsory transfer of Construction Bank (CB) to Vietcombank. Concurrently, the bank is decisively executing support frameworks for the VCB Neo ecosystem in strict accordance with the approved Mandatory Transfer Plan. Credit risk provisioning was fully executed in compliance with current legal frameworks, safeguarding the balance sheet against all potential systemic shocks.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Vietcombank has institutionalized its strategic objective of becoming a frontrunner in Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) practices, deeply integrating these principles into its sustainable development model across five key pillars:
- **Environmental (E):** VCB stands as a domestic pioneer in successfully issuing green bonds compliant with both local regulations and international benchmarks (specifically the International Capital Market Association - ICMA Green Bond Principles). The bank emphasizes scaling green credit deployment and embedding environmental and social (E&S) risk management into its credit underwriting process, aiming to minimize carbon emissions and facilitate the economy's ecological transition.
- **Social (S):** Vietcombank prioritizes human capital development, marking its 9th consecutive year as one of the best workplaces in Vietnam and its 5th consecutive recognition as a model enterprise for employees. Over the 2020–2024 period, the bank allocated VND 2,500 billion (contributing over VND 12,000 billion to the state budget in 2024 alone) toward corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, focusing on underserved remote areas, border regions, islands, and vulnerable socioeconomic groups.
- **Governance (G):** VCB’s corporate governance architecture operates with high transparency, aligning with international standards through specialized Board committees (such as the Risk Management Committee and Personnel Committee). In 2024, the Board of Directors maintained high operational efficiency, convening 54 meetings and executing 171 written resolutions. The bank is committed to meeting global sustainability reporting standards such as GRI and TCFD, securing a spot among the top 20 listed enterprises with the best sustainability indices on the market.
