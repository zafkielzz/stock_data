### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, notwithstanding severe disruptions stemming from prolonged lockdowns and social distancing mandates across key operational hubs—including Bac Ninh, Bac Giang, Ho Chi Minh City, and Hai Phong—Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation (KBC) demonstrated a robust financial recovery. Consolidated total revenue reached VND 4,420.9 billion, representing 179.1% of the FY2020 figure (though achieving only 66.98% of the annual target). Core growth was predominantly propelled by land rentals, factory leases, real estate sales, and ancillary services, which generated VND 4,246.0 billion, up 197.4 YoY and accounting for 96% of aggregate revenue. Consolidated profit after tax (PAT) surged to VND 953.8 billion (298.2% of FY2020), while net income attributable to shareholders of the parent company came in at VND 782.3 billion (349.2% of FY2020). 

A standout strategic driver for the year was the Quang Chau Industrial Park in Bac Giang, which secured 56.6 hectares of newly leased area, catalyzed by a wave of capital reallocation from high-tech conglomerates. This asset contributed 39.5% of total group revenue and pushed the occupancy rate of this 426-hectare project to 96.78%. Although pandemic headwinds precluded the full attainment of management's top-line targets, KBC successfully attracted over USD 3 billion in foreign direct investment (FDI) into Vietnam in 2021, reinforcing its positioning as a premier destination for multinational corporations within the clean-tech sector.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2021, KBC proactively expanded its capital base, successfully securing over VND 8,685 billion to fund its land-bank expansion strategy and execution of greenfield projects. Capital-raising initiatives comprised a private placement of 100 million shares to professional institutional investors (raising over VND 3,408.2 billion, with distribution finalized by 2025), a VND 1,500 billion public bond issuance, and a VND 2,400 billion private bond issuance. 

Beyond capital markets, the Group executed two credit facility agreements with Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank) – Que Vo Branch, establishing a credit line of VND 1,376.8 billion to finance infrastructure development at the Nam Son Hap Linh Industrial Park project. This debt mobilization and business scale-up drove total financial, selling, and administrative expenses to VND 1,291.8 billion (2.3x FY2020 levels). Specifically, borrowing costs escalated to VND 541.3 billion—a 2.7x expansion driven by an expanded debt load—exerting near-term pressure on liquidity and necessitating rigorous working capital management.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Amid surging operational expenditures associated with relationship-building networks, sales acceleration, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, KBC recorded selling and other expenses of VND 321.9 billion (a 5.9x increase), alongside general and administrative (G&A) expenses of VND 428.5 billion (up 1.6x YoY). Regarding post-balance sheet events, KBC executed treasury share disposal transactions between February 16, 2022, and March 16, 2022. 

Furthermore, the primary structural risks inherent to the industrial park development model pertain to regulatory shifts in investment policies, land clearance and compensation frameworks, tax regulations, and annual land rental tariffs. KBC primarily mitigates these legal risks through rigorous explanatory reporting, stakeholder engagement, and demonstrating the socio-economic efficacy of its operations to regulatory authorities to safeguard project execution timelines for its major developments.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** KBC has yet to establish a comprehensive quantification framework for Scope 1, 2, and 3 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, nor has it formally adopted a Net-Zero trajectory, largely attributable to its industrial infrastructure development model executed via independent general contractors. The Group neither engages in material recycling nor utilizes reclaimed wastewater; all effluent is discharged into the centralized wastewater treatment systems of the industrial parks, maintaining compliance with National Technical Regulations on Industrial Wastewater (QCVN 40:2011/BTNMT, Column A). Environmental strategy remains centered on attracting "smoke-free" high-tech investors to curb industrial pollution, complemented by investments in landscape lakes, parks, and green buffers across satellite urban developments.
- **Social (S):** KBC's infrastructure projects play an instrumental role in unlocking large-scale land banks in economically disadvantaged rural districts, indirectly generating hundreds of thousands of stable jobs while augmenting local household incomes and living standards. The Group maintains a dedicated construction safety committee to rigorously monitor occupational health and safety (OHS) standards on-site, alongside prioritizing social welfare initiatives, constructing public amenities (such as schools and nurseries), and contributing to municipal fiscal budgets.
- **Governance (G):** KBC has maintained public company status since 2007 and has been listed on the Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange (HOSE) since 2009. Nevertheless, in sustainability reporting, the Group acknowledges that it has yet to engage a suitable advisory firm to standardize metrics against established international frameworks (such as GRI standards or SDGs), limiting current disclosures to foundational quantitative KPIs. Auditing practices and corporate transparency adhere strictly to statutory mandates governing large-cap listed enterprises in Vietnam.
