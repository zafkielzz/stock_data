### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In the fiscal year 2021, Vincom Retail reported consolidated net revenue of nearly VND 5,900 billion and a net profit after tax of VND 1,315 billion. The VRE share price exhibited low market volatility, registering an annual decline of 4.44% to close the year at VND 29,070. The core drivers of the business stem from investment property leasing and associated services, alongside property transfers and other auxiliary operations. Despite severe headwinds from the COVID-19 pandemic, VRE successfully preserved its market position as Vietnam's premier retail real estate developer, managing and operating 80 shopping malls across 43 provinces and municipalities with a total gross leasable area (GLA) of approximately 1.7 million square meters. Strategic growth drivers for the year centered on attracting major domestic and international brands—such as Uniqlo, Muji, H&M, Decathlon, and Nike—to expand their footprints, alongside the launch of the innovative Grand World Phu Quoc retail-tourism complex in April 2021. Notably, to preserve strategic partnerships and support tenants through challenging operating conditions, VRE disbursed a cumulative relief package totaling up to VND 3,000 billion across 2020 and 2021 via targeted rental and service fee concessions. The company’s business operations are focused entirely within the domestic Vietnamese market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vincom Retail’s capital structure and liquidity throughout 2021 were sustained via rigorous cash flow management centered on cash and cash equivalents and short-term financial investments. Specifically, cash and cash equivalents comprised VND-denominated bank deposits with original maturities of 1 to 3 months, yielding interest rates ranging from 4.0% to 7.5% per annum (marking a notable increase compared to the 2.9%–4.0% per annum range at the beginning of the year). Short-term held-to-maturity investments (term deposits with original maturities of 6 to 12 months) generated yields between 3.70% and 6.03% per annum. Cash flow pressures during the year were significantly mitigated by expense benchmarking and operational streamlining. Nevertheless, capital structure risks persisted due to sizeable accounts receivable and capital immobilized in co-investment projects and uncollected project development deposits with related parties, including Vingroup JSC, Vinhomes, and other strategic partners.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding credit and asset risk management, Vincom Retail maintained provisions for short-term doubtful debts and inventory obsolescence write-downs in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards. Both short- and long-term asset items reflected substantial deposits placed with investment partners to collaboratively develop retail podium components within residential real estate projects absent specific collateral, thereby introducing recovery risks contingent upon the partners' project execution progress. From a legal and compliance standpoint, the operational framework rigorously adhered to pandemic safety protocols, wastewater management, and environmental standards. The company’s tangible fixed assets and investment properties were partially pledged as collateral to secure bond issuances by the company and its subsidiaries under covenant-defined terms. No systemic or material abnormal financial events were recorded subsequent to the balance sheet closing date of December 31, 2021.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vincom Retail accelerated its green operations and sustainability strategy by completing the installation of nearly 50 rooftop solar power systems across its shopping mall portfolio in 2021. Once operating at full capacity, total self-generated clean energy output will reach 13.6 MWp—comparable to a small-scale solar farm—meeting 10% of the portfolio's total electricity consumption. The company also deployed E-Water cooling technology to replace chemical agents, implemented a Building Management System (BMS) to optimize energy efficiency, piloted energy-saving solutions with Barghest Building Performance (Singapore), and executed the "Plastic-Waste Free" initiative encompassing source segregation and rigorous wastewater quality monitoring in compliance with discharge permits.
- **Social (S):** The company prioritized the health and safety of operations personnel, tenants, and shoppers by strictly enforcing epidemic prevention measures (periodic disinfection, temperature screenings, and hand sanitizer stations). VRE fostered a human-centric corporate culture centered on elite talent, emphasizing foundational and advanced training programs to elevate workforce capabilities across critical functions, alongside community outreach and cultural-artistic initiatives via the Vincom Center for Contemporary Art (VCCA), which hosted major exhibitions drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors.
- **Governance (G):** The company operated under a standardized governance framework subject to stringent oversight by the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, and the Control and Internal Audit Division. Financial transparency was safeguarded through reputable independent auditing firms. The internal governance system was standardized, streamlined, and automated to enhance operational efficacy, ensuring full compliance with prevailing Vietnamese legal standards governing the retail real estate sector.
