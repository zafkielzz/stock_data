Below is a standard qualitative analysis summary based on the 2023 Annual Report and authenticated data of the Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV - HOSE: BID):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, BIDV recorded positive business growth driven by a flexible management strategy closely aligned with the directives of the Government and the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV). Total assets surpassed the VND 2.3 trillion threshold, expanding by 8.5% compared to 2022, thereby maintaining its position as the joint-stock commercial bank with the largest total asset scale in Vietnam. Customer loans reached VND 1.78 trillion (up 16.8%), while customer deposits stood at VND 1.7 trillion (up 15.7%). Net interest income and similar income played a primary role at VND 152.76 trillion, resulting in net interest income (NII) of VND 47.93 trillion (up 2.5%). Consolidated profit before tax reached VND 27.589 billion, representing a 20.4% year-on-year growth. The core growth driver stemmed from channeling credit into priority sectors, production and business, and green lending packages. The retail banking and SME segments continued to serve as a solid anchor, maintaining a market-leading portfolio scale. BID’s stock performance during the year reflected the strong appeal of large-cap banking equities, opening at VND 26,380 and closing at VND 32,830 (a 24.45% gain), and peaking at an annual high of VND 33,220 with an average matching trading volume of 1,174,129 shares/session.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
BIDV's capital structure was managed prudently, ensuring strict compliance with liquidity safety ratios and SBV regulations. Total equity as of December 31, 2023, reached VND 122.867 billion, marking a robust 18% increase compared to 2022. Charter capital stood at VND 57.004 billion. Deposits and borrowings from other credit institutions (CIs) were recorded at VND 181.830 billion, comprising VND 143.569 billion in deposits from other CIs and VND 38.261 billion in borrowings from other CIs. The issuance of valuable papers played a crucial role in optimizing the maturity profile of funding sources, reaching VND 189.486 billion (up from VND 157.051 billion in the previous year), including certificates of deposit and issued bonds aimed at expanding medium- and long-term funding scale. The bank proactively lowered both lending and deposit interest rates throughout the year to support the economy, while deploying preferential credit packages with interest rates 0.5% - 2.5% p.a. lower to accompany businesses and individuals in overcoming difficulties.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Asset quality and risk management were tightly controlled amid macroeconomic volatility. The on-balance-sheet non-performing loan (NPL) ratio under Circular 11/2021/TT-NHNN was contained at 1.12%, outperforming the target assigned by the General Meeting of Shareholders. Total provisions for customer loans were recorded at VND 40.469 billion, including VND 27.242 billion in specific provisions and VND 13.226 billion in general provisions, providing a safe buffer against emerging credit risks. Off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent liabilities remained substantial, including loan guarantees (VND 6.763 billion), commitments for forward transactions (VND 1.433 billion), L/C commitments (VND 83.174 billion), and other guarantees (VND 199.816 billion). BIDV maintains a risk management system aligned with Basel II standards while gradually preparing conditions to approach Basel III standards to enhance its shock-absorption capacity against market volatility. No material abnormal financial events occurred after December 31, 2023, that would negatively impact the bank's operations.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
BIDV remains steadfast in its sustainable development strategy, seamlessly integrating ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) standards across all business operations:
- **Environmental (E):** The bank has been a pioneer in promoting green finance and green credit, with green credit outstanding loans exceeding VND 74.177 billion (accounting for approximately 4.24% of total outstanding loans), focusing on renewable energy, clean energy, and sustainable agriculture. BIDV successfully issued VND 2.5 billion in green bonds complying with international standards (ICMA) and established a Sustainable Loan Framework in accordance with the principles of the International Capital Market Association (ICMA) and the Loan Market Association (LMA/LSTA).
- **Social (S):** BIDV maintains a professional working environment for 29,997 employees with competitive welfare, training, and remuneration policies. The bank allocated significant resources to social security, with total sponsorship funds reaching VND 330 billion during the year, focusing on healthcare, education, housing for the poor, and the "Creating a Green Future" 1-million-tree planting program.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance system strictly complies with legal regulations and best international practices. The Board of Directors comprises members with high professional expertise, ensuring independence and effective oversight. Independent audits are conducted by Deloitte Vietnam Company Limited. BIDV comprehensively applies governance standards based on the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard criteria and maintains the highest level of information transparency for shareholders and investors.
