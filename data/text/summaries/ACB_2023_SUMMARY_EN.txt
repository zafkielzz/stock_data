Below is a standard qualitative analytical summary based on the 2023 Annual Report of Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (ACB) and verified actual data:

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, against the backdrop of a macroeconomic environment laden with headwinds, ACB still posted impressive business growth driven by its agile market adaptability. ACB’s total income reached VND 32,747 billion, up 14% year-on-year, comprising net interest income (NII) of VND 24,960 billion and non-interest income of VND 7,877 billion (up 48%). Core growth momentum stemmed from ACB proactively lowering lending rates faster than deposit rates to support customers amid economic difficulties, while expanding credit scale to nearly VND 488 trillion (up 17.9% year-to-date, outperforming the industry average of 13.78%). The retail segment continued to serve as the primary driver, with the Personal Customer Division contributing up to 55% of the division's total revenue, adding 1.5 million new customers during the year and expanding the retail customer portfolio to approximately 7 million individuals. Additionally, card business operations recorded positive results with nearly 2 million new cards issued, bringing the total cards in circulation to 5.4 million and contributing 5.3% to total income. Foreign exchange operations posted solid growth by capitalizing on favorable market conditions. Geographically, Ho Chi Minh City remained the leading region in terms of growth and market share, accounting for 63% of total deposits, 43% of total outstanding loans, and contributing 50% of the bank-wide total revenue.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
ACB’s capital and equity structure continued to be firmly consolidated in 2023, ensuring capital adequacy ratios in compliance with Basel II standards and paving the way for Basel III. Consolidated equity reached VND 70,956 billion, up 21% compared to 2022, of which charter capital stood at VND 38,841 billion. ACB’s mobilized capital scale reached VND 483 trillion (up 16.6% year-to-date), with the Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio reaching 22%, maintaining its 6th position in the market and leading the group of joint-stock commercial retail banks. The consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 12.48%, well above the State Bank of Vietnam’s minimum regulatory requirement of 8%. Within the capital structure, deposits and borrowings from other credit institutions reached VND 89,507 billion, reflecting proactive liquidity management and optimization of input capital costs. ACB maintained a rigorous interest rate risk management policy to mitigate the negative impact of market interest rate volatility on net interest income and the bank’s economic value.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Amid an industry-wide upward trend in non-performing loan (NPL) ratios, ACB’s NPLs (Group 3-5 loans) increased slightly to VND 5,887 billion, translating to an NPL ratio of 1.21%—ranking among the lowest and leading the market of joint-stock commercial banks. To counter credit risks, ACB’s credit risk provision expenses in 2023 were VND 1,804 billion (rising sharply from VND 71 billion in 2022), bringing the loan loss coverage ratio (LLR) to 91%. The overdue loan ratio was maintained at 1.9%. Off-balance-sheet commitments and guarantees were strictly managed and monitored through credit limit systems and internal loan classification under Basel II standards. Regarding stock performance, in 2023, ACB shares traded with an opening price of VND 11,120 and closed at VND 14,470, recording a growth rate of +30.13%, with an average matched trading volume of over 6.34 million shares/session. No unusual legal incidents or material financial events occurred after December 31, 2023, that could negatively impact the bank's operational status.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** ACB affirmed its position as a pioneer bank in sustainable development targeting Net-Zero. The bank has standardized its greenhouse gas emissions data, with total Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions strictly controlled; electricity emissions accounted for the largest proportion and were mitigated through energy-saving initiatives, LED lighting adoption, and process digitalization (Green Transactions, Go Paperless Credit), saving 134 tons of paper in 2023. ACB also deployed a VND 2,000 billion Green-Social credit package to prioritize financing for environmentally friendly and circular economy projects.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes human capital development as its most valuable asset, employing 13,655 staff members at the end of 2023. ACB maintains a professional, transparent working environment, ensures gender equality (female staff ratio reaching 65.29%), and continuously enhances welfare and healthcare through the "ACB Care" program and Eneji Station initiatives. The employee turnover rate continued to be controlled at a low level (10.46%). Total training hours reached 856,831 hours. Social security activities were intensified with a sponsorship budget of over VND 10.9 billion focused on education, healthcare, and local communities.
- **Governance (G):** ACB maintains a corporate governance system aligned with international standards and strictly adheres to the principles of the ASEAN and OECD Corporate Governance Principles. The Board of Directors operates independently and effectively with specialized committees (Risk Management Committee, Personnel Committee, Strategy Committee, Investment Committee). The Supervisory Board and Internal Audit operate independently, ensuring strict compliance with legal regulations and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), receiving high credit ratings from international organizations such as Moody's and Fitch Ratings.
