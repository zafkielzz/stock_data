Below is an in-depth qualitative analysis of the financial position, operational performance, and ESG execution of Phat Dat Real Estate Development Corporation (PDR) for the 2021 fiscal year, based on the Annual Report and verified actual data:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, PDR recorded exceptional business growth with total net revenue reaching VND 3,620.22 billion and net profit after tax attributable to shareholders of the parent company reaching VND 1,865.00 billion (a 52.8% increase YoY). The core growth driver continued to be the residential and urban real estate business, which contributed an overwhelming proportion to the revenue structure. Specifically, land transfer revenue reached VND 3,594.47 billion (accounting for over 99% of total revenue). This achievement stemmed from PDR accelerating project delivery progress and revenue recognition from large-scale projects in key markets such as Quang Ngai (Nhon Hoi Ecological Urban Area) and Binh Dinh, amid very high market absorption rates for land plots and shophouses in these areas. Property leasing and service provision contributed the remaining minor share. Thanks to optimized implementation processes and tight cost-of-goods-sold control, PDR's gross profit margin improved sharply to 76.3% (compared to 46.6% in 2020), underscoring the company's pricing power and intrinsic strength in the mid-to-high-end real estate segment.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PDR’s capital structure in 2021 witnessed a significant shift to finance land banks and new projects. Total assets at year-end reached VND 20,551.88 billion, up 32% year-to-date, with short-term assets accounting for 74.6% (VND 15,337.06 billion), primarily comprising inventories. Total liabilities were recorded at VND 12,407.37 billion, with short-term debt comprising VND 8,731.17 billion and long-term debt standing at VND 3,676.19 billion (down 39% YoY as the company restructured long-term loans into short-term or paid down a portion). To ensure flexible capital mobilization, PDR actively utilized corporate bond issuance, with total issuance value during the year reaching over VND 2,380 billion (predominantly 2-year tenors, with coupon rates ranging from 12% - 13% p.a.). Regarding bank borrowings, PDR maintained credit lines with major financial institutions such as Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank - HCMC Branch 11) and Military Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MB), with borrowing rates applied at the 12-month post-paid savings rate plus a margin (approximately 3.5% - 4.5% p.a.). The debt-to-total-assets ratio was maintained at a safe level of 16.7%, keeping liquidity and cash flow pressure under control.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
PDR’s risk management and provisioning were implemented conservatively to accurately reflect its financial reality. The ending balance of short-term doubtful debt provisions was recorded at VND 1.04 billion, established based on the recoverability assessment of outstanding receivables. Regarding inventories (VND 12,192.04 billion), management assessed that the net realizable value exceeded cost, hence no provision for inventory devaluation was incurred. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, PDR acted as a guarantor or used assets as collateral for bank loans of its subsidiaries and affiliates to execute projects. No material legal disputes or litigations adversely affecting business operations were recorded. Regarding share price movements, PDR experienced a positive trading year with the stock appreciating by 127.4% over the year, fluctuating within a range of VND 25,120 to VND 61,170 with an average matching volume of nearly 3.86 million shares/session. No abnormal financial events were recorded after December 31, 2021.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
PDR increasingly focused on integrating Sustainable Development (SD) criteria into its core strategy:
- **Environmental (E):** PDR applies green building design standards (such as EDGE and LEED standards) to optimize energy and water resource utilization across its projects (such as Astral City, The EverRich). The company promotes circular economy principles in construction by utilizing eco-friendly materials, reusing treated wastewater for landscape irrigation, and minimizing greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) across its operational value chain.
- **Social (S):** PDR is committed to strict compliance with the Labor Code, maintaining a safe and healthy working environment and fully implementing social and health insurance benefits for employees. Amid the COVID-19 pandemic, the company deployed multiple financial support packages, provided vaccines and food, and maintained 100% of employee income. Competitive compensation and welfare policies (including ESOP programs and performance-based bonuses) help retain talent and promote gender equality in the workforce structure.
- **Governance (G):** PDR complies with public company governance regulations under the Enterprise Law while adopting international standards such as the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard and the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) reporting standards. The Board of Directors maintains a high level of independence with independent and non-executive members closely supervising operations, combining with the Supervisory Board and Internal Audit Department to ensure transparency, fairness, and maximum protection of shareholders' and investors' rights.
