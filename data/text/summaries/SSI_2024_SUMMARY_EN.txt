### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2024, SSI Securities Corporation (SSI) achieved financial results that surpassed the targets mandated by the General Meeting of Shareholders across both Total Revenue and Profit Before Tax metrics, while successfully defending its preeminent rank as the industry leader in Total Assets and Shareholders' Equity among Vietnamese brokerage firms. The core macroeconomic growth driver stemmed from Vietnam’s 2024 GDP growth rate reaching +7.09% (exceeding the 6.5% – 7.0% target), with the nominal GDP scale estimated at USD 476 billion. The industrial and construction sector served as the primary catalyst, spearheaded by processing and manufacturing output expanding by 9.83% (the highest level since 2018) and contributing 2.5 percentage points to overall growth. Regarding international trade, 2024 export turnover increased by 14.3% year-on-year to reach USD 405 billion (with the processing and manufacturing category up 14%), imports rose by 16.7%, and the trade surplus reached USD 24.8 billion, with the United States as the largest export surplus market (USD 104.6 billion) and China as the largest trade deficit market (USD 83.7 billion). The services sector sustained its recovery momentum, underpinned by a 5.9% real increase in total retail sales of goods and services, alongside a 39.5% surge in international tourist arrivals to 17.6 million. Despite prevailing headwinds in the equity market characterized by subdued market liquidity and net foreign outflows, SSI's core business segments effectively leveraged money market and financial service resources to sustain robust growth momentum.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
SSI’s capital and debt structure was significantly influenced by money market fluctuations throughout 2024, notably the upward pressure from a 5.03% appreciation in the USD/VND exchange rate driven by balance of payments deficits. Per the Notes to the Financial Statements, the Company maintained a substantial foreign-currency-denominated principal loan portfolio across several commercial banks, totaling USD 110,000,000 (equivalent to VND 2,758,985,000,000). Sensitivity analysis indicates that a 10% appreciation or depreciation of the USD against the VND would induce a proportionate shift in the Company's financial expense ratio stemming from foreign currency borrowing costs. Regarding interest rate exposure, SSI’s bank deposits, certificates of deposit, corporate bonds, lending facilities, and debt borrowings primarily bear fixed interest rates determined by specific credit agreements or designated maturity terms. This structure enables the Company to effectively mitigate market interest rate volatility risks, though it remains exposed to global liquidity pressures amid persistently high interest rates and a strengthening USD.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Risk management and provisioning at SSI were executed rigorously through ongoing monitoring of impairment losses across financial assets, lending portfolios, available-for-sale (AFS) assets, and doubtful receivables (including doubtful receivables from financial asset sales involving Phuc Bao Minh Trading and Construction Co., Ltd., and doubtful brokerage service receivables). In terms of operational activities, SSI maintains contractual margin trading commitments to provide financing for investors purchasing securities across its headquarters in Ho Chi Minh City, the Hanoi branch, Nguyen Cong Tru branch, My Dinh branch, and Hai Phong branch. Furthermore, the Company incurs commitments pertaining to covered warrant issuances (spanning underlying asset portfolios such as MWG, HPG, MBB, and FPT), which necessitate that the warrant payable revaluation differential system be accounted for and periodically adjusted. Credit, market, and liquidity risk management policies are subject to continuous oversight by the Board of Management to ensure an optimal equilibrium between risk costs and operational efficiency.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Throughout 2024, SSI and its fund management subsidiary, SSIAM, accelerated the implementation of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards, achieving demonstrable milestones:
- **Environment (E):** SSIAM completed its inaugural annual ESG report in alignment with the United Nations Principles for Responsible Investment (UNPRI) framework, while fully establishing its dedicated ESG portal on the official corporate website to enhance transparency and accountability across investment activities.
- **Social (S):** SSI placed strong strategic emphasis on human capital development by deploying advanced personnel training and career advancement programs, alongside fostering an innovative workplace environment. Notably, SSIAM actively participated in community initiatives, collaborating on ESG scoring advisory projects for securities firms under the Vietnam Association of Securities Business (VASB) and acting as an expert mentor for the younger generation in ESG application competitions.
- **Governance (G):** The Company maintained a transparent governance framework anchored by the effective execution of the Board of Directors, the Audit Committee, the Legal and Compliance Division, and an independent internal audit and control apparatus. The integration of the ESG screening framework across the entirety of the private equity investment lifecycle empowers SSI to enforce rigorous adherence to sustainable development standards and enterprise-grade risk management.
