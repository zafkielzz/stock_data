### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FPT Digital Retail Joint Stock Company's (FRT) core operations for the 2022 fiscal year were driven by two primary operating segments: Information and Communications Technology (ICT) devices, mobile phones, components, and accessories; and the pharmaceutical segment (pharmacy chain). The company generated 100% of its revenue domestically, with no export activities or international operations outside of Vietnam. On the equity market, FRT's share price exhibited high volatility, reaching a 52-week high of VND 76,430 and a low of VND 31,930, before closing at VND 45,360 (representing a marginal increase of +5.34% compared to its opening price of VND 43,060). Share liquidity remained robust, with average matched trading volume reaching 1,423,765 shares per session, reflecting strong market sentiment and investor conviction in this dual-retail chain model. The allocation of economic risks and returns across both segments indicates that the company is progressively transitioning from its traditional, single-pillar reliance on the ICT segment toward the highly defensive, long-term growth engine of pharmaceutical retail.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

FRT's debt profile relies entirely on unsecured short-term bank borrowings from commercial banks to fund working capital requirements. The company demonstrates strong credit access across a diversified roster of financial institutions, encompassing major foreign institutional lenders (HSBC, UOB, Citibank, MUFG, ANZ, Standard Chartered, Cathay United, Shinhan Bank) and leading domestic commercial banks (BIDV, Vietcombank, Vietinbank, MB, MSB). Amid the monetary tightening cycle in 2022, FRT faced intensifying cost-of-capital pressures as VND-denominated loan interest rates surged to a range of 3.0% - 9.0% per annum (compared to 1.90% - 4.50% per annum in 2021). Conversely, USD-denominated borrowing costs softened slightly, fluctuating between 1.80% and 2.30% per annum (versus 1.80% - 2.67% per annum in 2021). Additionally, FRT engaged in financial transactions providing capital support to related parties (FPT) and third parties via unsecured loans with maturities under 6 months at an interest rate of 6.5% per annum. Cash flow pressures were further compounded by long-term operating lease commitments for retail floor space, spanning durations from under 1 year to over 5 years.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Inventories and short-term receivables represent two critical risk items that necessitate highly conservative accounting estimates from the Board of Management. FRT manages inventory obsolescence risk by establishing provisions—covering goods-in-transit, tools, supplies, and merchandise—with direct adjustments reflected in the cost of goods sold. Regarding doubtful accounts, the company classifies and provisions receivables based on aging schedules (under 1 year, 1 to 2 years, and 2 to 3 years). Concerning off-balance-sheet obligations, future retail space operating lease commitments constitute the company's largest mandatory financial obligation. The consolidated financial statements, audited by PwC Vietnam and approved on March 24, 2023, by General Director Hoang Trung Kien, reported no material litigation or extraordinary financial events subsequent to the December 31, 2022 balance sheet date.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** FRT did not publish a standalone sustainability (ESG) report for the 2022 fiscal year. Quantitative metrics regarding greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1/2/3), Net-Zero targets, or specific circular economy initiatives were not explicitly disclosed in current public filings.
*   **Social (S):** The company allocates significant resources to its human capital, reflected via personnel expenses embedded within selling expenses and general and administrative expenses. The executive management team exhibits gender balance and diversity, highlighted by the presence of a female Chief Accountant (Ms. Tran Thi Nga) alongside other male executive members.
*   **Governance (G):** Governance transparency is reinforced by having the financial statements audited by PwC Vietnam, one of the globally recognized Big Four accounting firms. A notable governance highlight is that all members of FRT's Board of Directors and Supervisory Board received zero remuneration in fiscal year 2022 (remuneration of VND 0) pursuant to Annual General Meeting Resolution No. 01.2022/NQ-ĐHĐCĐ/FRT, demonstrating strong alignment of interests and management's commitment to optimizing cost efficiency for the ultimate benefit of shareholders. Furthermore, financial and commercial transactions with the major shareholder (FPT) are rigorously monitored within the corporate ecosystem.
