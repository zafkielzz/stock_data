### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2021, VPBank delivered resilient financial performance despite the complex headwinds of the COVID-19 pandemic. Consolidated total operating income (TOI) expanded by 13.5% YoY to exceed VND 44 trillion, reinforcing the bank's leading position among private joint-stock commercial banks. Core growth catalysts stemmed from a comprehensive digital transformation strategy, driving consolidated credit growth up by 18.9%—surpassing the initial guidance and outperforming the industry average of 13.5%—with total consolidated gross loans reaching over VND 384 trillion. Strategic segments, particularly Retail Banking and Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs), registered robust growth of 33% year-to-date.

Consolidated net interest income (NII) inched up 6.2% YoY to over VND 34 trillion, lagging behind credit growth due to the bank's proactive stance in prudent credit management, reducing the proportion of unsecured lending, and rolling out preferential interest rate packages exceeding VND 1.2 trillion to support over 400,000 customers. This was partially offset by a breakout in non-interest income (Non-II), which surged to account for 22.5% of TOI (the highest level in over a decade). Consolidated net fee income (NFI) grew nearly 21% YoY to surpass VND 4 trillion, while net trading and investment securities gains jumped 2.3x to over VND 3 trillion. Consolidated profit before tax (PBT) reached VND 14.364 trillion (up over 10% YoY). The parent bank achieved a stellar breakthrough, generating nearly VND 38 trillion in PBT (a more than 4-fold increase), bolstered by the capital divestment gain from FE Credit—marking the largest M&A transaction in Vietnam's banking history—alongside a 51% increase in core operating profit (reaching over VND 14 trillion). At FE Credit, despite severe pandemic-induced impacts on the low-income consumer segment, credit growth reached 14.2%, enabling the entity to preserve its #1 market share.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VPBank’s funding structure witnessed a notable qualitative enhancement in 2021, underpinned by digital initiatives and a surge in low-cost funding mobilization. The Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio to total deposits climbed to 22.6% (predominantly driven by the retail segment), enabling the bank to trim its blended cost of funds by 1.7 percentage points, optimize capital allocation, and enhance capital efficiency. Profitability metrics remained at the forefront of the market, with consolidated ROA and ROE closing at 2.5% and 19.8%, respectively. To mitigate liquidity risk and macroeconomic volatility amid the pandemic, the bank maintained a prudent capital management policy, scaling up online mobilization channels to compress funding cost pressures and safeguard system-wide liquidity buffers.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Navigating pandemic-related challenges, VPBank proactively deployed conservative risk management frameworks and significantly reinforced its provisioning buffers. Total consolidated provisioning expenses for FY2021 escalated by 31.4% YoY, with the standalone bank reporting a 52.9% surge. The bank allocated over VND 1 trillion in supplementary provisions for restructured loans under Circular 14/2021/TT-NHNN and completed 30% of total mandatory supplementary provisions mandated by Circular 03/2021/TT-NHNN. The consolidated Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio was contained at 3.65%, supported by a reduction in unsecured lending exposure, a selective portfolio shift toward collateralized assets, and accelerated bad debt resolution. At the parent bank level, asset quality exhibited a marked recovery, with the NPL ratio trending downward continuously from 2019 to hit 1.51% by year-end 2021. Consolidated recoveries from written-off bad debts reached over VND 3.2 trillion, up 54.5% YoY, contributing materially to total earnings. The bank recorded no extraordinary legal disputes or material adjusting events subsequent to the financial year-ended December 31, 2021, that would exert a detrimental impact on its financial standing.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** VPBank reaffirmed its pioneering role in sustainable finance, recognizing it as an instrumental lever to mitigate climate risks and resource depletion. The bank maintained and upgraded its Environmental and Social Risk Management System (ESMS) in alignment with IFC performance standards and national legislation, while successfully establishing a Green Bond/Credit Policy Framework validated by Sustainalytics. In alignment with COP26 net-zero carbon targets by 2050, VPBank partnered with GIZ to conclude Phase 2 of the project enhancing capacity for climate-related financial risk disclosures under TCFD guidelines, alongside successfully mobilizing USD 300 million in international financing facilities dedicated to support SMEs and women-led enterprises.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritized labor rights protection, cultivating a robust corporate culture anchored in 6 core values (Customer-Centric, Trustworthy, Distinctive, People Development, Efficient, and Ambitious). Amid the pandemic-hit year of 2021, VPBank allocated over VND 500 billion toward COVID-19 relief operations and community engagement initiatives, while deploying agile working frameworks to safeguard employee health and workplace safety.
- **Governance (G):** VPBank strictly adhered to transparent corporate governance best practices. Information disclosure and sustainability reporting referenced the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standards (opted 'Core' level), subject to direct review and sign-off by the Chief Executive Officer. Driven by comprehensive execution, VPBank ranked first in the ESG index among Vietnam's top 10 commercial banks (assessed by Fair Finance Vietnam), attained a "Very Good" rating for development effectiveness (DERa) and ESG performance by DEG, and preserved its constituent status in the top 20 equities of the VN Sustainability Index (VNSI) on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange.
