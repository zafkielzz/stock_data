**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Amidst a severe cyclical downturn and industry consolidation phase, Phat Dat Real Estate Development Corporation (PDR) proactively executed a portfolio and financial restructuring strategy to successfully navigate the headwinds. The company's core growth driver during this period centered on an uncompromising focus on its primary real estate business, decisive divestments from subsidiaries, reprioritization of flagship projects, and a willingness to forego short-term gains to safeguard its long-term foundation. This strategic pivot enabled PDR to successfully untangle financial bottlenecks, alleviate liquidity pressures, and swiftly regain operational equilibrium. From a market perspective, the robust recovery and resurgence of the company's intrinsic strength catalyzed an impressive share price rally. Specifically, PDR shares recorded a capital appreciation of +99.74% over the year (opening at VND 11,660 and closing at VND 23,290, with an intraday high of VND 24,710 and a low of VND 8,310), accompanied by an average matched trading volume of 11,298,262 shares per session. This performance underscores strong market conviction and expectations regarding the company's early operational turnaround.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
PDR’s capital structure and debt management underwent a watershed transformation in FY2023 to mitigate cash flow pressures. The paramount highlight of PDR's financial landscape was the definitive resolution of short-term bond maturities. Specifically, on December 25, 2023, PDR successfully completed the buyback of two bond tranches with an aggregate par value of VND 800 billion, thereby officially settling all maturing and pre-matured bonds prior to the close of 2023. This milestone positions PDR as one of the few real estate developers in the market to achieve a zero-bond-debt status amid a stringent credit environment. Furthermore, to bolster project execution capabilities and optimize capital deployment, PDR secured comprehensive backing from tier-one financial institutions, most notably entering into a strategic partnership with MB Bank in late 2023 to finance the development of key projects, namely the Thuan An 1 and 2 high-end complex residential developments.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
In financial risk management, PDR maintained a prudent posture by provisioning for receivables and assets to accurately reflect its financial reality. Notably, within other receivables, the Company recorded short-term advances to individuals scheduled for maturity on June 30, 2023, and established an allowance for doubtful debts amounting to VND 9 billion. Inventories predominantly comprised cleared land banks, site clearance costs, construction in progress, and capitalized borrowing costs across ongoing developments such as The EverRich 2, The EverRich 3, Binh Duong Tower, Nhon Hoi - Binh Dinh, Bac Ha Thanh Residential Area, and Ngo May. Among these, property rights arising from The EverRich 2 and The EverRich 3 projects have been pledged as collateral at Vietnam Technological and Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VPBank) to secure business partners' credit facilities. Concurrently, the Group is finalizing legal procedures to transfer the remaining stakes pursuant to executed investment cooperation agreements with Big Gain and Dynamic Innovation. During the fiscal year, PDR also recognized capitalized borrowing costs totaling over VND 41.78 billion, directly funding project investment and development.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** PDR prioritizes the integration of sustainable development criteria into its real estate investment and operational activities, aiming for long-term alignment with international standards to mitigate environmental externalities and drive resource efficiency.
- **Social (S):** The enterprise enforces rigorous occupational health and safety standards, safeguards workforce welfare, and cultivates a professional, equitable, and experienced working environment to maximize human capital efficacy amid volatile operating conditions.
- **Governance (G):** PDR implements an advanced corporate governance framework referencing the "Corporate Governance Code of Best Practices" issued by the State Securities Commission (SSC) and the International Finance Corporation (IFC). The Board of Directors maintains independence and transparency, with clearly demarcated roles and responsibilities, while operating a robust three-line-of-defense risk management architecture spanning the Board, the Executive Board, and operational tiers. Information disclosure and sustainability reporting are executed rigorously against the latest Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, reaffirming the company's commitment to transparency and long-term value creation.
