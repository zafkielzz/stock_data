### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
FY2025 marked a historic milestone for Phu Nhuan Jewelry JSC (PNJ), delivering record-high financial performance with net revenue reaching VND 34,976 billion and profit after tax (PAT) hitting VND 2,828 billion. This robust operating performance was achieved amidst a challenging macro backdrop characterized by volatile market conditions, subdued consumer discretionary spending, supply-side constraints, and intensifying retail competition. Core growth momentum was driven by the disciplined execution of PNJ’s long-term strategy, focusing on enhancing customer experience, business model innovation, and leveraging its corporate culture anchored in three core values: Integrity, Caring, and Pioneering. The system-wide gross profit margin remained resilient at 21.26%. Segment-wise, retail operations involving gold, silver, gemstone, and accessory sales continued to serve as the primary top-line driver, complemented by the operational efficiency of its nationwide distribution network.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PNJ’s capital structure is managed with a high degree of prudence and conservatism, underscored by a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 29.74%. Short-term debt facilities are primarily deployed to fund working capital requirements, backed by reputable banking partners including Vietcombank, BIDV, VietinBank, ACB, KEB Hana Bank, Woori, HSBC, VIB, Shinhan, and SCB. These short-term borrowings are secured by inventories and tangible fixed assets. Notably, the balance sheet carries no material long-term debt, effectively mitigating principal repayment pressure and interest expense burdens, thereby insulating operating cash flows from liquidity risks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Provisioning and risk management at PNJ are executed rigorously in compliance with prevailing accounting standards. Inventory impairment provisions are actively maintained based on net realizable value (NRV) fluctuations to accurately reflect asset valuations. Regarding trade and other receivables, overdue short-term receivables totaled over VND 31.9 billion, while long-term receivables exhibited minimal default risk. Crucially, regarding post-balance sheet legal and financial events, on January 17, 2025, DongA Bank underwent a mandatory transfer to HDBank, being rebranded as Vikki Digital One Member Limited Liability Bank under 100% ownership of HDBank. Consequently, all rights and interests of PNJ in DongA Bank have been fully terminated, with this equity investment fully provisioned and comprehensively disclosed in the consolidated financial statements.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PNJ continues to integrate environmental criteria into its sustainable development strategy, highlighted by receiving the 2025 JWA Award in the 'Sustainability Leadership' category alongside other prestigious ESG accolades. The company prioritizes energy efficiency, promotes circular economy practices within its jewelry manufacturing facilities, and strictly controls emissions aligned with long-term environmental stewardship.
- **Social (S):** PNJ maintains exemplary workplace standards, ranking among the Top 10 Enterprises meeting Business Culture Standards under the Prime Minister’s criteria, and receiving multiple human resource accolades. The company places strong emphasis on Occupational Health and Safety (OHS), drives gender equality through its female leadership network, and implements comprehensive human capital development and employee welfare policies.
- **Governance (G):** PNJ’s corporate governance framework operates in alignment with international best standards (utilizing the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard - ACGS), supported by a highly independent Board of Directors comprising seasoned independent members. Information disclosure, Investor Relations (IR), internal audit, and the Audit Committee functions are executed with a high degree of transparency, strictly adhering to sustainable development frameworks (GRI / SDGs) to safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of shareholders and stakeholders.
