Below is the institutional equity research translation of the financial summary for VNDIRECT Securities Corporation (VND), based on the 2025 Annual Report and verified actual data.

***

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, VNDIRECT recorded robust earnings growth driven by a strong recovery in the Vietnamese stock market—particularly in the second half of the year, when the VN-Index surpassed the 1,600-point mark and market liquidity expanded significantly. Core growth momentum stemmed from two primary business segments: The brokerage and client services division posted high net revenue underpinned by a 34.3% increase in average daily trading volume to VND 28.9 trillion, alongside 65,000 newly opened accounts, bringing the total client base to 1 million accounts. Meanwhile, the capital-based business segment maintained its growth trajectory through margin lending activities (outstanding margin loans reached VND 14.319 trillion, up 38%) and bond issuance advisory, where it retained a market-leading share. Net revenue from financial assets (FVTPL, HTM) and interest income also made substantial contributions to the company’s overall profit structure.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, INDEBTEDNESS & INTEREST RATES
VNDIRECT’s capital structure was managed flexibly to safeguard liquidity and optimize the cost of capital amid an upward drifting interest rate environment toward year-end. Total liabilities as of December 31, 2025, stood at VND 30.726 trillion, with short-term borrowings accounting for the majority (VND 26.463 trillion, up 18% YoY), primarily funding securities advance payments and margin financing. Short-term loans were denominated mainly in VND with floating interest rates ranging from 2.25% to 8.0% per annum, backed by credit facilities extended by major domestic commercial banks, including Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank), Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV), and Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank). Additionally, the company issued bonds totaling VND 2.248 trillion to diversify its medium- to long-term funding sources, maintaining a debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio of 140.01% while current and quick liquidity ratios remained stable at 1.77x.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
The company maintains a stringent risk management policy governing its financial asset portfolio and margin lending operations. As of December 31, 2025, total provisions for the impairment of financial assets and collateral stood at VND 79.14 billion, comprising VND 30.92 billion in specific provisions for loans and VND 48.22 billion in FVTPL impairment provisions. Provisions for bad debts are strictly recognized based on actual aging schedules. Regarding contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet commitments, the company recorded underwriting operations, outstanding covered warrants, and credit limit commitments in compliance with applicable regulations. The audited financial statements recorded no material legal disputes or litigation impacting business operations, nor any extraordinary financial events occurring after the end of the accounting period (December 31, 2025) that would necessitate retrospective adjustments.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** VNDIRECT is committed to sustainable development through its "Green Office" strategy, implementing paperless operations, digitalizing internal approval workflows, utilizing eco-friendly materials, and deploying solar energy systems across its offices to conserve resources and reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Social (S):** The company maintains a diverse and inclusive working environment with 967 employees (48% male, 52% female). Comprehensive welfare policies include 24/7 health insurance, annual medical check-ups, and internal engagement programs such as teambuilding and sports tournaments. Employee training remains a priority, featuring numerous courses designed to enhance professional expertise and AI-driven management capabilities.
- **Governance (G):** The company operates under a transparent public corporate governance model, with a Board of Directors comprising highly qualified independent members. Financial statements are independently audited by Ernst & Young Vietnam Limited, ensuring strict compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) and stock market disclosure regulations.
