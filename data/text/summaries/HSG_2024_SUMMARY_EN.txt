### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In fiscal year (FY) 2023–2024, Hoa Sen Group demonstrated an impressive recovery in core operational efficiency, notwithstanding a volatile macroeconomic landscape and sluggish domestic demand recovery. Net revenue reached VND 39,272 billion, exceeding the target by 9%, while net profit after tax stood at VND 515 billion, outperforming the guidance by 3%. Volume growth was primarily driven by the traditional steel sheet (Ton Hoa Sen) segment, with sales volume reaching 1,941,694 metric tons (surpassing the target by 12%). This outperformance was underpinned by the synchronized optimization of the production and supply chain via ERP deployment, alongside agile coordination between domestic and export channels in response to intensifying international trade defense pressures. Consequently, the group’s gross margin expanded notably from 9.7% in the previous fiscal year to 10.8%.

Beyond the traditional steel segment, the Hoa Sen Home retail distribution network remained a pivotal strategic growth driver, reaching a milestone of 120 standalone stores out of over 400 affiliated branches by the end of FY2024. The diversification of high-margin proprietary product portfolios—including Lustile/Lustra tiles, Tuslo sanitary ware, Lustime roof tiles, and Luswell tools—has established a differentiated competitive moat, paving the way for the eventual spin-off of this retail segment into an independent corporate entity when market conditions prove opportune. On the equity market, despite operational turnaround, HSG's share price faced broad-based downward pressure, registering an annual correction of 17.64% to close at VND 13,770/share (opening price: VND 16,720/share, with a trading range of VND 13,100 to VND 19,480). However, liquidity remained exceptionally robust, with average matched trading volume sustaining at 10,433,632 shares/session.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Management's report highlights demonstrable efforts in financial restructuring and cash flow optimization, with financial expenses for FY2023–2024 plunging 19% year-over-year to VND 254 billion, compared to VND 314 billion in the prior fiscal period. This contraction underscores the efficacy of debt containment and interest expense optimization amidst a restrictive macroeconomic environment. 

Although granular breakdowns regarding short-term versus long-term debt balances, fixed-versus-floating interest rate exposure, and primary lending banking partners were not explicitly disclosed in the accompanying source documents, the sharp reduction in financing costs serves as empirical evidence that HSG has proactively curtailed its leverage profile, mitigated liquidity pressures, and enhanced capital self-sufficiency for core manufacturing and commercial operations.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

HSG faces substantial market risk stemming from the erratic and non-cyclical volatility of raw steel input prices, which complicates inventory management and the sustainability of stable gross margins. Domestically, competitive intensity has been exacerbated by the proliferation of counterfeit and imitation goods. On the export front, the proliferating implementation of trade defense measures across multiple jurisdictions remains a persistent structural headlock on the group’s export volumes. 

Detailed quantitative disclosures regarding inventory obsolescence provisions, specific doubtful debt allowances, off-balance-sheet credit guarantees, and ongoing legal disputes were omitted from the published materials. Regarding post-balance sheet events (subsequent to the fiscal year-end of September 30, 2024), a notable corporate milestone was the inauguration of the 120th Hoa Sen Home supermarket as of December 31, 2024, reinforcing the execution of its business diversification strategy.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Hoa Sen Group has proactively standardized its ESG roadmap by adopting Sustainability Reporting frameworks aligned with Vietnam's Corporate Sustainability Index (CSI), thereby balancing stakeholder interests.

*   **Environmental (E):** A strategic highlight is the partnership agreement forged with SP Group (Singapore) to develop clean energy infrastructure and deploy rooftop solar photovoltaics across domestic manufacturing plants. This signals a firm commitment to long-term carbon abatement goals, although quantified metrics for Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions were not detailed in the report.
*   **Social (S):** HSG continues to champion its corporate ethos of "Integrity – Community – Development" through its philanthropic sponsorship program, "Vietnamese Family Shelters." Within supply chain management, the group enforces a strict mandate against abusing market dominance to squeeze suppliers, alongside the rigorous enforcement of anti-bribery and anti-gift clauses across all commercial contracts.
*   **Governance (G):** The corporate governance structure for the 2024–2029 term ensures institutional independence and rigorous oversight, anchored by a 6-member Board of Directors (BoD), comprising 2 independent directors (compliant with Decree 155/2020/NĐ-CP standards) and 2 non-executive directors. Governance is highly specialized via the Strategic Development Committee (supported by the Strategy Board) and the Corporate Governance & Nomination/Remuneration Committee (supported by the Restructuring Board), designed to optimize decision-making workflows and ensure the highest standards of disclosure transparency for equity holders.
