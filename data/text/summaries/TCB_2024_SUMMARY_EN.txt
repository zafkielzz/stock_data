### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2024, Vietnam Technological and Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Techcombank - TCB) posted exceptional financial performance, further cementing its leadership position within Vietnam's financial architecture. Profit before tax (PBT) reached over VND 27.5 trillion, representing a 20.3% year-on-year (YoY) expansion. The core growth driver stemmed from a relentless customer-centric strategy, comprehensive digital transformation, and the optimization of its financial ecosystem. Regarding credit growth, customer loan book expanded by 20.8% YoY, significantly outperforming the industry average, thereby facilitating portfolio diversification and sustaining robust growth momentum. Total deposits surged by 24.3%, propelled by a record-high Current Account Savings Account (CASA) balance that propelled the CASA ratio to double the industry average, securing a structural low-cost-of-funds advantage. Furthermore, payment services and electronic transactions remained central to operations, with Techcombank capturing leading market shares in NAPAS transactions (approximately 15%), VietQR payments (approximately 20%), and corporate bond underwriting and issuance (accounting for nearly 50% of total issuance value, excluding bank bonds). New-to-bank customer acquisition reached 1.9 million during the year, expanding the total active customer base to over 15.4 million. *(Note: The bank operates within the domestic banking and financial services sector, with no exposure to export market segments).*

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Techcombank’s funding structure in FY2024 experienced substantial expansion, underpinned by customer deposits and institutional funding, with total deposits achieving a 24.3% YoY growth rate. For deposits and credit placements with other credit institutions (CIs), the bank maintained both demand and term deposits denominated in foreign currencies. VND-denominated deposit interest rates applied by the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) and other CIs adhered to regulatory thresholds both within and outside reserve requirement limits; specifically, VND deposits with tenors under 12 months were subject to statutory reserve ratios, with specific interest rate schedules at year-end clearly demarcated between compulsory reserves and excess balances. Regarding cash flow pressures and capital structure risks, the bank's strategic focus on scaling CASA and expanding its digital ecosystem (such as the C-Cash liquidity management solution) successfully optimized the Cost of Funds, mitigated short-term liquidity pressures, and established a resilient foundation for extending medium- and long-term credit to the broader economy.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Credit risk provisioning at Techcombank was executed rigorously in compliance with prevailing regulations across all asset classes, including customer loans (encompassing both general and specific provisions), credit extensions to other CIs, trading securities (provisioning for diminution in the value of trading securities), debt-purchasing activities, and investment securities (comprising general provisions, specific provisions for unlisted corporate bonds, and provisions for impairment of investment securities). Fluctuations in opening balances, provisions made or reversed during the period, and ending balances for each reserve fund were fully accounted for in the corresponding Notes to the Financial Statements. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent risks, the bank maintained payment guarantee commitments (such as Government-guaranteed bonds within the investment securities and loan portfolios). As of the close of FY2024, no material legal disputes, litigations, or extraordinary financial events had occurred subsequent to December 31, 2024, that would adversely impact the business continuity of the bank.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Techcombank accelerated its sustainable development agenda by scaling green credit financing to an all-time high of over VND 16.4 trillion. The bank became the first private commercial bank in Vietnam to formulate a Green Bond Framework and issue green bonds aligned with international standards set by the International Capital Market Association (ICMA), actively contributing to the national green transition and decarbonization trajectory.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes human capital development—treating talent as one of its three strategic pillars—by fostering a professional working environment, offering competitive compensation and benefits, and driving internal engagement initiatives. Additionally, Techcombank actively amplified its corporate social responsibility (CSR) footprint through community initiatives totaling nearly VND 205 billion (its highest level of contribution to date), while sustaining flagship athletic events such as the Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City International Marathons to promote active, healthy lifestyles.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to legal mandates and international best practices. The Board of Directors (BOD) comprises highly experienced professionals, including independent non-executive directors (such as Mr. Pham Nghiem Xuan Bac and Mr. Eugene Keith Galbraith), ensuring objectivity and transparency in strategic oversight. Ownership structures, related-party transactions, and executive remuneration are transparently disclosed in accordance with consolidated financial reporting standards and stock exchange disclosure regulations.
