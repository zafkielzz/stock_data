Below is a qualitative analysis summary of the business operations and development strategy of Vietnam International Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VIB) for 2024, based on the Annual Report and verified data systems:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2024, VIB’s total assets reached VND 493,158 billion (up 20% YoY), total credit outstanding reached VND 324,611 billion (up 22%), and total mobilized deposits reached VND 299,571 billion (up 15%). Profit before tax came in at VND 9,004 billion, recording a 16% decrease compared to 2023. This contraction was primarily driven by VIB's willingness to sacrifice short-term earnings to invest in the long-term interests of clients and shareholders. Specifically, the bank lowered lending rates by 2-3% to support individuals and businesses in recovering their production and business activities, while concurrently maintaining a conservative provisioning approach. Core business operations were spearheaded by the Retail Banking Division, whose outstanding loans accounted for 80% of the bank's total credit portfolio (retail outstanding loans reached nearly VND 260,000 billion, up 14%), solidifying its #1 market share position in auto loans and credit card issuance. Meanwhile, the Corporate Banking Division and the Treasury & Foreign Exchange Division continued to play vital roles, with corporate credit posting a strong growth of 60% to over VND 48,000 billion, underpinned by a disciplined risk-control orientation focused on manufacturing and trading sectors.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWING & INTEREST RATES
VIB maintained a safe capital structure and liquidity profile by developing diversified domestic and international funding channels. Charter capital reached VND 29,791 billion (up 17% YoY), while the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) under Circular 41 consistently held at a high level of 11.89%. Amid persistently high USD interest rates, VIB proactively adjusted its foreign funding strategy, shifting a portion of its structure toward shorter tenors to optimize the cost of capital and secure VND liquidity. The bank continued to reinforce its credit and trade finance relationships with major regional and international financial institutions (such as the ADB, IFC, and other foreign financial entities), thereby diversifying medium- and long-term funding sources at reasonable costs. Funding products flexibly applied both fixed and floating interest rates depending on customer segments to effectively manage interest rate risk.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISK, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
VIB enforced a conservative and proactive risk management and provisioning policy in strict compliance with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) regulations. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio as of December 31, 2024, was contained at 2.4% (below the target ceiling of 3%). The credit portfolio remained isolated from high-risk sectors such as real estate corporate bonds (at negligible or zero exposure), BOT transport infrastructure, renewable energy, and equities. The bank maintains a risk management system aligned with Basel II (advanced approach) and is progressively finalizing Basel III standards, deploying automated credit scoring models (Application and Behaviour Scorecards) to monitor and dynamically provision loans. Guarantee commitments, foreign exchange, and derivatives were rigorously managed within the safe limits prescribed by the SBV. There were no unusual legal disputes or material financial events occurring after the accounting period-end that could adversely affect the bank's operational standing.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environment (E):** VIB actively deployed its green credit strategy, focusing on energy-efficiency and circular economy projects. The bank optimized internal operations through process digitalization (paperless office) to reduce paper and toner consumption across its entire network of offices and branches; simultaneously, it applied Euro 4 emission standards to transport fleets and installed energy-saving equipment at transaction points.
- **Social (S):** The bank ensures a professional, transparent, and safe working environment (OHS) for over 11,700 employees, with average compensation ranking among the top tier in the industry. VIB maintained a female workforce ratio of 54%, demonstrating a robust commitment to gender equality. Capacity-building training programs via digital platforms (VIB Learning) alongside comprehensive welfare and regular healthcare packages were implemented synchronously to elevate employee engagement.
- **Governance (G):** VIB’s corporate governance strictly complies with the Law on Enterprises, the Law on Credit Institutions, and best international practices (such as IFC and SSC recommendations). The Board of Directors comprises 5 members (including an independent member serving as the Chair of the Human Resources Committee and a member of the Risk Management Committee), ensuring objectivity in overseeing executive operations. Financial statements and Sustainability Reports are prepared in compliance with GRI standards and independently audited by reputable international audit firms, ensuring maximum transparency for domestic and foreign shareholders and investors.
