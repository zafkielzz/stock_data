### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Hai An Transport and Stevedoring JSC (HAH) experienced a highly dynamic trading year in 2024, with the closing price surging 50.42% to VND 36,100/share from an opening price of VND 24,000/share. The stock traded within a 52-week range of VND 23,340 to VND 37,410/share, supported by robust liquidity with an average daily trading volume of 3,179,647 shares. This strong capital inflows reflect high market expectations regarding the company's core growth driver: an aggressive fleet expansion strategy. In 2024, HAH successfully took delivery of four newly built 1,800 TEU container vessels (the "SDARI Bangkok Max IV" design). This capacity addition enables the company to aggressively penetrate Intra-Asian trade routes (Southeast Asia, Northeast Asia, and the Middle East) by flexibly combining self-operated services, time charters, and slot-swap arrangements with international carriers. Management has set a strategic target for 2025 to increase international cargo volume to 30%–40% of the fleet's total annual throughput. Regarding ancillary business segments—comprising Port Operations, Depots, and Logistics—near-term growth momentum will focus entirely on optimizing labor productivity and tightening cost controls to improve operating margins relative to 2024.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Although current reporting documents do not disclose absolute figures for short- and long-term debt or the identities of financing credit institutions, HAH's capital structure exhibits significant foreign exchange risk exposure. Driven by continuous fleet expansion, HAH maintains foreign-currency-denominated debt to finance capital expenditure projects. In 2024, the average USD/VND exchange rate appreciated by approximately 5%, pressured by a persistently elevated DXY index resulting from the Federal Reserve's delayed monetary easing cycle, a surge in domestic USD demand starting in late Q2 2024 for trade activities, and heightened volatility in the gold market driving forex demand. While HAH’s operational inputs are not directly exposed to foreign exchange fluctuations given that raw materials and supplies are sourced entirely domestically, a 5% appreciation in the USD/VND exchange rate directly inflates financial expenses (via both realized and unrealized FX losses) and places substantial pressure on foreign-currency debt service and principal repayment cash flows.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Management’s explanatory notes currently omit quantitative disclosures regarding inventory write-downs, allowances for doubtful accounts, off-balance-sheet guarantees, or legal disputes arising during the 2024 fiscal year. From a systemic risk perspective, HAH is heavily exposed to industry-specific legal and regulatory risks. The company’s operations are tightly coupled with amendments to the Enterprise Law, the Securities Law, and regulatory frameworks governing border-gate commerce (tariffs, fees, quarantine regulations, hygiene standards, and import-export commodity quality benchmarks). To mitigate these risks, management proactively conducts periodic legal training for personnel and consults with specialized legal institutions to ensure compliance and minimize potential litigation risks. No material extraordinary financial events were recorded subsequent to December 31.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
HAH has not yet published a standalone Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) report for the 2024 fiscal year.
*   **Environmental (E):** The company has not disclosed quantitative greenhouse gas emission data (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) or a formalized Net-Zero pathway commitment. However, indirect environmental stewardship is demonstrated through its strategic focus on digitizing management and commercial operations across the entire fleet to optimize voyage routing and reduce energy consumption.
*   **Social (S):** Key performance indicators regarding occupational health and safety (OHS), gender diversity, employee training, and staff welfare are not quantified in the annual report.
*   **Governance (G):** HAH’s governance structure comprises the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Supervisory Board (3 members), and the Board of Directors (BOD, 7 members). Board independence is low, with only 1 independent member, Ms. Tran Thi Hai Yen, representing 14.3% of the total board. In 2024, the top-tier governance structure underwent notable changes, including the dismissal of BOD member Mr. Tran Tien Dung on March 5, 2024, and the appointment of Mr. Le Anh Minh on April 26, 2024. Significantly, HAH's Board of Directors currently lacks specialized subcommittees to support governance and oversight functions.
