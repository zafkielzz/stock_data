ITA (FY2022) EQUITY RESEARCH QUALITATIVE REPORT SUMMARY

1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

During the 2022 fiscal year, ITA shares experienced severe bearish momentum, heavily reflecting pervasive investor sentiment and risk aversion. The equity opened the year at VND 17,100 and closed at VND 4,050, representing a substantial value destruction of -76.32%. Price volatility was pronounced, with a 52-week high of VND 19,600 and a 52-week low of VND 2,410. Nevertheless, secondary market liquidity remained elevated, registering an average matching volume of 8,235,239 shares per trading session, indicative of continuous speculative capital rotation.

Operationally, due to the complete omission of the Notes to the Financial Statements in the corporate disclosures, actual segment-level revenues and earnings for FY2022 cannot be unbundled or specifically quantified. Nonetheless, based on stated core strategies, ITA’s long-term growth vectors concentrate on three primary pillars: industrial park infrastructure development (anchored by Tan Duc projects), healthcare and education services (Tan Tao University Medical Hospital and Tan Tao University), and high-tech agriculture via Itarice Clean Rice JSC (featuring the Nang Yen and Vua Lieu brands). The paucity of granular financial data for FY2022 severely constrains any quantitative assessment of the actual earnings contribution from these segments.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Owing to the absence of the financial statement footnotes, all metrics pertaining to capital structure, short-term and long-term debt profiles, and interest rate structures (fixed versus floating) for ITA in FY2022 were left undisclosed. Consequently, identifying the company's principal credit-providing banking partners remains impossible, as are evaluating latent liquidity risks or cash flow pressures stemming from debt-servicing costs amid a volatile macroeconomic interest rate environment. This information asymmetry represents a critical blind spot for analysts assessing the company's financial resilience.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Parallel to the debt profile, the lack of financial statement footnotes precludes any verifiable data regarding ITA's inventory write-down provisions or allowances for doubtful accounts in FY2022. Material disclosures regarding off-balance-sheet guarantees extended to related parties, ongoing legal disputes and litigation, and extraordinary financial events materializing after December 31, 2022, are entirely absent. For an entity operating in industrial real estate and large-scale project development, the tail risks associated with these contingent liabilities remain exceptionally high and warrant heightened scrutiny.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Environmental (E): The 2022 reporting suite failed to disclose quantitative metrics regarding greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1/2/3, Net-Zero trajectory) or direct energy-efficiency gains. ITA’s environmental mandate is predominantly reflected indirectly through legacy initiatives, such as the development of the Itarice clean rice brand (recipient of a prestigious Southeast Asian award in 2016) targeting sustainable agriculture, and the execution of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) for the Kien Luong 1 BOT Thermal Power Plant project, aimed at long-term energy transition.

Social (S): The company sustained its long-term social contributions through essential welfare infrastructure projects established in prior periods. Key assets include the operation of the Tan Tao University Medical Hospital (operating under License No. 173/BYT-GPHĐ) to support public healthcare; the establishment of the Red Sun Kindergarten in Binh Chanh (Decision No. 13438/QĐ-UBND); and the operation of Tan Tao University to elevate regional human capital via specialized training in medicine and biotechnology. However, updated metrics regarding occupational health and safety (OHS) and specific employee welfare programs for FY2022 remain unreported.

Governance (G): ITA’s executive management team during the year was anchored by the Board of Management, led by General Director Nguyen Thanh Phong, alongside dedicated functional personnel in risk management, construction, and finance (Chief Accountant Nguyen Thi Hoa). Nevertheless, due to the absence of audited disclosures, the independence of the Board of Directors and the degree of compliance with GRI / SDGs standards in FY2022 cannot be comprehensively evaluated. Ultimate governance oversight continues to bear the strong personal imprint of Chairwoman Dang Thi Hoang Yen (Mme. Maya Dangelas), supported by her leadership accolades earned between 2013 and 2016.
