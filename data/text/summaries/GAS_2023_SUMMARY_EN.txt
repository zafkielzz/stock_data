Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative financial analysis summary for PetroVietnam Gas Joint Stock Corporation (PV GAS - GAS), based on the 2023 fiscal data and accompanying documentation:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, PV GAS’s business operations faced numerous challenges stemming from global geopolitical volatility, weakening energy demand, and a decline in average Brent crude prices compared to the previous year. However, owing to proactive management of the integrated gas pipeline system and the optimization of its value chain—ranging from gas gathering and transportation to processing and product commercialization—the company maintained its leading position in the national energy market. Core growth drivers continued to be derived from dry gas supply to power plants, fertilizer producers, and industrial zones, alongside the retention of a major market share in the Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) business. Regarding end-markets, domestic consumption served as a robust anchor, complemented by the export of LPG and condensate to regional markets. This enabled the company to sustain stable cash flows despite margin compression driven by output selling prices tracking global market fluctuations.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PV GAS’s capital structure in 2023 remained sound and highly secure, characterized by a low financial leverage ratio that reflects robust financial autonomy. The company maintained a substantial reserve of cash and short-term deposits, minimizing its reliance on external commercial debt. Existing short- and long-term borrowings are primarily utilized to finance key infrastructure investment projects (such as the Block B gas-to-power project chain and LNG terminal warehouses). Interest rates applied to borrowings fluctuated in line with market conditions, encompassing both floating and fixed rates under credit agreements with major domestic and international commercial banks. Capital structure risks and cash flow pressures were well-contained by steady operating cash flows, ensuring the timely repayment of principal and interest alongside an attractive cash dividend payout for shareholders.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Financial risk management was prudently executed by PV GAS through the full provisioning for inventory write-downs and allowance for doubtful accounts in compliance with prevailing accounting standards, accurately reflecting asset values at the close of the fiscal year. Non-performing loans and inventory risks remained minimal due to strict credit policies applied to major off-takers (such as power and chemical conglomerates). Regarding off-balance-sheet guarantees and legal disputes, no material events were recorded that could directly threaten the company's financial position. Financial events subsequent to December 31, 2023, showed no abnormal occurrences that would adversely affect the business continuity and stability of the corporation's operations.

Energy management programs, resource conservation, and the promotion of a circular economy model were integrated across all gas facilities and processing plants.
