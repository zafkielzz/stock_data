1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
FY2024 witnessed exceptional operational performance by Gemadept Corporation (GMD) despite macro headwinds stemming from global geopolitical volatility and Red Sea disruptions. The primary macroeconomic tailwind stemmed from Vietnam's robust economic recovery, with GDP growth reaching 7.09%. Consequently, GMD’s Profit Before Tax (PBT) reached VND 2,099 billion (inclusive of gains from capital divestments) and VND 1,781 billion (excluding divestment transactions). Core operating performance posted strong year-over-year growth of 36%, outperforming the annual target by 32%.

By segment, the port exploitation sector continued to serve as the core growth engine, with earnings expanding by approximately 44% YoY. In the Northern region, the Nam Dinh Vu port complex is accelerating the construction of Phase 3 (scheduled for commercial operation in Q4 2025), which will elevate the cluster's aggregate capacity to nearly 2 million TEUs. Concurrently, the Ha Nam Channel dredging and upgrading project, pioneered by GMD, significantly enhances the competitive advantage of the Hai Phong port cluster. In the Southern region, the Gemalink Deep-water Port Phase 1 continuously set throughput productivity records, propelling the Cai Mep area to 7th globally in operational efficiency, thereby laying the groundwork for the initiation of Phase 2A construction in 2025.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
GMD’s balance sheet expanded robustly, with total assets reaching VND 17,998 billion as of December 31, 2024, up 33% YoY. Notably, current assets surged 97% to VND 6,674 billion, underpinned by an abundant liquidity buffer of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term bank deposits. Non-current assets stood at VND 11,324 billion (+11% YoY), primarily concentrated in long-term financial investments and other capital expenditures dedicated to capacity expansion.

Regarding the capital structure, total equity expanded by 42% to VND 13,772 billion, driven by share issuance premiums from rights offerings and solid retained earnings. Consequently, the Equity-to-Total Assets ratio strengthened to a highly conservative 77%. Total liabilities edged up 11% to VND 4,226 billion, driven primarily by the drawdown of new long-term debt facilities to finance major infrastructure projects. The combination of equity expansion and prudent cash flow management enabled GMD to maintain an exceptionally healthy Debt-to-Equity ratio of 0.3x, eliminating short-term liquidity pressure and optimizing its weighted average cost of capital (WACC).

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Notwithstanding a general reduction in short-term liabilities, GMD proactively increased short-term provisions, focusing primarily on loss provisions for potential compensations to preemptively address operational disputes arising within the supply chain. The company also faces intense competitive pressures as domestic port supply expands rapidly, compounded by shifting alliances among major container liners.

Regarding post-balance sheet events, the announcement of new US tariff policies in early 2025 is projected to induce volatility in Vietnam's import-export activities. GMD's executive board assesses the three-month window following the US temporary suspension of reciprocal tariffs as a critical timeframe to assist clients in accelerating customs clearance, actively negotiate, and prepare resource-allocation scenarios to mitigate the risk of abrupt volume contractions.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Gemadept is systematically operationalizing its sustainable and long-term value creation strategy through a comprehensive ESG framework:
*   **Environmental (E):** GMD’s Port and Logistics ecosystem is aggressively pursuing "Greenification" and "Digitalization". The deployment of technological applications such as SmartPort, Smart Gate, and River Gate optimizes operational capacity and significantly curtails vessel and truck turnaround times, thereby directly abating carbon emissions. Afforestation initiatives remain strategically anchored as a core long-term environmental conservation investment.
*   **Social (S):** GMD is committed to enforcing stringent occupational health and safety standards, deploying competency frameworks, and offering competitive employee welfare packages. The “Learning and Growth” pillar within the Balanced Scorecard (BSC) framework is being reinforced to elevate human capital capabilities ahead of the wave of digital transformation and artificial intelligence (AI) adoption.
*   **Governance (G):** The corporate governance structure maintains a high degree of independence and transparency, featuring a 10-member Board of Directors (BOD)—including 3 highly qualified independent members (Mr. Nguyen Van Hung, Mr. Lam Dinh Du, and Mr. Nguyen Thai Son)—supported by a BOD-affiliated Audit Committee. This commitment to transparency is underscored by the maintenance of bilingual (English-Vietnamese) disclosures, winning the IR Award 2024, and being honored for two consecutive years in the "Top 20 Non-Financial Companies with Best Annual Reports" by HOSE, HNX, and Investment Newspaper.
