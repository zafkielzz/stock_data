### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2025, Vinhomes delivered robust operational outperformance, surpassing management’s initial guidance. Total consolidated converted revenue reached nearly VND 183.1 trillion, representing a 29% year-on-year increase, while net income stood at VND 43.3 trillion. Core growth drivers stemmed from disciplined execution of commercial strategies and adherence to construction timelines across large-scale developments, notably Vinhomes Royal Island and the Vinhomes Ocean Park 2 and 3 clusters within the Ocean City complex. 

Strategically, Vinhomes expanded into the affordable housing segment via the "Happy Home" brand (initiating 4 projects toward a 500,000-unit target) alongside the simultaneous rollout of 4 major commercial mega-projects (Vinhomes Golden City, Vinhomes Wonder City, Vinhomes Green City, and Vinhomes Green Paradise), which yielded exceptionally high absorption rates (e.g., Vinhomes Wonder City achieved a 92% take-up rate on its launch day). Notably, the company initiated its international expansion by conducting feasibility studies and advancing large-scale urban development projects in the Democratic Republic of Congo, India, Uzbekistan, and other high-potential markets, thereby widening its long-term growth runway.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinhomes’ financial architecture was substantially reinforced in 2025 via flexible capital-raising capabilities. The company successfully issued bonds totaling VND 31.540 trillion during the year, securing adequate liquidity for long-term development pipelines. As of FY2025 year-end, the Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio was maintained at a prudent 39.37%. 

Cash and short-term financial investments yielded interest rates ranging from 1.6% to 4.75% p.a. for short-term deposits and 2.9% to 7.4% p.a. for term deposits. On the credit deployment side, corporate partner loans generated yields between 11% and 12% p.a. (maturing in 2026), retail partner loans carried an interest rate of 7.0% p.a., and unsecured related-party loans bore a fixed interest rate of 12.0% p.a. While the maintenance of centralized, authorized payment accounts optimized treasury management, underlying liquidity pressures warrant rigorous monitoring of cash collections from business cooperation and investment receivables.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Provisioning and risk management frameworks in 2025 reflected a conservative posture regarding impaired assets, encompassing overdue or uncollectible receivables, advances, earnest money, and loans extended to corporate and individual counterparties. 

Regarding major asset acquisitions executed in December 2025, Vinhomes completed the 100% equity acquisition of VinES Energy Solutions Joint Stock Company for a total consideration of VND 12.300 trillion, and acquired an 80.6% capital contribution in Huong Duong Real Estate Development Co., Ltd. for a total consideration of VND 7.650 trillion. The acquisition of Huong Duong generated a differential between the transfer price and the net asset value, which was recognized directly in retained earnings and non-controlling interests. Additionally, site-clearance advances are backed by equity collateral from affiliated entities within the Group, whereas substantial earnest money and investment cooperation contributions (such as VND 12.196 trillion in consortium capital, VND 2.790 trillion in contractual investments, and VND 23.659 trillion in project development deposits) necessitate rigorous post-balance sheet monitoring for latent legal and counterparty risks.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vinhomes prioritized sustainable development by implementing a "green construction site" model across multiple projects, strictly mitigating dust, noise, and construction waste while optimizing resource utilization during construction. The company also aligns its urban development pipeline with green transformation mandates and modern infrastructure benchmarks.
- **Social (S):** The company underscored its corporate social responsibility by scaling the Happy Home affordable housing portfolio to address homeownership demands among middle-income demographics. Concurrently, Vinhomes sponsored large-scale community engagement initiatives, including the "My Vietnam 2025" marathon (attracting over 25,000 runners) and a series of cultural-entertainment events drawing millions of visitors, thereby enriching residents' quality of life.
- **Governance (G):** Vinhomes operated under stringent, transparent corporate governance frameworks, anchored by independent auditing and financial reporting standards compliant with rigorous benchmarks. The company's managerial excellence and market prestige were further underscored by winning "Southeast Asia Developer of the Year 2025" at the Dot Property Southeast Asia Awards and securing a spot in Time's top 50 Asian enterprises.
