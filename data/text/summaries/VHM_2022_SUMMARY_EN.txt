Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the Vinhomes (VHM) FY2022 qualitative financial analysis summary:

---

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, Vinhomes' business operations faced considerable headwinds from broader macroeconomic and real estate market volatilities. The core growth driver for the enterprise continued to stem from real estate development and transfers, particularly the handover of major mega-townships such as Vinhomes Ocean Park 2 - The Empire and Vinhomes Smart City. However, due to complex developments in the corporate bond and real estate credit markets during the year, profit margins and revenue recognition velocity encountered certain challenges. Investment property leasing and the provision of operational management services continued to serve as stable revenue streams, supporting recurring cash flows for the business. Regarding its market footprint, VHM remained heavily focused on the domestic market with satellite mega-townships located across major cities (Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and adjacent provinces), registering no notable international real estate exports given the inherent characteristics of the sector.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinhomes' capital and debt structure in 2022 reflected a flexible capital-raising strategy aimed at securing liquidity for its large-scale mega-townships. The company maintained a balance between equity and financial debt (comprising short-term borrowings for working capital replenishment and long-term debt for project financing). Borrowings were impacted by fluctuations in the prevailing interest rate environment amid tightening monetary policy. The primary credit providers for Vinhomes' projects are typically leading commercial banks in Vietnam (spanning both state-owned and reputable private joint-stock commercial banks). Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks required the enterprise to rigorously manage sales progress and customer collections to mitigate debt dependency and optimize interest expenses within a rising floating-rate environment.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Provisioning for 2022 was executed by Vinhomes in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards, focusing on inventory write-downs for delayed projects and allowances for doubtful debts to conservatively reflect market risks. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, guarantees, and contingent obligations, the company maintained tight control over payment guarantees and fulfilled contractual obligations to customers and construction partners. No material, unusual legal disputes or litigations were recorded that could significantly impact the financial position. Regarding subsequent events, on March 31, 2023, the company finalized and published its 2022 Annual Report in full compliance with regulatory requirements for information disclosure on the stock market (Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange), ensuring transparency for shareholders and investors.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** Vinhomes continued to integrate sustainable development criteria into the master planning, design, and operations of its mega-townships. Initiatives included optimizing green spaces, implementing energy-saving solutions across smart urban areas, promoting solar-powered lighting infrastructure, and managing waste toward a circular economy model, thereby contributing to the reduction of the carbon footprint across the operational value chain.
- **Social (S):** The company places strong emphasis on a safe working environment, ensuring occupational health and safety (OHS) standards for employees and contractors. Human resources policies prioritize gender equality, fair career development opportunities, professional training programs, and comprehensive welfare and insurance regimes for the workforce.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework complies with current stock market regulations under the oversight of a Board of Directors with a requisite degree of independence. Financial statements and periodic disclosures are independently audited by top-tier reputable audit firms, ensuring a high level of compliance with advanced governance practices.
