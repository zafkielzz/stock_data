Below is an in-depth qualitative analysis summary of the operational performance, financial condition, and ESG execution of Nam Kim Steel Joint Stock Company (NKG) for FY2023, based on the Annual Report and verified data.

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, NKG reported net revenue of VND 18,596 billion (down 19.4% YoY) and profit after tax of VND 117.4 billion, achieving 93% of its revenue target. The top-line contraction was primarily driven by volatile global macroeconomic conditions, widespread weakness in aggregate steel demand, and intense pricing pressure from foreign competitors, which drove the average selling price (ASP) down from approximately $540/tonne to below $500/tonne compared to 2022. Nevertheless, full-year steel sales volume still reached 861,228 tonnes (up a modest 2% YoY, supported by coated sheet sales reaching 4.6 million tonnes). NKG's core growth driver stemmed from maintaining and efficiently operating its system of 5 coated sheet production lines, specializing in hot-dip galvanized steel, galvalume, pre-painted galvanized steel (color-coated sheets), and high-quality steel pipes. In export markets, the company solidified its competitive edge across more than 65 countries and territories, with a strategic focus on penetrating demanding markets with stringent standards such as Europe, the US, and Oceania, while preserving a stable domestic market share.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
NKG’s capital structure in 2023 remained conservative, maintaining optimized operational efficiency. Total assets at year-end stood at VND 12,235 billion (down 9.5% YTD), of which equity accounted for VND 5,423 billion, representing 44.3% of total capital. The total liabilities-to-total assets ratio continued its downward trend, remaining stable at 55.6%. Total short-term debt and financial lease obligations were recorded at VND 4,767 billion, predominantly comprising short-term loans denominated in Vietnamese Dong (VND) and US Dollars (USD) with floating interest rates ranging from approximately 3.0% to 5.8% per annum, depending on the currency. Commercial banking partners extending the largest credit lines to NKG included Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV – Phu My Branch), Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank – Binh Duong Branch), Vietnam International Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VIB), and Standard Chartered Bank (Vietnam) Limited. Cash flow pressure was well-contained as the company proactively generated positive net operating cash flow of VND 263.2 billion alongside strict working capital turnover management.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
NKG maintained a prudent provisioning policy across asset classes to accurately reflect its financial reality. Specifically, the provision for inventory write-downs ended the year at VND 130.3 billion (down sharply from VND 336.8 billion in the previous year), driven by optimized inventory management and provision reversals amid stabilizing steel prices. Regarding non-performing loans (NPLs) and bad debts, the company upheld strict trade credit control policies, recording no material contingent liabilities or major legal disputes that could adversely impact business operations. Off-balance-sheet guarantees and L/C commitments were managed strictly within approved bank credit limits. Financial events occurring after December 31, 2023, primarily revolved around the execution of business plans and the allocation of resources for key capital expenditure projects such as the Nam Kim Phu My Coated Sheet Plant, with no extraordinary events noted that would alter the nature of the consolidated financial statements.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environment (E):** NKG is committed to minimizing its environmental footprint by investing in advanced, eco-friendly manufacturing technologies (implementing toxic-emission-free technologies, wastewater recycling, and thorough solid waste treatment). The company proactively enhances energy efficiency, conserves raw materials and fuel across its plants, and focuses on developing "green" product lines that meet international standards to capture the tailwinds of the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
- **Social (S):** The enterprise ensures a safe working environment, strictly complying with Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) regulations and fire prevention standards. NKG effectively promotes gender equality and comprehensively safeguards the material and spiritual well-being of its workforce of over 1,300 employees through competitive compensation packages, 24/24 accident insurance, and regular professional skill-enhancement and vocational training programs.
- **Governance (G):** The company strictly adheres to public corporate governance regulations, featuring an independent and transparent Board of Directors that works in close coordination with the Supervisory Board and the Board of Management. The annual financial statements are independently audited by PwC Vietnam, ensuring full compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), GRI standards, and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
