### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2025 marked exceptional scale expansion for the Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV) against a favorable macroeconomic backdrop, characterized by 8.02% GDP growth and inflation contained at 3.31%. Consolidated total assets surpassed the VND 3.27 trillion threshold (+20.5% YoY), reinforcing its position as Vietnam's largest commercial bank by assets. Core growth drivers emanated from the credit segment, with gross loans outstanding reaching VND 2.32 trillion (+15.2% YoY), while total mobilization scaled to VND 2.43 trillion (+13.7% YoY), capturing a 14.3% market share of system-wide deposits. Consolidated Profit Before Tax (PBT) reached VND 37,788 billion (+17.8% YoY), primarily propelled by the commercial banking operations, which generated VND 35,509 billion in PBT (+15.7% YoY).

Regarding non-interest income (non-II) composition, net fee income stood at VND 5,708 billion (+1.4% YoY; achieving an organic growth rate of 10.7% when stripping out the accounting reclassification of certain trade finance products to "other income" effective July 1, 2024), spearheaded by settlement services (+11% YoY) and trade finance (+19% YoY). Conversely, net trading income from foreign exchange plunged 29.4% YoY to VND 3,718 billion, pressured by adverse FX volatility hovering near the State Bank of Vietnam’s (SBV) regulatory ceiling. Operational efficiency improved notably, with the Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR) compressing from 33.5% to 32.7%. Subsidiaries and joint ventures also delivered breakthrough results, notably Vietnam-Russia Joint Venture Bank (VRB) with PBT of VND 1,016 billion (+65% YoY, ROE of 18.5%) and Vietnam Aircraft Leasing Company (VALC), which posted $363.8 million in revenue and $114 million in PBT following the completion of its asset restructuring. On the equity market, BID shares closed the year at VND 35,990 (+2.95% vs. opening price of VND 34,960), implying a P/E multiple of 9.40x and a P/B multiple of 1.75x.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

BIDV robustly reinforced its financial capacity and capital adequacy in FY2025 through charter capital augmentation to VND 70,214 billion. The bank’s standalone equity reached VND 163,017 billion, doubling its level at the beginning of the 2021 cycle, driving market capitalization beyond VND 273,000 billion (~$11 billion). Total mobilized capital reached VND 3,052,107 billion—encompassing customer deposits, valuable paper issuance, borrowings from the Government/SBV, and interbank placements—thereby securing a solid liquidity buffer for total credit extension and investments totaling VND 3,098,740 billion.

To support the real economy, BIDV proactively optimized operating expenditures to reduce lending rates by 50 bps to 200 bps per annum, providing relief to nearly 400,000 corporate and retail clients while actively executing debt restructuring. Although granular figures concerning the floating-to-fixed rate mix and the roster of principal funding counterparties were not publicly disclosed in the reporting documents, the bank affirms that prudential ratios and Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR) strictly adhere to SBV regulatory thresholds. Notably, the bank maintained a conservative investment posture, refraining from executing any new equity investments or corporate acquisitions throughout FY2025.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

BIDV’s asset quality remained tightly managed, with the Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio under Circular 31/2024/TT-NHNN sustained at 1.26%, comfortably below the SBV’s mandated ceiling of 1.6%. The bank maintained full provisioning write-downs in strict compliance with statutory regulations, building a robust loss-absorption buffer against unpredictable market headwinds. Within joint-venture affiliates such as VRB, risk management frameworks continued to be institutionalized toward Basel II standards. Off-balance sheet contingent commitments, complex legal disputes, and financial events materializing after December 31, 2025, showed no anomalous fluctuations, underscoring the stability and rigor of BIDV’s internal control apparatus.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

ESG pillars at BIDV are implemented synchronously and explicitly anchored in its long-term corporate development strategy:
- **Environmental (E):** BIDV formulated and submitted its development strategy to 2030, with a vision toward 2045, to the SBV, centering on the core objective of becoming a "Leading Regional Large-Scale, Robust, and Green Financial-Banking Group," steering credit allocations preferentially toward green projects, environmental conservation, and sustainable development.
- **Social (S):** The bank demonstrated profound social responsibility by lowering lending rates for 400,000 clients impacted by natural disasters and macroeconomic volatility. Concurrently, BIDV was voted "Best Workplace in Vietnam" for the third consecutive year, underpinned by competitive compensation policies, continuous competency-based talent development programs, and a strong commitment to elevating employees' standard of living.
- **Governance (G):** BIDV executed a decisive restructuring of its managerial apparatus under the guiding motto of "Responsibility - Streamlined - Efficient." The bank reorganized its Head Office operating model to delineate functional mandates, eliminate operational redundancies, and significantly trim administrative procedures via digital transformation. Transparency was further cemented by aligning the remuneration and bonus policies for the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and the Executive Board directly with operational performance, capping incentives at a maximum of 0.26% of FY2025 consolidated net profit pursuant to resolutions approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders.
