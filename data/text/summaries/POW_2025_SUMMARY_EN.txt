### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, Petrovietnam Power Corporation (POW) achieved total consolidated revenue of VND 35,364 billion (completing 93% of the annual target and up 110% YoY), while profit before tax reached VND 3,234.3 billion (achieving 656% of the target). POW's core growth driver stemmed from its gas-fired power production and trading segment, which recorded segment revenue of VND 20,379.47 billion and delivered the largest gross profit contribution, driven by favorable dispatch rates in 2H25—particularly for the Nhơn Trạch 1, Nhơn Trạch 2, and Cà Mau power plants. The hydropower segment (comprising Đakđrinh and Hủa Na) posted a sharp recovery in profit margins and commercial output due to more favorable hydrological conditions compared to the previous year. Meanwhile, coal-fired power plants such as Vũng Áng 1 faced notable challenges arising from complex fluctuations in fuel supply and pricing, alongside the requirement to derate generation capacity to comply with environmental regulations. All of POW's revenue and primary business operations are concentrated within the domestic market in Vietnam.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
POW’s capital structure remains at a prudent level, with an authenticated Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 66.96%. Total liabilities as of December 31, 2025, stood at VND 51,396 billion, comprising VND 37,008 billion in short-term debt and VND 19,784 billion in long-term debt (primarily bank loans dedicated to capital expenditures for key projects such as the Nhơn Trạch 3 and Nhơn Trạch 4 power plants). The interest rate structure includes both USD- and VND-denominated borrowings carrying floating rates tied to market benchmarks (such as SOFR/EURIBOR for foreign currencies and reference rates of major domestic commercial banks). POW’s primary credit-financing banking partners include HSBC (HCMC Branch), Techcombank, VietinBank, and BIDV. Cash flow pressure primarily stems from heavy capital expenditure requirements for new power source projects and USD/EUR foreign exchange rate volatility risks affecting large foreign-currency-denominated loans.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
POW’s provisioning practices are executed conservatively in strict compliance with current regulations. Provisions for short-term doubtful trade receivables, inventories, and receivables are reviewed periodically to accurately reflect actual losses from electricity off-takers (primarily EVN) who experience payment delays. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, POW maintains performance bonds, loan guarantees, and financial commitments related to the Nhơn Trạch 3 & 4 LNG power projects. No material major legal disputes were recorded during the year. Regarding subsequent events, POW successfully executed a public share offering to existing shareholders to increase its charter capital to VND 27,868,210,960,000, pursuant to the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders’ Resolution and business registration certificates fully granted in early 2026.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** POW actively manages its greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) in accordance with Petrovietnam (PVN) guidelines, while researching scientific and technological solutions to mitigate emissions. The thermal power plants maintain strict emission treatment and circulating wastewater systems. The company promotes renewable energy development and implements a fuel-switching roadmap toward LNG, hydrogen, and ammonia to advance toward its Net-Zero target by 2050 in alignment with Power Development Plan VIII (PDP VIII).
- **Social (S):** The company strictly complies with labor laws, ensuring compensation, bonus schemes, and social and health insurance for employees. Average monthly income in 2025 reached VND 26.67 million per person. POW places strong emphasis on Occupational Health and Safety (OHS), organizes professional skills training programs, maintains gender equality, and actively participates in local social welfare activities with a total budget amounting to tens of billions of dong.
- **Governance (G):** POW’s governance framework operates in compliance with the Law on Enterprises and the Law on Securities. The Board of Directors (BOD) comprises highly qualified members, maintaining the required ratio of independent members to enhance objectivity in strategic decision-making. Independent audits are conducted by Deloitte Vietnam. The company strictly complies with public disclosure standards and transparency requirements for related-party transactions.
