### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, despite headwinds from the COVID-19 pandemic and prolonged social distancing measures, Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank) delivered stellar business performance driven by agile executive management and a robust macroeconomic recovery in Q4 (full-year GDP growth of 2.58%, inflation contained at 1.84%). Core growth momentum stemmed from mobilization and credit expansion surpassing targets; total outstanding credit grew 15% YoY to VND 972.68 billion, landing squarely within the State Bank of Vietnam’s (SBV) credit growth ceiling. Total assets expanded 7% YoY to VND 1,414.673 billion, while total mobilized funds rose 9% YoY to VND 1,152.712 billion. Consolidated pre-tax profit for 2021 reached VND 27,389 billion, representing a 19% YoY increase. The bank steadfastly executed its three strategic pillars of "Retail Banking - Services - Investment," cementing its position as the premier banking institution in terms of asset quality and operational efficiency, while leading the industry in profitability scale and non-interest income.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vietcombank’s funding structure maintained stability and sustainability, with total equity recording positive growth, forming the bedrock for credit extension and investment activities. The loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR) was tightly managed to ensure systemic liquidity safety. The bank focused on structuring its funding base toward sustainable efficiency, aligning deposit mobilization velocity with capital deployment and credit growth rates. Furthermore, Vietcombank advanced its charter capital increase plan utilizing retained earnings post-2020 cash dividend payout to fortify its Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) under Circular 41/2016/TT-NHNN, thereby complying with stringent international risk management standards under Basel II Standardized Approach while paving the way for the Advanced Approach.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Credit quality at Vietcombank in 2021 remained best-in-class among large-scale banking institutions. The standalone non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was kept exceptionally low at 0.63% (equivalent to VND 6,121 billion), with the Group 2 loan ratio at 0.36%. A standout highlight in risk governance was Vietcombank’s proactive 100% provisioning for all restructured and debt-group-retained loans impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic, achieving this milestone two years ahead of the SBV’s regulatory deadline. The loan loss reserve (LLR) balance reached VND 25,976 billion, propelling the NPL coverage ratio to an industry-record high of ~424%. Guarantees, derivatives, and other off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities were stringently monitored within approved risk limits to safeguard operations against macroeconomic volatility.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** In response to the Vietnamese Government’s historic COP26 commitment targeting net-zero emissions by 2050, Vietcombank scaled up capital financing for green projects. The bank actively prepared operational frameworks to integrate environmental risk management into its credit-granting activities per SBV guidelines, fostering a mindset centered on "green" consumption and investment within the corporate community.
- **Social (S):** Vietcombank prioritizes human capital development with a workforce of nearly 22,000 employees, sustaining a connected working environment, regular professional training, competitive employee benefits, and routine labor conferences. Additionally, the bank demonstrated profound corporate social responsibility through comprehensive social welfare programs, committing approximately VND 662 billion in 2021—including ~VND 381 billion channeled toward COVID-19 containment efforts and supporting frontline healthcare workers.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to international best practices and regulatory mandates, operating under a robust framework comprising the General Meeting of Shareholders, the Board of Directors (supported by specialized committees including the Risk Management Committee, Personnel Committee, and Strategy Committee), the Supervisory Board, and the Executive Board. The bank upheld exemplary transparency via independent audits, maintained proactive engagement with shareholders and investors, and was honored among the Top 10 enterprises recognized for business cultural standards in Vietnam.
