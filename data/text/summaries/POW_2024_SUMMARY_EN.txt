1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
For the fiscal year 2024, PetroVietnam Power Corporation (POW) delivered financial results exceeding management guidance, underpinned by high operational flexibility. Total consolidated power generation reached 16 billion kWh, accounting for 5.2% of the national grid's total output. Consolidated revenue hit VND 32,112 billion, achieving 101% of the full-year target, while profit before tax surged to VND 1,383 billion, outperforming the objective by 39%. Core growth drivers stemmed from the stable operational yield of existing thermal and hydroelectric generation assets, alongside a state budget contribution of nearly VND 1,263 billion (151% of plan), reaffirming the corporation's robust financial standing. Strategically, operations remained predominantly focused on satisfying domestic demand to safeguard national energy security, supplemented by technical electrical services and fuel supply chains directly supporting the core power generation segment.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
POW’s financial structure and cash flow liquidity are encumbered by restricted cash balances and credit facilities across several financial institutions. Specifically, restricted bank deposits and term deposits with original maturities under 3 months at Modern Vietnam One Member Limited Liability Bank (formerly OceanBank) totaled VND 285.06 million and VND 3.8 billion, respectively, pending specific regulatory resolutions from the State Bank of Vietnam. Similarly, held-to-maturity investments recorded VND 26.12 billion in restricted deposits at this institution. Cash flow pressures and contingent guarantee obligations are clearly evidenced by POW utilizing term deposits (VND 25.45 billion as of December 31, 2024) to secure long-term debt obligations for its subsidiary, Hua Na Hydropower JSC, alongside VND 35.54 billion in deposits pledged by PV Power Renewable Energy JSC (PV Power REC) at Vietcombank and BIDV as collateral for loan facilities. Structurally, the self-borrowing and self-repaying mechanism mandated by the Government for power infrastructure projects necessitates stringent capital management by POW to mitigate refinancing risks amidst volatile interest rates and foreign exchange fluctuations.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
POW maintains a prudent provisioning policy for long-term financial investments in underperforming entities. Specifically, the Executive Board has provisioned VND 18.2 billion against a total investment book value of VND 18.202 billion in PVC Mekong (PXC—an entity that has ceased operations, accumulated net losses, and faced trading suspension on the HNX), and recorded a provision of VND 0.3 billion against an investment capital of VND 1 billion in Petroleum Electro-Mechanical JSC. Regarding post-balance sheet events, a notable financial development occurred on January 20, 2025, when Nam Chien Hydropower JSC (NCC) distributed dividends for fiscal years 2018 and 2019, yielding total cash dividends of VND 58.45 billion to POW, following the reduction of POW’s equity stake in NCC from 20.16% to 15.49% and the formal loss of associate status on November 29, 2024. Furthermore, residual headwinds stemming from primary fuel supply shortages (natural gas and coal), stringent environmental emission standards imposed by the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, and ongoing Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) renegotiations within the competitive power market continue to present substantial operational and regulatory risk management challenges.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** POW remains steadfast in its green growth strategy, actively researching and investing in clean and renewable energy projects (wind, solar, biomass, and waste-to-energy) in alignment with Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8) to advance greenhouse gas reduction targets. Nevertheless, the corporation faces significant headwinds as the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment enacts stricter emission thresholds, necessitating substantial technological retrofitting across existing thermal power plants to maintain compliance.
- **Social (S):** The corporation continues to uphold its corporate social responsibility toward human capital, earning recognition as an "Outstanding Enterprise for Employees" for the fourth consecutive year. Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) and fire prevention protocols have been tightened via modernized automated systems and 24/7 on-duty response teams across all plants, complemented by comprehensive talent development frameworks and structured corporate social responsibility initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** POW’s corporate governance framework operates on principles of transparency, accountability, and strict adherence to domestic and international benchmarks. The corporation has been a perennial constituent of the Top 100 Sustainable Enterprises in Vietnam (CSI 100) compiled by VCCI and secured top rankings in the non-financial sector for best annual reports, demonstrating an unwavering commitment to balancing the interests of shareholders, the state, and employees under independent auditing oversight.
