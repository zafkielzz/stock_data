### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, the business operations of the Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV - Ticker: BID) were significantly impacted by a volatile macroeconomic environment driven by the complex developments of the COVID-19 pandemic. Nevertheless, the bank maintained stability through a strategy of shifting its income structure, scaling up its services segment, and executing efficient cost controls. Core growth momentum continued to stem from retail and SME lending, alongside non-interest income derived from payment services, insurance, and trade finance. As a large-scale joint-stock commercial bank, BIDV does not participate directly in physical goods export markets; however, its credit and import-export financing operations serve as a vital springboard supporting the manufacturing corporate sector. The Net Interest Margin (NIM) was well-defended through the flexible adjustment of cost of funds and the optimization of the interest-earning asset portfolio, thereby cushioning the pressure from rising credit risk provisioning mandated to support pandemic-affected clients.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
As a credit institution boasting the largest total asset scale among state-owned commercial banks, BIDV’s capital structure is primarily formed through deposit mobilization from economic organizations and residential sectors, combined with equity and debt securities issued by the bank. Tier 2 borrowings and debt (interbank borrowings, loans from international financial institutions) are strictly managed in accordance with liquidity limits and prudential safety ratios stipulated by the State Bank of Vietnam. Deposit and lending rates maintained a downward trend throughout the year to support the broader economy, with the majority of the credit portfolio utilizing floating rates adjusted across reset periods (3-month, 6-month, or 12-month) tied to benchmark rates, shielding the bank from interest rate risk. Cash flow pressure primarily stemmed from the need to match asset-liability durations (short-term vs. long-term) and the imperative to maintain Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR) under Basel II standards, requiring the bank to continuously reinforce its capital buffer through retained earnings and capital-raising issuances.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Credit risk management and provisioning in 2021 served as a strategic focus for BIDV to address potential non-performing loan (NPL) risks originating from clients distressed by the pandemic. The bank executed adequate risk provisioning in compliance with State Bank of Vietnam regulations, while proactively augmenting general and specific provisions to elevate its NPL coverage ratio. Off-balance-sheet commitments and guarantees (including financing guarantees, performance guarantees, and letters of credit - L/Cs) were rigorously controlled via a centralized credit approval system. Regarding subsequent events, on April 20, 2022, BIDV officially published its finalized 2021 Annual Report in accordance with regulatory mandates. Trading dynamics for BID shares during the year recorded an opening price of VND 25,350 and closed the year at VND 24,900 (down a marginal 1.78%), fluctuating within a range of VND 19,380 to VND 26,860 with an average matching liquidity volume of 2,649,162 shares/session, reflecting a cautious yet stable market sentiment toward the bank's stock.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** BIDV has progressively integrated environmental and social risk standards into its credit appraisal process, prioritizing capital allocation toward renewable energy, clean energy, and green economic development projects. The bank emphasizes internal operational optimization to conserve energy, curb greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2), and promote paperless offices, targeting Net-Zero emissions in alignment with national commitments.
- **Social (S):** The bank maintains a safe, egalitarian, and professional work environment, strictly adhering to occupational health and safety (OHS) regulations. Human resource policies ensure gender equality regarding promotion opportunities and compensation, alongside an emphasis on professional capacity-building training for employees. Employee welfare regimes, health insurance, and physical-mental well-being initiatives are consistently prioritized.
- **Governance (G):** BIDV’s corporate governance structure strictly complies with the Law on Credit Institutions and public company governance standards. The Board of Directors maintains a reasonable degree of independence with the participation of independent members, operating alongside transparent internal audit and external independent audit systems. The bank is progressively standardizing its reporting in accordance with the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) framework and aligning its strategic operations with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
