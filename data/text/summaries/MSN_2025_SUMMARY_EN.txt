### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2025, MSN’s equity performance recorded a +9.84% capital appreciation (opening at VND 70,100 and closing at VND 77,000), accompanied by a gross profit margin of 32.78%. The group’s core growth catalysts stem from its integrated Consumer-Retail-Technology ecosystem strategy. Masan Consumer (MCH) sustained its market leadership as a premier multi-industry FMCG enterprise, anchoring robust portfolios in condiments, convenience foods, packaged beverages, and instant coffee. WinCommerce (WCM) serves as Vietnam’s largest modern grocery retailer, operating 130 WinMart supermarkets and 4,462 WinMart+/WiN convenience stores as of year-end 2025, with rural outlets emerging as a bright spot delivering superior margin efficiency. The Retail Supreme digital retail platform directly covered approximately 420,000 points of sale, boosting salesforce productivity by 40% and increasing SKUs per order by 70% year-on-year. Furthermore, the Digital Retailer Membership program connected approximately 320,000 partners, while the WinX loyalty platform onboarded 250,000 new consumers within a two-month span, creating robust cross-selling momentum across the ecosystem.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Masan’s capital structure in 2025 posted a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 141.51%, reflecting a sustained financial leverage profile deployed to fund retail network expansion and supply chain scaling strategies. The group enforces rigorous asset-liability management policies, holding cash and cash equivalents comprising cash on hand, unrestricted bank deposits, and highly liquid short-term investments to service near-term liquidity obligations. Held-to-maturity investments consist of term bank deposits, recognized at historical cost less allowances for doubtful accounts. Foreign exchange risk management is operationalized by translating foreign-currency-denominated assets and liabilities at prevailing commercial bank exchange rates as of the annual balance sheet date, ensuring accurate representation of financial liabilities and mitigating adverse foreign exchange volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Provisions and risk management frameworks were rigorously executed by Masan Group in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards. Specifically, trading securities comprising short-term bonds and certificates of deposit are provisioned for impairment when market values fall below carrying amounts, with reversals recognized up to the initial cost basis upon market recovery. Equity investments in other entities are provisioned for diminution in value when investee losses threaten capital erosion. Inventories are accounted for under the perpetual inventory method, valued at the lower of cost (determined via the weighted-average method) and net realizable value. Property, plant, and equipment—including mining machinery and equipment at the Nui Phao project—are depreciated on a straight-line basis or via the units-of-production method based on determined mineral reserves, adhering to prudency principles without any unrecorded off-balance sheet contingent liabilities or anomalous post-balance sheet events subsequent to December 31, 2025.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Masan embeds environmental objectives into its sustainable development strategy via a commitment to reduce Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 15% by 2030, targeting net-zero emissions by 2050. The group has deployed environmental stewardship initiatives, including a 5-10% reduction in water consumption per unit of production through water recycling, scaling waste recycling rates to 50%, and accelerating the adoption of renewable energy by installing rooftop solar photovoltaic systems across manufacturing facilities.
- **Social (S):** The group prioritizes human capital development, targeting an employee satisfaction rate of at least 70% annually, maintaining female representation at managerial levels and above at 38% (outperforming the Asia-Pacific regional average), and achieving 100% compliance with food safety standards. Community development programs focus on public health, food science, and nutrition, integrating sustainable agriculture with technology.
- **Governance (G):** Sustainable governance frameworks have been institutionalized via the Group-level ESG Committee established in 2023, operating under the oversight of the Board of Directors and the Chief Executive Officer to steer strategy, manage risk, and integrate international standards such as the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and IFC Performance Standards. 100% of employees completed mandatory training on the Code of Conduct, anti-bribery, and anti-corruption policies, reinforcing corporate business ethics and transparency.
