1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Against the backdrop of a highly volatile and challenging equity market in 2022—characterized by a sharp downward correction in both the VN-Index and VCI's share price—Viet Capital Securities Joint Stock Company (VCSC) reported a clear divergence in performance across its business segments. Specifically, total pre-tax profit (PTP) reached VND 1,060 billion, down 43% year-on-year, achieving 56% of the full-year target (VND 1,900 billion), while the 12-month trailing ROE stood at 13.3% (or 16.8% excluding asset revaluation differentials).

VCSC’s core growth driver during the year was its **Investment Banking** division, which posted revenue of VND 431 billion (up 28%) and a PTP of VND 342 billion (up 48% YoY). This outperformance was underpinned by the successful advisory on landmark transactions, including advising the founding shareholders of the Phuc Long tea chain on the sale of a controlling stake to Masan Group (MSN), Masan Meat Life's divestment of its feed business to De Heus Vietnam, PNJ's private placement, and Hung Thinh Group's acquisition of Song Tien JSC. Conversely, the **Brokerage** segment was adversely impacted by contracting market liquidity (average daily trading value across the market declined by 25% to USD 862 million) driven by investor caution amid regulatory crackdowns on the real estate and securities sectors, alongside global macroeconomic headwinds. Brokerage revenue fell 27% to VND 609 billion, while PTP dropped 44% to VND 192 billion. Within this segment, retail brokerage PTP reached VND 51 billion (down 66%), and institutional brokerage PTP stood at VND 141 billion (down 27%). VCSC maintained its ranking as the 7th largest broker on the HOSE by market share at 4.72%. (Note: The company's operations are strictly focused on domestic financial services, with no export activities.)

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As of the end of fiscal year 2022, VCSC's total equity remained stable, registering a marginal 1% decrease YoY. The company's capital structure maintained adequate flexibility to support core business operations, notably margin lending and proprietary trading. Regarding debt composition, short-term borrowings, short-term issued bonds, and other payable obligations were managed stringently in accordance with prudential financial standards applicable to securities firms. Cash flow pressures during the year were influenced by broader market liquidity fluctuations and short-term settlement obligations arising from securities trading activities; nevertheless, the company continuously maintained regulatory capital adequacy ratios above mandated thresholds.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Provisions for financial asset devaluation, impairment allowances for receivables, and loss mitigation were executed conservatively by VCSC in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards to accurately reflect underlying market risks. Regarding contingent liabilities, off-balance sheet guarantee commitments, and legal disputes, the internal risk management framework demonstrated a high level of compliance, minimizing potential legal exposures. Subsequent to the balance sheet date (December 31, 2022), no abnormal financial events or material contingencies were recorded that could adversely impact the going concern assumption or the operational stability of the firm.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** VCSC focuses on optimizing energy and resource efficiency within its workplace by digitalizing operational workflows, minimizing paper usage, and fostering internal environmental awareness, aligning with broader sustainable development standards in the financial sector.
- **Social (S):** Human capital development is prioritized under the philosophy of "Investing in People." VCSC maintains a professional work environment that ensures gender equality and cultural diversity, recruiting personnel globally and from top-tier domestic and international universities. The firm emphasizes comprehensive training policies to enhance professional expertise—notably increasing the proportion of staff holding international designations such as CFA and ACCA—alongside competitive employee benefits and occupational safety standards.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance operates on a foundation of transparency and strict regulatory compliance. The Board of Directors, the Board of Management, and the Supervisory Board maintain independent oversight roles, upholding professional ethics, integrity, client confidentiality, and international reference frameworks in investor relations.
