**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**

FY2025 demonstrated FPT Corporation’s robust growth trajectory despite ongoing macroeconomic volatility. Consolidated revenue reached VND 70,113 billion, representing an 11.6% YoY increase. Notably, net profit after tax surged by 19.1% YoY to VND 11,232 billion, outpacing top-line growth and reflecting effective operating expense optimization. Gross margin remained resilient at a high 39.24%. Diluted EPS for the year stood at VND 5,216 (up 21.5% YoY), while trailing twelve-month (TTM) EPS was recorded at VND 5,772.14. However, amid broader equity market pressures, FPT’s share price experienced a 26.40% drawdown over the year (opening at VND 116,760 and closing at VND 85,930), with average matching volume sustaining high liquidity at over 7.56 million shares per session.

FPT’s core growth engine continued to be driven by its overseas IT Services segment, which generated VND 35,382 billion in revenue, up 14.3% YoY. The company's international competitive positioning is underscored by total newly signed contract value exceeding the USD 1.5 billion milestone (equivalent to VND 40,636 billion, a 23.2% increase YoY), alongside a doubling of contracts valued above USD 10 million. Digital Transformation (DX) revenue remained a pivotal growth driver, accounting for 47.3% of total overseas IT services revenue, bolstered by major global contract wins with Chelsea FC and Clearlake Capital. In the domestic market, FPT prioritized scaling national and municipal digital transformation initiatives in alignment with Resolution No. 57-NQ/TW, reinforcing its domestic technological leadership.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**

As of the close of FY2025, FPT maintained a moderate financial leverage profile with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 50.95%. Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA) stood at 5.91% and 2.98%, respectively.

Regarding debt structure and cash flow dynamics, corporate disclosures primarily focused on capital transactions and internal cash management. The group frequently executes liquidity optimization operations—comprising short-term lending, borrowing, debt recovery, and periodic internal interest settlements with subsidiaries and affiliates (such as FPT Investment Co., Ltd., FPT Smart Cloud, FPT Software, FPT IS, and FPT Education) under the governance framework of Resolution No. 02.03-2021/NQ-HĐQTFPT. Granular data regarding external commercial bank debt, precise short- versus long-term maturity profiles, floating-to-fixed interest rate sensitivities, and primary credit-extending financial institutions were not comprehensively detailed in the current disclosures. Consequently, capital structure risk is predominantly mitigated through a centralized treasury coordination mechanism between the parent company and its ecosystem of member entities.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**

The current financial statements do not explicitly disclose quantitative figures regarding provisions for inventory obsolescence or allowances for doubtful accounts, nor do they detail off-balance-sheet guarantees or material ongoing legal disputes.

Regarding post-balance-sheet events, corporate governance records indicate that the Board of Directors (BoD) approved the appointment of PwC (Vietnam) Limited as the independent auditor for the FY2025 financial statements. Furthermore, the company completed legal procedures to update its enterprise registration regarding charter capital increases stemming from bonus share issuances to existing shareholders, alongside the implementation of the first tranche of the 2025 cash dividend advance. The BoD also approved an ESOP issuance plan for high-performing employees and formulated a management share option scheme for senior executives spanning the 2026–2030 period to ensure long-term alignment of interests.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**

Environmental (E): FPT actively pioneers the "twin transition" paradigm (concurrent digital and green transitions). The group has invested heavily in sustainable technology infrastructure by commissioning two AI data centers (FPT AI Factories) in Vietnam and Japan. Operating at a 70% capacity utilization rate and processing over 1.1 trillion tokens, these facilities optimize energy consumption through the fine-tuning of Large Language Models (LLMs). Although the report does not disclose specific quantitative metrics regarding Scope 1, 2, and 3 greenhouse gas emissions or a detailed Net-Zero roadmap, the "Made by FPT" ecosystem focuses on developing software solutions that assist enterprises in optimizing production processes and sustainability management.

Social (S): The corporation prioritizes human capital development, managing a workforce of over 54,000 professionals across 30 countries, including more than 4,000 international experts representing 82 nationalities. FPT is committed to heavy investment in education and training via the establishment of the Quantum AI & Cybersecurity Institute, targeting the development of 100 Ph.D. holders and 2,000 high-tech specialists, alongside plans to train 1,500 Cloud engineers and 200 advanced Cloud architects. Within its education segment, FPT pioneers the "flipped classroom" pedagogical model integrated with AI to personalize learning outcomes and enhance societal intellectual welfare.

Governance (G): FPT’s governance framework operates rigorously, overseen by a 7-member Board of Directors and ensured through mandatory quarterly meetings involving the Supervisory Board. Financial transparency is reinforced by the appointment of PwC Vietnam as the independent auditor. Related-party transactions are stringently controlled via BoD resolutions to mitigate conflicts of interest, ensure strict compliance with integrity standards, and protect the best interests of shareholders.
