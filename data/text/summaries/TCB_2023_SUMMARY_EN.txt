### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Against a challenging macroeconomic backdrop in 2023, Vietnam Technological and Commercial Joint Stock Bank’s (Techcombank - TCB) total operating income (TOI) and profit before tax (PBT) contracted by 1.2% and 10.5% YoY, respectively. Notwithstanding these headwinds, the bank demonstrated key strategic bright spots, underpinned by concerted efforts to diversify fee-based income. Specifically, net fee income (NFI) expanded by 9.5% YoY, bolstered by an overhauled life insurance distribution strategy that propelled Annualized Premium Equivalent (APE) back to the #1 market ranking for a three-month period (September–November 2023), alongside the scaling of foreign exchange (FX), cash management, and corporate finance solutions. The Wholesale Banking (WB) segment served as a core growth engine, delivering a spectacular >60% YoY surge in non-real estate loan balances, while segment fee income recorded robust 60%+ growth compared to 2022. This outperformance was spearheaded by sectors such as utilities, tourism and leisure, fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), and financial services. Furthermore, Techcombank onboarded a record 2.6 million new-to-bank customers (more than double the 2022 acquisition rate), expanding its total customer base to over 13.4 million while defending a CASA ratio approaching the 40% threshold as of December 31, 2023.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2023, Techcombank maintained its industry-leading capital buffer, reporting a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 14.4%. Credit growth and deposit mobilization printed at 19.2% and 26.9% respectively, outpacing system-wide averages. Regarding the funding mix and capital structure, CASA balances sustained a steady upward trajectory across three consecutive quarters, facilitating cost-of-fund (COF) optimization. Capital allocation strategies prioritized credit portfolio diversification to optimize risk-adjusted returns and long-term sustainability, while maintaining strict adherence to regulatory limits and safety ratios stipulated in Circular 22/2019/TT-NHNN. Credit extension and funding activities were governed rigorously under an advanced risk management framework compliant with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) mandates and international best practices, thereby mitigating liquidity pressures and interest rate risk exposure.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Techcombank’s asset quality remained tightly contained in 2023, with credit costs anchored at a low 0.8%. The Non-Performing Loan (NPL) coverage ratio reached a healthy 102%, underpinned by large corporate NPL ratios hovering near 0%—a testament to disciplined risk governance and prudent project finance underwriting (in the real estate sector, the bank selectively financed only projects with robust legal provenance in prime locations). For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, the bank fully provisioned 100% of the additional specific reserves required for borrowers with restructured debt repayment schedules, interest exemptions, and reductions under Circular 03 and Circular 02. Credit risk management, general provisioning, and specific provisioning were executed in strict compliance with Circular 11/2021/TT-NHNN, ensuring robust loss-absorption capacity against macroeconomic volatility and corporate bond market dislocations.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Techcombank demonstrated a decisive commitment to sustainable development through substantial capital allocation toward sustainable infrastructure projects in Vietnam, spanning green mobility, clean energy generation, agriculture, textiles, electric vehicles, and green construction. Operationally, the bank officially inaugurated two state-of-the-art, internationally certified green-energy headquarters in Hanoi and Ho. Chi Minh City, while actively championing a sustainable lifestyle through its long-standing title sponsorship of the Hanoi and HCMC Marathons.
- **Social (S):** Placing human capital at its core, the bank cultivated a high-performing workforce exceeding 11,600 employees. Techcombank stands as the first and only bank in Vietnam to be certified as a "Great Place to Work" (Large Enterprise category). Additionally, the bank partnered with the community to champion social impact initiatives, delivering a record close to VND 150 billion in charitable contributions throughout 2023.
- **Governance (G):** Techcombank maintained a corporate governance architecture strictly aligned with domestic legal frameworks (including the Securities Law, Enterprise Law, Law on Credit Institutions, and regulatory circulars from the SBV and State Securities Commission of Vietnam - SSC) alongside international benchmarks. The bank instituted a transparent delegation of authority framework to delineate accountability and prevent conflicts of interest via its Internal Governance Regulation and a robust matrix of Board-level and Management-level committees. The deployment of digital transformation, a proprietary "Data Brain", and internal control standards aligned with Circular 13/2018/TT-NHNN further reinforced institutional transparency and operational efficiency across all business verticals.
