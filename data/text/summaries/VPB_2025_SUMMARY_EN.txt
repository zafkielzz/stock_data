### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, VPBank’s business operations demonstrated a robust breakthrough, reflected in a consolidated profit before tax of VND 30,625 billion, achieving 121% of the General Meeting of Shareholders' target. Core growth momentum was driven by the scaled expansion of the entire ecosystem, underscored by a 54.08% surge in total resource mobilization measured by annual share price volatility, alongside a 35.6% increase in total ecosystem mobilization propelled by product diversification and client base expansion. Subsidiary business segments delivered distinct strategic milestones: VPBankS ascended to the tier of leading securities firms via an industry-record IPO valued at over VND 12,700 billion; FE CREDIT posted profitability for the second consecutive year on the back of comprehensive restructuring; OPES outperformed its business targets; and the integration of new subsidiary GPBank yielded profitability immediately following its inaugural year of restructuring. Furthermore, consolidated total assets officially crossed the milestone of VND 1 quadrillion mid-year and reached VND 1.26 quadrillion by year-end, solidifying its pole position among private joint-stock commercial banks without state capital in Vietnam.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Regarding resource mobilization capacity and capital architecture, VPBank continued to reinforce its solid reputation in the international capital markets through medium- and long-term funding facilities totaling USD 2.36 billion, arranged and underwritten by premier global financial institutions. The bank maintained a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) among the industry leaders while successfully completing the transfer of a 15% charter capital stake to strategic shareholder Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC) of Japan, elevating equity to nearly VND 140,000 billion. The backing of strategic partner SMBC and a tightly consolidated capital structure enabled the bank to optimize its cost of capital, mitigate cash flow pressures, and establish a robust runway for the acceleration phase of its fourth 5-year strategic development plan.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
VPBank’s asset quality was rigorously controlled amidst scale expansion, with the consolidated non-performing loan (NPL) ratio under Circular 31 maintained below 3%. In risk governance, the bank finalized and reinforced its operational risk management framework, deploying synchronized tools such as loss data collection and analysis, Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), and scenario analysis. This significantly reduced the volume of operational risk events recorded during the year, with no material impacts arising. Concurrently, VPBank scaled up its Group-wide Operational Risk Insurance Program and prepared the roadmap for adopting the Standardised Measurement Approach (SMA) under revised Basel III standards, while expanding third-party risk management to ensure business continuity.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** VPBank pioneered the integration of ESG risk management into its overarching governance framework. The bank implemented three core pillars: integrating climate risk factors into its risk appetite framework when setting credit limits for the coal-fired thermal power sector; developing and applying ESG risk measurement tools such as scenario analysis and stress testing; and strengthening environmental and social (E&S) risk management in credit extension via the Environmental and Social Risk Management System (ESMS). For the first time, the bank published a standalone Sustainability Report and secured third prize at the Vietnam Listed Company Awards (VLCA) 2025.
- **Social (S):** The bank maintained heavy investments in human capital, sustaining robust welfare and career development policies to maximize the capabilities of its nearly 30,000-strong workforce. Pursuing its mission of "Mental Prosperity," VPBank executed distinctive cultural programs to build an attractive workplace environment, attract and retain talent, and tie business operations to social and community responsibilities.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adhered to high standards of transparency and discipline. Remuneration data for non-executive Board of Directors (BOD) members and the Board of Management was transparently disclosed in the Notes to the Financial Statements and the Bank’s Charter. VPBank maintained a close coordination mechanism with strategic shareholder SMBC, executed timely bilingual (Vietnamese-English) periodic disclosures (within 120 days of fiscal year-end), and proactively preserved transparent relations with regulatory authorities, shareholders, and equity analysts.
