### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Phat Dat Real Estate Development Corp (PDR) maintained a robust gross profit margin of 39.96%, reflecting strong operational efficiency in product segment selection and cost-of-goods-sold management. However, return metrics faced significant headwinds, with ROE and ROA settling at 0.46% and 0.21%, respectively, while trailing-twelve-months (TTM) EPS was recorded at VND 615.83. Core business momentum for the year prioritized a comprehensive restructuring program aimed at fortifying operational foundations, auditing governance systems, refining financial strategies, and streamlining the project pipeline, rather than pursuing short-term top-line scale expansion. The company’s strategic orientation focuses on developing products tailored to genuine market demand across core geographical markets, namely Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai. PDR has no export operations due to the domestic nature of the real estate sector, and future growth drivers are anchored in the 2026–2030 5-Year Plan, which targets aggregate revenue of approximately VND 44,848 billion and net income of approximately VND 11,812 billion.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PDR’s capital structure maintained a relatively prudent profile in FY2025, with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 48.05%. Within its asset and capital composition, the company reported notable long-term receivables, including a working capital loan extended to Commonwealth Properties Real Estate JSC at a fixed interest rate of 5.0% p.a. (maturing on April 3, 2027) secured by third-party collateral. Additionally, PDR maintains receivables from Saigon - KL Real Estate JSC pursuant to a debt settlement and equity transfer schedule, which is currently backed by third-party payment guarantees. Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks are mitigated through stringent financial discipline enforced under the restructuring program; nevertheless, the company must maintain a laser focus on the legal progress and liquidity profiles of its flagship projects to ensure timely debt servicing.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding risk management and provisioning, PDR has established a 100% doubtful debt provision amounting to VND 25,000,000,000 for a personal loan extended under Loan Agreement No. 01/2022/TTMT dated June 7, 2022. Regarding contingent liabilities and guarantees, receivables associated with the Saigon - KL project are backed by third-party collateral to mitigate credit risk. With respect to post-balance sheet events, the Board of Management confirms that, barring the disclosures set forth in Note 36 of the Consolidated Financial Statements, no material events occurred subsequent to December 31, 2025, that would exert a material impact requiring financial statement adjustments or ad-hoc disclosures, thereby upholding reporting stability and transparency in compliance with prevailing regulatory standards.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PDR’s 2025 Sustainability Report was prepared in reference to the GRI Standards 2021, IFC guidelines, and Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC. The company emphasizes the integration of environmental considerations into its decision-making processes, developing systematically planned urban projects oriented toward high-quality living spaces and long-term sustainability.
- **Social (S):** PDR upholds corporate social responsibility by aligning corporate growth with local community development. Key initiatives include active participation in infrastructure and social welfare projects, such as the expedited development of the Phan Dinh Phung Gymnasium landscape park in Ho Chi Minh City and the sponsorship for the construction of the Binh Chuan 3 Primary School as a model educational facility, thereby generating tangible value for resident communities.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to best practices and legal mandates. The Board of Directors comprises seasoned professionals, complemented by the effective operations of the Audit Committee (chaired by an Independent Board Member). The FY2025 financial statements were audited by UHY Auditing and Consulting Co., Ltd., ensuring a high degree of transparency and full compliance with stock market disclosure standards.
