### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Fiscal year 2021 marked a pivotal milestone for Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank - HOSE: CTG) as it successfully completed its charter capital augmentation from VND 37,234 billion to over VND 48,058 billion. This capital expansion serves as the core catalyst for bolstering financial capacity, broadening credit growth headroom, and accelerating digital transformation initiatives, spearheaded by the implementation of the new Core Banking system (SunShine). On the equity market, CTG demonstrated a resilient performance, delivering an annual total return of +28.04% to close the year at VND 20,500/share (compared to an opening price of VND 16,010/share), underpinned by robust market liquidity with an average matched trading volume of 13,308,962 shares per session.

Regarding subsidiary and affiliate operations, Indovina Bank (IVB—an associated commercial bank in which VietinBank holds an equity stake, possessing a charter capital of USD 193 million) delivered stable yet divergent operating results. IVB’s total assets inched up 2.7% year-on-year to VND 54.9 trillion, of which gross customer loans accounted for 60% of total assets, reaching VND 33.2 trillion (+0.7% YoY). However, IVB’s total operating income for 2021 stood at VND 3,150 billion; within this, net interest income (NII) contracted marginally by 0.4% YoY to VND 1,404 billion, while net fee income declined by 4.3% YoY to VND 61.5 billion. Bolstered by targeted cost-optimization initiatives, IVB’s total operating expenses (Opex) decreased by 5.2% to approximately VND 566 billion; nevertheless, pre-tax profit (PBT) retreated 13% YoY to VND 989.8 billion.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

VietinBank maintained a robust capital structure, with total shareholders' equity reaching VND 93,649,511,000,000 at the close of the fiscal year. The bank boasts an institutional shareholding structure characterized by high strategic alignment, anchored by cornerstone investors MUFG Bank (Japan), holding a 19.73% stake, and the International Finance Corporation (IFC), holding 10% of charter capital.

Regarding liquidity management with the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV), VietinBank rigorously adhered to statutory reserve requirements. The interest rate applied to VND compulsory reserves as of December 31, 2021, was 0.5% p.a. (down from 0.8% p.a. in 2020), while foreign currency reserves yielded 0% p.a. For foreign currency deposits exceeding the statutory reserve thresholds, the applicable remuneration rate was adjusted downward to 0% p.a., compared to 0.05% p.a. in the previous year. This adjustment mirrors the broader monetary easing cycle, necessitating active management of short-term liquidity pools by the treasury desk to optimize blended funding costs.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Asset quality containment remained a paramount objective amidst macroeconomic volatility. At its joint-venture affiliate IVB, the non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was tightly contained at 1.41%. At the parent-bank level, credit risk classification was rigorously enforced across Loans 1 through 5 for both corporate and retail credit exposures. The bank’s derivative financial instruments portfolio—comprising forward exchange contracts and cross-currency swaps—was actively deployed to hedge foreign exchange and interest rate risks on behalf of institutional and retail clients.

Regarding material corporate governance events toward year-end, the Board of Directors (BOD) appointed Mr. Dương Văn Quân as the Head of Corporate Governance on December 2, 2021, succeeding Mr. Đỗ Trọng Nghĩa. The executive board also underwent a key leadership transition, with Mr. Nguyễn Hoàng Dũng assuming the role of Deputy CEO in Charge of the Executive Board effective September 7, 2021, ensuring seamless continuity in operational resilience and risk management post-period.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

**Environmental (E):** VietinBank acts as a frontrunner in deploying energy and resource-efficiency standards, maintaining strict compliance with environmental protection regulations. Green finance principles are strategically integrated into underwriting standards, channeling capital toward environmentally sustainable and socially responsible projects to actualize its sustainable development mandate of "Forging the Future."

**Social (S):** Supported by an extensive footprint—comprising its Head Office in Hanoi, 155 branches spanning 63 provinces and municipalities, 958 transaction offices, alongside an international presence (2 branches in Germany, 1 subsidiary bank in Laos, and 1 representative office in Myanmar)—VietinBank ensures comprehensive financial inclusion for the broader community. The bank heavily invests in human capital development via the VietinBank Training and Human Resource Development School, safeguarding equitable workplace safety standards and competitive welfare packages for all employees.

**Governance (G):** Corporate governance is rigorously executed in alignment with international best practices, subjected to independent external audits by Ernst & Young Vietnam. Transparency is underscored by strict adherence to disclosure mandates regarding insider transactions (such as equity acquisitions executed by BOD member Ms. Trần Thu Huyền) pursuant to Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC. Furthermore, the aggregate remuneration disbursed to the BOD and the Supervisory Board in 2021 was capped at 0.16% of net profit after tax, comfortably below the maximum ceiling of 0.28% ratified by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM).
