### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, amidst macro volatility in the Vietnamese stock market and tight monetary policy in the first half of the year prior to a mild recovery, SSI Securities Corporation (SSI) delivered financial results exceeding its target. Net revenue reached VND 7,281 billion and profit before tax reached VND 2,849 billion, outperforming the General Meeting of Shareholders' target by 12.2%. Core growth drivers stemmed from agile capital allocation strategies and adept navigation of market inflection points. The Securities Services segment maintained its market-leading position with brokerage market shares of 10.44% on the HOSE and 7.73% on the HNX, contributing 43% of total revenue. This was driven by continuous upgrades to digital trading platforms (such as iBoard Pro and App Pro) and major promotional campaigns attracting both retail and institutional investors. The Treasury and Financial Services segment was the second-largest revenue contributor (42% of total revenue, reaching VND 3,133 billion), benefiting from the effective utilization of low-cost capital from domestic and international credit institutions to fund margin lending and investments in secure fixed-income instruments. The Investment segment delivered exceptional performance with VND 3,206 billion (44% of total revenue), where the equity portfolio remained focused on industry-leading enterprises with robust fundamentals, such as The PAN Group. The Fund Management segment (SSIAM) and Investment Banking Services continued to post stable growth, reinforcing SSI's comprehensive footprint across the financial market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
SSI’s capital structure remained prudent and optimized to support its business strategy. As of December 31, 2023, total assets stood at VND 69,241 billion (an increase of 32.6% compared to 2022), with equity reaching VND 23,241 billion. Total liabilities were recorded at VND 45,939 billion, accounting for 66.4% of total funding, primarily comprising short-term borrowings deployed for margin lending and investments in marketable securities. Loan interest rates were influenced by prevailing monetary market trends; the State Bank of Vietnam implemented four policy rate cuts in 2023 to support system liquidity, enabling SSI to access debt financing at reasonable costs. SSI's primary credit providers include leading domestic joint-stock commercial banks (such as VietinBank, BIDV, Vietcombank, and Agribank) alongside reputable international financial institutions from South Korea and Taiwan. SSI strictly complies with financial safety ratios; its liquidity coverage (capital adequacy) ratio throughout 2023 consistently maintained high levels ranging from 357% to 480% (well above the minimum 180% threshold mandated by the State Securities Commission of Vietnam - SSC), ensuring a resilient liquidity buffer against market shocks.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Risk management at SSI is rigorously executed through a "three lines of defense" model, exercising comprehensive control over market, credit, and liquidity risks. In 2023, SSI maintained full provisioning for the impairment of financial assets and bad debts in accordance with regulations, prudently reflecting asset quality amid volatility in the corporate bond and real estate markets during the first half of the year. Regarding contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet commitments, SSI maintained large-scale underwriting limits for covered warrants and margin trading commitments, which were closely monitored via real-time automated alert systems. Regarding events subsequent to the accounting period end (December 31, 2023), no extraordinary financial events or material legal disputes arose that could adversely impact the company's going-concern status or Consolidated Financial Statements.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
SSI continued to advance its sustainable development strategy, integrating ESG standards across all business operations:
- **Environment (E):** SSI proactively monitored and minimized its environmental footprint by optimizing electricity and water consumption at its headquarters and branch network. The company implemented internal awareness campaigns on environmental protection, promoted green, energy-efficient workspaces, and prioritized investment portfolios favoring enterprises with stringent environmental compliance.
- **Social (S):** The company places high emphasis on human capital development, maintaining a headcount of 1,637 employees as of year-end 2023 (of which 672 held professional securities practice certificates). SSI ensures a safe working environment, strictly complies with Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards, fosters gender equality, and provides comprehensive welfare packages (premium healthcare insurance, regular medical check-ups). The company invested heavily in training via its E-Learning platform, logging thousands of learning hours, while fulfilling its corporate social responsibility through charitable funds and community education and healthcare support totaling billions of VND.
- **Governance (G):** The company maintains a corporate governance framework aligned with international standards and Vietnamese regulatory requirements. The Board of Directors comprises highly qualified professionals, ensuring the required proportion of independent members to preserve objectivity in strategic oversight. Independent audits (conducted by Ernst & Young Vietnam) alongside Board-subcommittees (Audit Committee, Risk Management Committee, Development Strategy Committee) operate transparently and strictly adhere to disclosure principles under GRI (Global Reporting Initiative) standards and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
