### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, the Vietnamese stock market experienced some of the most adverse performances globally, with the VN-Index suffering its steepest decline since 2008. The market faced intense headwinds from global monetary tightening, inflation and foreign exchange risks, and domestic corporate bond market volatility. Despite unavoidable impacts from the macro environment and failing to meet initial targets, SSI Securities Corporation (SSI) demonstrated relative outperformance versus industry peers, underpinned by business acumen and adherence to its core principles. Specifically, FY2022 net revenue reached nearly VND 6,517 billion (down 16% YoY), while consolidated profit before tax (PBT) came in at VND 2,110 billion (down 37%). This performance was supported by contributions from core business segments: Securities Services, Investment, Treasury and Financial Services, Investment Banking, and Asset Management. Notably, 2022 marked a pivotal baseline for SSI's strategic transformation to enhance investor value through an AUM-driven approach, highlighted by the establishment of the Wealth Management Division.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
SSI maintained a robust internal financial profile, with total assets as of December 31, 2022, expanding 3% YoY to VND 52,226 billion, solidifying its top-tier ranking in the securities sector by asset scale. Short-term assets constituted a predominant 93.3% of the total asset composition (nearly VND 48,732 billion, up 5.7%). Within this, FVTPL financial assets surged 153.6% to VND 30,493 billion, heavily weighted toward cash and certificates of deposit (reaching VND 16,437 billion, a 2.4x increase) and highly rated major bank bonds to optimize yields while preserving liquidity. Conversely, credit-based assets (margin lending and advances from sales) contracted sharply by 53.3% to VND 11,057 billion amid a shrinking market demand. On the funding side, the company incurred short-term borrowings from major commercial banks—including BIDV, Vietcombank, and VietinBank—alongside other institutions to supplement working capital. Certain foreign-currency-denominated unsecured loans reached USD 228 million at year-end, partially hedged via foreign exchange forward contracts with banks, with the residual exposure remeasured at prevailing spot exchange rates.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Anchored by a commitment to rigorous and transparent risk management, SSI maintained ample liquidity buffers across its core business segments throughout 2022, adequately supporting investment demand with zero non-performing loans (NPLs) reported. The company executed provisions and reversals for impairment losses on financial assets (such as loans and AFS financial assets) and provisions for doubtful debts in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards. In covered warrant operations, SSI remained one of the three most active market makers while maintaining an appropriate hedging portfolio. Regarding post-balance sheet events, entering early 2023, the market continued to grapple with pressures stemming from international banking turmoil and the fallout from the domestic corporate bond market, necessitating strict adherence to operational discipline and elevated risk management frameworks at SSI.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** SSI is strategically aligned with sustainable development, integrating business operations with support for corporate clients transitioning toward ESG standards. A prime example within Investment Banking is SSI's successful advisory on the issuance of preference shares to DEG (the German Development Finance Institution under KfW, which specializes in supporting enterprises meeting ESG standards with a focus on renewable energy), thereby fostering clean energy projects and green growth in Vietnam.
- **Social (S):** The company’s workforce of 1,600 employees is viewed as its most valuable asset in driving client success and capital market development. SSI prioritizes human capital development, employee training, and comprehensive welfare benefits, while actively contributing to the State Budget (reaching VND 1,586 billion in 2022) and maintaining corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** SSI's corporate governance practices are progressively being refined to align with regional and international best practices, benchmarked against the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard by the State Securities Commission (SSC) and the International Finance Corporation (IFC). SSI excels across four core pillars: shareholders' rights, equitable treatment of shareholders, the role of stakeholders, and transparent disclosures. The Board of Directors operates in strict compliance with the law, working alongside the Audit Committee (comprising independent board members) to provide independent oversight and review of all company operations.
