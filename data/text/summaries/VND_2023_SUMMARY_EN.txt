Below is the institutional-grade translation of the 2023 Annual Report qualitative summary for VNDIRECT (VND):

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
In 2023, VND reported a pre-tax profit of VND 2,482 billion, exceeding its target by 124%, despite a contraction in broader market liquidity (average trading value on the HOSE declined by 13%). Core growth drivers shifted from traditional brokerage to proprietary trading and capital deployment. Financial assets recognized at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL) served as the primary growth engine, generating VND 2,813 billion—a sharp 127.2% year-on-year increase—supported by an optimized portfolio strategy in a volatile interest rate environment. Conversely, the brokerage segment experienced revenue pressure, declining 29% to VND 867 billion, reflecting cautious retail investor sentiment. Market recovery in the second half of 2023 fully offset early-year headwinds, propelling VND's share price up by an impressive 64.27% for the year.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VND maintained a prudent capital structure, with a debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio of 152.4% as of December 31, 2023 (down slightly from 166.6% in 2022). Total liabilities predominantly consist of short-term borrowings (VND 20,468 billion, accounting for 81.1% of total liabilities), indicating a heavy reliance on working capital from major commercial banks, including VietinBank, Vietcombank, BIDV, and Agribank. Although borrowing costs adjusted downward in alignment with the SBV's monetary easing policy, interest expense pressures persist as margin loan balances (reaching VND 10,286 billion, up 13.5%) accounted for 24.6% of total assets. Floating interest rate risk remains a significant challenge, compelling VND to maintain a high liquidity buffer (cash and cash equivalents surged 97.8% to VND 4,862 billion) to ensure solvency.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
VND faced credit risk exposure within its corporate bond portfolio following the real estate market disruptions of 2022. By the end of 2023, the company had proactively set aside specific provisions of VND 42.597 billion and general provisions of VND 76.781 billion. A substantial reduction in the unlisted bond portfolio (from VND 9,167 billion down to VND 7,546 billion) underscores management's efforts to mitigate non-performing loan (NPL) risks. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, VND focuses on managing underwriting and margin trading risks via an independent "three lines of defense" model. No material legal disputes were recorded subsequent to December 31, 2023; however, the impending corporate bond maturities of real estate developers in 2024 remain a latent variable that could weigh on asset quality if not managed proactively.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** VND advanced its digital transformation via eKYC platforms and process digitization (iLead), cutting paper-based workflows by 70%. The company also actively promoted energy-saving initiatives across its offices and fostered a "green living" mindset (Gobio) within its workforce.
- **Social (S):** The company maintained a workforce of 1,174 employees with a balanced gender ratio (53% female, 47% male). Internal training programs (IPAM Retreat) were prioritized, recording 3,504 training participations aimed at leadership development and employee welfare.
- **Governance (G):** The governance framework was standardized through four organizational mapping tools (FMAP, CMAP, PMAP, GMAP). The Board of Directors ensures transparency, supported by the participation of independent members. The company remains compliant with the State Securities Commission's (SSC) disclosure regulations and is progressively aligning with international ESG reporting standards to enhance its appeal to foreign institutional investors.
