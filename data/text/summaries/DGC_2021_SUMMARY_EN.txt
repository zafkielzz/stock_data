Below is the institutional equity research translation of the qualitative financial analysis for Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC) for FY2021, based on verified annual report data and actual figures:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC) delivered a strong breakout performance despite the complex evolution of the COVID-19 pandemic. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 9,550 billion, up 53% YoY, while profit after tax surged 165% YoY to VND 2,513 billion, exceeding the AGM’s target by 128%. The core drivers stem from plants operating at full capacity, alongside the commercial operation of the Apatit mine – Mining Area 25, which significantly lowered input costs and enhanced production efficiency. Additionally, favorable global market volatility boosted demand for the Group's core products. Regarding the geographical revenue breakdown, export revenue reached VND 6,470 billion (accounting for 67.7%) and domestic revenue stood at VND 3,080 billion (accounting for 32.3%). The high proportion of export revenue allows DGC to maintain robust USD cash balances. This serves as an effective natural hedge, providing the Company with proactive foreign currency sources for import settlements while insulating it from FX risks when borrowing USD for daily operational activities.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DGC’s financial structure maintains a high degree of safety, characterized by low debt-to-total-assets and debt-to-equity ratios on a consolidated basis (0.26x and 0.35x, respectively). Total short-term debt as of December 31, 2021, was recorded at VND 2,188 billion, while long-term debt was negligible (VND 100 million). Key banking partners extending major credit facilities to the Group include Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - Transaction Office), HSBC, and Shinhan Bank. Short-term borrowing rates for VND-denominated loans ranged from 3.85% to 7.1% per annum, whereas USD-denominated loans benefited from preferential rates between 1.15% and 2.6% per annum. Supported by substantial cash and bank deposits (including short-term time deposits exceeding VND 3,631 billion), DGC faces minimal cash flow pressure, with healthy current and quick ratios of 2.7x and 2.1x, respectively.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
The Company maintains a prudent risk management policy. Ending inventory stood at VND 1,386 billion (up 98.9% YoY); however, the Group did not record any provision for inventory obsolescence as products enjoy stable off-take and their net realizable value exceeds historical cost. Short-term trade receivables are tightly controlled with a low non-performing loan (NPL) ratio. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent liabilities, the Group holds long-term operating lease commitments (total minimum lease payment obligations of nearly VND 492.18 billion) and unexecuted capital commitments for construction-in-progress of fixed assets reaching VND 362.33 billion. Furthermore, the Group is subject to environmental restoration and rehabilitation obligations for the Apatit ore mine at Mining Area 25 pursuant to Decision No. 1057/QĐ-BTNMT (with total estimated costs of over VND 8 billion deposited periodically in escrow). No adverse legal disputes or material abnormal financial events occurred after the accounting period ended (December 31, 2021).

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DGC focuses on optimizing energy efficiency in phosphorus production, capturing and utilizing 100% of the exhaust gases from its phosphorus plants to directly reduce electricity consumption and thermal emissions into the environment. The Group operates a closed-loop wastewater treatment system achieving a water recycling rate of 90-95%, while deploying circular economy initiatives and afforestation programs across industrial zones to minimize its carbon footprint.
- **Social (S):** The Group ensures a safe working environment, strictly complying with labor protection regulations and organizing regular health check-ups for its 2,246 employees. Average employee income improved to VND 19 million/person/month (up 22.5% compared to 2020). The company maintains comprehensive welfare policies, scholarship funds, and housing support for long-tenured employees, while actively participating in social security and community charity activities with budgets scaling in the tens of billions of VND.
- **Governance (G):** DGC's governance apparatus operates under a standard joint-stock company model, overseen by the Supervisory Board and specialized sub-committees. The 2021 consolidated financial statements were independently audited by PwC Vietnam, receiving an unqualified (clean) opinion and ensuring full compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), enterprise accounting regimes, and transparent disclosure regulations on the stock market (HOSE: DGC).
