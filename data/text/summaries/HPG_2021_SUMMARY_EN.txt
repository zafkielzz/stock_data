### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2021 marked a record-breaking growth trajectory for Hoa Phat Group (HPG), directly reflected in the equity’s strong outperformance on the stock exchange. The share price surged +50.35% over the year (opening at VND 15,770, closing at VND 23,710, and peaking at VND 29,850), backed by robust liquidity with average matching trading volume reaching 25,467,485 shares per session. This momentum was underpinned by stellar financial results: net revenue reached VND 150,800 billion (+65% YoY, outperforming the full-year target by 26%), and net profit achieved a historic milestone of VND 34,520 billion (+156% YoY, exceeding the target by 92%).

Core growth was driven by the steel manufacturing and trading segment, the structural backbone of the Group. HPG capitalized adeptly on soaring early-year construction steel prices and a broader global economic recovery (+5.9% in 2021) to scale up international exports via the EVFTA and RCEP trade agreements, establishing Vietnam as Southeast Asia's largest net steel exporter.

Concurrently, the Group executed strategic segment restructuring. HPG completed the full divestment of its interior furnishings segment (transferring a 99.6% stake in Hoa Phat Interior to Eden Vietnam for VND 896.4 billion against a book value of VND 398.4 billion) and ancillary livestock units (Van Thang Phu Tho and Lang Son Livestock). Conversely, the Group officially established the Home Appliance Corporation in Q4 2021 to capture global manufacturing shifts and shape a sustainable multi-industry structure alongside its Agricultural and Real Estate divisions.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Reflecting the characteristics of a large-scale manufacturing enterprise, imported raw materials (iron ore, coking coal) accounted for 73% of Hoa Phat's total cost of goods sold in FY2021, rendering the Group's profit margins highly sensitive to USD/VND exchange rate fluctuations and borrowing costs. To optimize financial expenses and maintain ample working capital, HPG flexibly deployed derivative hedging instruments and balanced short- and long-term debt denominated in both VND and foreign currencies.

Notably, to secure credit facilities supporting manufacturing operations, Hoa Phat significantly increased its collateralized term deposits at banks, reaching VND 7,934.6 billion as of December 31, 2021 (surging from VND 46.1 billion at the beginning of the year). These deposits yielded interest rates ranging from 2.30% to 7.80% per annum. Additionally, the Group maintained VND 115 billion in term deposits pledged as collateral for Letter of Credit (L/C) credit facilities extended to its subsidiary, Hoa Phat Pre-stressed Concrete Steel Company.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

The primary external risk facing Hoa Phat's international trade operations in 2021 was the rising tide of protectionism and trade defense investigations in export markets, compounded by intense domestic competition from low-priced Chinese steel imports. Regarding internal risk management, the Group maintained stringent provisioning policies based on historical data for contingent liabilities, particularly product warranty expenses.

From an accounting perspective, because HPG's held-to-maturity financial instruments lack active secondary market quoted prices and Vietnam Accounting Standards (VAS) currently lack specific valuation guidance, the fair value of these deposits has not been detailed in the consolidated financial statements and may diverge from book values. Major restructuring events, such as the divestment of subsidiaries (interior furnishings and agriculture segments) executed right in Q1 2021, effectively unburdened the Group of significant non-core liabilities and assets ahead of the severe fourth wave of the Covid-19 pandemic.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

**Environmental (E):** Hoa Phat has initiated its sustainable development strategy with a strategic focus on emission reduction and the research of green steel manufacturing technologies. Although granular metrics for Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions or a definitive Net-Zero roadmap were not explicitly quantified in the FY2021 financial statements, the Group remains committed to deep capital investments in technical innovations designed to optimize energy efficiency and promote a circular economy model across its integrated iron and steel complexes.

**Social (S):** Corporate social responsibility served as a core highlight for HPG during the pandemic year. The Group safeguarded employee health and maintained stable employment for all 27,600 personnel by achieving a 100% double-dose Covid-19 vaccination rate and implementing stringent "3-on-site" production protocols. HPG contributed a record VND 12,500 billion to the state budget (+>70% YoY). Furthermore, the Group allocated over VND 160 billion to community philanthropic initiatives, including VND 150 billion donated to the National Vaccine Fund alongside pandemic-prevention medical equipment. The "Heartbeat of Love" program successfully funded cardiac surgeries for 83 underprivileged pediatric patients in 2021 (bringing the cumulative total to 320 assisted cases over 6 years of implementation).

**Governance (G):** Hoa Phat’s governance framework operates robustly under the oversight of the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and the Internal Audit Department. The Group strictly adheres to newly enacted regulatory frameworks effective January 1, 2021 (including the 2020 Enterprise Law, 2019 Securities Law, and 2020 Investment Law) and closely collaborates with reputable independent auditors to ensure the highest standards of transparency and accuracy for global shareholders and investors.
