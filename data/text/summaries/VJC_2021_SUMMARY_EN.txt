### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Entering its second consecutive year severely impacted by the Covid-19 pandemic, Vietjet Aviation Joint Stock Company (VJC) faced substantial operational headwinds; however, management dynamically pivoted its business model to ensure corporate survival. The core growth driver for the year stemmed from the cargo transportation segment, underpinned by a strategic focus on maximizing aircraft utilization during peak outbreak periods. Specifically, cargo operations delivered an impressive revenue growth rate of **200%**, reaching nearly **VND 3 trillion**. Concurrently, Thai VietJet emerged as the domestic market leader in passenger market share within Thailand by aggressively expanding its route network as vaccine rollouts gained traction. Across both domestic and international markets, Vietjet served as a critical trade artery, operating hundreds of timely rescue flights while bolstering its operational readiness by taking delivery of its first wide-body Airbus A330-300 aircraft to capture pent-up demand upon market normalization.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Amid liquidity pressures exerted by the pandemic, Vietjet proactively deployed flexible financial instruments to shore up its capital base, supporting its business expansion strategy and post-crisis recovery roadmap. On March 5, 2021, the Board of Directors approved a treasury share disposal plan executed between March 30, 2021, and April 28, 2021, generating **VND 2,349 billion** in proceeds to supplement working capital. Concurrently, the company augmented its debt financing through a private placement of corporate bonds, raising a total issuance value of **VND 8,373 billion**. This capital structure optimization preserved liquidity, securing vital resources to fund the wide-body fleet acquisition program and the domestic and international route network expansion.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vietjet remained exposed to idiosyncratic and macroeconomic risks driven by the protracted fallout of the Covid-19 pandemic on the global aviation sector. To mitigate these risks, the company maintained an online risk management and safety reporting system via Coruson software, alongside dedicated safety action teams to monitor and control identified hazards across ground operations, flight operations, and engineering. Regarding financial events and post-balance sheet developments, the company successfully executed its treasury share divestment plan in 1Q21 and 2Q21 to reinforce its cash position. Other major financial commitments were anchored by multi-hundred-million-dollar strategic agreements with premier international partners, including Rolls-Royce ($400 million for wide-body aircraft engine supply) and SR Technics ($150 million for technical maintenance services), laying the groundwork for post-pandemic operational scaling.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vietjet prioritized emissions management and operational efficiency through rigorous fuel consumption monitoring, spare parts management, in-flight consumable optimization, and environmental wastewater treatment. The company actively promoted environmental awareness while continuously upgrading its fleet with next-generation, fuel-efficient aircraft to align with aviation sector sustainability mandates.
- **Social (S):** Throughout 2021, Vietjet steadfastly executed a human-centric human resources strategy: **achieving zero layoffs while sustaining workload distribution**, focusing on training enhancement, CRM standardization, and operational discipline. The Vietjet Aviation Academy (VJAA) seamlessly transitioned to a digital learning platform, ensuring uninterrupted personnel deployment for examinations, certifications, and license renewals for pilots, cabin crew, engineers, and mechanics, thereby safeguarding workforce welfare and job security.
- **Governance (G):** The company upheld a transparent corporate governance framework underpinned by rigorous oversight from the Board of Directors and its specialized subcommittees, ensuring independence and strict compliance with disclosure regulations. Vietjet operated its Safety Management System (SMS) in strict adherence to standards set by the Civil Aviation Authority of Vietnam (CAAV) and international bodies such as IATA, AAPA, Airbus, Navblue, Ideagen, and SCS, facilitating comprehensive risk mitigation and enhanced corporate governance quality.
