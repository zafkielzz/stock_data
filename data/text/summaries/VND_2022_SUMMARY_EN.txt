**1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In 2022, VNDIRECT Securities Corporation (VND) reported financial results that were adversely impacted by macroeconomic volatility and the downturn of the Vietnamese stock market. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 6,829 billion, representing a modest increase of 13% compared to 2021; however, profit before tax (PBT) declined by 36.8% to VND 1,535 billion, and profit after tax (PAT) came in at VND 1,220 billion (down 37%). This contraction was primarily driven by a narrowing of overall market liquidity, as average trading value across the three exchanges dropped by 24.6% to VND 20,249 billion per session. Within the segment revenue breakdown, the brokerage and client services division generated VND 2,849 billion in net revenue, while proprietary trading contributed VND 3,293 billion, and capital management generated VND 399 billion in profit before tax. Brokerage revenue experienced downward pressure, falling 22.6% to VND 1,249 billion due to fierce competition for market share and shrinking market-wide liquidity.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Amid a tightening monetary policy environment and rising interest rates, VND proactively restructured its operations to optimize capital resources. Total short-term financial debt as of December 31, 2022, stood at VND 19,311 billion (a slight decrease of 5.56% year-on-year), comprising VND 13,370 billion in loans from domestic banks and VND 5,941 billion in international borrowings. The debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio improved significantly from 283.71% to 167.65%, maintaining regulatory capital safety (well below the maximum statutory limit of 5x). Major bank borrowings included credit lines from BIDV, VietinBank, VIB, and international financial institutions, subject to floating interest rate mechanisms adjusted in accordance with money market developments. Cash flow pressure and interest expenses increased substantially due to escalating deposit and lending rates in the second half of 2022.

**3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS**
VND maintained a prudent provisioning policy for financial assets and the margin lending portfolio. Total allowances for impairment of loan receivables as of December 31, 2022, reached VND 230.38 billion (comprising specific provisions of VND 161.21 billion and general provisions of VND 69.16 billion), reflecting a provisioning ratio of 1.84% against outstanding margin loans. Regarding the corporate bond portfolio, VND maintained strict control by keeping exposure below 70% of equity and applying the "5C" credit appraisal process to mitigate credit risk from the debt market. No unusual contingent liabilities or material legal disputes were recorded that would exert an adverse impact on the consolidated financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2022, as audited by Ernst & Young Vietnam.

**4. ESG EXECUTION**
- **Environmental (E):** As a financial services firm, VND's direct environmental impact remains low. The company proactively optimizes eco-friendly workspaces, prioritizing biodegradable and recycled materials, while implementing electricity, paper, and water conservation measures across its nationwide branch network.
- **Social (S):** The company strictly complies with labor regulations, implements comprehensive welfare policies, and prioritizes human resource development through the IPAM competency framework. Internal engagement programs and community-oriented philanthropic initiatives—particularly supporting underprivileged students in remote areas—are regularly maintained to promote shared social values.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework operates under an independent "three lines of defense" model, ensuring high standards of transparency and compliance. The Board of Directors comprises independent members with extensive expertise, working in close coordination with the Supervisory Board and specialized sub-committees. Independent audits are conducted by Ernst & Young, strictly adhering to Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) and information disclosure regulations set by securities market regulators.
