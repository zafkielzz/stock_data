Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the financial report summary for Vinhomes JSC (VHM):

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, Vinhomes completed and published its Annual Report in full compliance with stock market disclosure regulations, reinforcing its leading position in Vietnam's residential real estate development sector. The company’s core business drivers continued to stem from the handover of large-scale mega-urban projects deployed previously, namely Vinhomes Ocean Park, Vinhomes Smart City, and Vinhomes Grand Park. 

Despite market headwinds and challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic, the core real estate development and transfer segment sustained its cash flow and business performance supported by a flexible combination of wholesale and retail sales strategies, alongside disciplined construction and on-schedule handover velocities. Regarding the addressable market, the residential real estate sector primarily catered to robust domestic housing demand from the domestic middle and upper classes, while the commercial and integrated service products within these mega-townships continued to attract long-term capital investment. The rise in per capita income and rapid urbanization across major cities remained solid structural pillars driving high-quality housing demand, contributing a substantial share to the group's consolidated revenue and profit for the 2021 financial year.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinhomes' capital structure and treasury management maintained flexibility throughout 2021 to secure adequate financial resources for the execution of mega-projects requiring tens of trillions of dong in capital expenditure. To optimize the cost of capital amid financial market volatility, the company deployed a mix of short-term and long-term borrowings from major domestic commercial banks alongside other professional fundraising channels. 

Borrowings comprised both fixed and floating interest rates, structured in alignment with cash inflows generated from real estate sales launches and handovers. The primary credit-providing financial partners included leading Vietnamese joint-stock commercial banks, securing long-term credit lines on competitive terms. Capital structure risks were closely managed by the board of directors through maintaining a safe debt-to-equity ratio, ensuring debt service capabilities for maturing liabilities, and mitigating short-term cash flow pressures even amidst periods of tightening in the real estate credit market.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Vinhomes’ financial risk management was executed prudently through strict provisioning policies for inventory and doubtful debts, accurately reflecting a conservative approach under prevailing accounting standards. Provisions for inventory write-downs (for land banks and projects under construction) and short-term doubtful debt provisions were periodically assessed based on actual recoverability and market fluctuations. 

Regarding off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments and contingent debt obligations, the company tightly controlled financial support commitments and loan guarantees extended to homebuyers and related partners in compliance with statutory regulations. Throughout 2021 and subsequent to December 31, no material legal disputes or litigations capable of exerting a major adverse impact on the enterprise's financial standing were recorded, nor did any extraordinary financial events outside the ordinary course of business materialize.

- **Environmental (E):** Vinhomes actively deployed sustainable development initiatives through its smart mega-city model, focusing on optimizing energy consumption, achieving compliance standards in wastewater and waste management, expanding green spaces and water surfaces within urban areas to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, with a trajectory toward long-term emission reduction targets), and promoting energy-saving solutions across public spaces.
- **Social (S):** The company prioritized fostering a professional working environment, ensuring occupational health and safety (OHS) standards for employees, promoting gender equality, and maintaining competitive compensation policies. Regular professional training programs and comprehensive welfare benefits were systematically rolled out, while the group consistently fulfilled its corporate social responsibility through large-scale community and social welfare initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework strictly adhered to Vietnamese securities regulations for large-scale public companies, ensuring the independence of the Board of Directors, financial information transparency via independent audits conducted by top-tier auditing firms, and the progressive adoption of international standards on sustainable governance aligned with the GRI framework and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
