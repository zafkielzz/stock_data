### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2023 marked a stellar transformation for FPT Corporation, with net revenue reaching VND 52,618 billion and profit before tax (PBT) hitting VND 9,203 billion, representing robust year-on-year (YoY) growths of 19.6% and 20.1%, respectively. The core growth driver stemmed from the overseas IT Services segment, which crossed the USD 1 billion revenue milestone for the first time. This achievement was propelled by an aggressive pivot toward high-value-added services, notably Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Automotive Software (FPT Automotive). Revenue from AI and Cloud surged an impressive 70% YoY to VND 421 billion. 

Conversely, the domestic IT Services segment faced substantial headwinds from constrained enterprise and public sector IT spending amid a challenging macro environment, resulting in a modest revenue of VND 7,161 billion (+4.6% YoY) and a slight 2.5% contraction in PBT to VND 380 billion. Nevertheless, the "Made by FPT" product ecosystem acted as a resilient structural cushion, delivering a 42.3% surge in revenue to VND 1,637 billion, underpinned by a strategic shift from project-based delivery to platform-as-a-service (PaaS) models. The Telecommunications division maintained its defensive appeal, with broadband internet subscribers reaching the 4-million milestone and the Data Center segment posting growth exceeding 30%. The Education division continued its long-term defensive footprint expansion, establishing a presence across 20 provinces and serving a sizable converted student body, thereby cultivating a vital talent pipeline of high-tech and semiconductor human resources for the next secular growth cycle.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

FPT’s capital structure was optimized to flexibly finance capacity expansion and working capital requirements. Short-term borrowings primarily comprised unsecured bank loans utilized for short-term working capital needs, bearing interest rates ranging from 0% to 8.0% p.a., down slightly from 0.45% - 8.8% in the previous year. Unsecured long-term borrowings were channeled toward financing key capital expenditures, such as the Hoa Lac 3 Project, the District 9 Data Center, and the FPT University campus in Da Nang, carrying interest rates between 0% and 6.75% p.a. (a decrease from the 7.2% ceiling observed in FY2022). 

Foreign exchange risk on foreign currency-denominated principal debt (USD and JPY) was rigorously managed through a dual-hedging framework: first, derivative financial instruments including foreign exchange swaps and forward contracts; and second, a robust natural currency hedge derived from substantial software export cash inflows. The group’s cash flow pressure remained minimal, underpinned by ample cash, cash equivalents, and short- to long-term bank deposits, complemented by steady cash dividend streams from large-scale associated entities such as Synnex FPT and FPT Retail.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

FPT enforces a prudent provisioning policy to safeguard balance sheet integrity. Inventory write-downs were primarily concentrated in the technology distribution and retail segments, which remain inherently sensitive to fluctuations in consumer purchasing power. The short-term trade receivables portfolio exhibits well-dispersed counterparty risk; key corporate clients accounting for over 2% of total receivables are highly reputable institutions, including Vietcombank, LG Electronics, Petronas Digital, Cox Automotive, and the National Board of Revenue in Bangladesh. Bad debts were fully provisioned in compliance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS). 

Regarding financial investments, FPT recorded fair values based on closing market prices as of December 31, 2023, for listed/publicly traded equities such as TPBank and FPT Securities (FPTS). Investments in unlisted entities were carried at book value due to the absence of detailed VAS valuation guidelines; however, no indication of material impairment was identified. The financial statements reflected no high-risk off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments or material litigation that could structurally alter the company’s financial profile post-balance-sheet date.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

FPT reinforced its sustainability leadership in Vietnam, standing out as the sole technology constituent in the VNSI 20 index (HoSE) and earning a Silver rating for sustainable business practices from EcoVadis. 

*   **Environmental (E):** The group actively championed green office initiatives and energy optimization across its data centers via centralized data governance systems and 42 internal digital transformation projects, curtailing operational expenditures while indirectly mitigating its carbon footprint.
*   **Social (S):** FPT generated 48,162 jobs (up 13.6% YoY) within a "Great Place to Work"-certified environment, boasting an 87% positive employee feedback score. The voluntary attrition rate across the group dropped sharply by 4 percentage points compared to FY2022. Furthermore, FPT allocated VND 205.2 billion to corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, continually expanding its "happy school" model across 20 provinces nationwide.
*   **Governance (G):** The corporation strictly adhered to best corporate governance practices under the guidance of the Board of Directors in collaboration with the Vietnam Institute of Directors (VIOD). FPT’s Investor Relations (IR) transparency was widely recognized by both retail and institutional investor communities as a top-tier large-cap benchmark.
