**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2021, amidst complex COVID-19 developments and prolonged lockdown measures, Novaland Group (NVL) demonstrated resilience by turning adversity into opportunity, successfully meeting its core business targets as committed to shareholders and investors. The Group's primary growth driver stemmed from the stellar execution of its pre-sales targets in the residential property segment. Impressive M&A activities expanded the total land bank under research and development from 5,000 hectares to 10,600 hectares. Flagship projects maintained rapid construction momentum, progressively commissioning large-scale utility infrastructure to establish new destinations for regional tourism. The corporate strategy remains anchored in the mid-to-high-end real estate segments—comprising urban properties in Ho Chi Minh City and adjacent provinces, alongside tourism-centric urban developments in high-potential localities. Additionally, the Group accelerated the R&D of industrial real estate land banks in key provinces such as Ba Ria - Vung Tau, Dong Nai, and Long An.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Reflected through multi-year solvency metrics, Novaland maintained a sound financial profile characterized by a risk-averse debt restructuring toward an increased weight of long-term debt and a reduction in short-term obligations. Specifically, the current ratio remained stable at 3.17x compared to 3.63x in FY2020, while the quick ratio improved positively from 0.87x to 0.93x. The consistent maintenance of these liquidity ratios underscores the Group’s robust capital structure management and reinforces its credibility and commitment to shareholders, investors, and creditors. Looking ahead to the next cycle, the Group aims to further intensify capital market activities domestically and internationally through strategic partnerships with major financial institutions to secure capital for its expansion strategy.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
To mitigate risks and safeguard system-wide efficacy, Novaland’s operating procedures are subject to continuous review and iterative enhancements aimed at identifying and preempting latent risks. Internal control and internal audit functions are rigorously deployed with a materiality-based risk focus, enforcing regulatory compliance, reviewing related-party transactions, and overseeing the preparation and presentation of financial statements. The Group’s FY2021 financial statements were independently audited by PwC (Vietnam) Ltd., ensuring consistency, transparency, and full compliance with prevailing accounting standards and capital market disclosure practices.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** The Group prioritizes enhanced governance to manage environmental footprints and optimize resource efficiency. Novaland is evaluating the deployment of smart-building solutions, integrating novel construction technologies tailored to individual projects, and championing energy-efficient practices to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions and foster sustainable development.
- **Social (S):** Executive Management remains steadfast in its commitment to human capital development, supported by flexible short-, medium-, and long-term remuneration frameworks. Compensation and welfare policies are strategically calibrated to maintain market competitiveness, attract top-tier talent, and foster organizational cohesion. Furthermore, the Group contributed over VND 320 billion to the community through high-impact social security initiatives, including localized workforce training scholarships, regional investment and tourism promotion, vaccine fund contributions, zero-dong supermarket chains, and afforestation programs.
- **Governance (G):** Novaland adopts advanced corporate governance models, seamlessly integrating sustainability dimensions across all operational facets. The annual report is structured as an integrated report adhering to international frameworks, including the International Integrated Reporting Council (IIRC) standards, the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard, GRI sustainability reporting standards, and OECD Corporate Governance Principles. A robust internal control system, specialized board committees, and enhanced information transparency are continually prioritized to maximize the legitimate rights and interests of shareholders and stakeholders.
