### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2023, Hoa Phat Group (HPG) recorded a robust market recovery with its share price appreciating by +52.75%, closing at VND 18,880/share, accompanied by an average matching liquidity of over 23.4 million shares per session. This price action reflected market expectations despite underlying earnings facing severe headwinds: consolidated net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company contracted to VND 6,835 billion (compared to VND 8,484 billion in 2022). 

The steel manufacturing and trading segment remained the core driver dictating the group's entire value chain. However, segment margins were heavily compressed due to volatile raw material costs (iron ore, coking coal, and scrap steel), which accounted for 70–75% of production costs. Notably, the Russia-Ukraine conflict triggered a 5x to 7x surge in coal prices during the early months of the year, exerting immense cost pressures upstream. 

To optimize its portfolio, in November 2023, Hoa Phat liquidated 100% of its equity interest in three subsidiaries—Hoa Phat Foreign Investment Joint Stock Company, Hoa Phat Trading International Pte Ltd., and Dragon Trading and Investment Pty Ltd.—with transfer values of VND 220,000 million, VND 24,050 million, and VND 24,065 million, respectively. This corporate action streamlined HPG's operating structure and reallocated resources toward key export markets currently confronting increasingly complex trade defense barriers in the EU, the US, and Asian nations.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Hoa Phat's capital structure was significantly influenced by global monetary tightening cycles and foreign exchange risks. Given that imported raw materials accounted for over 70% of total COGS in 2023, net raw material imports exposed the group to substantial USD/VND exchange rate volatility. To hedge against interest rate risks, the group dynamically adjusted its debt portfolio. 

On the asset side, HPG optimized short-term cash flow via term deposits yielding annual interest rates ranging from 3.35% to 11.5%. As of December 31, 2023, the group had pledged VND 5,436 billion in term deposits as collateral for credit facilities (up sharply from just VND 5.7 billion at the beginning of the year). 

Concurrently, liquidity and debt pressures were mitigated by encumbering other current assets: short-term trade receivables valued at VND 299 billion and inventories reaching up to VND 24,178 billion (a sharp increase from VND 15,603 billion as of January 1, 2023) were utilized as collateral for borrowings from partner banks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
HPG’s accounting conservatism was clearly demonstrated by recognizing VND 20,212 billion of inventory at Net Realizable Value (NRV), with inventory devaluation provisions charged directly to cost of goods sold. 

Current legal risks are primarily concentrated in the real estate segment, specifically the Technical Infrastructure Project of Subzone A – North National Highway 5 Urban Area (Hung Yen) developed by a subsidiary. This project currently records VND 1,326 billion in work-in-progress production costs and VND 303 billion in other short-term receivables related to site clearance compensation costs yet to be validated by state authorities. 

On December 28, 2023, the Government Inspectorate issued Conclusion No. 3136/KL-TTCP recommending that the People's Committee of Hung Yen Province revoke Official Letter No. 1488 regarding the allocation of the project to HPG's subsidiary and mandating a competitive bidding process to re-select the developer. This constitutes a material legal and contingent liability risk, although as of the reporting date, the group had not received any formal enforcement decision from the province. 

Additionally, loans extended to individual partners (at 5%–6% interest p.a.) were offset against long-term farm lease payments. HPG confirmed that no material events occurred subsequent to December 31, 2023, that would require adjustments to the financial statements.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** HPG proactively adapts to the EU’s Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) and its Net-Zero 2050 commitment by making substantial capital expenditures in circular economy technologies. Environmental capital expenditure accounts for up to 30% of total fixed asset investments across projects. The Dung Quat Iron and Steel Complex has attained ISO 14001:2015 certification. The group self-generates over 80% of its electricity demand for production utilizing waste heat recovery and top-gas pressure recovery turbines (CDQ dry coke quenching and BPRT turbine technologies). HPG is also researching roadmaps to adopt green steel technologies such as Direct Reduced Iron (DRI), Near Net Shape casting, and Carbon Capture.
- **Social (S):** With a workforce of nearly 29,000 young employees, HPG focuses on mitigating staff turnover by restructuring vertical management tiers from the Group down to its subsidiaries. HPG has standardized its employee grading system to ensure competitive remuneration and partnered with technical universities and colleges to develop high-quality human resources.
- **Governance (G):** HPG's financial statements are independently audited by KPMG, ensuring a high degree of transparency. The accounting books and records are systematically maintained to furnish data for international regulatory authorities in trade defense investigations, ensuring consistency and strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards.
