1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In FY2024, Bao Viet Holdings (BVH) demonstrated robust equity market outperformance, delivering an annual price appreciation of +31.34% (closing at VND 49,700/share compared to an opening price of VND 37,840/share, within a trading range of VND 36,590 to VND 54,610/share) alongside an average daily trading volume of 595,066 shares per session. BVH’s core growth drivers stem from maintaining its undisputed market-leading position across four primary operational pillars: Life Insurance (Bao Viet Life Corporation), Non-Life Insurance (Bao Viet Insurance Corporation), Fund Management (Bao Viet Fund Management Company Limited - BVF), and Securities (Bao Viet Securities Joint Stock Company - BVSC).

The Group has propelled its growth model through a "Dual Transformation" strategy (Digital Transformation and Green Transformation). Cross-selling synergies between the life and non-life segments—aimed at generating comprehensive, integrated financial-insurance solutions—play a pivotal role in expanding market share. Furthermore, enhancing investment efficiency has optimized yield generation for clients and shareholders alike, establishing a solid foundation for achieving the strategic targets set for the 2021–2030 period.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Bao Viet maintains a flexible capital structure designed to accommodate working capital requirements and system-wide development capital expenditure. Short-term bank borrowings feature floating and fixed interest rates ranging from 3.00% to 6.50% p.a., deployed to supplement operational liquidity, execute certificates of deposit transactions, and invest in government bonds. Short-term credit lines are concentrated among Tier-1 banking partners, including Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank), Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank), Maritime Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MSB), and Ho Chi Minh City Development Joint Stock Commercial Bank (HDBank). These debt facilities are secured by cash-collateralized deposit contracts or structured on an unsecured basis.

Regarding long-term debt, the Group maintains a credit facility with Vietcombank at interest rates ranging from 5.35% to 5.55% p.a., secured by time deposits to finance key information technology capital expenditure projects. Additionally, Bao Viet engages in reverse repurchase agreements (repos) for government bonds with maturities under 12 months with counterparties such as BaoViet Bank, Southeast Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (SeABank), and MSB. The Group's financial standing and capital structure continue to receive robust backing from strategic shareholder Sumitomo Life, particularly in reinforcing managerial capacity and long-term strategic positioning.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

As a large-scale financial-insurance conglomerate, Bao Viet’s operational risk profile is inextricably linked to the robust governance of its extensive technical insurance reserves. The Group maintains full provisioning for technical reserves across both life and non-life segments in strict compliance with regulatory frameworks, encompassing mathematical reserves, unearned premium reserves, outstanding loss reserves, policyholder dividend reserves, minimum investment return guarantee reserves, balancing reserves, and catastrophe reserves. Within general and administrative expenses, the provisioning and reversal dynamics for asset impairments and doubtful debts are meticulously monitored across each insurance segment and auxiliary business line.

Regarding legal risk management and contingent liabilities, Bao Viet enforces stringent controls over related-party transactions (including the Ministry of Finance, SCIC, Sumitomo Life, BaoViet Bank, and associated entities). These transactions must strictly adhere to arm's length principles, mandate the public disclosure of Board of Directors Resolutions within 24 hours, and comply with statutory prohibitions against granting loans or guarantees to insiders absent prior General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) approval. The Internal Control System and Internal Audit Division are tasked with overseeing the end-to-end process to mitigate litigation and dispute risks.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

* **Environmental (E):** Bao Viet executes its Green Transformation strategy by channeling capital flows into sustainable financial portfolios, greening its asset mix, and developing insurance products intrinsically tied to environmental stewardship—thereby advancing emissions-reduction targets and aligning with the national Net-Zero commitment.
* **Social (S):** The Group prioritizes occupational health and safety, gender equality, and human capital development. The Internal Audit Division has provided limited assurance services for standardized Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) metrics, including GRI 2-7 (employment data), GRI 202-1 (ratios of standard entry-level wage by gender compared to local minimum wage), GRI 405-1 (diversity of governance bodies and employees), GRI 413-1 (local community engagement programs), and GRI G4-FS7 (monetary value of products and services designed to deliver social benefits).
* **Governance (G):** Bao Viet adheres to advanced corporate governance standards in accordance with GRI frameworks, Financial Services Sector Disclosures (FSSD), and the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard. The Internal Audit Division functions as an independent evaluator pursuant to Decree No. 05/2019/NĐ-CP. The Board of Directors maintains exemplary transparency through a rigorous conflict-of-interest management policy, bilingual public disclosures (Vietnamese-English), and enhanced governance efficacy facilitated by the direct participation of subject-matter experts from strategic partner Sumitomo Life.
