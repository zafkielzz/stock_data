**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2021, notwithstanding macroeconomic headwinds induced by the Covid-19 pandemic—which restricted Vietnam’s real GDP growth to a decade-low of 2.58%—MBB delivered outstanding financial performance, driven by the steadfast execution of its strategic motto: “Digital Acceleration - Retail Breakthrough - Safety and Efficiency.” The core growth engine stemmed from an aggressive digital transformation strategy, enabling the bank to acquire 6.3 million new customers in 2021 (matching the cumulative client acquisition of the preceding 26 years) and bringing the total customer base to over 12.9 million. Digital banking revenue reached approximately VND 3 trillion, expanding 2.5x year-over-year, alongside roughly 390 million transactions channeled through digital platforms. On a consolidated basis, total group assets reached VND 607,140 billion, while profit before tax (PBT) surged 55% YoY to VND 16,527 billion. Of this total, the Parent Bank contributed VND 14,398 billion, while subsidiaries accounted for VND 2,326 billion (a 14% contribution). All subsidiaries sustained positive growth momentum: MIC advanced into the Top 5 by insurance revenue, Mcredit solidified its Top 3 position in the consumer finance market, and MB Ageas Life climbed three notches to rank in the Top 9 for agency distribution channels.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
During FY2021, MBB successfully completed a 35% stock dividend payout and advanced subsequent phases of its charter capital augmentation plan as greenlit by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders. By year-end, MBB’s charter capital stood at VND 37,783 billion. The capitalization and funding structures were rigorously managed to comply with the State Bank of Vietnam’s (SBV) prudential limits and Basel II standards. The bank successfully implemented advanced credit risk modeling, augmented IT risk governance, and maintained exemplary liquidity risk management—earning MBB the prestigious international accolade of “Liquidity Risk Management Award” for Best Liquidity Risk Management in Asia-Pacific for 2021.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
MBB’s asset quality was tightly insulated amidst macroeconomic pressures stemming from the pandemic. The Parent Bank’s Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio remained subdued at 0.68% (0.90% on a consolidated basis), while the NPL coverage ratio (LLR) scaled an exceptional 349% at the parent bank level, cementing its position at the forefront of the Vietnamese banking sector. Regarding provisioning policies, MBB strictly adhered to newly enacted regulations, including Circular 11/2021/TT-NHNN and Circular 03/2021/TT-NHNN concerning loan restructuring, interest rate exemptions/reductions while maintaining loan classification, and fully executing the roadmap for specific and general provisions (maintained at 0.75% of total outstanding loans, net of deposits/loans to other credit institutions and Group 5 loans). The Risk Resolution Committee operated efficiently to remediate non-performing assets. There were no material legal disputes, litigations, or extraordinary financial events occurring post-December 31, 2021, that would exert a material adverse effect on the enterprise's financial standing.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** MBB integrated Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors into its underwriting and investment frameworks, actively championing “green credit” and “green banking” initiatives to steer the economy toward sustainable, low-carbon growth. The bank established a proprietary green credit statistics indicator suite alongside a standardized economic sector taxonomy to elevate the share of capital allocated to renewable and clean energy. Concurrently, it reviewed and updated its environmental and social risk management policies within credit operations.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritized human capital management, supporting its 15,910-strong workforce by safeguarding health and maintaining a stable working environment amid pandemic disruptions. Marrying commercial operations with corporate social responsibility (CSR), MBB channeled approximately VND 386 billion into community initiatives in 2021, including roughly VND 285 billion dedicated to Covid-19 pandemic relief efforts across 38 provinces/cities and 15 nationwide units.
- **Governance (G):** MBB upheld stringent regulatory compliance and high-tier corporate governance standards, standing as a pioneer in adopting Basel II through a robust "Three Lines of Defense" governance model comprising the Board of Directors (BoD), the Supervisory Board, the Risk Management Committee, and Internal Audit. Information transparency and sustainable development practices were underscored by MBB’s retention in the Top 20 best sustainable development indices on the market (VNSI, co-developed by HOSE and PwC) and the Top 30 largest enterprises in Vietnam.
