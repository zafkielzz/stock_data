### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In the 2022 fiscal year, Vincom Retail reported a robust operational recovery, generating total revenue of VND 7,361 billion, representing a 25% year-on-year increase. Profit after tax surged by an impressive 111% year-on-year to VND 2,777 billion, achieving 116% of the full-year target and establishing a high net profit margin of 38%. Core growth momentum was driven by investment property leasing and rendering of related services, underpinned by a strong recovery in the domestic retail market post-pandemic, evidenced by a 19.8% nationwide expansion in total retail sales of goods and consumer services. In terms of operational scale, VRE reinforced its market position as the sole retail real estate developer to open 3 new shopping malls (including Vincom Mega Mall Smart City) during the year, all of which recorded committed occupancy rates exceeding 94%. Geographically, the company and its subsidiaries operate entirely within the territory of Vietnam; consequently, no export operations or international geographical segment reporting were recorded.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Based on the financial data system and notes to the financial statements, Vincom Retail’s capital structure incorporates short-term investments and flexible cash flow management. Cash and cash equivalents as of December 31, 2022, comprised bank deposits with original maturities of under 3 months yielding interest rates of 5.1%–6.0% p.a. (compared to 3.7%–4.0% p.a. at the beginning of the year) and corporate bonds held for under 3 months yielding an 8.5% p.a. interest rate. Short-term held-to-maturity investments (bank deposits with original maturities exceeding 3 months and remaining terms under 12 months) yielded interest rates ranging from 3.9% to 10.7% p.a. The corporate financial structure also maintains unsecured investment and business cooperation deposits for shopping mall components in real estate projects with related parties (such as Vingroup JSC, Thai Son, Can Gio, and Thanh Pho Xanh). However, due to limited detailed disclosure regarding specific bank credit lines and the exact weighting of fixed versus floating-rate debt in the provided notes, short-term liquidity pressures are primarily managed through the optimization of working capital turnover and operating cash flows.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vincom Retail’s credit risk and non-performing loan (NPL) management is demonstrated through a stringent monitoring policy for accounts receivable. The notes to the financial statements indicate that no customer overdue receivables accounted for more than 10% of total overdue receivables across the reported benchmark dates. Regarding long-term assets, the enterprise maintains deposits with partners to acquire transfers or preemptive purchase rights for shopping mall components in real estate projects without accompanying collateral. Additionally, certain tangible fixed assets and investment properties of the company were pledged as collateral for issued bond tranches, while land use rights and assets attached to land of one shopping mall were utilized as collateral for a related company's loan. System data records no material legal disputes or anomalous financial events occurring after the closing date of December 31, 2022.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vincom Retail focuses on mitigating environmental impacts by completing the installation of rooftop solar power systems across 50 shopping malls in 2022 (with expansion planned for an additional 8 shopping malls in 2023). Cumulative solar power generation from 2018 through year-end 2022 reached 23.2 million kWh, contributing to the abatement of 17 thousand metric tons of CO2 emissions. Given the operational nature of shopping mall management—which does not directly engage in heavy industrial manufacturing—the company generates no heavy polluting raw materials and strictly complies with periodic environmental monitoring standards conducted by independent third parties.
- **Social (S):** The company implements a sustainable human capital development strategy by safeguarding employee rights, ensuring workplace equality, maintaining non-discrimination policies, and strictly prohibiting child or forced labor. A conducive working environment is sustained through occupational safety training programs, regular performance appraisals mapped to job descriptions, and employee satisfaction surveys covering welfare benefits and facilities.
- **Governance (G):** Vincom Retail’s corporate governance framework is standardized to ensure transparency, fairness, and the comprehensive protection of rights for shareholders and stakeholders alike. Governance operations function under the rigorous oversight of the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, the internal audit system, and the independent auditor, aligning with best-practice standards in modern corporate governance.
