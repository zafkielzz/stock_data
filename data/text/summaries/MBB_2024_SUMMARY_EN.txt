### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2024, MB demonstrated robust growth, with total assets crossing the VND 1 quadrillion threshold for the first time to reach VND 1,129 trillion, expanding by 19.5% YoY. This core growth momentum was propelled by a strategic asset reallocation towards higher-yielding assets, notably by increasing the proportion of loans to customers (particularly within the retail segment). Total credit extension (comprising loans and corporate bonds) reached over VND 811 trillion, marking a strong 24.5% increase. On the funding side, customer deposits (including certificates of deposit) rose by 20% to VND 806 trillion, securing the bank's position as the second largest among listed peers. Crucially, MB maintained its undisputed #1 industry ranking in Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio, with CASA volumes reaching VND 281 trillion—accounting for 39.3% of total deposits—thereby optimizing its cost of funds amid heightened market competition. In the equity market, MBB shares exhibited a positive trajectory, delivering a full-year return of +37.17% (opening at VND 9,550 and closing at VND 13,100), alongside an average matching trading volume of over 16.6 million shares per session.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
MB's capital structure was further reinforced through scale expansion of charter capital and the optimization of funding sources. As of December 31, 2024, the bank’s charter capital stood at VND 53,063,241 million, reflecting its continuously enhanced financial capacity. Funding activities were executed flexibly across short-, medium-, and long-term deposits and the issuance of valuable papers. Notably, the trading book portfolio includes Government bonds with tenors ranging from 3 to 20 years (yielding 2.00%–8.10% per annum) and bonds issued by domestic credit institutions and economic entities at competitive rates, facilitating asset-liability duration matching. The successful sustainment of a high CASA ratio serves as a strategic springboard, enabling MB to effectively control its Cost of Funds, mitigate upward pressure on lending rates amidst macroeconomic volatility, and ensure full compliance with the State Bank of Vietnam’s liquidity safety ratios.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Despite aggressive credit expansion (24.5%), MB’s asset quality remained tightly controlled and superior to the industry average. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio at year-end 2024 was stable and well-contained at low levels: 1.6% at the consolidated level and 1.4% for the parent bank. Credit risk provisioning for deposits, loans to credit institutions, and the investment portfolio was adequately executed in accordance with internal policies and regulatory mandates. The bank deploys an early warning model, systematically reviews risk-latent customer portfolios, and prioritizes the recovery and resolution of overdue debts, NPLs, and off-balance-sheet loans. During the year, MB completed procedures for ESOP share issuances and private placement bond offerings to supplement its capital base, while recording no extraordinary legal events or material disputes subsequent to the financial year-end of December 31, 2024.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** MB acts as an industry pioneer in driving green finance trends, embedding Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria into the group's long-term strategic objectives. The bank prioritizes environmental risk management across its credit underwriting activities, channeling capital toward sustainable development projects, circular economy initiatives, and clean energy to support broader low-carbon economic goals.
- **Social (S):** MB places strong emphasis on cultivating high-caliber human capital through intensive training programs, fostering a professional working environment, and implementing compelling remuneration policies to elevate employee engagement and retention. Beyond core operations, the bank actively executes corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, supporting community development and social welfare to propagate sustainable human values.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance at MB strictly adheres to advanced international best practices and statutory regulations. The bank maintains a transparent governance framework characterized by effective oversight from the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and specialized committees. Financial disclosures are rigorously audited by independent auditors, ensuring accuracy, safeguarding shareholders' legal rights, and meeting international standards of transparency.
