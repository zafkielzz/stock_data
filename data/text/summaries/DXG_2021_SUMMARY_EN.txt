### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

FY2021 marked a robust operational recovery and breakout for Dat Xanh Group Joint Stock Company (DXG) following its corporate restructuring phase. Consolidated total revenue reached VND 10,000bn, while net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company stood at VND 1,157bn, driving total assets past the VND 28,000bn threshold. The equity market responded favorably to these results, with DXG shares surging +145.12% over the year (from VND 9,730 to VND 23,850/share) on an average daily trading volume of nearly 8.95m shares.

Core growth momentum stemmed from two primary pillars: real estate development and real estate services. Within the property development segment, top-line growth was propelled by the execution and handover of key projects, including Gem Sky World, Opal Skyline, St. Moritz, and Opal Boulevard. The Group actively expanded its land bank via M&A, notably acquiring a 100% stake in Phuoc Son Investment Company for VND 1,680bn and divesting a 55% stake in Xuan Dinh Company, generating VND 343.75bn. In the real estate services segment, the successful IPO of Dat Xanh Real Estate Services Joint Stock Company (DXS) cemented DXG's market dominance in real estate distribution. Amid pandemic-related social distancing mandates in Ho Chi Minh City, DXG swiftly pivoted its sales model online via proprietary tech platforms such as Real Agent and Careplus, sustaining stable sales momentum.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

DXG maintained a diversified capital structure to fund its land-banking strategy and project infrastructure development. The Group's debt portfolio comprised short- and long-term bank credit facilities alongside corporate bonds. Major lending banking partners included VietinBank (Branch 4 and Quang Ninh), TPBank, MBBank, MSB, and HDBank. Term deposit interest rates ranged from 3.3% to 7.3% p.a., with VND 151.37bn pledged as collateral for credit facilities.

Collateral underpinning debt obligations was diverse, encompassing land use rights (LURs) at the Gem Sky World and Gem Riverside projects, 23 land parcels in Can Tho (via Dat Xanh Mien Tay), and real estate assets in Bien Hoa City. Capitalized borrowing costs for FY2021 totaled VND 228.99bn (down from VND 359.44bn in FY2020). To bolster long-term financial capacity, the Board of Directors approved a plan to issue up to USD 300m in international bonds. Short-term cash flow pressures were partially alleviated through business cooperation agreements (BCAs), exemplified by a VND 1,080bn cooperation contract executed with Ha An SG for the Dat Xanh Home Park City project.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Regarding asset risk management, DXG applied a conservative provisioning policy for short-term doubtful receivables, advances to suppliers, and other receivables associated with project marketing and distribution contracts (such as Tien Hai City Center, Dat Quang Riverside, and Ngoc Duong). In M&A transactions, following the finalization of the purchase price allocation (PPA) and fair value assessment of Linkgroup Real Estate Company’s net assets in 2021, management confirmed no variance from preliminary appraisals, leaving goodwill unchanged at VND 157.69bn.

Regarding off-balance-sheet obligations and post-balance-sheet events, project legal risks were mitigated by structuring transactions as asset acquisitions rather than business combinations when acquiring entities holding pipeline projects only (such as Phuoc Son). Concerning capital-raising initiatives, as of the preparation date of the FY2021 consolidated financial statements, DXG was actively advancing procedural requirements to execute the USD 300m international bond issuance mandated by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM).

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

- **Environment (E):** DXG focuses on developing green urban complexes integrated with circular utility ecosystems across key projects. The Group has accelerated digital transformation via ERP systems and electronic office models, mitigating energy consumption, optimizing paper usage, and reducing scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions across its operational footprint.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is treated as a core asset. DXG established the Strategy Academy to cultivate high-caliber talent. The company maintained a safe working environment and preserved employee welfare during pandemic disruptions. Additionally, the Group actively contributed to society through sponsorship programs for solidarity housing and Tet charitable initiatives across local communities.
- **Governance (G):** The Group finalized its restructuring into a Holdings model overseeing five strategic verticals (Real Estate, Services, Construction, Finance - Investment, and Technology). Governance practices are benchmarked against and integrated with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Vietnam's National Action Plan, ensuring information disclosure transparency and safeguarding shareholder rights.
