Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the 2024 annual report summary for Vietcap Securities Joint Stock Company (VCI):

---

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
In 2024, Vietcap’s financial performance staged a robust recovery, driven by the resurgence of the Vietnamese stock market and improved market liquidity. Total operating revenue reached VND 3,695 billion (up 50% YoY), and profit before tax (PBT) reached VND 1,089 billion (up 91%), achieving 156% of the full-year target.
- **Brokerage Segment:** Generated revenue of VND 769 billion (up 50% YoY) and PBT of VND 297 billion (up 190% YoY). Vietcap’s brokerage market share on the HOSE maintained a Top 5 position at 6.08% (improving from 8th place and a 4.47% market share in 2023), while retaining its leadership in institutional client market share at approximately 30%.
- **Margin Lending Segment:** Margin loan balances reached a record high of VND 11,222 billion at year-end 2024 (up 40% YoY). This segment generated VND 932 billion in revenue (up 18%) and VND 236 billion in PBT (up 54%), supported by a safe margin-to-equity ratio maintained at 0.9x.
- **Investment Banking (IB) Segment:** Recorded revenue of VND 35 billion and PBT of VND 2 billion. Vietcap successfully executed several major landmark transactions, including acting as the advisory firm for the transfer of a 49% stake in Summit Agro International, as well as advising on divestment and UPCOM registration for Sabeco (ticker: SAB) and Asia Ingredients Group (AIG).
- **Proprietary Trading (Investment) Segment:** Remained the primary growth driver, recording revenue of VND 2,014 billion (up 74% YoY) and PBT of VND 554 billion (up 60% YoY) via a high-quality equity portfolio comprising blue-chip names such as ACB, MBB, CTG, FPT, MWG, etc.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vietcap’s capital structure was significantly strengthened in 2024 through an increase in charter capital to VND 7,180 billion, elevating total equity to VND 12,944 billion (up 76% compared to year-end 2023).
- **Debt Scale:** Total short-term borrowings as of December 31, 2024, stood at VND 12,573,864 million (compared to VND 8,979,275 million in 2023), consisting entirely of short-term debt funding margin lending operations. The company carries no long-term debt.
- **Interest Rate Structure & Credit Partners:** Borrowings comprise domestic bank loans at interest rates ranging from 5.0% to 7.5% p.a., and foreign bank loans priced at a reference rate plus a spread (or floating rates tied to SOFR). Vietcap maintains substantial credit facilities with international financial institutions (such as syndicated loans worth USD 100 million and USD 119 million arranged by Maybank Securities Pte. Ltd., Taipei Fubon Commercial Bank, Taishin International Bank, etc.). Foreign exchange and interest rate risks on foreign currency borrowings are actively managed through currency and interest rate derivative contracts. The total debt-to-equity ratio dropped sharply from 121.8% to 97.1%, reflecting enhanced financial self-sufficiency.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
- **Provisioning:** The company maintains a conservative risk management policy. Provisions for impairment of short-term receivables were recorded at VND 17,737 million, with overdue and impaired debts fully provisioned. For FVTPL and AFS financial assets, Vietcap periodically marks them to market/fair value, recognizing valuation gains/losses directly through the income statement or equity.
- **Contingent Liabilities & Commitments:** Total assets carrying credit risk exposure (including underwriting commitments, guarantees, and assets under client management) are strictly controlled. The company’s Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 565% at the end of 2024, well above the regulatory minimum of 180%.
- **Subsequent Events:** Pursuant to resolutions of the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders and early 2025 Board of Directors resolutions, the company proceeded with the first interim cash dividend payment for 2024 at a rate of 4% (VND 250/share) on a charter capital of VND 7,180,994,800,000, distributed in February 2025. No material abnormal events or legal disputes have been recorded subsequent to the accounting period end.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** Vietcap prioritizes the comprehensive digitization of operational processes to minimize paper consumption and optimize energy usage at its headquarters and branches. The company integrates Environmental (ESG) assessment criteria into its investment research and issuance advisory activities, steering its financial asset portfolio toward green and sustainable finance.
- **Social (S):** Total headcount in 2024 stood at 389 employees, with female employees accounting for 42.93%. The working environment strictly complies with occupational health and safety (OHS) regulations while ensuring gender equality. Vietcap focuses on human capital development through intensive internal training programs (CFA, CMT certifications, securities professional training) combined with competitive compensation and comprehensive welfare policies (premium PVI health insurance, annual ESOP for staff). Furthermore, the company actively contributes to the community through flagship philanthropic initiatives such as the "Spring Bus" program, which assists workers in returning home for Tet, and the "Connecting Rings of Love" fund, with total sponsorship exceeding VND 21.8 billion.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework adheres to the highest standards of transparency. The Board of Directors comprises members with deep expertise, working alongside an independent and objective Supervisory Board. The company applies rigorous independent auditing standards (PwC), fully complies with public disclosure regulations, and aligns with GRI standards and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) within Vietnam's financial sector.
