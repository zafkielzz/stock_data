1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2022, Nam Kim Steel Joint Stock Company (NKG) reported net revenue of VND 23,128 billion, achieving 82.6% of its annual target, while registering a net loss of VND 124 billion. This bottom-line deterioration was principally driven by mounting macroeconomic headwinds across both global and domestic markets: a precipitous and persistent decline in Hot-Rolled Coil (HRC) prices (down 38% from the Q3 2021 peak), a frozen domestic real estate sector, and elevated inflation that compressed global purchasing power. Examining core segment drivers, galvanized sheet sales volume reached 706,700 metric tons, contracting by 24.2% year-over-year; conversely, the steel pipe segment emerged as a bright spot, with sales volume expanding by 14.4% year-over-year to 168,600 metric tons. The integration of the SAP HANA S/4 ERP system enabled the company to sustain production run-rates exceeding 100% of capacity despite ongoing supply chain disruptions. Internationally, NKG maintained its export footprint across more than 65 countries and territories, securing a competitive edge underpinned by the lowest anti-dumping duty rates in Canada, Mexico, Australia, and Malaysia.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
NKG pursued its strategy of reinforcing internal financial resilience by accelerating long-term debt amortization and capturing preferential short-term funding sources. Specifically, over the 2018–2022 period, the company completed the repayment of over VND 1,200 billion in long-term debt, leaving its long-term debt burden virtually negligible. Regarding short-term debt composition, the company maintained credit facilities with prominent banking partners, including BIDV (Phu Nhuan Branch), VietinBank (Branch 2 HCMC), Vietcombank (Bac Binh Duong and Dong Nai Branches), Petrolimex Saigon, SinoPac, Maritime Bank, VIB, and MB. Short-term borrowing rates ranged from 4.5% to 11.5% per annum for VNDdenominated loans and from 3.2% to 3.8% per annum for USD-denominated loans, while maturing long-term debt carried an interest rate of 8.2% per annum. Cash flow pressures and capital structure risks were partially mitigated as the cash-to-total-assets ratio rose sharply from 7.8% in 2021 to 9.3% in 2022, alongside the suspension of the Nam Kim Phu My Galvanized Steel Plant project amid a high-interest-rate environment.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Driven by the rapid and continuous downward trajectory of HRC prices in the second half of 2022, NKG was required to continuously provision for inventory write-downs from Q4 2021 through Q4 2022, booking an additional provision of VND 290 billion in Q4 2022 alone. Regarding receivables, overdue short-term trade receivables stood at VND 8.8 billion as of December 31, 2022, and were fully provisioned for. In terms of encumbered assets, revolving inventories, term bank deposits, bonds, land use rights, machinery, equipment, and transport vehicles were pledged as collateral for bank borrowings and finance lease agreements (such as the finance lease contract with BIDV – SuMi Trust Leasing Company featuring a fixed interest rate of 8.2% per annum for the initial three months). The company proactively formulated macro-scenario models to optimize risk mitigation against prevailing macroeconomic volatility.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Acknowledging the net-zero carbon emissions target by 2050 under COP26 commitments and the Circular Economy Development Scheme, NKG remains committed to prioritizing sustainable growth over indiscriminate output expansion. The company implements eco-friendly technological initiatives, enforces strict protocols for raw material storage and waste management, maintains emissions below standard thresholds, and operates a spent acid regeneration system (recovering 18% HCl acid as feedstock for the pickling line) alongside wastewater recycling.
- **Social (S):** The enterprise provides stable employment for nearly 1,400 personnel and fully fulfills its fiscal obligations (contributing VND 738 billion to the state budget in Binh Duong for 2022). NKG places significant emphasis on human capital development through periodic professional training programs, guarantees workplace safety, and actively engages with the community via social welfare initiatives and support for disadvantaged groups.
- **Governance (G):** NKG maintains a robust corporate governance framework characterized by close coordination among the Board of Directors, the Board of Management, and the Supervisory Board. The company operates the SAP HANA S/4 ERP system to optimize information transparency, safeguard equitable rights for all shareholders (including minority shareholders), and align with sustainable development standards anchored in the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
