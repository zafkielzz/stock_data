### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2024, against the backdrop of a challenging Vietnamese dairy industry where total sector value contracted by 0.3% YoY, Vietnam Dairy Products Joint Stock Company (Vinamilk - VNM) successfully defended its market leadership through agile and effective commercial strategies. Consolidated net revenue grew by 2.2% YoY, achieving 97.9% of the full-year target, while consolidated net profit increased by 4.8% YoY, beating the target by VND 77bn. Net profit margin remained stable at 0.36%, underpinned by disciplined cost optimization.

The core growth driver stemmed from a pronounced divergence between domestic and overseas operations. The domestic market continued to serve as the bedrock, contributing 82% of consolidated net revenue at VND 50,799bn (up 0.4% YoY) despite headwinds from natural disasters. Meanwhile, international operations staged a robust breakout, accounting for 18% of consolidated net revenue with an impressive growth rate of 12.6%—a 5-year high. Specifically, overseas subsidiaries (such as Angkor Milk in Cambodia and Driftwood in the US) posted revenues of VND 5,319bn (up 12.9% YoY), while the export segment generated VND 5,664bn (up 12.4% YoY) by effectively penetrating premium customer bases in Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Canada, and the US. Key product lines posting double-digit growth included condensed milk, set yogurt, Probi probiotic yogurt, Green Farm fresh milk, adult powdered milk, and plant-based milk.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinamilk's capital structure in FY2024 was managed dynamically to optimize financial leverage in alignment with prevailing market interest rates. Total short- and long-term debt as of December 31, 2024, stood at VND 9,273bn, representing a 9.7% increase YoY. The debt-to-total-assets and debt-to-equity ratios recorded slight upward adjustments to fund strategic capital expenditure projects. Overall, current and quick ratios remained robust, ensuring a safe liquidity cushion to support ongoing business operations and sustain a reliable dividend payout policy for shareholders.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vinamilk’s risk management framework and asset quality maintained a high safety profile throughout FY2024. According to operational reports and management assessments, the company reported no material non-performing receivables, bad debts, or impaired assets that could materially impact financial performance. Inventory and working capital management remained stringent, resulting in inventory turnover and receivables collection cycles remaining broadly stable YoY. No major litigation or material off-balance-sheet guarantees arose that could adversely affect the company's financial position subsequent to the balance sheet date of December 31, 2024.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
*   **Environmental (E):** Vinamilk steadfastly advanced its strategic action program, "Vinamilk toward Net Zero 2050," anchored by four pillars: Sustainable Agriculture, Green Manufacturing, Eco-Friendly Logistics, and Sustainable Consumption. A key highlight in FY2024 was the Vietnam Beverage Factory officially receiving recognition as the third facility to achieve carbon neutrality under the international PAS 2060:2014 standard, a testament to its "dual action" approach of cutting direct emissions while maintaining carbon-sink forestry funds.
*   **Social (S):** The company prioritized human capital development via comprehensive welfare policies, an enhanced working environment, and the deployment of new technologies to boost productivity and alleviate physical strains on employees. Furthermore, corporate social responsibility was prominently demonstrated through community initiatives, notably providing nearly 1.8 million essential nutritional products valued at approximately VND 10bn to residents and children in disaster-affected regions.
*   **Governance (G):** Vinamilk’s corporate governance strictly adheres to international best practices and listing regulations. The Board of Directors maintained a transparent governance structure supported by specialized board committees (Audit Committee, Nomination Committee, Remuneration Committee, and Strategy Committee). The company exceeded minimum disclosure requirements, successfully hosted its Annual General Meeting of Shareholders online, and maintained rigorous investor relations, earning Vinamilk numerous top accolades in the 2024 Listed Companies and IR Awards.
