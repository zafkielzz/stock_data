### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Mobile World Investment Corporation (MWG) steadfastly executed its comprehensive restructuring strategy themed "Quantity Reduction – Quality Enhancement," focusing on cost optimization, operational efficiency, and margin expansion across all retail chains. Consequently, MWG reported record consolidated net revenue of VND 155,928 billion, up 16% YoY and outperforming the full-year target of VND 150,000 billion. Consolidated net profit surged 89% YoY to exceed VND 7 trillion, significantly surpassing the profit after tax target of VND 4,850 billion.

Core growth momentum was propelled by solid contributions from key retail formats: the `thegioididong.com` chain generated approximately VND 37.3 trillion; the Dien May XANH chain reached roughly VND 68.4 trillion (up 18% YoY); and the Bach Hoa XANH grocery chain delivered nearly VND 46.9 trillion (up 14% YoY). Notably, Bach Hoa XANH marked its second consecutive year of profitability, recording multi-fold earnings growth compared to FY2024 and reaching a pivotal milestone by achieving net profitability at the corporate entity level following its restructuring phase. Furthermore, online channel revenue reached VND 9.2 trillion, accounting for 5.9% of total revenue. An Khang Pharmacy, the Erablue consumer electronics retail chain in Indonesia, and Avakids all demonstrated pronounced operational improvements, with Erablue and Avakids turning profitable in the final quarters of the year.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As of the end of FY2025, MWG’s financial structure remained stable, with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 88.38%. This metric reflects a prudent financial leverage profile amid capital deployment directed toward retail footprint expansion and the restructuring of various retail chains. Profitability ratios registered notable enhancements, with Return on Equity (ROE) reaching 5.35% and Return on Asset (ROA) standing at 2.16%, indicating a progressive recovery in capital and asset efficiency in alignment with management's margin-enhancement mandate. *(Note: Granular data regarding short/long-term debt composition, floating/fixed interest rate breakdowns, and the identities of primary credit-providing banking partners are currently unavailable in the provided data repository).*

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
MWG demonstrated a robust commitment to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles, earning multiple prestigious accolades including "Best Solution ESG Vietnam" (The Asset), Top 10 Outstanding Comprehensive ESG Execution Enterprises 2025 (Dan Tri), and Top 50 Typical Sustainable Development Enterprises in Vietnam 2025 (Nhip Cau Dau Tu):
- **Environmental (E):** The group prioritized mitigating adverse ecological impacts and optimizing resource utilization. Over 5,000 stores were equipped with IoT systems to streamline operations and curb emissions; more than 1,300 locations integrated rooftop solar energy systems. MWG also actively promoted circular economy practices and green transition initiatives, including second-hand apparel collection kiosks (in partnership with RESHARE), used toothbrush recycling programs (in partnership with Colgate), the afforestation of 1,000 trees on Ong Mountain (Lam Dong), and collaboration with V-Green to deploy over 3,000 EV charging stations and battery-swap cabinets nationwide.
- **Social (S):** MWG maintained an equitable and diverse working environment, prioritizing employee empowerment, consistently securing a spot in the Top 10 Best Places to Work in Vietnam's Retail Sector (Viet Research). Concurrently, the group underscored its corporate citizenship through high-impact social welfare initiatives.
- **Governance (G):** The Board of Directors (BOD) operated in strict compliance with principles of transparency, fairness, and shareholder rights protection, adhering to State Securities Commission (SSC) and Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE) standards. The BOD exercised strategic oversight, risk governance, and conflict-of-interest prevention, maintaining strict accountability via its dedicated Internal Audit Department by adopting best corporate governance practices.
