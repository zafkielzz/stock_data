### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, notwithstanding headwinds from COVID-19-related social distancing restrictions and a national GDP growth rate of 2.6%, Vietnam Technological and Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Techcombank or TCB) sustained robust financial performance underpinned by agile adaptability and a comprehensive digitalization strategy. Core growth drivers stemmed from both qualitative and quantitative expansion of the customer base, resource optimization, and the generation of sustainable revenue streams. Net fee income (NFI) to total operating income stood at 21.6% (incorporating foreign exchange and bond distribution fees), placing the bank among the industry leaders. Within the lending segment, advanced digital solutions such as the multi-channel retail lending platform Omni (Smart Credit) compressed approval turnaround times to 5–15 minutes, robustly propelling credit card issuance (with a 92% active card ratio and a 96% customer satisfaction rate). Furthermore, the Data and Analytics (DnA) division directly contributed approximately VND 400 billion to operating revenue. From a macroeconomic and trade perspective, while the indirect impacts of the Russia-Ukraine conflict remained ambiguous, the economy registered widening export opportunities across select sectors—such as rice, steel, cooking oil, and fertilizers—alongside a sharp resurgence in foreign direct investment (FDI) inflows as international investors returned.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
TCB’s capital structure was further reinforced through the diversification of funding sources and the preservation of a low cost of funds (CoF) advantage, which contracted to 2.2%—ranking among the lowest in the sector, driven by a high current account savings account (CASA) ratio. During 2021, TCB cemented its superior market standing by successfully completing a landmark USD 800 million international syndicated loan facility, building upon the momentum of its USD 500 million package in 2020. This capital raise reaffirms the confidence of international financial institutions while injecting supplementary working capital to satisfy the bank's expanding medium- and long-term loan demand. A pre-defined risk appetite and a prudent lending policy enabled the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) to be maintained at a robust 15.0%, securing a resilient capital buffer against macroeconomic volatility and effectively supporting the credit scale expansion strategy.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
TCB’s asset quality and risk management framework remained sound throughout the two-year pandemic period. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio (Loans in Groups 3–5) was tightly contained at a very low 0.7%, while the credit cost decreased to 0.7% (compared to 0.9% in 2020). The bank successfully curtailed its COVID-19 restructured loan portfolio by nearly 80%. Concurrently, TCB proactively supported borrowers through relief packages totaling up to VND 41 trillion, encompassing debt restructuring, interest rate waivers, reductions, and maturity extensions, with interest concessions granted to clients reaching VND 540 billion in 2021 alone. The bank continues to align with international standards and elevate its institutional capacity by upgrading stress-testing models for credit, liquidity, and operational risks, concurrently deploying the Kamakura system and establishing a cloud-based Anti-Money Laundering (AML) platform.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** TCB actively researches, develops, and executes programs and policies to incentivize lending toward "green," environmentally friendly projects, integrating an Environmental and Social (E&S) risk management framework that spans all business and lending activities to satisfy the stringent standards of the International Finance Corporation (IFC) during international capital-raising campaigns.
- **Social (S):** The bank places human capital at its core with a comprehensively upgraded human resources program spanning recruitment, development, and talent retention, having newly recruited 4,375 employees with a strategic focus on technology, digitization, and data skill sets. Amidst the pandemic, TCB spearheaded community and public support initiatives, administering early vaccinations to 100% of employees and their families, contributing approximately VND 400 billion to the COVID-19 vaccine fund, supporting the procurement of testing supplies, constructing specialized treatment hospitals, and providing direct donations to impacted individuals and collectives.
- **Governance (G):** TCB strictly adheres to regulatory corporate governance mandates stipulated by state authorities (such as Decree No. 71/2017/NĐ-CP, Circular No. 13/2018/TT-NHNN, Circular No. 22/2019/TT-NHNN, and other applicable laws). The Board of Directors refined its operational model via the establishment of the Risk Management Committee and the Human Resources Committee, alongside the promulgation of amended Internal Governance Regulations to secure a transparent, open, and efficient governance mechanism aligned with international best practices and standards.
