### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
For the fiscal year 2022, Vietnam Dairy Products Joint Stock Company (Vinamilk - HOSE: VNM) reported consolidated gross revenue exceeding VND 60 trillion, remaining stable year-over-year and achieving 94% of its annual target. However, consolidated profit before tax reached VND 10,496 billion (completing 88% of the target), impacted by the compounding headwinds of rising raw material inflation, elevated freight costs, and foreign exchange volatility.

*   **Domestic Market:** Vinamilk generated VND 50,704 billion in consolidated net revenue and VND 20,556 billion in gross profit domestically. Growth was primarily driven by the "Dream Milk" (Giấc mơ Sữa Việt) retail chain, which expanded by over 20% (supported by nearly 40 newly opened stores, bringing the total network to nearly 650 locations), the Key Accounts channel, which grew by over 10% on the back of the tourism and hospitality sector recovery, and the E-commerce channel, which posted impressive growth exceeding 30%. As of December 31, 2022, the distribution network spanned over 230,000 points of sale nationwide. Product-wise, eating yogurt, probiotic drinking yogurt, condensed milk, and the Super Nut plant-based milk line (achieving high double-digit growth) served as key growth catalysts. Mộc Châu Milk Joint Stock Company reported net revenue of VND 3,133 billion (up 7.1%), propelled by traditional channel development and the aggressive expansion of its proprietary retail footprint, which grew by over 50% to nearly 60 points of sale.
*   **Overseas Markets:** Net revenue reached VND 9,252 billion (comprising VND 4,828 billion from exports and VND 4,424 billion from overseas subsidiaries), generating a gross profit of VND 3,342 billion. Driftwood delivered over 30% revenue growth through distribution channel diversification, successfully offsetting the delayed recovery of the school channel. Angkormilk recorded growth exceeding 10%, driven by new product rollouts and distribution scaling. The export division onboarded 10 new clients and stepped up contract manufacturing exports to stringent markets, including the US, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Australia, and New Zealand. The Vinamilk-Del Monte joint venture in the Philippines continued its successful market penetration, demonstrating high customer retention rates across liquid milk, drinking yogurt, and milk tea product lines.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Vinamilk’s capital structure remained resilient, backed by an optimal mix of equity and short-term/long-term bank credit facilities to finance operational activities and capital expenditure. The Group's short-term borrowings and current portions of long-term debt are backed by premier domestic and international banking partners, including Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (HCMC Branch), Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - HCMC Branch), MUFG Bank, Ltd. (HCMC Branch), DBS Bank (Singapore), BNP Paribas (HCMC Branch), Mizuho Bank, Ltd. (HCMC Branch), HSBC Vietnam, ANZ Laos, and HSBC USA.

The vast majority of short-term borrowings are unsecured. Notably, the long-term loan from HSBC USA is secured by assets with a carrying value of VND 896,805 million as of December 31, 2022 (up from VND 801,145 million at the beginning of the year). Despite soaring interest rate environments toward the end of 2022, Vinamilk’s fortress balance sheet, robust operating cash flow generation, and high cash dividend payout ratio (projected at VND 3,850 per share, equivalent to over 94% of the parent company's distributable net income) enabled management to effectively mitigate liquidity pressures and minimize exposure to floating interest rate risks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
During fiscal year 2022, Vinamilk adhered strictly to Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) regarding the provisioning and reversal of allowances to reflect a prudent financial profile:
*   **Inventories:** The Group maintained a disciplined inventory write-down policy for obsolete, slow-moving, or impaired goods based on rigorous periodic assessments.
*   **Receivables:** Movements in the allowance for doubtful debts were closely monitored, accounting for beginning balances, additional provisions, reversals, or write-offs during the year, alongside foreign exchange translation impacts.
*   **Contingent Liabilities & Other Items:** Trade payables to related parties (such as APIS JSC, Asia Coconut Processing JSC, Miraka Holdings Limited, and Saigon Food Ingredient JSC) represent unsecured, non-interest-bearing transactions with credit terms within 60 days from the invoice date. The financial statements recorded no material legal disputes, litigation, or unusual off-balance-sheet commitments arising after the accounting period ended December 31, 2022.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Vinamilk reinforced its pioneering position in sustainable development by embedding Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria across its entire value chain:
*   **Environmental (E):** Vinamilk remains steadfast in its commitment to achieve Net-Zero emissions by 2050. The Group champions a circular economy model and deploys renewable energy solutions (solar power and biogas systems powered by advanced GEA waste treatment infrastructure) across its 15 domestic and international dairy farms. Green energy substitution for fossil fuels (gasoline, diesel) across these farms currently approaches 87%, abating over 62,000 metric tons of CO₂ annually (equivalent to planting 3.4 million trees). A prime illustration is the Tây Ninh Green Farm Eco-Farm, where a circular biological system processes 30 tons of waste daily—supplying organic fertilizer to over 500 hectares of GlobalG.A.P-certified agricultural land while capturing CH₄ gas for milk pasteurization and fodder drying, yielding electricity cost savings of over VND 2 billion annually. All Green Farm complexes (Tây Ninh, Quảng Ngãi, and Thống Nhất Thanh Hóa) strictly adhere to regenerative agriculture standards and are free from growth hormones.
*   **Social (S):** The company places strong emphasis on animal welfare (featuring cooling systems, stable microclimate barns, automated massage brushes, and natural pastures) to safeguard the health of its 20,000 dairy cows and guarantee superior raw fresh milk quality. Furthermore, Vinamilk maintains a professional work environment, enforces robust Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) standards, promotes gender equality, continuously invests in human capital development, and executes high-impact corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives.
*   **Governance (G):** The company strictly complies with corporate governance regulations for listed companies on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE), ensuring absolute transparency through ESG disclosures aligned with domestic and international benchmarks. Independent audits, internal control systems, and internal audit functions operate efficiently under the direct oversight of the Audit Committee and the Board of Directors, safeguarding the long-term interests of shareholders and stakeholders alike.
