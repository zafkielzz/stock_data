**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Fiscal year 2023 was characterized by a challenging macroeconomic and consumer environment, which was reflected in MSN’s equity performance as the stock fell -29.47% (declining from an opening price of VND 95,000 to close at VND 67,000, trading within a 52-week range of VND 56,000 - VND 106,000 with an average daily trading volume of 1,501,613 shares). Nevertheless, the Group’s core operating verticals maintained a robust foundation underpinned by its "Core-Focused, Value-Maximization" strategy. Masan Consumer (MCH) continued to cement its position as a fast-growing FMCG player in Southeast Asia, posting a Net Profit After Tax (NPAT) margin of 20% and over 30% earnings growth in 2023 amid a subdued consumer landscape, with new innovations contributing over 50% of top-line growth. A strategic pivot during the year involved redefining its Total Addressable Market (TAM) from 100 million Vietnamese consumers to an 8 billion global consumer base, yielding encouraging results as CHIN-SU chili sauce ranked among the Top 8 best-selling products on Amazon (US) and secured the #1 position on Coupang (South Korea). Regarding WinCommerce (WCM), while yet to turn net profitable, the retail chain reached a key inflection point with 90% of its stores achieving EBITDA breakeven (compared to just 5% at the time of acquisition), capturing a 25% modern trade market share and a 50% network footprint share through store-model optimization and localized product assortments.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
In managing its capital structure and balance sheet, Masan maintains stringent capital allocation discipline to mitigate emerging-market cyclicality. Regarding debt instruments, ordinary bonds issued are initially recognized at cost less issuance costs, with discounts, premiums, and issuance expenses amortized on a straight-line basis over the tenor of the instruments. The Group enforces a rigorous risk management framework by establishing provisions and discounting projected future cash flows at pre-tax discount rates that appropriately reflect the market’s current assessment of the time value of money and specific risks inherent to each liability. The balance sheet is closely monitored to maximize operating cash flow generation during scale-up phases, ensuring sustained operational efficiency across consumer-retail pillars and strategic associates such as Techcombank (TCB).

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
Masan’s provisioning policies strictly adhere to prevailing accounting standards. Provisions are recognized when the Group has a present legal or constructive obligation arising from past events that can be reliably estimated. For specialized operations such as mineral extraction at Masan High-Tech Materials (MHT), the Group recognizes mine closure and environmental rehabilitation provisions by discounting estimated future cash flows, wherein initial outlays are capitalized into mineral assets and amortized over time, while the accretion expense resulting from discounting is recognized within finance costs. Severance allowances are accrued based on the number of years of service and the average salary of the preceding six months. Regarding contingent liabilities and employee benefit obligations, the Group recognizes retirement benefit funds and remeasurements of post-employment benefit liabilities (including actuarial gains/losses and deferred income taxes) directly under "Other equity reserves" in accordance with regulatory financial guidelines.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
Masan comprehensively integrates Environmental, Social, and Governance (G) factors into its long-term growth strategy under the philosophy of "Doing well by doing good," targeting full ESG integration across all business operations by 2030.
- *Environmental (E):* The Group is committed to adhering to stringent IFC Environmental and Social Performance Standards and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs). Environmental management is closely tied to emissions control, sustainable supply chain management, responsible sourcing, and the strict execution of mine rehabilitation obligations in compliance with Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment (MONRE) regulations.
- *Social (S):* Masan prioritizes talent development, employee welfare, occupational health and safety (OHS), and meaningful contributions to local communities. Human capital policies encompass comprehensive benefit schemes, capacity-building programs, and the maintenance of a fair, transparent working environment.
- *Governance (G):* Corporate governance is anchored by a leadership structure aligned with sustainability performance metrics. The Group applies international benchmarks such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards 2021 and the MSCI ESG rating framework to ensure transparency, year-over-year data comparability, and adherence to global best governance practices.
