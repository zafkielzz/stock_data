Below is a professional institutional equity research summary for Ho Chi Minh City Infrastructure Investment Joint Stock Company (CII), based on the 2022 Annual Report and actual market data:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2022, the macroeconomic environment faced significant headwinds from global inflation and monetary tightening policies. However, CII's core operations remained underpinned by key drivers in transport infrastructure (BOT toll collection) and real estate. Major transport infrastructure projects continued to serve as foundational cash-flow generators due to their essential nature and post-pandemic traffic volume recovery. Meanwhile, the real estate segment was heavily impacted by credit tightening and delays in project legal approvals, leading to a year-on-year contraction in revenue and margins compared to initial expectations. Given its domestic focus in Ho Chi Minh City and neighboring provinces, CII has no international export operations; its growth drivers depend entirely on public investment disbursement progress, urban transport infrastructure demand, and the absorption capacity of the domestic real estate market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
CII’s financial structure is characterized by a high financial leverage ratio, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of the infrastructure investment and real estate sectors. The capital structure features a high proportion of long-term commercial bank loans from major domestic institutions and corporate bond issuances to finance large-scale BOT and real estate projects. Amid a sharp upward trend in market interest rates throughout 2022, floating-rate debt servicing costs increased significantly, exerting pressure on net profit margins. Capital structure risks primarily center around bond maturity pressure and short-term cash flow constraints, as projects failed to achieve commercial operations on schedule to generate steady cash flows. This necessitated agile asset portfolio restructuring and negotiations with credit institutions for debt maturity extensions.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Financial risk management in 2022 included prudent provisioning for doubtful accounts and inventory write-downs to accurately reflect the subdued conditions of the real estate market. Off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities and guarantee commitments were primarily related to financial obligations in public-private partnerships (PPPs) and credit guarantees for subsidiaries. Regarding stock market performance, CII shares experienced high volatility during the year, opening at VND 37,200 and closing at VND 10,000 (representing a 73.12% decline), trading within a 52-week range of a low of VND 7,750 and a high of VND 47,740, with an average matched trading volume of 6,586,206 shares/session. The severe correction in share price reflected investor caution regarding debt leverage pressures and the broader uncertainty in the real estate market.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environment (E):** CII continues to orient its infrastructure development toward sustainability, focusing on optimizing energy efficiency at toll stations and office facilities. The company is progressively researching and integrating greenhouse gas emission reduction criteria (Scope 1 and Scope 2) into the operation of transport infrastructure projects, while advancing circular economy models to minimize negative impacts on the urban ecological environment.
- **Social (S):** The company prioritizes maintaining occupational health and safety (OHS) standards for its workforce, particularly at infrastructure construction sites. Human resource policies ensure gender equality, full provision of welfare and social insurance benefits, and a strong focus on professional training and safety technical capacity building for employees.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance complies with prevailing regulations for listed companies, featuring oversight by the Board of Directors and an independent auditing system to ensure financial transparency. Periodic and ad-hoc information disclosures are duly executed through the electronic portals of the State Securities Commission (SSC) and Stock Exchanges, safeguarding the rights of shareholders and stakeholders.
