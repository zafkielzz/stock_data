### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, KDH reported a trailing twelve-month (TTM) EPS of VND 948.78, accompanied by profitability metrics reflecting an ROE of 0.63% and an ROA of 0.40%. The gross profit margin remained robust at 43.20%, indicating strong COGS management and pricing power for delivered real estate inventory. The core growth driver continues to align with the company's strategy of developing projects with transparent legal profiles in Ho Chi Minh City. Specifically, over the 2026–2028 period, KDH aims to deploy projects across a land bank exceeding 160 hectares, anticipated to supply over 1,000 low-rise residential units, more than 5,000 high-rise apartments, and 60 hectares of industrial land designated for factories and warehousing. Furthermore, the strategic vision through 2030 encompasses the finalization of legal procedures for a land bank of nearly 400 hectares, securing long-term revenue visibility and pipeline longevity for the group.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
KDH's capital structure in 2025 was managed with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 37.82%, reflecting a conservative and prudent financial leverage profile within the real estate sector. Credit facilities are financed by premier banking partners, including Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank - Hanoi Branch) funding the Phong Phú 2 Residential Area project, and Military Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MB - Transaction Office No. 2) financing the expanded Lê Minh Xuân Industrial Park (Phase 1) and Residential Area 11A (Phase 1). Interest expense pressure is evidenced by total borrowing costs capitalized into properties under construction reaching VND 800,955,004 thousand and into construction-in-progress (expanded Lê Minh Xuân Industrial Park) amounting to VND 31,319,491 thousand in FY2025. Regarding short-term liquidity, cash and cash equivalents with original maturities of under three months yielded interest rates ranging from 4.5% to 4.75% per annum, while short-term held-to-maturity investments (over three months to under one year) earned rates between 4.3% and 6.5% per annum, effectively optimizing idle cash flows.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Regarding asset quality and provisioning, as of December 31, 2025, the group recorded a provision for long-term doubtful receivables of VND 2,665,789 thousand, whereas short-term trade and other receivables exhibited no overdue or non-performing balances with impairment indicators. Inventories are predominantly concentrated in real estate under construction, comprising cleared land funds, leveling costs, and construction expenditures across projects such as Khang Phúc - Tân Tạo Residential Area, Bình Trưng Đông, Đoàn Nguyên, Khang Phúc - Bình Hưng 11A Residential Area, Khang Phúc - Phong Phú 2 Residential Area, and Bình Trưng Mới. Land use rights, attached assets, and derivative property rights arising from these projects have been pledged as collateral for bank borrowings. Regarding M&A transactions, KDH has advanced 50% and 45% of the values under the principal contracts for the acquisition of shares in An Vinh Phát Real Estate Development JSC and An Lập Real Estate Development JSC, respectively, with transfer procedures for An Lập currently nearing completion.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** KDH emphasizes the integration of sustainable development principles into its operations, managing environmental impacts and complying with regulatory standards. The company optimizes energy and material consumption across construction sites to mitigate ecological footprints.
- **Social (S):** The group maintains a robust commitment to human capital, debuting in the Top 10 Best Places to Work in Vietnam's Real Estate Sector (co-published by Viet Research and the Investment & Finance Newspaper). Policies concerning employment, compensation, occupational health and safety, and professional training are implemented comprehensively. Additionally, KDH actively contributes to society through philanthropic initiatives, social security programs, social housing construction, and prominent urban beautification projects such as the Lê Lợi street renovation (District 1, HCMC), for which it received a certificate of merit from the Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework operates transparently, underpinned by a Board of Directors structure that integrates executive, non-executive, and independent members (including representatives from investment funds and international financial experts). Information disclosure strictly adheres to market regulations while actively adopting international reporting frameworks such as GRI Standards and referencing reputable evaluation indices from leading domestic rating organizations.
