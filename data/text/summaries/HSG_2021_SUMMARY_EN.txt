### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In the 2020–2021 financial year, Hoa Sen Group (HSG) shares recorded exceptional outperformance (+79.53%), closing at VND 22,010/share (compared to an opening price of VND 12,260/share, peaking at VND 30,190/share) alongside robust liquidity, with average matching trading volume exceeding 10.6 million shares per session. This positive price action reflects the company’s record financial performance, underpinned by net revenue of VND 48,727 billion and consolidated net profit after tax of VND 4,313 billion, with total sales volume hitting a milestone of 2,253,733 tonnes.

HSG’s core growth driver stemmed from its flexible volume allocation strategy between domestic and export markets. In the domestic arena, HSG cemented its absolute market leadership by capturing nearly 37% of the nation's galvanized sheet market share and 21% of the steel pipe market share. On the export front, despite trade barriers and the resurgence of the Covid-19 pandemic, HSG established a new export record as monthly volume surpassed 100,000 tonnes. The enterprise maximized the competitive advantages of its network of 10 manufacturing plants situated in proximity to major seaports to optimize logistics costs, scaling up exports to high-margin markets experiencing supply shortages such as North America and Europe, while effectively leveraging tariff preferences under the CPTPP to deeply penetrate the Mexican market. Furthermore, the business model transformation via the "Hoa Sen Home" building materials supermarket chain and the e-commerce platform (hoasenhome.vn) is shaping a new long-term growth catalyst for the retail segment.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

HSG’s capital structure is engineered to finance a large-scale manufacturing network and an extensive distribution footprint (comprising 55 provincial branches and 520 retail outlets). Given that the publicly disclosed documentation does not itemize specific financial statement disclosures regarding individual bank borrowings and floating interest rates applicable as of September 30, 2021, qualitative analysis indicates that HSG’s funding base experiences substantial pressure from short-term loans and finance lease liabilities utilized to fund working capital requirements (particularly the stockpiling of hot-rolled coil [HRC] raw materials).

The company's cash flow pressure is mitigated through rapid inventory turnover cycles and the optimization of accounts payable. Regarding shareholders' equity, effective January 1, 2021, Hoa Sen prospectively adopted a new statutory accounting policy concerning treasury shares. Consequently, excluding the buyback of odd-lot shares, all repurchases of common stock are deducted directly from share capital at par value and additional paid-in capital, superseding the previous practice of recognizing a separate treasury stock account. This adjustment streamlines the capital structure and more intuitively reflects the intrinsic value of circulating equity.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

The paramount financial risk confronting HSG lies in the heightened volatility of raw material steel prices. The enterprise adheres to strict inventory risk management principles based on the lower of cost and net realizable value (estimated as selling price less costs to complete and sell). Provisions for inventory write-downs are executed to safeguard gross margins against adverse downside shocks in the HRC market. Similarly, short-term accounts receivable are reflected on a net basis after deducting allowances for doubtful accounts to ensure the soundness of current assets.

Regarding long-term investments in subsidiaries (such as Hoa Sen Nghe An and Hoa Sen Nhon Hoi), the parent company recognizes impairment provisions for investment losses whenever these entities incur losses that threaten capital erosion. Off-balance sheet guarantees and legal disputes were not itemized in the current disclosure documents. Regarding post-balance sheet events, the revised accounting methodology for share repurchases effective January 1, 2021, has been applied consistently, ensuring regulatory compliance and eliminating the risks of retrospective adjustments to prior-year financial statements.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

**Environmental (E):** HSG closely aligns with the World Steel Association's sustainability reporting framework. Although quantitative greenhouse gas emissions metrics (Scope 1, 2, and 3) were not detailed in the accompanying documentation, the enterprise has demonstrated a commitment to green manufacturing through its premium Hoa Sen Gold galvanized sheet product line, featuring exceptional durability (offering anti-perforation warranties of up to 30 years), thereby extending the lifecycle of construction assets and fostering a circular business model. Advanced NOF (Non-Oxidizing Furnace) technologies deployed across the Phu My and Nghe An manufacturing complexes minimize energy consumption and satisfy the rigorous environmental standards stipulated by the Ministry of Industry and Trade, thereby maintaining its designation as a "Reputable Exporter."

**Social (S):** With a workforce numbering 7,584 employees, HSG focuses on optimizing welfare policies and occupational health and safety standards. Amidst the fourth wave of the Covid-19 outbreak, the company demonstrated robust corporate social responsibility by organizing nine relief drives across nearly 120 pandemic hotspots in Ho Chi Minh City and southern provinces, earning a certificate of merit from the Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee for its community contributions.

**Governance (G):** HSG’s governance framework is reinforced by the synchronized deployment of an enterprise resource planning (ERP) system across the entire group, spanning the parent company, manufacturing plants, and subordinate distribution branches. The establishment of the Strategy and Development Investment Committee under the Board of Directors plays a pivotal role in formulating restructuring strategies for operational models geared toward specialization, enhanced transparency, and the rigorous protection of shareholder interests.
