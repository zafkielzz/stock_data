### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2022 recorded impressive operating results for Lien Vien Post Joint Stock Commercial Bank (LPB), with the bank achieving the highest pre-tax profit in its 15-year operational history at VND 5,690 billion, representing a 56% year-on-year growth and exceeding the annual target by 19%. Core growth momentum was propelled by maintaining a credit growth rate of 12.79% alongside rigorous asset quality control. Furthermore, the bank continued to accelerate key business segments, including retail banking, corporate banking, as well as life and non-life insurance sectors, to drive operational efficiency and revenue diversification. The successful simultaneous implementation of rigorous international standards further reinforced the bank's market position and enhanced its competitive edge within the financial landscape.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2022, LPB successfully executed its plan to elevate its charter capital to VND 17,291 billion, thereby substantially improving its stable funding structure through an increase in medium- and long-term capital liabilities. Total assets stood at VND 327,000 billion as of December 31, 2022. The bank maintained a robust Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and proactively managed capital liquidity with agility across its operations. Amidst highly volatile and unpredictable monetary and interest rate environments in 2022, the bank actively assessed risks, deployed hedging instruments via foreign exchange derivatives and interest rate swaps, and flexibly adjusted its interest rate policies in tandem with market developments and State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) directives to optimize capital allocation efficiency and safeguard liquidity safety ratios.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Credit risk management and provisioning were executed rigorously throughout the network, spanning from head office down to business units. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was tightly contained at 1.45%, underpinned by early risk identification via data analytics systems and direct audits across the three lines of defense. The bank proactively reinforced risk provisioning to mitigate potential exposures from overdue loans. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, the bank recorded items including loan guarantees, foreign exchange transaction commitments (spot/forward foreign currency purchases and sales, swaps), Letter of Credit (L/C) issuance commitments, other guarantees, and sundry commitments. Effective market, liquidity, and interest rate risk management—alongside the completion of the Basel III project and the deployment of technological components for anti-money laundering (AML)—enhanced the bank's capacity to absorb potential adverse shocks.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** LPB strictly adhered to Directive No. 03/CT-NHNN and Decision No. 1604/QĐ-NHNN regarding the promotion of green credit growth and environmental and social (E&S) risk management in credit granting activities. The bank prioritized environmental stewardship, improved resource and energy efficiency, and systematically integrated E&S risk management into its overarching risk governance framework to advance sustainable development objectives for 2023.
- **Social (S):** The bank remained committed to elevating employee compensation and welfare standards while consistently maintaining and expanding community-focused social welfare initiatives. Training programs designed to elevate staff awareness and accountability were deployed systematically across the entire organization.
- **Governance (G):** LPB successfully executed the simultaneous implementation of Basel III and IFRS 9—two globally rigorous risk management benchmarks—underscoring its operational transparency and elevating its international standing, as evidenced by Moody’s upgrading its credit rating to Ba3 (Stable Outlook). Corporate governance strictly adhered to regulatory compliance, synchronizing internal control systems with comprehensive risk management.
