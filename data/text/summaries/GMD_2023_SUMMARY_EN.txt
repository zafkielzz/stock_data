### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Amid a macroeconomic backdrop characterized by a 6.6% contraction in national import-export turnover and a 5% global trade deceleration in 2023, Gemadept Corporation (HOSE: GMD) delivered financial performance exceeding targets. Consolidated Profit Before Tax (PBT) reached VND 3,147 billion, bolstered by a non-operating windfall gain from the divestment of its entire equity interest in Nam Hai Dinh Vu Port. Excluding this extraordinary gain, core PBT stood at VND 1,307 billion, remaining flat year-over-year and achieving 115% of the General Meeting of Shareholders' (GMS) target.

Gemadept’s core growth drivers continue to stem from its vertically integrated Port Operations and Logistics ecosystem:
*   **Port Operations Segment:** Contributed 73% to total operating PBT. The segment maintained its role as a key earnings pillar, underpinned by the operational efficiency of the Nam Dinh Vu port cluster (Phase 2 commercialization) and the Gemalink deep-water port.
*   **Logistics Segment:** Contributed 27% to PBT, registering robust double-digit growth. Primary catalysts included contract logistics services, cold storage facilities (Mekong Logistics), ICD transportation, and the automotive distribution center (KGL).

The company’s gross profit margin expanded, supported by a 2.2% reduction in the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) to Net Revenue ratio. The aggressive implementation of digital transformation initiatives—including SmartPort, River Gate, and Smart Gate systems—alongside stringent operating expenditure (opex) rationalization measures, enabled GMD to preserve core earnings momentum amidst an intensely competitive landscape.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Gemadept maintains a prudent balance sheet structure with conservative financial leverage. As of December 31, 2023, Total Assets expanded by 4% year-to-date to VND 13,546 billion, with Non-Current Assets accounting for 75% (VND 10,166 billion). Current Assets grew by 29% to VND 3,380 billion, primarily driven by an accumulation of bank deposits and short-term loans funded by asset divestment proceeds.

*   **Debt & Equity Composition:** Total Liabilities dropped sharply by 25% to VND 3,814 billion. This contraction was predominantly driven by the settlement of short-term borrowings and the release of a VND 1,000 billion deposit associated with the Nam Hai Dinh Vu Port transaction. Total Equity reached VND 9,732 billion (+22% YoY), lifting the Equity-to-Total-Assets ratio to a robust 72%. The Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio remained pristine at 0.4x.
*   **Cost of Debt:** The Interest Expense-to-Net Revenue ratio inched up by a negligible 0.1%, despite the execution of multiple large-scale infrastructure projects. Backed by a rock-solid balance sheet and strong credit profile, GMD continued to secure credit facilities at preferential interest rates, effectively mitigating cash flow pressures and floating-rate interest rate volatility amid monetary tightening cycles.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

*   **Bad Debt & Inventory Provisions:** In 2023, the company incurred negligible provisioning expenses for doubtful accounts, which exerted no material impact on overall earnings and underscored robust credit risk management and receivables collection.
*   **Short-Term Provisions:** The company recorded a decline in short-term provisions, reflecting the completion of disbursements and lower provisioning requirements for channel dredging, container yard maintenance, and construction warranty obligations.
*   **Contingent Liabilities & Post-Balance Sheet Events:** No material off-balance-sheet legal disputes or guarantee commitments posing operational risks were reported. The watershed financial event of the year was the successful finalization of the Nam Hai Dinh Vu Port divestment, which unlocked substantial liquidity to redeploy into capital expenditure (CapEx) for the Nam Dinh Vu port cluster (Phase 3) and pre-construction works for the Gemalink deep-water port (Phase 2).

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** Gemadept acts as an industry pioneer in deploying the "Green Port" and "Green Logistics" frameworks. The company fosters a circular economy and optimizes energy consumption through the full digitalization of port operations (SmartPort, Smart Gate), curtailing vessel and truck turnaround times and directly abating greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.
*   **Social (S):** The company places strong emphasis on Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) across its port and warehousing network, continuously enhancing employee welfare, human capital development, and equitable workplace standards. GMD has consistently ranked among Forbes Vietnam’s Top 50 Listed Companies.
*   **Governance (G):** The Board of Directors for the 2023–2028 term comprises 10 members, including 3 independent directors (achieving the mandatory 1/3 threshold), ensuring objective oversight and structural transparency. The board exhibits diversity in its composition (1 foreign national, 2 female directors). Gemadept’s Annual Report and Sustainability Report ranked in the Top 20 market-wide for excellence, reflecting top-tier compliance with corporate transparency and disclosure standards.
