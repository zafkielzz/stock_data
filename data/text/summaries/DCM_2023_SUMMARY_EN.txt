### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In FY2023, Petrovietnam Ca Mau Fertilizer Joint Stock Company (DCM) reported total revenue of VND 13,172 billion and profit before tax of VND 1.255 billion. These operating results reflect management's agility in navigating domestic demand contraction by aggressively scaling up export and international operations to alleviate inventory pressures.

*   **Production & Domestic Operations:** The Ca Mau Fertilizer Plant remained the core operational driver, maintaining an operating rate of 110% to 115% of nameplate capacity (surpassing the cumulative milestone of 10 million metric tons of Urea) and achieving over 350 consecutive days of safe operation certified by Haldor Topsoe. Additionally, the Ca Mau NPK Plant, with a nameplate capacity of 300,000 metric tons per annum utilizing liquid urea technology, serves to complete the crop nutrition portfolio, progressively capturing market share in strategic agricultural regions including the Mekong Delta, Southeast, and Central Highlands.
*   **Export Markets:** International trade emerged as a breakthrough growth driver during the period. Export volume reached 344 thousand metric tons, accounting for approximately 26% of total sales volume; export value reached USD 136 million, representing roughly 25% of fertilizer revenue with a footprint spanning 18 countries. Cambodia remained the paramount export destination, contributing over 60% of the company's total export volume and value.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

DCM's capital structure maintains a high degree of safety with a low financial leverage profile, while operating cash flow adequately covers working capital self-funding requirements.

*   **Debt Breakdown:** The company predominantly utilizes short-term bank borrowings to finance feedstock natural gas procurement. The primary credit provider is Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank) – Thu Thiem Branch, utilizing unsecured short-term loan facilities (2-month tenor) designated for gas feedstock settlements with Vietnam National Oil and Gas Group (Petrovietnam).
*   **Interest Rates & Cash Flow Pressures:** Short-term VND-denominated borrowing rates remained highly favorable, ranging from 2.8% to 3.0% per annum. Small-scale maturing long-term debt obligations, held by a subsidiary (Petrovietnam Packaging Joint Stock Company) at VietinBank Bac Lieu, are secured by property, plant, and equipment. The company faces negligible floating interest rate risk or debt refinancing risk. Liquidity risk is prudently managed through substantial bank deposit balances, notwithstanding a restricted deposit of VND 332 billion at OceanBank – Ca Mau Branch, which management assesses as fully recoverable.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

*   **Inventory & Receivables Provisioning:** During the year, DCM recorded inventory write-downs for NPK finished goods and merchandise where carrying costs exceeded net realizable values as of the reporting date. Short-term receivables were assessed conservatively, with the company maintaining adequate allowance for doubtful accounts regarding overdue balances.
*   **Legal Contingencies & Feedstock Gas Pricing:** Contingent liabilities are closely tied to gas feedstock costs. DCM has fully remitted the "development potential value" payment to Petrovietnam in accordance with the Government Inspectorate’s Conclusion and Official Dispatch No. 261/DKVN-KHĐT. Currently, DCM and Petrovietnam are in the process of finalizing official gas pricing for the 2015–2018 preferential gas pricing period. Furthermore, the company has accrued provisions for gas feedstock expenses for the first seven months of 2023 under the PM3 CAA and Block 46 Gas Sales and Purchase Agreement (GSPA), figures which remain subject to adjustment pending final settlement.
*   **Litigation & Guarantees:** No material litigations or legal disputes arose that would adversely affect the company's going-concern status.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** DCM continues to champion agricultural "greening" by optimizing energy efficiency at the Ca Mau Fertilizer Plant, thereby reducing greenhouse gas emissions and fuel consumption per unit of output. Sustained high-capacity operations have significantly minimized wasted energy. Advanced, eco-friendly fertilizer product lines (such as N46.PLUS and N.HUMATE+TE) continue to be developed to align with sustainable farming trends.
*   **Social (S):** The company maintains exemplary workplace safety standards (achieving a record of over 350 safe operating days), guarantees competitive employee welfare, and invests in human capital development. FY2023 marked the second consecutive year DCM was honored as a "Community-Centric Enterprise" in recognition of its active sponsorship of social welfare programs and ongoing support for local farmers.
*   **Governance (G):** Corporate governance practices are aligned with best practices. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence, with the Audit & Risk Management Committee Chair (Ms. Do Thi Hoa) and the Nomination & Remuneration Committee Chair (Mr. Truong Hong) both serving as Independent Board Members. In March 2024, DCM formally established an ESG Committee under the Board of Directors to elevate its sustainability strategy. The Internal Audit Department, operational since 2020, functions as a specialized unit in compliance with Decree No. 05/2019/NĐ-CP, reporting independently to the Board of Directors.
