### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal Year 2021–2022 witnessed a severe correction in the steel sector, which was clearly reflected in the price performance of Hoa Sen Group (HSG) shares, plummeting by **-63.26%** (opening at VND 22,210 and closing at VND 8,160, within an extreme trading range from a peak of VND 25,600 to a trough of VND 4,840). Nevertheless, equity liquidity remained highly robust, with average matched trading volume exceeding 10.3 million shares per session, indicating substantial market participation and capital inflows.

HSG’s core operational driver lies in its vertically integrated manufacturing value chain, spanning from Hot-Rolled Coil (HRC) to Cold-Rolled Coil (CRC), ultimately yielding high-quality galvanized steel sheets compliant with stringent international standards (JIS, ASTM, EN, AS). This closed-loop process enables the enterprise to optimize production costs across each stage and proactively manage unit costs to sustain its "low-cost leadership" strategy.

From a market perspective, HSG’s export segment reinforced its dominant position by capturing nearly half of the sector's total galvanized sheet export volume, reaching over 87 countries and territories, having previously achieved a record monthly export volume of 121,000 tonnes with revenues surpassing the USD 100 million/month milestone during peak periods. In the domestic market, new growth momentum has decisively shifted toward retail distribution through the **Hoa Sen Home** Building Materials & Interior Supermarket chain. The rapid expansion of this retail network past the milestone of **110 stores** nationwide marks a strategic initiative to mitigate the inherent cyclicality of the traditional steel manufacturing segment and deeply penetrate the high-potential building materials retail value chain.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
HSG’s capital structure exhibits characteristics typical of a large-scale manufacturing and trading enterprise, heavily influenced by working capital volatility. On the balance sheet, liabilities are predominantly concentrated in short-term debt, with "Short-term borrowings" and "Accounts payable to trade suppliers" comprising the largest proportions. These financial instruments serve as the primary funding sources for raw material inventories (HRC) and finished goods warehousing designed to support the extensive distribution network.

Although the documentation does not disclose specific credit-granting banks or floating interest rates, the reliance on short-term debt to sustain inventory turnover amid a volatile steel market engenders relatively high cash flow management pressures. HSG’s capital structure risk lies in its sensitivity to short-term interest rate fluctuations and financing costs (particularly interest expenses). Consequently, the capacity to negotiate credit limits and optimize payment terms with major suppliers remains critical to mitigating direct cash flow pressures on equity.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Due to volatile raw material prices throughout fiscal year 2022, inventory risk represents a key area requiring close monitoring. HSG applies a prudent accounting principle by valuing inventories at the lower of cost and net realizable value (NRV), necessitating sizeable provisions for "Provision for devaluation of inventories" during market downturns.

Additionally, the sales policy executed via provincial agencies and branches necessitates the maintenance of an "Allowance for doubtful debts" to safeguard operating cash flow against customer credit risks. Regarding long-term investments, the parent company has provisioned for the impairment of investments in subsidiaries (such as manufacturing units in Phu My, Nhon Hoi, Binh Dinh, and Yen Bai) upon the incurrence of losses, thereby ensuring the integrity of the separate financial statements. Off-balance-sheet guarantees or contingent liabilities associated with commercial disputes exhibited no abnormal fluctuations, indicating that management is proactively cleaning up the balance sheet through aggressive provisioning prior to entering the new fiscal year.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Hoa Sen Group is a pioneer within the steel sector in publishing sustainability reports compliant with **GRI** standards and the Vietnam Corporate Sustainability Index (**CSI**), aiming to balance stakeholder interests.

*   **Environmental (E):** HSG strictly controls material and fuel consumption quotas across its manufacturing plants. The technical management system focuses on energy conservation, resource circularity within the closed-loop process, and the minimization of hazardous environmental waste.
*   **Social (S):** The enterprise maintains a robust commitment to Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) and environmental protection via dedicated specialized departments (Department of Occupational Safety, Sanitation, and Fire Prevention). Human resources operations target stable employee welfare while actively contributing to community development and social security initiatives.
*   **Governance (G):** HSG’s governance framework complies effectively with Decree 155/2020/NĐ-CP, featuring a 6-member Board of Directors (BOD) that ensures independence through 3 non-executive members (including 2 independent members). The oversight system operates professionally via BOD-affiliated committees, namely: the Audit Committee (overseeing financial reporting, internal controls, and liaising with the independent auditor), the Strategic Development Committee, and the Governance, Nomination & Remuneration Committee. Support from these specialized committees enhances transparency, regulatory compliance, and the minimization of internal conflicts of interest.
