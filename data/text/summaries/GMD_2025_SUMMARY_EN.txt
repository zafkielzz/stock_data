GMD FINANCIAL AND QUALITATIVE EQUITY RESEARCH REPORT – FY2025

**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**

Fiscal year 2025 marked the successful completion of GMD's 2021-2025 strategic vision, with financial performance outperforming guided targets. Operational efficiency is evidenced by a robust gross margin of 43.98%, an EPS of VND 3,762.25, an ROE of 2.89%, and an ROA of 2.23%. 

GMD’s core growth drivers stem from the strong synergy between its two strategic business segments:

*   **Port Operations Segment:** In the Northern region, the commissioning of Nam Dinh Vu Phase 3 in late 2025 expanded total cluster capacity to 2 million TEUs, cementing its position as the largest contiguous port cluster in the region. GMD's collaboration in upgrading the Ha Nam channel infrastructure continues to yield significant competitive advantages. In the Southern region, the Gemalink deep-water port remains a key volume driver and is aggressively preparing for the implementation of Phases 2 and 3, which will more than double its capacity over the next two years to capture the shifting trends in global supply chains.
*   **Logistics Segment:** The integrated ecosystem spanning six verticals (including the SCSC air cargo terminal, distribution centers, and multimodal transport) continues to optimize the value chain for clients. Despite significant macro headwinds in international trade—driven by protectionist trends and volatile freight rates—GMD successfully capitalized on the import-export recovery momentum, propelled by its industry-leading position and global partner network.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**

As of December 31, 2025, GMD’s total assets exceeded VND 19,826 billion, representing a 10% increase year-to-date. Reflecting the capital-intensive nature of the industry, non-current assets accounted for a dominant share of nearly 69% of total assets (reaching VND 13,620 billion, up 20%), driven primarily by capital expenditures in fixed assets, equipment, and increased equity contributions to joint ventures and associates. Current assets contracted by 7% to VND 6,206 billion, as the company actively deployed cash for these growth-oriented investments.

Regarding the capital structure, total liabilities rose by 16% to VND 4,908 billion, driven primarily by newly disbursed long-term borrowings to directly finance port infrastructure projects. GMD maintains an exceptionally conservative capital structure, with an actual Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 13.61% (total debt-to-equity ratio recorded at 0.3x), while equity accounts for 75% of total assets (reaching VND 14,918 billion, up 8% on retained earnings accumulation). While current documentation does not disclose granular details regarding the identity of the largest credit-providing banks or the fixed/floating interest rate breakdown of the new borrowings, this low leverage ratio enables GMD to tightly manage structural capital risks and minimize cash flow pressure during its peak capital expenditure cycle.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**

GMD enforces a rigorous risk control framework. The reporting system records no abnormal fluctuations regarding provisions for inventory obsolescence or doubtful debts. To mitigate legal risks and contingent liabilities stemming from major M&A transactions over the past five years (such as capital divestments at Nam Hai Port and Nam Hai Dinh Vu Port, and the acquisition of Hai Minh Port shares), GMD consistently engages reputable independent advisory firms to conduct transaction valuations, ensuring the optimization of economic benefits for shareholders.

Regarding post-balance sheet events, entering early 2026, escalating geopolitical risks in the Middle East along strategic maritime shipping routes represent a major variable capable of driving up global logistics costs and inducing supply chain disruptions. To counter this, the Executive Board proactively finalized the 2026 budget plan for Board of Directors approval as early as January 2026, remaining poised to review and adjust business targets on a semi-annual basis to adapt flexibly to market dynamics.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**

*   **Environmental (E):** GMD remains steadfast in executing the comprehensive "Greenification and Digitization" strategy across its Port-Logistics ecosystem. Although Scope 1, 2, and 3 emission metrics are not quantitatively detailed in this report, GMD has underscored its commitment to sustainable development, earning a spot in the Top 3 Sustainability Reports (Non-Financial Sector) at the 2025 Listed Companies Awards.
*   **Social (S):** The company focuses on optimizing welfare policies and cultivating high-quality human capital to support the 2026-2030 strategy, while rigorously fulfilling its corporate social responsibilities—most notably through emergency relief operations for communities affected by natural disasters and floods in the Central region.
*   **Governance (G):** GMD aligns closely with international best practices under the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) and OECD standards. Transparency is underpinned by the maintenance of bilingual English-Vietnamese disclosure compliant with HOSE standards. Governance structures are reinforced by the effective operation of the Audit Committee under the Board of Directors, working alongside independent specialized departments for Risk Management (RM), Human Resources (HR), and Legal Compliance, collectively helping GMD secure the "Top 10 Large-Cap Non-Financial Enterprises with Best IR Activities in 2025" award.
