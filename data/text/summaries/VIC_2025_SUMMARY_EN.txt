1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Vingroup successfully executed a revenue diversification strategy across multiple sectors and markets to mitigate concentration risk. In addition to its three traditional core business pillars, the Group expanded into three strategic new pillars: Infrastructure, Green Energy, and Culture. Within the real estate segment, Vinhomes maintained its anchor role via a multi-polar township model situated in proximity to major urban centers, including Vinhomes Ocean Park 1, 2, and 3, and Vinhomes Grand Park. During 2025, Vinhomes broke ground on several mega-projects, namely Vinhomes Green City (Tay Ninh), Vinhomes Wonder City (Hanoi), Vinhomes Golden City (Hai Phong), and Vinhomes Green Paradise (HCMC), while concurrently pursuing co-development partnerships for minor components alongside direct sales to diversify cash inflows. In the Technology & Industrial segment, VinFast completed its smart electric vehicle lineup spanning from compacts to E-segment models, while launching and delivering specialized Green EVs tailored for transport services—notably the VinFast Limo Green 7-seat MPV, which recorded robust market uptake and ranked among the best-selling vehicles in Vietnam in 4Q25. In the hospitality segment, Vinpearl partnered with world-leading hotel operators Marriott and Meliá to leverage international brand equity and deep domestic market expertise, developing a comprehensive multi-experience tourism model integrating hospitality, entertainment, and performing arts.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Regarding financial structure and leverage, Vingroup’s Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio stood at 157.38% as of year-end 2025, reflecting elevated capital intensity as the Group concurrently executes multiple flagship projects and scales its business pillars. Key financial risks facing the Group comprise liquidity risk, interest rate risk, and foreign exchange risk. On a quarterly basis, the Treasury Department and specialized divisions conduct capital and financial market assessments to proactively optimize total debt portfolios and payable obligations. Prior to executing high-value or foreign-currency-denominated transactions, the Treasury consults with domestic and international banking and financial experts to formulate risk mitigation strategies and deploy appropriate instruments, such as derivatives. To curtail liquidity risk, Vingroup maintains proactive cash flow management and rigorous monitoring of short-term liabilities.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vingroup’s risk governance framework operates on a "three lines of defense" model to ensure independence and objectivity. The first line comprises the Chief Executive Officer, corporate departments, and P&L business units, which bear direct ownership and operational risk management responsibilities. The second line consists of the Board of Directors (executive members) and the Risk Management Department, tasked with formulating risk management policies, procedures, workflows, and tools. The third line is the Internal Audit Department, responsible for evaluating the efficacy and efficiency of the overall framework. The risk management process adheres to six sub-processes benchmarked against ISO 31000 standards: risk identification, risk analysis, risk prioritization, risk treatment, risk communication and consultation, and risk monitoring. Risk management policies are submitted by the CEO to the Board of Directors for approval at least annually, while the risk register is reviewed at least quarterly to promptly navigate macroeconomic shifts, regulatory changes, and intense competitive pressures from both multinational conglomerates and domestic peers.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vingroup aligns its developmental trajectory with global economic greening trends by scaling new pillars such as Green Energy and expanding smart electric vehicle offerings (VinFast), thereby contributing to greenhouse gas emissions abatement and accelerating Vietnam's clean energy transition.
- **Social (S):** The Group prioritizes communication and training initiatives to elevate employees' risk awareness and procedural comprehension, while actively surveying and engaging stakeholders to ensure comprehensive alignment regarding roles and responsibilities within its sustainable development ecosystem.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance adheres to an independent P&L model featuring distinct profit centers that account autonomously and bear full accountability for financial performance. The corporate headquarters acts in a supportive, supervisory, appraisal, and major-decision-approval capacity. The control architecture is reinforced by the active participation of the Board of Directors, the Risk Management Department, and Internal Audit, ensuring strict operational compliance with the Group's long-term strategic directives.
