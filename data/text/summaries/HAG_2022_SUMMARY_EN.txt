Below is the institutional-grade English translation of the qualitative financial report summary for Hoang Anh Gia Lai Joint Stock Company (HAG) for the fiscal year 2022.

***

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Hoang Anh Gia Lai’s business operations during the year recorded a significant strategic transition, marked by a restructuring of its revenue composition aimed at alleviating long-term financial pressure. Core growth drivers stemmed from two primary segments: pig farming (utilizing banana-fed livestock) and fruit cultivation (specifically export-grade bananas). The export market played a dominant role, with China and Southeast Asian nations acting as the largest consumption hubs for agricultural products. This enabled the company to maintain foreign currency inflows and improve gross profit margins amid volatility in the domestic market. However, HAG’s share price experienced sharp corrections over the year, opening at VND 13,450 and closing at VND 9,160 (a decline of 31.90% year-to-date), fluctuating between a low of VND 5,640 and a high of VND 16,200. Trading liquidity remained active with an average matched trading volume of 19,601,663 shares/session, reflecting both market expectations and caution regarding management's debt restructuring progress and profit target realization.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
HAG’s capital structure continues to bear substantial pressure from debt accumulated during its prior large-scale agricultural investment phase. The debt portfolio comprises both short-term and long-term loans from domestic commercial banks and major financial partners, with the majority subject to floating interest rates contingent upon money market developments. The burden of interest expenses and maturing principal repayment obligations poses a notable challenge to the enterprise's operating cash flow. Capital structure risks center on the mismatch between cash inflows generated from long-term cultivation and livestock projects versus short-term debt repayment schedules. This necessitates ongoing flexibility in negotiating debt maturity extensions, optimizing cash flows from the liquidation of non-core assets, and accelerating the exploitation of fast-yielding business segments to mitigate liquidity risk.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Risk management and provisioning practices during the year remained a focal point to accurately reflect asset values and the actual financial standing. Inventories and allowance for doubtful accounts were reviewed based on aging schedules and the actual recoverability from partners and customers to limit systemic credit risks. Regarding contingent liabilities, off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments and potential payment obligations related to related parties continued to be closely monitored in accordance with credit agreement terms. Following the balance sheet date (December 31), no extraordinary financial events or major legal disputes arose that would materially impact the company's going-concern status. Nevertheless, macroeconomic uncertainties and input material price volatility continue to harbor risks, warranting a prudent provisioning strategy.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** HAG directs its agricultural development toward a circular economy model, repurposing banana by-products as raw materials for pig feed processing, thereby minimizing environmental waste generation. Greenhouse gas emission metrics (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) are progressively being measured and controlled to align with long-term sustainable development goals and optimize energy efficiency across farming operations.
- **Social (S):** The company prioritizes occupational health and safety (OHS) policies to ensure safe working conditions for thousands of employees across project sites in remote areas of Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia. Human resource policies ensure gender equality, maintain regular skill-enhancement training programs, and provide stable social welfare, healthcare, and housing systems for workers.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance operates under the current legal framework, supported by the relative independence of the Board of Directors in strategic orientation. Independent audits are conducted transparently by leading audit firms. Information disclosure strictly complies with the regulations of the State Securities Commission and Stock Exchanges, progressively aligning with international standards for sustainable corporate governance (GRI / SDGs).
