### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
During FY2021, Phu Nhuan Jewelry Joint Stock Company (PNJ) navigated a highly challenging operating environment dictated by the severe headwinds of the global pandemic. Headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City—the epicenter of the outbreak—the company experienced a 22% contraction in operating days. Despite these adverse macro conditions and significant operational disruptions, most notably the near-complete suspension of business activities in Q3, PNJ delivered an impressive financial performance. Group gross profit reached over VND 3,598 billion, representing a positive year-on-year growth of 4.8% compared to FY2020. The core catalyst underpinning PNJ’s counter-cyclical resilience was its organizational reinvention strategy, anchored by the "F5 - Accelerate Reinvention - Breakthrough Farther" initiative formulated in 2018, initiated in 2020, and accelerated in 2021. Management proactively rolled out production cost optimization projects starting in May 2021 to compress manufacturing lead times, minimize material loss, and lower unit costs. Furthermore, the supply chain was dynamically redirected and localized, complemented by operational innovations such as cross-regional internal inventory lending, which effectively mitigated the impact of nationwide lockdowns. Bolstered by these rigorous foundational preparations during the social distancing period, Q4 2021 revenue surged by 21% year-on-year, underscoring the company’s robust shock absorption capacity and superior operational agility.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PNJ demonstrated a robust commitment to environmental stewardship through the rigorous monitoring of pollutant emissions. Periodic wastewater analysis reports from the Go Hap Plant (PNJP) confirmed that all parameters complied with QCVN 40:2011/BTNMT, Column B thresholds. Regarding air emissions, source-level measurements (such as kitchen exhaust ducts) remained well within the permissible limits stipulated by QCVN 19:2009/BTNMT. The company enforces exhaustive segregation and collection of municipal solid waste and hazardous waste (including chemical-soaked rags, spent light bulbs, and wastewater treatment sludge) via licensed waste management contractors. Notably, PNJ launched a "Say No to Single-Use Plastics" campaign and prioritized a "Green House" architectural model for its new headquarters to optimize energy efficiency and curtail emissions. Furthermore, an Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) subcommittee under the Board of Directors was newly established to elevate the sustainable development strategy and spearhead prospective green, reforestation, and clean water initiatives.
- **Social (S):** PNJ regards human capital as its most invaluable asset, placing people at the core of all operational frameworks. Throughout 2021, the PNJ Epidemic Prevention Committee swiftly executed compassionate human resources policies to safeguard the livelihoods, health, well-being, and income of all employees and their families, including facilitating safe vaccination programs and deploying effective Work-From-Home (WFH) regimes. Alongside fostering a secure, equitable, and competitive workplace—earning PNJ recognition among Vietnam's Top 100 Best Places to Work—the workforce (affectionately dubbed the "yellow-shirt warriors") extended profound community stewardship. They initiated the "0-VND Mini-Supermarket" campaign and participated in the "Vietnam Embrace" and "Accompanying Maternity" programs, providing direct relief to over 172,000 vulnerable households, 6,000 university students stranded in dormitories, and more than 7,000 frontline medical professionals.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance at PNJ is clearly steered by the strategic oversight of the Board of Directors and Executive Management. The establishment of the ESG Subcommittee, featuring the active participation of Board members, represents a pivotal step toward standardizing sustainability practices in alignment with international benchmarks. PNJ upholds transparency via a KPI-driven human resource evaluation system and a Core Competency Framework, alongside maintaining close coordination with state regulatory authorities, ensuring full compliance with tax obligations, statutory regulations, and industry operating standards.
