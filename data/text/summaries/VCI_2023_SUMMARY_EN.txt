### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Throughout the 2023 fiscal year, the share price performance of Viet Capital Securities Joint Stock Company (VCI) exhibited a robust recovery and growth trajectory in tandem with the broader market. Specifically, the stock opened the year at VND 12,570 and closed at VND 23,470, representing a full-year price appreciation of +86.71%, with a 52-week high of VND 27,830 and a low of VND 12,570 (average matched trading volume stood at 5,625,573 shares/session). Vietcap’s core growth momentum continued to stem from its synchronized strategy of enhancing product and service quality, pioneering technological adoption within the securities sector, and aggressively scaling key business divisions, including Brokerage, Investment Banking, Corporate Finance, and Research & Analysis. Notably, Vietcap’s Research division maintained its leading market position by covering 79 listed companies—accounting for approximately 80% of the VN-Index’s total market capitalization and roughly two-thirds of Vietnam's total stock market capitalization—thereby reinforcing its brand equity and attracting a substantial institutional and retail client base.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VCI’s capital structure and treasury resource management in 2023 were closely tied to short-term funding operations and liquidity-enhancement instruments. Per the financial statement disclosures, the Company utilized term bank deposits with an original maturity of no more than three (3) months (yielding interest rates ranging from 4.00% to 4.75% per annum, down from 5.00% to 6.00% p.a. in 2022) as pledged assets to collateralize short-term borrowings. Concurrently, Vietcap maintained escrow deposits at custodian banks to guarantee settlement obligations for Covered Warrants (CW) issued by the Company, maintaining a minimum encumbrance of 50% of the total issued warrant value throughout their operational lifecycle as mandated by Circular 107/2016/TT-BTC. Capital structure is stringently managed to balance working capital requirements for margin lending and proprietary trading activities, ensuring compliance with the State Securities Commission's (SSC) liquidity coefficient benchmarks.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Risk management and provisioning were executed prudently by Vietcap to accurately reflect the fair value of financial assets. The portfolio of financial assets at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL) and held-to-maturity (HTM) investments was closely monitored against market price volatility and amortized cost, supplemented by impairment allowances on receivables and financial assets when deemed necessary. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, obligations pertaining to the issuance of covered warrants were secured by strictly restricted escrow accounts held with custodian banks. No extraordinary legal events or material disputes arose subsequent to the fiscal year-end of December 31, 2023, that would exert a material adverse effect on the Company's financial position.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vietcap champions sustainable development by optimizing operations and digitizing workflow processes (such as integrating AI assistants into the E-Learning portal and undergoing digital transformation across financial service offerings) to curtail paper consumption and energy usage across its office footprint.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is identified as a core strategic pillar. In 2023, Vietcap intensified periodic internal training programs across the Brokerage, Business, Investment Banking and Corporate Finance divisions (obtaining Appraiser certifications), the Research division (incentivizing CFA charter attainment), and the Information Technology division (network administration). The Company emphasizes transparent recruitment policies, fostering partnerships with premier universities (Foreign Trade University, National Economics University, RMIT, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, etc.), while offering competitive compensation packages and equitable promotion pathways to attract and retain elite talent.
- **Governance (G):** Vietcap maintains a transparent corporate governance framework under the executive leadership of the Board of Management (led by CEO Mr. To Hai) and rigorous oversight by the accounting and auditing apparatus. The Company strictly adheres to Vietnamese securities regulations while progressively standardizing its operations in alignment with international best practices and long-term sustainable development objectives.
