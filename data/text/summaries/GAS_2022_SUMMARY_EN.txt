### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
During fiscal year 2022, Petrovietnam Gas Joint Stock Corporation (GAS) demonstrated a total annual price return of +7.60% (opening at VND 63,250, closing at VND 68,060, within an intraday trading range of VND 61,360 to VND 87,460), accompanied by a robust average matching trading liquidity of 754,454 shares per session. This price action reflects the enterprise's high resilience against global geopolitical headwinds causing energy supply-demand imbalances, alongside severe volatility in Brent crude oil and CP LPG prices. GAS's medium-to-long-term growth catalysts are structurally defined by a dual-pillar strategic governance model for the 2021–2025 period. The first pillar maintains a leadership position across the traditional gas value chain, targeting 100% market share in dry gas, over 50% in LNG, and over 70% in nationwide LPG, aiming for a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) in revenue of 7% to 9% and a return on equity (ROE) exceeding 20%. The second pillar focuses on deep processing, diversification of non-power gas products, and expansion into renewable energy to align with the global energy transition trend. Currently, GAS's core revenue stream is underpinned by the stable delivery of approximately 8 billion cubic meters ($bcm$) of gas annually to power generation, fertilizer, and low-pressure industrial off-takers.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
GAS maintains a sound financial profile supported by abundant liquidity stemming from bank deposits. Cash equivalents with maturities under 3 months yielded interest rates ranging from 0.2% to 6.0% per annum, while held-to-maturity investments (term deposits between 3 and 12 months) generated attractive yields of 4.0% to 10.6% per annum. Regarding debt obligations, the corporation capitalized on its top-tier credit standing to secure long-term unsecured borrowings from major financial institutions. Key lending partners comprise Mizuho Bank, Ltd (floating rate of LIBOR + 1.74%, maturing in 2027), Taipei Fubon Commercial Bank (LIBOR + 1.7%, 42-month tenor), SeABank (nearly VND 896 billion, maturing in 2027), SHB (nearly VND 611 billion), and Eximbank (nearly VND 294 billion). Cash flow pressure and capital structure risk remain minimal, with collateralized assets accounting for a negligible proportion, comprising approximately VND 29.9 billion in tangible fixed assets and nearly VND 34.0 billion in intangible assets. Interest rate risk predominantly stems from foreign currency-denominated loans subject to floating rates tied to LIBOR fluctuations. Furthermore, GAS temporarily deferred the appropriation of its development and investment fund from 2021 net income to facilitate a planned charter capital increase sourced from retained reserves, slated for shareholder approval in 2023.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
As of December 31, 2022, GAS's credit risk exposure from overdue short-term trade receivables stood at VND 1,111,126,019,618, marking a significant decrease from VND 1,309,958,396,651 in the prior year. Overdue counterparties primarily consist of intra-group affiliates and oil and gas sector partners, including Nhon Trach Power JSC, Nhon Trach 2 Power JSC, Petroleum Engineering Consultancy JSC (PVE), and Petroleum Construction Corporation 2. Additionally, other overdue short-term receivables were recorded at over VND 54.8 billion. Regarding capital-work-in-progress, GAS is concentrating substantial capital on flagship infrastructure assets, notably the 1 million metric tons per annum (MMtpa) Thi Vai LNG Terminal, the Block B - O Mon pipeline, the Thi Vai - Phu My LNG pipeline, and Nam Con Son 2 (Phase 2). Contingent liabilities associated with take-or-pay gas purchase commitments with Vietnam National Energy Group (Petrovietnam / PVN) continue to be reflected through long-term prepaid expenses and short-term unearned revenue. Post-balance-sheet events include a notable C-suite personnel transition with the appointment of Mr. Tran Nhat Huy as Deputy General Director effective December 16, 2022, alongside Mr. Nguyen Thanh Nghi stepping down from his executive role. Regarding project ownership structures, GAS completed the acquisition of a 20% contributory capital stake from PVE in the PV Gas Tower project in May 2022, elevating its ownership interest under this Business Cooperation Contract (BCC) to 90%.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
*   **Environmental (E):** GAS consumed 92.22 million kWh of electricity during the year. The company proactively transitioned its power generation systems from diesel generators to harnessing centralized associated gas from extraction platforms, generating hundreds of billions of VND in annual production cost savings while mitigating direct greenhouse gas emissions. However, circular economy efficiency regarding water resources remains constrained, as total water consumption reached 343 thousand cubic meters ($m^3$) while the water recycling and reuse rate remained at 0%.
*   **Social (S):** GAS remains steadfast in its mission to deliver clean energy to drive economic development and secure balanced stakeholder interests. Management identifies the breakthrough solution centered on "human capital" as one of three core pillars to foster sustainable growth, although granular data regarding gender diversity or occupational health and safety (OHS) metrics are not explicitly disclosed in current documentation.
*   **Governance (G):** The Board of Directors demonstrated active oversight by issuing over 170 resolutions and convening 07 in-person meetings during the year to direct strategic alignment with the Fourth Industrial Revolution and the energy transition. Concurrently, GAS implemented a revised Key Performance Indicator (KPI) appraisal system and refined compensation policies to optimize executive management capabilities, while preparing for the deployment of an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system synchronized with its parent entity, PVN. Existing disclosures do not explicitly reference the adoption of international reporting frameworks such as GRI or SDGs.
