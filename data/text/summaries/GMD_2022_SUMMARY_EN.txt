### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

In 2022, despite headwinds from the global economic downturn and the contraction in international maritime freight volumes in Q4, Gemadept Corporation (GMD) delivered robust operational results, underpinned by its integrated ecosystem strategy. Port operations served as the core growth engine, contributing nearly 80% of total consolidated revenue with a system-wide throughput exceeding 3 million TEUs. In the Southern region, growth was propelled by the Gemalink deep-water port, which handled 2 million TEUs in its inaugural year of operations, complemented by the optimal operational efficiency of Binh Duong Port and PIP. In the Northern region, the Hai Phong port cluster (Nam Hai, Nam Hai Dinh Vu, Nam Dinh Vu, Nam Hai ICD) maintained steady performance driven by the integration of Port Logistics services and the groundbreaking of Nam Dinh Vu Phase 2.

The Logistics segment accounted for the remaining 20% of revenue, propelled by specialized verticals including the SCSC air cargo terminal, heavy-lift transportation, cold-chain logistics, and automotive logistics. Operating expense optimization and robust profit contributions from joint ventures and associates enhanced GMD's profitability profile, resulting in a gross margin of 44% and a net profit margin of 30%. Operating efficiency metrics demonstrated solid expansion, with ROA and ROE reaching 9.8% and 15.5%, respectively. On the equity market, GMD shares exhibited a mild contraction of -2.00% for the full year (open: VND 35,030; close: VND 34,330; trading range: VND 27,520 to VND 45,880) alongside robust liquidity, boasting an average daily trading volume of over 1.8 million shares.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

GMD maintained a sound capital structure to safeguard its balance sheet for ongoing scaling initiatives. As of December 31, 2022, total equity stood at VND 7,948 billion (up 13% YoY, driven by retained earnings accumulation), representing a stable 61% of total assets. Non-current assets expanded by 15% to VND 10,412 billion, primarily reflecting capital expenditures deployed into construction-in-progress for the Nam Dinh Vu Phase 2 project. Total liabilities increased by 38% to VND 5,083 billion, largely driven by higher short-term advances received and trade payables owed to contractors and material handling equipment suppliers for the company's key Northern infrastructure project.

The debt-to-equity ratio was prudently controlled at 0.6x. Although the current ratio (0.82x) and cash ratio (0.42x) remained moderate, GMD’s cash flow pressure was substantially mitigated by its strong cash-generative capacity. Debt-service coverage improved markedly, with the interest coverage ratio surging to 11.01x (a 3.25x increase compared to 2021). Regarding market risk, currency fluctuations resulted in a net foreign exchange loss of VND 10.6 billion. However, the impact was negligible given the low proportion of USD-denominated debt—primarily utilized to finance machinery and equipment for Nam Dinh Vu and Gemalink—which enjoys a natural hedge via direct foreign currency revenues from international shipping lines. During the year, the Board of Directors approved major credit facilities with Kasikornbank (HCMC Branch) and Vietnam Maritime Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MSB - HCMC Branch).

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

A prominent operational risk in 2H22 stemmed from declining import-export demand, which caused shipping lines to return empty containers and accumulate massive volumes of container yards across Vietnam, triggering widespread congestion at depots in the Hai Phong cluster, such as Nam Hai ICD. GMD proactively deployed service-routing and handling measures to contain rising storage costs induced by oil price volatility.

Regarding corporate restructuring and major financial events, the Board of Directors approved the divestment of the entire equity stake in Nam Dinh Vu Port Joint Stock Company during 2022 to optimize the asset portfolio and reallocate resources toward priority mega-projects. Additionally, resolutions pertaining to rights offerings for existing shareholders were enacted and adjusted to align with prevailing market conditions, aimed at supplementing long-term working capital.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** GMD advanced its "Green Port" strategy through the establishment of the Digital Transformation Committee and the ESG Committee in March 2022. The company successfully scaled its SmartPort application across its entire port network, streamlining administrative workflows, reducing vessel and truck turnaround times, curtailing electricity consumption, and minimizing direct environmental emissions.
*   **Social (S):** The company focused on human capital development by standardizing its BSC/KPI performance management framework down to individual levels. Concurrently, GMD rolled out the Successor Workforce Development Project and the Competency Framework Project, complemented by flexible, output-based remuneration policies and competitive compensation adjustments to elevate employee welfare, ensure occupational safety, and uphold its commitment to workforce sustainability.
*   **Governance (G):** GMD’s governance structure maintained high transparency, overseen by a 10-member Board of Directors comprising 1 independent director (Mr. Nguyen Van Hung) and 3 non-executive directors, ensuring objective oversight. During the year, the Board convened 9 meetings and issued 22 rigorous resolutions/minutes under the strict supervision of the Supervisory Board, while fulfilling all regulatory disclosure and sustainability reporting obligations in a timely manner.
