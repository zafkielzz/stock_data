1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Dat Xanh Group's (DXG) equity valuation exhibited high volatility throughout fiscal year 2025, opening at VND 11,220 and closing at VND 15,030, representing an annual return of +33.96%. The stock traded within a broad range between a 52-week high of VND 21,530 and a low of VND 9,230, accompanied by an average matching liquidity of 18,170,011 shares per session. Price action reflects market anticipation regarding the structural reorganization of the company's operations. On a fundamental basis, trailing twelve-month (TTM) EPS stood at VND 367.97, implying a relatively demanding P/E multiple of 42.94x and a P/B ratio of 0.81x. Although return metrics remain modest—with ROE at 0.30% and ROA at 0.15%—the primary bright spot is the exceptionally high gross profit margin, which held firm at 55.17%.

DXG’s core business drivers shifted decisively over the past year from traditional brokerage services toward high-end residential property development. The flagship project "The Privé" serves as a strategic milestone initiating a new developmental cycle, elevating DXG’s product portfolio toward larger-scale and higher-value offerings. In the short term (2026–2027), key growth drivers will depend on the absorption velocity at The Privé, alongside projects with fully secured legal status in Ho Chi Minh City and satellite urban areas, aimed at optimizing operating free cash flow.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

DXG’s capital structure displays a conservative profile, with the Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio controlled at a prudent 44.17%. Short-term liquidity is underpinned by cash and bank deposits, where cash equivalents under 3 months yield annualized interest rates ranging from 3.0% to 4.75%, and term deposits of 3 to 12 months fluctuate between 3.3% and 6.6% per annum. However, underlying liquidity risks persist as a substantial portion of these bank deposits and held-to-maturity investments are encumbered as collateral for credit facilities.

Notably, capitalized borrowing costs surged to VND 329,005,792,809 during the year (compared to VND 115,582,994,914 in the prior year), representing a sharp increase of 184.6%. Capitalizing a major portion of financing costs into properties under development alleviates immediate financial expense pressures on the Income Statement, but simultaneously indicates that corporate capital is tightly locked in long-term projects. This creates refinancing and debt-service pressure, tightening actual operating cash flows if project pre-sales or handover schedules face delays.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

DXG’s operational risks are clearly manifested in its inventory concentration, heavily skewed toward real estate under development across key projects including The Privé, Gem Sky World, Home Park City, Regal Legend, and Tam Nhinh Xanh. The company has mortgaged land use rights and future-formed assets of these projects to secure both bank borrowings and corporate bond issuances. As of year-end 2025, DXG recorded VND 5,478,450,376 in restricted bank deposits earmarked exclusively for project-specific purposes, thereby diminishing available cash flow flexibility.

Furthermore, the company maintains provisions for doubtful debts covering trade receivables and short-term advances to suppliers. Contingent liabilities reside in large-scale advances to individuals for land use right acquisitions and business cooperation contributions for project development. Regarding post-balance sheet events, on January 30, 2026, DXG completed the comprehensive disclosure of related-party transactions and its 2025 corporate governance report to regulatory authorities (the State Securities Commission - SSC and the Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange - HOSE), ensuring post-reporting-period legal transparency.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

On the Environmental (E) front, while specific Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions data are yet to be disclosed, DXG has integrated green building standards into its project design, construction, and operational workflows. The group focuses on executing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), including SDG 6 (efficient water management in urban areas), SDG 7 (deployment of energy-saving solutions), SDG 12 (sustainable materials control), and SDG 13 (climate risk identification in real estate investment).

On the Social (S) front, the corporate value system prioritizes human capital. DXG is committed to ensuring a safe living environment for residents and rigorous labor standards for employees (SDG 3), promoting internal training programs (SDG 4), enforcing gender equality within the organization (SDG 5), and maintaining stable employment alongside productivity enhancement (SDG 8).

On the Governance (G) side, DXG’s governance framework operates tightly under a three-tiered model subject to independent oversight by the Audit Committee. Governance discipline is underscored by a 100% attendance and resolution-approval rate by the Board of Directors. The company is progressively aligning with international benchmarks by referencing the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard and updating in accordance with the VNCG Code 2026, thereby reinforcing transparency in the disclosure of insider and related-party transactions.
