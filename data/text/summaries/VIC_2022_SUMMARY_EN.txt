### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, the macroeconomic landscape navigated considerable headwinds stemming from lingering post-pandemic disruptions and the Russia-Ukraine military conflict, which exacerbated global supply chain frictions and pushed global inflation up from 4.7% in 2021 to multi-decade highs. Domestically, propelled by proactive fiscal and monetary interventions, Vietnam’s full-year GDP expanded by an impressive 8.02%—outperforming the broader Southeast Asian region for the 2011–2022 period—while inflation was successfully contained at 2.59%. Nevertheless, capital markets, equities, and the real estate sector continued to face structural liquidity and valuation pressures.

Amidst this operating environment, Vingroup demonstrated resilience, driving operational recovery across its core verticals: Industry & Technology, Commerce & Services, and Philanthropic Initiatives. The standout catalyst was the Industry & Technology segment, underscored by VinFast’s historic pivot to a pure-play electric vehicle (EV) manufacturer. VinFast recorded aggregate sales exceeding 24,000 units in the domestic market. Notably, VinFast successfully penetrated the highly stringent U.S. market by securing regulatory compliance and certifications from the EPA, CARB, and NHTSA, delivering its inaugural batch of VF 8 City Edition units to U.S. consumers, and expanding its footprint with 22 retail and after-sales service centers across North America and Europe. Within the Commerce & Services segment, Vinpearl executed a strategic asset-light pivot by partnering with top-tier global hospitality operators—transferring management rights for 13 hotels to Meliá and 6 hotels to Marriott—while simultaneously launching cutting-edge leisure offerings at VinWonders to capture the post-pandemic rebound in international tourism.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
To fortify its liquidity profile and fund growth initiatives, Vingroup successfully mobilized nearly $1.1 billion from international capital markets in 2022. A prime transaction included VinFast securing a $139 million climate financing package arranged by the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to support the production of electric buses and the expansion of the EV charging infrastructure.

Regarding cash flow and credit architecture, the Group maintained a diversified portfolio of short-term bank deposits, term deposits, and corporate bond investments, with deposit yields ranging from 1.2% to 10.7% per annum and bond yields spanning 7.5% to 9.3% per annum depending on tenure. In relation to related-party and partner financing, Vingroup extended collateralized short- and long-term loans carrying prevailing interest rates of 9.0% to 10.5% per annum, inclusive of financing arrangements executed via debt assignment contracts with Saigon-Hanoi Commercial Joint Stock Bank (SHB) alongside various corporate and retail counterparties. Total borrowing costs capitalized during the fiscal year amounted to VND 1,740 billion (compared to VND 1,164 billion in 2021), determined based on a weighted average capitalization rate of 7.59% per annum.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
In portfolio risk management, Vingroup maintained a prudent provisioning policy across asset classes. As of December 31, 2022, the Group booked accumulated provisions of VND 274.9 billion against irrecoverable short-term supplier advances, marking a significant increase from VND 92.7 billion in fiscal year 2021.

Inventories, property, plant, and equipment, as well as investment properties, were extensively deployed as collateral to secure credit facilities: the carrying values of pledged assets comprised VND 20,226 billion in inventories and VND 3,084 billion in net book value of investment properties mortgaged to financial institutions to back the Group's borrowings and corporate bonds. Furthermore, restricted cash balances and pledged bank deposits earmarked as collateral for operational activities totaled VND 1,548.5 billion, representing a sharp escalation compared to VND 458.6 billion in 2021. In a landmark post-balance sheet development, VinFast filed a registration statement on Form F-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in December 2022 for a proposed Initial Public Offering (IPO) of its common stock, aimed at elevating the brand onto the global stage.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vingroup and VinFast underscored an unwavering commitment to global climate objectives. On July 15, 2022, VinFast officially phased out internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicle production to complete its transition into an all-electric automaker, backed by an industry-leading 10-year warranty program and a secured supply chain of components across the vehicle lifecycle. On September 21, 2022, VinFast joined "The Climate Pledge," committing to achieve net-zero carbon emissions by 2040—a decade ahead of the Paris Agreement timeline—while also endorsing the COP26 declaration on accelerating the transition to 100% zero-emission cars and vans. On the ecological front, VinFast initiated the "Greening the Future" afforestation project in Phu Quoc and successfully planted 20,000 trees to establish a mangrove forest belt in Dong Hung commune, Tien Lang district, Hai Phong, to mitigate global climate vulnerability.
- **Social (S):** The Group prioritizes human capital development, regarding its workforce as its most valuable asset while fostering an equitable, upright, and collaborative corporate culture. Concurrently, Vingroup integrated international benchmarks into its social service ecosystems, notably partnering with Brighton College (UK) to scale premium K-12 international education and integrating Vinmec Times City International Hospital into the Cleveland Clinic Connected global network (USA) to deliver world-class healthcare standards domestically.
- **Governance (G):** Vingroup operates under a transparent corporate governance framework, strictly adhering to regulatory statutes governing publicly listed entities, maintaining a rigorous internal audit function, and implementing a Sustainable Finance Framework for the issuance of international green bonds and syndicated facilities.
