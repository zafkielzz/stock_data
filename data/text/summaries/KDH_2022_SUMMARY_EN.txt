### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

The Vietnamese real estate market in 2022 experienced a protracted stagnation phase, heavily impacted by adverse macroeconomic tailwinds that directly reflected on Khang Dien House Trading and Investment JSC's (KDH) equity performance. During the year, KDH shares suffered a steep drawdown of -43.71%, opening at VND 35,350/share and closing at VND 19,900/share (experiencing significant volatility between a 52-week high of VND 39,240/share and a low of VND 13,560/share), accompanied by an average matched liquidity of 1,635,406 shares/session.

Despite the challenging backdrop, Khang Dien's core growth impetus remained anchored in its long-term clean landbank accumulation strategy focused exclusively on key focal markets within Ho Chi Minh City (primarily Thu Duc City, Binh Chanh District, and Binh Tan District). Low-rise landed properties, high-end villas, and residential apartment projects such as The Classia, Verosa Park, and Lovera Vista continued to serve as brand-defining bright spots, underpinned by transparent legal frameworks and robust absorption rates. Although the residential property segment faced margin pressure, project development investment activities were aggressively maintained, evidenced by capitalized borrowing costs associated with work-in-progress construction inventory surging to VND 552.62 billion (compared to VND 232.42 billion in 2021). This reflects an unyielding pace of capital disbursement and on-site execution across key construction projects despite broader market headwinds.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

Khang Dien maintains a flexible capital structure to accommodate the capital-intensive nature of its ongoing projects. In 2022, the company augmented its medium- and long-term funding mix via the successful private placement of VND 800 billion in corporate bonds. Financial expense pressures escalated in tandem with project deployment milestones, with applicable interest rates calculated based on actual borrowing costs incurred on a project-by-project basis. Alongside traditional bank borrowings, KDH proactively alleviated operating cash flow pressures by leveraging off-balance-sheet co-investment capital. Specifically, the company secured a cumulative VND 362 billion in capital contributions from affiliated partners (comprising VND 252 billion from Phuc Dat Real Estate JSC and VND 110 billion from Nam Hung Real Estate Investment and Development Co., Ltd.) to develop a residential project spanning over 16.4 hectares in Binh Chanh District.

Capital structure risks are mitigated through the collateralization of land use rights and land-attached assets across a portfolio of large-scale projects—including Khang Phuc - Tan Tao, Doan Nguyen - Binh Trung Dong, Binh Trung - Binh Trung Dong, Khang Phuc - Binh Hung 11A, and the expanded Le Minh Xuân Industrial Park—against bank credit facilities. This necessitates steady progress in legal compliance to safeguard the continuity of existing credit lines.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Khang Dien’s provisioning policy demonstrates a high degree of conservatism, with the company maintaining its long-term doubtful debt provision balance unchanged at VND 2.0 billion for both the 2021 and 2022 fiscal years. Short-term receivables and advances to suppliers were tightly managed, with no material overdue non-performing loans or solvency defaults incurred.

Nevertheless, underlying operational risks persist regarding land compensation obligations at the Tan Tao Hamlet 2 Residential Area project. These settlement obligations will not be satisfied via cash outflows; instead, they carry a mandate to be settled and fulfilled through physical land plots in future periods, creating a substantial off-balance-sheet commitment. Furthermore, financial reporting transparency faces valuation risks, as the fair values of the investment in CVH Xuan Mai Co., Ltd. and the entire portfolio of investment properties held for lease have not been formally appraised and determined as of the reporting date, complicating the accurate reflection of the company’s net asset value.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Regarding Environmental (E) factors, Khang Dien targets the mitigation of ecological footprints throughout construction and operational phases via rigorous material management and the implementation of energy- and water-conservation initiatives. These strategic directions are aligned with the GRI Standards and Circular No. 96/2020/TT-BTC (specifically clauses 6.1 through 6.5). Although granular greenhouse gas emissions data (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) has not been explicitly quantified in the report, the company maintains an unwavering commitment to environmental statutory compliance.

On the Social (S) front, the company rigorously enforces talent attraction and retention policies, fully complying with the Labor Code by ensuring workplace safety, periodic health screenings, and comprehensive social insurance coverage for all employees. Corporate social responsibility is operationalized through practical philanthropic initiatives, including the construction of charity homes, rural infrastructure development (bridges and roads), and contributions to social welfare funds across its operational footprints.

Regarding Governance (G), KDH’s governance framework operates under the 2020 Enterprise Law, overseen by a 5-member Board of Directors (BoD). Independence and oversight are reinforced by having 4 non-executive members (including 1 independent director) alongside a 3-member Supervisory Board operating independently from the Board of Management. Notably, the backing of prominent institutional heavyweights such as VinaCapital (holding approximately 13% of charter capital) and Dragon Capital (holding approximately 10% of charter capital) serves as a robust anchor, elevating KDH's governance standards toward international benchmarks in disclosure transparency and sustainable development.
