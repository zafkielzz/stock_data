### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Hoa Sen Group (HSG) concluded the 2024–2025 financial year with an impressive earnings recovery, reporting a consolidated net profit of VND 735 billion, up 43% year-over-year and exceeding its target by 47%. The core growth driver stemmed from the synchronized implementation of operational optimization initiatives, technological upgrades to production lines, and stringent manufacturing cost controls. Alongside the traditional steel sheet manufacturing segment, the Hoa Sen Home retail system is progressively asserting its role as a new growth pillar for the Group by scaling operations, standardizing its management model, and developing exclusive product lines. In the international arena, despite escalating global trade protectionism, HSG’s export division maintained operational agility through flexible market diversification and close coordination with regulatory authorities to address trade defense barriers. The full-year gross margin stabilized at 11.20%, with Return on Equity (ROE) reaching 0.55%, reinforcing the company's intrinsic profitability despite downward pressure on the equity price, which declined 12.13% over the year to close at VND 12,100 per share.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

At the close of the 2025 financial year, HSG maintained a sound financial structure, keeping leverage at a prudent level with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 54.00%. This represents an optimal leverage ratio for a heavy industrial manufacturing enterprise, mitigating liquidity risk and cash flow pressure during periods of macroeconomic volatility. The Group's capital management and credit mobilization are closely coordinated by the Finance & Shareholder Relations Committee alongside the Restructuring & Resource Management Board. Although detailed lists of lending institutions and floating interest rate structures are not explicitly disclosed in the reporting documents, the low D/E ratio underscores HSG’s strategic intent to reduce reliance on external debt, prioritize working capital turnover optimization, and upgrade its ERP system for real-time cash flow monitoring to minimize incurred interest expenses.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

The management of material risks and contingent liabilities at HSG is rigorously institutionalized through the independent oversight of the Board of Audit (under the Board of Directors) and the Internal Audit Implementation Board. This framework ensures the integrity of financial reporting and enforces strict controls over related-party transactions to minimize conflicts of interest. For the 2025–2026 period, executive management notes that the macroeconomic outlook remains exposed to significant headwinds, including global economic deceleration, protracted geopolitical tensions, and an incomplete recovery in the domestic real estate and construction materials markets. Consequently, the company is proactively preparing contingency scenarios for raw material price volatility and trade defense measures in key export markets to forestall adverse financial shocks following the balance sheet date.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles are deeply embedded across HSG’s operational model and long-term strategic roadmap. On the Environmental (E) front, the enterprise focuses on technical enhancements and manufacturing line upgrades aimed at clean energy adoption, efficient emissions management, and optimized resource consumption. On the Social (S) front, HSG upholds a distinct corporate culture grounded in Buddhist philosophy, emphasizing three core values: "Integrity – Community – Development." The company not only prioritizes the development of high-quality human resources and succession planning but also actively disseminates human-centric values through long-term community initiatives such as the "Vietnamese Family Shelter" program. On the Governance (G) front, HSG’s governance structure aligns with best practices for public companies, featuring an independent, highly specialized personnel composition supported by esteemed independent Board members, including Assoc. Prof. Dr. Nguyen Van Luan (Chairman of the Audit Committee) and Mr. Dinh Viet Duy, M.Sc. (Chairman of the Finance & Shareholder Relations Committee), thereby ensuring information transparency and safeguarding shareholder value.
