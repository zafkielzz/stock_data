### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2023 presented considerable headwinds for the Vietnamese banking sector, characterized by systemic liquidity tightness, a protracted corporate bond market crisis, subdued macroeconomic growth, and deteriorated asset quality among borrowers. Consequently, Vietnam Prosperity Joint Stock Commercial Bank (VPBank) faced distinct operational pressures that impacted several short-term strategic targets. Nevertheless, the bank preserved its core growth drivers by aggressively expanding scale across key performance indicators. Specifically, consolidated gross credit extension reached over VND 600 trillion, marking a year-on-year expansion of 25.2%. Parent bank credit growth surged by 31.8%, significantly outperforming the industry average of 13.7%. Funding operations experienced a breakout trajectory, with total deposits growing by 33.6% to surpass VND 490 trillion, underpinned by a proactive liability management strategy focused on funding diversification, which firmly secured liquidity metrics. Furthermore, the bank’s ecosystem-wide customer base expanded from 24 million to over 30 million clients. Notably, decisive execution under the operational turnaround program at FE CREDIT successfully steered the consumer finance subsidiary back onto a recovery trajectory. Disbursement volumes rebounded during the final two quarters of the year, accompanied by a progressive improvement in quarterly net income, catalysed by the parent bank’s direct intervention in the restructuring process.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VPBank’s capital structure and balance sheet foundation were substantially reinforced in 2023 through landmark strategic transactions. Most notably, the bank successfully completed a 15% equity placement (equivalent to 1.19 billion shares) to its strategic Japanese shareholder, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC), injecting approximately VND 36 trillion in gross proceeds. This transaction propelled VPBank’s charter capital to nearly VND 79.34 trillion—ranking first in the banking system—while total equity scaled to nearly VND 140 trillion, securing the second position industry-wide. SMBC’s ongoing sponsorship was further demonstrated by the successful drawdown of USD 400 million in medium- and long-term syndicated loans during 2023, bringing the cumulative SMBC-backed international credit facilities to nearly USD 1.7 billion over a three-year period. Additionally, VPBank secured a USD 300 million financial support commitment from the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation (DFC) via a bilateral loan agreement to accelerate sustainable finance initiatives. Bolstered by the substantial capital injection from SMBC, VPBank’s consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 17.1%, sustaining its Tier-1 industry positioning, completely alleviating liquidity pressures, and providing a robust capital buffer to aggressively scale asset deployment.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
VPBank’s credit risk management and provisioning practices in 2023 strictly adhered to prevailing regulatory frameworks, including Circular 11/2021/TT-NHNN, Circular 02/2023/TT-NHNN, and subsequent transitional directives issued by the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV). The bank executed granular asset classification, general provisioning (maintained at 0.75% of total outstanding balances for applicable exposures), and specific provisioning net of discounted collateral values. For debt-restructured assets granted repayment forbearance while retaining their current classification to support distressed borrowers, VPBank accrued supplementary provisions in strict compliance with mandated regulatory timelines. Regarding market and operational risk management, the bank accelerated its digital transformation by integrating the Kondor system with cloud-based market data verification protocols, enabling real-time volatility tracking and effective risk mitigation. VPBank’s credit profile continued to be rated Ba3 with a Stable outlook by Moody’s Investors Service amidst a volatile financial landscape. No material anomalous legal contingencies or post-balance sheet events impacting the financial position were recorded subsequent to the fiscal year-end.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
*   **Environmental (E):** VPBank pioneered the establishment of a comprehensive ESG risk management framework independently evaluated by a premier consultancy, aligning with international standards and best practices. The bank proactively manages environmental and social risks through the implementation of an Environmental and Social Management System (ESMS) across credit-granting activities, alongside the deployment of a Green and Social Finance Framework to channel capital toward sustainable projects. Notably, for two consecutive years, VPBank remained the sole domestic financial institution to publish an annual Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) report, while progressively formulating its Net-Zero transition roadmap in alignment with government directives.
*   **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes human capital development of high-caliber talent and expanded its community-servicing customer base to over 30 million individuals across its integrated ecosystem. During the year, VPBank unveiled five new core corporate values—*Aspiration, Integrity, Efficiency, Discipline, and Innovation*—designed to shape corporate culture, foster organizational cohesion, and elevate institutional labor productivity.
*   **Governance (G):** Corporate governance practices were systematically standardized in accordance with international and ASEAN benchmarks. The Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and the Executive Board operate with high transparency; related-party transactions, executive remuneration policies, and board compensation structures are publicly disclosed in compliance with the Bank’s Charter and audited financial statement disclosures. The internal control system and independent audit functions maintain rigorous operational efficacy to ensure strict adherence to regulatory norms and sustainable development mandates.
