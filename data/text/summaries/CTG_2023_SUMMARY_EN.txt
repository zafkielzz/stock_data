1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Amid a macroeconomic backdrop characterized by headwinds from the global slowdown—yet cushioned by resilient Vietnam GDP growth of 5.05% and controlled inflation of 3.25%—Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank - HoSE: CTG) effectively leveraged its position as a primary capital channel to scale its total assets beyond the VND 2 trillion threshold. The bank’s core growth drivers for the year stemmed from a comprehensive operational overhaul, most notably the launch of "Project X01"—a total digital transformation roadmap comprising 108 strategic initiatives directly aligned with the 2024–2026 medium-term business strategy. The extensive deployment of digital banking within credit operations and the automation of lending workflows optimized operational efficiency, facilitating a reduction in benchmark lending rates and fee waivers to support corporates and retail clients in their post-pandemic recovery.

This marked enhancement in core operational capacity directly translated into CTG’s equity performance on the secondary market. Throughout 2023, the stock demonstrated a positive trajectory, registering a +10.76% capital appreciation to close at VND 18,320 compared to an opening price of VND 16,540, and reaching an intraday high of VND 20,340. The equity exhibited robust liquidity, with average matched trading volume exceeding 4.15 million shares per session.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

As of year-end 2023, CTG’s capital structure was notably reinforced, with total equity reaching approximately VND 126,000 billion, representing a substantial step-up from its baseline foundation. Crucially, the successful execution of the charter capital augmentation plan on December 1, 2023, served as a pivotal catalyst in bolstering the bank’s Tier 1 capital base and improving its Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR).

Regarding funding structure and interbank liquidity, CTG maintained close credit and cooperative ties with its strategic partner, MUFG Bank, Ltd. – Ho Chi Minh City Branch (a major shareholder holding an equity stake of nearly 20%). Both institutions executed transactions via VND-denominated master deposit agreements and interbank credit line frameworks. Furthermore, foreign exchange line transactions and funding commitments were systematically established with its subsidiary, Lao VietinBank, to optimize cash flow dynamics and mitigate foreign exchange risk. Cash flow and liquidity pressures were similarly well-contained, underpinned by flexible treasury management and strict adherence to the State Bank of Vietnam’s regulatory liquidity metrics.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Asset quality containment remained a paramount priority for CTG in 2023, driven by enhanced asset-monitoring protocols spanning from Head Office down to individual branches, enabling the early identification of credit risk events. To optimize non-performing loan (NPL) resolution, CTG executed a principal debt-recovery authorization contract with its wholly-owned subsidiary, VietinBank Asset Management Company (VietinBank AMC).

Regarding contingent liabilities and off-balance sheet commitments, the bank executed stringent guarantee transactions, asset hypothecation, and negotiable instrument pledges with VietinBank Insurance Joint Stock Corporation (VBI) pursuant to Board of Directors (BoD) resolutions, ensuring legal transparency and mitigating cross-risk exposure. In FY2023, the institution recorded no insider equity transactions among key management personnel, highlighting stability in the upper-tier ownership structure and minimizing susceptibility to anomalous market volatility.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** CTG demonstrated clear environmental stewardship by prioritizing credit allocation toward green financing initiatives, environmental protection, and climate change mitigation. Accelerating the digitization of lending processes not only reduced operational overheads but also significantly curtailed physical paper consumption, advancing the bank toward a zero-emission operating model.
*   **Social (S):** The philosophy of "Human capital as the core driver" was actualized by streamlining the organizational matrix and tightly managing headcount across the network to enhance labor productivity, while safeguarding employee welfare and upgrading human resource capabilities via the VietinBank Training and Human Resource Development School. The bank also remained a pioneer in nationwide corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives.
*   **Governance (G):** Governance transparency was strongly upheld, with actual remuneration disbursed to the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board accounting for a mere 0.14% of net profit after tax in 2023, comfortably below the 0.25% ceiling approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders. All related-party transactions (involving MUFG and subsidiaries) received explicit BoD resolutions, strictly complying with disclosure mandates and aligning with best international governance practices.
