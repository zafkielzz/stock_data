### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

The 2021 fiscal year marked a stellar operational breakout for Gemadept Corporation (GMD) despite severe supply chain disruptions precipitated by the COVID-19 pandemic. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 3,206 billion, while consolidated profit before tax (PBT) came in at VND 806 billion, both outperforming the most optimistic scenario approved by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM) by 15% (optimistic target: revenue of VND 2,800 billion and PBT of VND 700 billion). This exceptional earnings beat catalyzed a +46.97% appreciation in GMD’s share price over the year, closing at VND 34,890 per share with an average daily trading liquidity of 3.15 million shares per session.

The Port Operations segment served as the primary growth engine, driving the bulk of the Group’s top and bottom lines. Although facing substantial headwinds in Q2 and Q3 due to lockdowns, maritime congestion, and elevated operating expenses stemming from the "3-on-site" operational model, the port division staged a robust recovery in Q4. Core growth drivers included:
- The Hai Phong port cluster (comprising 4 terminals), which delivered a surge in throughput volume toward year-end.
- The Gemalink deep-water port, which commenced commercial operations at the beginning of Q1 2021 and contributed accretive earnings immediately in its inaugural year of operation.
- The Dung Quat port, which surpassed its targets by capitalizing on bulk cargo handling opportunities.

Within the Logistics segment, despite all six service lines sustaining direct blows from paralyzed freight circulation, the division demonstrated operational agility to meet and exceed its guided targets. Gemadept successfully maintained its position within the Top 10 Reputable Logistics Companies in 2021. Looking ahead, the Board of Directors (BoD) has set guidance for 2022 targeting a 19% increase in revenue and a 24% growth in PBT relative to 2021 actuals, supporting a 5-year strategic roadmap (2021–2025) aimed at tripling 2025 PBT compared to the 2020 baseline.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

Gemadept has formally integrated Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles into its 2021–2025 long-term strategy via a dedicated Sustainability Report:

- **Environmental (E):** The company prioritizes the optimization of port and logistics operational workflows to minimize environmental footprints. While specific greenhouse gas emission metrics (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) were not quantified in the extracted text, initiatives toward port greening and infrastructure digitalization are being actively promoted to enhance energy efficiency.
- **Social (S):** A paramount priority in 2021 was safeguarding the occupational health and safety of employees amidst pandemic peaks. The enterprise successfully deployed the "3-on-site" production model, safeguarding workforce welfare, preserving jobs, and maintaining stable benefit packages. Furthermore, the company reinforced human capital development initiatives, compensation policies, and BSC/KPI evaluation frameworks.
- **Governance (G):** Gemadept’s governance framework reflects high institutional standards, anchored by an 11-member Board of Directors (including non-executive and international members) and an independent 5-member Supervisory Board. In 2021, the company fortified its executive leadership by appointing a new Chief Executive Officer (Mr. Nguyen Thanh Binh) effective May 6, 2021, ensuring a clear segregation of duties between the strategic oversight of the BoD and the operational execution of the Executive Board.
