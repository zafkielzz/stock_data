### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Against the backdrop of an impressive macroeconomic recovery with GDP growth reaching 7.09%, CTG demonstrated a strong market performance, closing the year at VND 25,550/share (delivering a full-year total return of +39.24%, supported by robust average daily liquidity of 8,115,936 shares/session). VietinBank's core growth driver in FY2024 stemmed from its sustainable financial asset restructuring strategy: total assets expanded by 17.4%, gross credit outstandings reached VND 1.73 trillion (+16.8% YoY), and total mobilized deposits scaled to VND 1.8 trillion (+15.2% YoY).

The non-credit segment maintained its growth momentum, with non-interest income (NII) accounting for nearly 27% of total operating income (TOI), spearheaded by market-leading growth in guarantee fee income (+35.1%). The crown jewel of the performance was the **Retail Banking Division**: average retail loan books reached VND 586 billion (+19.5% YoY), elevating retail credit market share to 9.17% (ranking second among the Big 4 state-owned banks in market share expansion velocity). Total retail segment revenue hit VND 27.7 billion (+5.7% YoY), within which retail net fee income (NFI) surged by 17.5% YoY to VND 3,149 billion. Average retail funding stood at VND 779 billion (+13% YoY), firmly reinforcing the structural revenue diversification of the banking group.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

As of December 31, 2024, VietinBank’s charter capital stood at VND 53,699,917 million, maintaining a highly concentrated and resilient shareholding structure with 64.46% held by the State, alongside MUFG Bank acting as the primary strategic foreign partner. Throughout the fiscal year, pressure on the cost of funds (COF) was substantially alleviated by a breakthrough in low-cost funding acquisition. Ending Current Account Saving Account (CASA) balances reached VND 137 billion (+30% YoY, achieving an average growth velocity of 25.4%), driving the bank-wide CASA ratio to 24.8% and propelling VietinBank past BIDV to secure the #2 position system-wide in CASA scale.

The average deposit mobilization interest rate compressed sharply by approximately 30% compared to the prior-year-end. This, combined with a deposit mix shift that increased the weight of short-term tenors under 12 months by ~2.5 percentage points, established an efficient interest rate risk management safety margin. The bank continued to strictly comply with all major liquidity prudential ratios, including the liquidity reserve ratio, the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR), and the regulatory cap on short-term funding utilized for medium- and long-term lending set by the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV).

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Asset quality and risk management frameworks were rigorously monitored in accordance with the stringent criteria stipulated in Circular 31/2024/TT-NHNN. Specifically within the retail customer loan portfolio, the non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was tightly contained at approximately 1%, comfortably within the risk appetite thresholds approved by the Board of Directors.

Despite severe macro headwinds from natural disasters and typhoons, VietinBank proactively executed its economic support mandate via an emergency credit package aimed at mitigating the aftermath of Typhoon Yagi, achieving an actual disbursement scale of up to VND 31,000 billion, while concurrently allocating over VND 500 billion to corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives. Regarding conflict-of-interest governance, related-party transactions were disclosed with high transparency; actual remuneration disbursed to the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board for FY2024 was optimized at 0.12% of profit after tax (PAT), notably lower than the maximum threshold of 0.25% sanctioned by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS).

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** VietinBank pioneered the implementation of the "Sustainable Finance Framework" (SFF) to strategically direct credit flows. The bank prioritizes capital allocation toward climate-mitigation projects aimed at curbing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and fostering climate resilience, while proactively tapering credit exposure to sectors with adverse impacts on biodiversity and ecosystems, thereby aligning with the Government’s national Net-Zero target by 2050.
*   **Social (S):** Corporate social accountability was operationalized through the allocation of over VND 500 billion toward social welfare. The bank aggressively deployed preferential credit facilities for social housing, aquaculture and forestry, and gold market stabilization programs. Human capital capabilities were systematically upgraded via the Digital Factory model and Agile methodologies under the X01 digital transformation roadmap (successfully completing 45 foundational initiatives).
*   **Governance (G):** VietinBank maintained uncompromising transparency in corporate governance. The FY2024 financial statements were independently audited by Deloitte Vietnam Company Limited. The risk governance framework operated synchronously through Board-level committees and executive management councils, ensuring full adherence to international disclosure standards and advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
