### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Amid a 2023 real estate market characterized by macro headwinds, supply constraints, and compressed secondary liquidity, KDH delivered resilient equity performance. The stock generated a total return of +28.42% for the year, closing at VND 25,940 (with a 52-week trading range of VND 18,020 to VND 28,500), supported by an average daily trading volume of 1,706,723 shares.

KDH’s core revenue drivers stem from its disciplined focus on landed properties (townhouses and villas) and high-rise residential developments featuring clear legal titles and punctual delivery schedules across Thu Duc City, Binh Chanh District, and Binh Tan District. Beyond its traditional residential portfolio, the company restructured its industrial park infrastructure segment—highlighted by the expansion of the Le Minh Xuan Industrial Park—to optimize operational efficiency. Notably, KDH’s medium- to long-term growth trajectory is underpinned by joint ventures with reputable foreign partners. A prime strategic partnership is the collaboration with Singapore’s Keppel Corporation to develop a 12-hectare master-planned project cluster in Binh Trung Dong Ward (Thu Duc City), allowing KDH to de-risk its balance sheet while positioning itself for the next cycle of large-scale township developments.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

KDH’s capital structure reflects a strong credit profile, bolstered by the long-term backing of institutional funds such as VinaCapital and Dragon Capital. In terms of liquidity management, the company optimized short-term bank deposits yielding annual rates between 2.7% and 4.3%, alongside long-term held-to-maturity investments generating yields of up to 7.05% per annum.

Nonetheless, capital intensity remains elevated due to pipeline developments under construction. Borrowing costs capitalized into inventory rose significantly by 37.4%, advancing from VND 552.66 billion in 2022 to VND 759.40 billion in 2023. The company has pledged land-use rights (LURs) and attached assets across multiple core projects—including Tan Tao, Binh Trung Dong, Phong Phu 2, Binh Hung 11A residential areas, and the expanded Le Minh Xuan Industrial Park—as collateral for bank facilities. This reliance on financial leverage to fund long-term land banks necessitates stringent presale execution to safeguard operating cash flows and mitigate credit tightening risks and floating interest rate volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

KDH exhibits a healthy asset quality profile, with management reporting no overdue or impaired short-term trade receivables, short-term prepayments to suppliers, or other receivables as of December 31, 2023. Regarding inventory risk, the primary operational hurdles involve site clearance compensation and the perfection of legal titles for large-scale land banks. The company has extended substantial advances to organizations and individuals for site clearance compensation across Thu Duc City, Binh Chanh, and Binh Tan.

A notable corporate finance event during the year was the acquisition of Loc Minh Real Estate Development Joint Stock Company, which generated a substantial bargain purchase gain driven by the fair value of net assets exceeding the acquisition cost. Key residual risks warranting close monitoring include the execution timeline for finalizing LUR transfers associated with land prepayments, which is critical to circumventing prolonged working capital lockups.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** KDH remains committed to strict adherence to Vietnam's environmental regulations, implementing energy-efficient, water-saving, and intelligent material management solutions throughout construction and asset operations. The company’s developments prioritize green landscapes, carbon footprint reduction, and ecological compatibility, standardized in alignment with GRI Standards and Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC.
*   **Social (S):** The company enforces rigorous Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards for both internal personnel and onsite contractors. KDH maintains an equitable workplace, competitive compensation frameworks, and superior employee welfare benefits (such as corporate fitness sponsorships and periodic health screenings) to drive talent retention. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives are actively deployed via charitable housing programs, healthcare and educational sponsorships, and local infrastructure enhancement.
*   **Governance (G):** KDH operates under a transparent governance framework overseen by a 5-member Board of Directors—comprising 1 non-executive independent director and 3 non-executive directors to ensure objective oversight. The Supervisory Board consists of 3 members operating entirely independently from the Board of Management. Sustainability reporting and corporate disclosures consistently align with GRI standards and statutory mandates, cementing the confidence of institutional investors and major shareholders.
