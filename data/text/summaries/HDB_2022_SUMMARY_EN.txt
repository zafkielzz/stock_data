Based on Ho Chi Minh City Development Joint Stock Commercial Bank's (HDBank) 2022 Annual Report and verified actual data, the following is a standardized qualitative analysis summary structured according to the requested sections:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, HDBank maintained steady growth momentum despite macroeconomic volatility and monetary tightening. The bank's core business drivers stemmed from retail banking, consumer finance, and digital banking services. The strategic focus on the agricultural ecosystem, supply chains, and the micro-merchant and individual customer segments continued to serve as a launchpad for net interest income (NII) and non-interest income. HDBank's key markets remained heavily concentrated in major urban centers such as Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, and key southern economic zones, where demand for credit and financial services is high. Core business segments continued to be optimized through the digitization of operational processes, helping to well-control the cost-to-income ratio (CIR) and enhance the return on equity (ROE) and return on assets (ROA).

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, INDEBTEDNESS & INTEREST RATES
As a credit institution, HDBank’s capital structure comprises shareholders' equity, customer deposits (from individuals and economic organizations), valuable papers issued, and borrowings from domestic and foreign credit institutions. Amid a surging market interest rate environment in the second half of 2022 driven by inflation and exchange rate pressures, HDBank’s cost of capital faced intense competitive pressures regarding deposit rates. The bank maintained flexible credit limits and strictly managed the ratio of short-term funds used for medium- and long-term loans in compliance with State Bank of Vietnam standards. Interest rate risk was monitored through asset-liability management (ALM) models, ensuring that the net interest margin (NIM) was maintained at a reasonable level and minimizing short-term liquidity pressures.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Credit risk management and provisioning were prudently implemented by HDBank to address potential risks from the real estate market and consumer lending segments. The non-performing loan (NPL) ratio was strictly controlled below safe thresholds through debt collection enforcement measures and adequate risk provisioning in accordance with regulations. Off-balance-sheet commitments, including letters of credit (L/C), payment guarantees, and other financial commitments, underwent rigorous limit appraisals to mitigate systemic risk. From the end of the fiscal year on December 31, 2022, up to the publication of the Annual Report in April 2023, there were no recordable abnormal financial events or major legal litigations capable of materially affecting the bank's financial position and business performance.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** HDBank shapes its sustainable development strategy through green lending, financing renewable energy projects, high-tech agriculture, and energy conservation. The bank advocates minimizing its carbon footprint in internal operations, promoting paperless offices, and optimizing energy efficiency across its nationwide branch network.
- **Social (S):** The bank regards human resources as a core asset, maintaining a safe, equitable, and professional working environment in strict compliance with occupational health and safety (OHS) standards. HDBank consistently prioritizes annual investments in professional capacity-building training programs, compensation and benefits policies, comprehensive health insurance, and community-focused social welfare activities.
- **Governance (G):** HDBank strictly complies with public corporate governance models aligned with international standards and Vietnamese law. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence with the participation of experienced independent members. Independent audits are conducted by leading audit firms, ensuring transparency, the public disclosure of financial information, and adherence to best practices in risk management and sustainable development.
