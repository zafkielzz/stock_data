### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING

Fiscal year 2024 marked a historic turning point for Loc Phat Vietnam Commercial Joint Stock Bank (LPBank - Ticker: LPB), delivering exceptional financial performance. Profit before tax reached a record VND 12,168 billion, surging 73% year-on-year, driving Return on Equity (ROE) to 25.1% and positioning the bank among the industry leaders in operational efficiency.

The core growth driver stemmed from net interest income expansion, achieved through credit portfolio restructuring focused on prime borrowers with strong debt-servicing capacity and sectors aligned with sustainable green growth. Concurrently, the bank actively diversified its revenue streams to boost fee income, exemplified by the launch of the JCB Ultimate premium credit card line targeted at affluent urban segments. Operational efficiency improved markedly under the "Lean to Lead" business model, supported by comprehensive digital transformation. Compressing the T24 Core Banking deployment timeline to just 8 months and operationalizing the treasury trading system within 6 months established a robust technological foundation, optimizing operating expenses and enhancing customer experience.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES

As of December 31, 2024, LPBank’s total assets surpassed VND 508,330 billion. Regarding the capital structure, charter capital reached VND 25,576,164 million, serving as a solid buffer for future scale expansion. LPBank maintained robust liquidity buffers and comfortably complied with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) regulations, including liquidity reserve ratios and 30-day liquidity coverage ratios for both VND and foreign currencies.

Funding sources remained stable, anchored by customer deposits and valuable paper issuances, shielding the bank from cost-of-fund pressures amid market interest rate volatility. On the equity market, LPB shares recorded a stellar trading performance with a price appreciation of 131.18%, closing the final trading session of the year at VND 27,510/share (compared to an opening price of VND 11,900/share). Average matched trading volume exceeded 4.08 million shares per session. Foreign ownership remained negligible at 0.67%, providing ample headroom to attract strategic foreign investors in subsequent development phases.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS

Credit risk management at LPBank was executed rigorously and proactively. The bank strictly adopted new regulatory frameworks under Circular 31/2024/TT-NHNN regarding asset classification and Decree 86/2024/NĐ-CP concerning provisioning methodologies. Asset quality was well-contained through accelerated provisioning and proactive non-performing loan (NPL) resolution, preserving a safe on-balance-sheet NPL ratio.

Off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities—including loan guarantee commitments, foreign exchange commitments, swap agreements, and letters of credit (L/C)—were closely monitored to mitigate systemic liquidity shocks. Regarding post-balance-sheet events, on January 21, 2025, LPBank received Decision No. 125/QĐ-NHNN from the SBV approving amendments to its operating license concerning its newly increased charter capital. Furthermore, VIS Rating (a Moody’s affiliate) assigned LPBank a long-term credit rating of A+ with a "Stable" outlook, alongside its inclusion in the league table of 14 systemically important banks for 2024, reaffirming the bank's strong standing and market reputation.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION

*   **Environmental (E):** LPBank positioned itself as a pioneer by releasing its inaugural standalone Sustainability Report (ESG) on June 28, 2024, themed "Environmental Resilience." The bank remains committed to supporting the Government’s Net-Zero targets by channeling green credit flows, financing green energy transition projects, protecting the environment, and fostering a circular economy.
*   **Social (S):** As of year-end 2024, LPBank’s headcount stood at 11,189 employees. The bank restructured and officially transitioned to a lean, multi-task operating model on December 16, 2024, to optimize labor productivity. LPBank prioritizes human capital by enhancing welfare packages, refining compensation structures, providing comprehensive healthcare, and fulfilling corporate social responsibility, notably co-organizing the VTV LPBank 2024 International Marathon in Cat Ba.
*   **Governance (G):** The corporate rebranding to Loc Phat Vietnam Commercial Joint Stock Bank reflects its long-term commitment to sustainable development and the harmonization of stakeholder interests. The governance framework has been standardized against best international practices, ensuring maximum disclosure transparency and maintaining proactive, professional Investor Relations (IR).
