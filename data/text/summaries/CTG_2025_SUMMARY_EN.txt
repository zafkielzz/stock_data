### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank - HOSE: CTG) demonstrated an impressive share price appreciation of +37.11%, closing at VND 35,210/share with robust market liquidity (average matched volume exceeding 8.7 million shares/session). Financial performance remained stable, yielding an EPS of VND 4,804.77, a P/E of 8.63x, a P/B of 1.45x, an ROE of 3.58%, and an ROA of 0.22%.

The core growth driver for VietinBank stemmed from its Retail Banking segment, which marked its 11th year of operations. Retail Total Operating Income (TOI) reached over VND 30 trillion, expanding by 8.3% YoY and contributing up to 47% of total client segment revenues (corporate and retail combined). The contribution of retail non-interest income to total TOI improved markedly to 12%.

Regarding credit metrics, average retail outstanding loans reached VND 736.9 trillion, up 25.5% YoY, achieving 106.7% of the annual target. The portfolio mix shifted favorably toward medium- and long-term tenors (accounting for 33.3% of total retail loans, an increase of VND 58.7 trillion), driven by enhanced collaboration with prime real estate developers. Retail credit market share stood at 9.57%, cementing its position as 3rd system-wide. On the liability side, despite headwinds in the household business and payroll segments, average retail CASA grew by 22.5% to VND 168.1 trillion, lifting the retail CASA ratio to ~20% (compared to 17.6% in 2024). Retail term deposits reached VND 696 trillion, up 7.2% YoY.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VietinBank's capital foundation was continuously reinforced throughout its transformation phase, with charter capital reaching nearly VND 77.67 trillion and total assets surpassing the USD 100 billion threshold. The bank managed its capital structure flexibly by executing a mixed dividend payout plan: 4.5% in cash and 44.6% in stock, optimizing shareholder returns while fortifying its financial capacity for long-term balance sheet expansion.

The bank’s shareholder base features the strategic partnership of MUFG Bank (holding a 19.73% equity stake). Funding is predominantly sourced from domestic customer deposits (with the retail segment contributing VND 696 trillion in term deposits alongside robust growth in low-cost CASA funds). Cash flow pressures and structural funding risks are prudently managed through strict adherence to State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) prudential ratios, including liquidity reserve ratios, the loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR), and caps on short-term funding utilized for medium- and long-term lending.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Credit risk management at VietinBank was executed stringently in accordance with SBV Circular 31/2024/TT-NHNN on an unconsolidated basis. The loan classification system spanning Groups 1 to 5 closely tracks quantitative criteria regarding overdue days and actual recoverability (specifically, Group 2 for past-due loans up to 90 days; Group 3 for 91-180 days; Group 4 for 181-360 days).

Credit loss provisioning was fully provisioned to insulate the balance sheet against adverse market volatilities. The bank maintains a rigorous focus on controlling non-performing loan (NPL) and Special Mention loan (Group 2) ratios over total outstanding credit to mitigate provisioning pressures on earnings. Off-balance-sheet commitments, such as guarantees and letters of credit (L/Cs), were operated conservatively. The FY2025 financial statements were independently audited by PwC (Vietnam) Ltd., ensuring transparency and integrity, with no material legal disputes or abnormal post-balance sheet events recorded subsequent to the accounting cutoff date (December 31, 2025).

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
*   **Environmental (E):** VietinBank actively aligns with the Government’s national commitment to achieve Net-Zero emissions by 2050. The bank integrates environmental standards into its credit underwriting process, prioritizing green capital allocation toward climate change mitigation and adaptation projects, natural resource and biodiversity conservation, and pollution control, while restricting financing for environmentally detrimental projects. At its headquarters, the bank strictly implements wastewater monitoring, occupational environmental monitoring, and contracts certified waste disposal providers in compliance with statutory regulations.
*   **Social (S):** Regarding social responsibility, VietinBank scaled up credit financing for essential infrastructure, social housing, and public service accessibility. The bank promulgated system-wide occupational health and safety guidelines supported by performance KPIs to prevent health and security incidents across office facilities. Targeted capacity building and specialized training for ESG personnel were strongly emphasized.
*   **Governance (G):** VietinBank completed the institutionalization of its dedicated ESG personnel framework spanning from Head Office down to 100% of its branch network. Management oversight is transparently conducted by the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, the Executive Board, and specialized committees (Risk Management Committee, Policy Committee, Strategy Committee, etc.). Financial statements and capital adequacy indicators are independently audited by PwC Vietnam, ensuring full compliance with public market disclosure standards.
