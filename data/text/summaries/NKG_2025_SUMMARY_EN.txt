### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Nam Kim Steel navigated a challenging operating environment as export markets faced severe headwinds from technical barriers, tariffs, and global steel price volatility. According to the Vietnam Steel Association (VSA), coated sheet consumption volumes contracted by 12.5% year-over-year (YoY), within which the export segment recorded a steep decline of 47.5%. Nevertheless, the company demonstrated operational agility by optimizing its business and production methodologies and deploying innovative strategies to sustain operations. Consequently, FY2025 total revenue reached VND 14.899 trillion, achieving 64.7% of its full-year target, while total assets expanded by 22% YoY to VND 16.493 trillion. The core growth driver stemmed from a focused acceleration in capital expenditures to expand production capacity—most notably the flagship Nam Kim Phu My Steel Plant project, which is progressing on schedule, thereby laying a robust foundation for long-term growth and enhancing the company's competitive positioning in both domestic and international markets.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Nam Kim Steel has made significant strides in integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards, earning recognition in the Top 10 Green Vietnam ESG Enterprises in 2025.
- **Environmental (E):** The company is firmly committed to green manufacturing, driving green transformation, optimizing energy efficiency, and standardizing its operational framework toward a lean, flexible, and modernized model to minimize environmental footprints. Flagship product lines, such as ZINMAG® zinc-magnesium alloy coated steel, have been certified by the Ministry of Industry and Trade as eco-friendly building materials.
- **Social (S):** The company places strong emphasis on human capital development and welfare enhancement. It was honored as one of the Top 2 Best Places to Work in Vietnam's Construction Materials Industry and ranked among the Top 500 Employers in Vietnam for 2025 (VBE500) by Viet Research, underscoring its competitive human resources policies and attractive workplace environment.
- **Governance (G):** The company maintains a high standard of transparent corporate governance, which has been widely recognized by the market and reputable institutions through premier rankings such as Forbes Vietnam’s Top 50 Best-Performing Listed Companies in 2025, reinforcing its robust governance capabilities and sustainable operational efficiency.
