### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
The real estate market in 2024 received strong structural momentum following the concurrent implementation of the 2024 Land Law, the 2023 Housing Law, and the 2023 Real Estate Business Law on August 1, 2024. Market sentiment improvement was clearly reflected in KDH’s equity performance, with the closing price reaching VND 32,790/share (delivering a +26.41% total return for the year) alongside a stable average trading liquidity exceeding 2.9 million shares per session. The core growth driver stemmed from the high-end urban housing segment, which recorded a 100% absorption rate across newly launched projects. Notably, The Privia high-rise residential project in Binh Tan District (comprising 1,043 units) served as a primary earnings catalyst, driving handovers through a strategy of securing full construction completion and clear legal title prior to pre-sales. Furthermore, the company is proactively restructuring its industrial park infrastructure segment to generate recurring revenue streams, while concurrently expanding its land bank by actively seeking strategic partnership opportunities with major domestic and international investment funds.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Khang Dien’s capital structure exhibits high capital intensity to finance its large-scale development pipeline of work-in-progress projects. Capitalized borrowing costs reached VND 769.01 billion in 2024 (up marginally from VND 759.40 billion in 2023), reflecting persistent debt servicing pressures. To secure credit facilities, the company pledged land-use rights and attached assets across a series of core projects, including the Tan Tao, Binh Trung, Doan Nguyen, Binh Hung 11A, and New Binh Trung residential areas. Regarding short-term liquidity management, cash equivalents with maturities under 3 months yielded interest rates ranging from 1.5% to 4.6% per annum, while held-to-maturity investments with 3-to-12-month tenors generated stable returns between 3.65% and 6.3% per annum. The company applies the borrowing cost capitalization method based on the actual interest rates incurred on specific loans allocated to individual projects, thereby optimizing financial expense accounting.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Khang Dien maintains prudent credit risk management, with no overdue or impaired short-term receivables or prepayments reported at the close of the fiscal year. Nevertheless, the company maintained a long-term doubtful debt provision of VND 2.67 billion. Near-term capital encumbrance was primarily concentrated in short-term advances for site clearance and compensation in Thu Duc City, Binh Chanh District, and Binh Tan District, as well as the prepayment for the equity acquisition of An Vinh Phat Real Estate. Notably, subsequent to December 31, 2024, the company executed a series of legal restructuring initiatives to streamline its corporate hierarchy. Subsidiaries including Long Phuoc Dien and Nam Phu (completed on December 31, 2024) and Saigon Mùa Xuan (on December 3, 2024) were liquidated. Subsequently, the subsidiary Vila also completed its liquidation on February 3, 2025, following the return of capital contributions to the Group; prior to Vila’s liquidation, KDH acquired a 99.96% equity stake in Thuy Sinh Company from this entity to maintain direct control.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
Regarding Environmental (E) factors, Khang Dien is committed to mitigating ecological impacts through strict adherence to environmental regulations, deploying resource-conservation measures in project design, construction, and operations to foster clean and sustainable green spaces. On the Social (S) front, the company prioritizes safeguarding employee welfare by complying with the Labor Code, establishing competitive compensation packages, and providing medical insurance, periodic health check-ups, and professional development programs. The company also demonstrated profound corporate citizenship by allocating approximately VND 50 billion from welfare and bonus funds toward philanthropic, educational, and healthcare initiatives throughout 2024. In terms of Governance (G), the enterprise’s sustainability report is rigorously compiled in accordance with Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC and benchmarked against international GRI Standards. Board independence and transparency are reinforced by the presence of independent member Nguyen Thi Cam Van (Master of Cadastre) alongside a senior management team possessing extensive financial and auditing expertise from major institutions such as KPMG and EY.
