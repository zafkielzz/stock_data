1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
For the fiscal year 2022, Vietnam Prosperity Joint Stock Commercial Bank (VPBank) delivered resilient operational performance amidst macroeconomic volatility. Consolidated total operating income (TOI) reached approximately VND 57,800 billion, marking a year-on-year increase of nearly 31%. Profit before tax expanded by 48% year-on-year to VND 21,220 billion, positioning VPBank among the top 5 most profitable banks in the banking system. The primary catalyst for this outperformance was the strength of its multi-layered ecosystem and digital technology platform, which enabled the bank to optimize its cost of capital and enhance operational efficiency. Consolidated credit extension reached VND 480,000 billion, with credit growth at the parent bank registering approximately 30%—double the industry average of 14.5%—while customer deposits at the parent bank also grew by nearly 30%, or 5 times the industry average.

The retail banking strategy continued to serve as a core pillar, with retail outstanding loans exceeding VND 310,000 billion at year-end 2022, cementing its leadership among private joint-stock commercial banks across the Individual Customers, Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs), and consumer finance segments. However, the consumer finance segment under FE CREDIT was more severely impacted than forecasted due to a sluggish economic recovery and lingering pandemic headwinds, resulting in a shortfall against its 2022 targets. Nonetheless, with favorable long-term prospects, FE CREDIT remains a strategic anchor within the bank's ecosystem. Furthermore, the successful completion of the mergers of VPBank Securities (VPBankS) and OPES Insurance, alongside an exclusive partnership agreement with AIA Vietnam, has finalized the value chain of a comprehensive financial conglomerate serving over 24 million customers.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
VPBank entered fiscal year 2022 with a robust financial foundation, notably reinforced following the largest M&A transaction in Vietnam's financial history. Consolidated total equity surpassed VND 103,000 billion, and charter capital reached over VND 67,000 billion, preserving its position as the commercial bank with the largest charter capital in the system. Market capitalization stood at VND 120,000 billion, maintaining VPBank's status as the largest private bank by market capitalization in Vietnam. Regarding the funding structure and capital base, customer deposits were mobilized across various tenors in both VND and foreign currencies, with issuing yields on valuable papers (certificates of deposit and bonds) ranging from 3.00% to 15.00% per annum.

Regarding borrowings and financing received, as of year-end 2022, loans from the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) had tenors ranging from 3 months to 1 year with interest rates between 3.30% and 6.33% per annum. Meanwhile, entrusted funds, investment ủy thác (trust) capital, and loans from risk-bearing credit institutions carried tenors of 4 to 10 years at an interest rate of 4.332% per annum. Proactive resource mobilization drove high deposit growth, enabling the bank to sustain robust liquidity buffers while maintaining a consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of nearly 15% (specifically, standalone and consolidated Basel II CAR stood at 12.63% and 14.9%, respectively). This places VPBank at the forefront of the market, comfortably exceeding the SBV's regulatory minimum of 8%.

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Against a macroeconomic and financial landscape fraught with inflationary, interest rate, and liquidity pressures, VPBank executed a rigorous and disciplined risk management framework. Driven by prudent oversight, the bank's standalone and consolidated Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratios remained well within safe thresholds at 2.19%. The restructured loan portfolio extended its sharp downward trajectory, contracting to one-quarter of the prior-year level. Notably, starting from Q2 2022, VPBank ceased provisioning for Covid-19 restructured loans as an increasing number of borrowers successfully graduated out of this portfolio.

Regarding related financial activities, the financial statement disclosures recorded credit risk provisions for trading securities, investment securities (available-for-sale and held-to-maturity), debt purchasing activities, credit extension to other credit institutions, and provisions for other on-balance-sheet assets (including bad debt provisions and credit risk asset provisions). Notably, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2022, the Bank and its subsidiaries recorded no write-offs of unrecoverable receivables. The total par value of valuable papers pledged and mortgaged at other credit institutions and utilized as credit limits at the SBV amounted to VND 11,305,000 million, while the total par value of valuable papers sold under repurchase agreements (repo commitments) reached VND 3,745,537 million at year-end.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
VPBank remains steadfast in its pursuit of a sustainable development strategy aligned with its long-term corporate vision, ranking among the frontrunners in Vietnam and benchmarking favorably against regional peers according to Moody’s assessments.

- **Environmental (E):** The bank has institutionalized an Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Risk Management Policy aligned with international best practices and SBV mandates, thereby heightening awareness of climate-related financial risks. Proactively evaluating its ESG risk management framework enables the bank to seamlessly integrate environmental metrics into its core operations.
- **Social (S):** The bank's development agenda is anchored in its mission of "For a Prosperous Vietnam," dedicated to generating physical, emotional, and financial value for the community and society. The bank's ecosystem currently serves over 24 million customers nationwide (representing one-quarter of Vietnam’s population), facilitating broader public access to modern financial services and empowering enterprises to scale their operations. Concurrently, human capital development remains a core strategic priority, bolstered by targeted talent incubation programs.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance and risk management practices have been institutionalized in accordance with advanced international standards, earning recognition and prestigious accolades from premier institutions such as the IFC and Asia Risk. VPBank has sustained its status as one of the top 5 large-cap constituents in the Vietnam Sustainability Index (VNSI) portfolio published by HOSE for four consecutive years.
