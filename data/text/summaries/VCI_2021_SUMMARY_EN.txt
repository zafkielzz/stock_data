### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2021 marked a breakthrough and spectacular transformation for Viet Capital Securities Joint Stock Company (VCSC - Ticker: VCI) against the backdrop of a booming Vietnamese stock market, with the VN-Index closing the year at 1,498 points. Full-year net revenue reached VND 3,733 billion, representing a robust year-on-year growth of 115%. Profit before tax (PBT) came in at VND 1,851 billion, up 95% year-on-year, successfully outperforming the Annual General Meeting’s (AGM) target of VND 1,250 billion by 48%. This core growth momentum was driven by a confluence of a favorable market environment, a sharp surge in system-wide liquidity, and the agile execution capabilities of core business segments. Earnings per share (EPS) reached VND 4,502, up 93%, while the return on average equity (ROAE) sustained an exceptionally high level of 27.1%. VCSC continued to cement its position as a financial institution with profitability margins significantly outperforming the industry average, bearing testament to its intrinsic strength and capital allocation efficiency across the organization.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Regarding the financial structure and leverage profile, VCSC’s net debt-to-equity ratio shifted upward to 1.1x in 2021 from 0.6x in 2020. This increase was primarily driven by the company's proactive capital mobilization strategy via an offshore syndicated loan executed in 2021. Accessing international funding sources enabled the firm to capitalize on low interest rate environments globally, thereby expanding its capacity to provide highly competitive financing packages for clients' margin lending activities. Concurrently, equity recorded a strong growth rate of 44.7%, serving as a robust buffer that significantly enhanced the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) and reinforced the firm's resilience against macroeconomic volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Throughout the 2021 fiscal year, VCSC’s risk management and provisioning practices were rigorously maintained to safeguard its asset portfolio against financial market fluctuations. The company strictly adhered to provisioning regulations for the impairment of financial assets and receivables, ensuring a true and fair view of its financial health. Asset quality and operational safety metrics were continuously monitored under a standardized internal risk management framework. Data recorded from the system revealed no material off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities or unusual legal disputes that could exert an adverse impact on cash flows or business performance following the financial year-end of December 31, 2021.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** VCSC is deeply committed to a sustainable development vision, focusing on optimizing internal resources, enhancing the efficient use of office supplies and energy, and comprehensively digitalizing operational processes to minimize its carbon footprint within the context of specialized financial services.
- **Social (S):** Human capital is positioned at the core of the strategic framework. Amid the complex developments of the COVID-19 pandemic, VCSC deployed comprehensive welfare policies to ensure a safe, equitable, and transparent working environment. Driven by effective long-term human resource strategies, the staff turnover rate in 2021 plummeted by 50% compared to 2020—dropping from 17.24% to 8.71%—reflecting a high degree of organizational engagement and workforce retention.
- **Governance (G):** VCSC maintained high corporate governance standards through information transparency and strict adherence to the Professional Code of Ethics by all employees. Independent audits were conducted objectively, coupled with a strategic orientation to align stakeholder interests with international sustainable development standards, thereby solidly reinforcing shareholder and market confidence.
