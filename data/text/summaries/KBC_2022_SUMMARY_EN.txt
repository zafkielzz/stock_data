### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In the 2022 fiscal year, Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation (HOSE: KBC) exhibited a bearish price performance, with its closing price plummeting by -46.80% (sliding from an opening price of VND 45,490 to VND 24,200). The stock traded within a 52-week range of VND 13,250 to VND 47,220, underpinned by an average matching trading volume of 6,675,611 shares per session. Core operational drivers are anchored in the management and development of a massive industrial park (IP) land bank totaling 6,386.54 hectares—representing approximately 5.19% of the nationwide aggregate IP land area—alongside 1,262.8 hectares of urban and residential land. The conglomerate derives predictable recurring cash flows from four fully absorbed IPs (aggregate area of 1,013 hectares) and one IP achieving a 95.89% occupancy rate (426 hectares), monetized through stable annual utility and management fees, potable water supply, and wastewater treatment services. KBC sustains its premier market positioning in capturing foreign direct investment (FDI) inflows, cultivating deep-rooted relationships with tier-one global technology conglomerates, notably Canon, Foxconn, LG, JA Solar, Goertek, Fuyu, Luxshare, Jufeng, and Ingrasys. Furthermore, the synergistic integration of IPs with adjacent urban township models—exemplified by the Phuc Ninh Urban Area in Bac Ninh—coupled with the expansion of ready-built factories and office spaces for lease, effectively addresses the diversified operational requirements of satellite enterprises, compresses time-to-market, and optimizes closed-loop supply chains.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DỢT & INTEREST RATES
Amid macroeconomic volatility, monetary policy shifts, and credit tightening cycles, real estate developers broadly and KBC specifically face persistent structural headwinds. Historical performance illustrates that KBC exhibits high sensitivity to credit tightening; during the 2011–2014 credit crunch, the company faced restricted credit access, necessitating complete financial self-sufficiency via internal cash generation while prompting the temporary suspension of several commercial real estate developments. Conversely, during accommodative monetary cycles (2015–2016), the group leveraged cost-effective credit facilities with interest rates hovering between 9.5% and 10.0% p.a. The evolving regulatory landscape governing real estate credit risk-weight coefficients—which historically fluctuated from 100% to 250% pursuant to Circular 13/2010/TT-NHNN, alongside subsequent revisions—demands agile balance-sheet management from KBC. Nevertheless, weathering multiple monetary tightening cycles has honed management’s proficiency, ensuring the Group's ongoing capacity to deploy capital efficiently and sustain its land bank expansion trajectory across strategic municipal hubs, including Hai Phong, Hung Yen, Hai Duong, Quang Ninh, Tay Ninh, Vung Tau, and Tien Giang.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
The paramount exogenous risk confronting KBC’s operational and investment activities stems from prospective shifts in state regulatory frameworks governing industrial parks. These risks encompass potential friction in securing new project approvals, land expropriation or mandatory land-use conversion, alterations in investment incentive regimes, and volatility in tax policies and site-clearance compensation benchmarks. To mitigate these legal and regulatory overlays, KBC proactively engages with competent authorities, submitting comprehensive economic impact analyses and policy advocacy briefs. Regarding project execution pipelines, notwithstanding the government's approval of investment policies in 2022 for several expansions—such as the Quang Chau IP Expansion (90 ha), Tan Tap IP (654 ha), and Loc Giang IP (466 ha)—the Trang Due 3 IP project has encountered an protracted approval cycle originating in 2016. A pivotal legal inflection point materialized on March 30, 2023, via the issuance of Prime Ministerial Decision No. 323/QĐ-TTg approving adjustments to the master plan for Hai Phong City, establishing a robust statutory foundation for Trang Due 3 to secure its formal investment policy approval anticipated in Q3 2023.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** KBC strategically positions its industrial parks to attract high-quality FDI, prioritizing high-tech, low-emission projects to curtail environmental footprints. Rigorous infrastructure maintenance protocols are enforced to uphold modern, pristine, and ecologically sound IP landscapes, complemented by municipal public-utility developments such as centralized regulating lakes across operational localities.
- **Social (S):** The Group prioritizes human capital development, reporting a total headcount of 210 employees at the parent company level and 554 personnel across the consolidated Group as of December 31, 2022 (representing a net addition of 06 personnel compared to 2021). The workforce exhibits a diversified educational mix, spanning post-graduates, university and college graduates, vocational degree holders, skilled technicians, and dedicated security details. A core competitive advantage in KBC’s human resources strategy is its steadfast commitment to preserving headcount and shielding compensation packages from cuts during macroeconomic downturns, paired with aggressive talent acquisition initiatives designed to forge a robust succession pipeline for the forthcoming decade. Furthermore, the integration of industrial parks with urban townships and social housing developments successfully alleviates regional housing deficits, elevates the material and spiritual well-being of the labor force, generates thousands of local jobs, and contributes substantial fiscal revenue to municipal budgets.
- **Governance (G):** KBC maintains a sophisticated corporate governance framework propelled by an experienced executive roster adept at investment promotion and high-level negotiations with multinational corporations. Corporate culture is anchored in institutional accountability, organizational cohesion, and long-term employee retention, thereby safeguarding the elite reputation and market leadership of the KINHBAC CITY brand across domestic and international arenas.
