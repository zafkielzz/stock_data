### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Vietnam Technological and Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Techcombank) delivered robust financial performance amidst market volatility, reporting a Net Profit After Tax (NPAT) of VND 25,954 billion, up 19.3% YoY. This core growth momentum was propelled by a comprehensive customer-centric transformation strategy, capturing synergistic value across its financial ecosystem. During the year, the bank onboarded 2.7 million new-to-bank (NTB) customers, expanding its total client base to over 18 million. The Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio maintained its industry-leading position, while fee income market share—driven by foreign exchange transactions and bond distribution—climbed to #1 in the sector. Business units, namely the Retail Banking Group (RBG) and the Corporate and Institutional Banking Group (CIBG), operated in close coordination to accelerate digitalization, leveraging data analytics and AI to deliver superior financial solutions and elevate enterprise-wide operational efficiency and productivity.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Techcombank preserved its top-tier capital adequacy profile, underscored by a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 14.6% at the close of 2025. S&P Global Ratings upgraded the bank’s credit rating to BB with a Stable outlook, while Fitch Ratings assigned a long-term Issuer Default Rating (IDR) of BB- with a Positive outlook for the first time, reflecting a sound capital structure and a prudent risk governance framework. As a specialized credit institution, the bank's capital structure predominantly comprises customer deposits, valuable paper issuances, and shareholders' equity. These are subjected to stringent oversight by the Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) to mitigate interest rate and liquidity risks while optimizing the cost of capital in a volatile macroeconomic environment.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Techcombank’s asset quality remains among the best in the market, with Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) tightly controlled. The bank enforces rigorous asset classification and credit risk provisioning in strict compliance with prevailing regulations, including Circular 31/2024/TT-NHNN and Decree 86, which mandates a 0.75% general provisioning rate for performing loans within scope. Adequate provisions for impairment and credit risk were recognized for trading securities and unlisted corporate bonds, ensuring robust loss-absorption capacity. Off-balance-sheet commitments (such as letters of credit and guarantees) are managed under a three-lines-of-defense governance model supervised by the Supervisory Board and the Risk Committee. No material adverse legal contingencies or extraordinary financial events were recorded post-balance sheet date of December 31, 2025.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Sustainability remains central to Techcombank’s corporate strategy. The bank expanded its green credit portfolio to over VND 18.7 trillion to finance sustainable development projects. In the retail segment, over 1 million Techcombank Visa Eco cards (Debit & Credit) were issued, offsetting approximately 201.9 tonnes of CO₂. Operationally, the bank pioneered paperless branch operations to enhance energy efficiency and optimize resource utilization.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes human capital development, gender equality, workplace safety, and employee welfare. Key community initiatives during the year included marathon series in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City attracting 36,000 runners to promote healthy lifestyles; the "Dong Trao" campaign, mobilizing nearly 9,000 employees to plant over 40,000 trees; and a total contribution of VND 231 billion directed toward nationwide social welfare programs.
- **Governance (G):** The ESG governance framework was fortified through an ESG Advisory Board directly chaired by CEO Jens Lottner, with participation from core division heads. The bank strictly enforces advanced corporate governance practices, complying with the Law on Credit Institutions, international standards such as the COSO internal control framework, and Vietnam Sustainability Index (VNSI) criteria. Consequently, TCB shares were officially inducted by HOSE into the VNSI Index, ranking among the Top 20 listed companies with the best sustainability practices in the market.
