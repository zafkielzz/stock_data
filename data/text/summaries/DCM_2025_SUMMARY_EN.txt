### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2025, Petrovietnam Ca Mau Fertilizer Joint Stock Company (DCM) posted consolidated revenue of VND 17,033 billion, demonstrating robust growth despite macro headwinds. Consolidated net profit reached VND 1,962 billion, reflecting a strong earnings recovery. Key growth drivers stemmed from the stable operations of the Ca Mau Fertilizer plant at high capacity utilization (113–118% of design capacity) alongside optimized production costs (an 18% reduction in energy consumption for NPK production). The export segment served as a vital pillar with a footprint across 22 countries, generating export turnover exceeding VND 4,747 billion. Notably, the Cambodian market and emerging high-potential markets helped mitigate domestic competitive pressures. However, gross profit margins faced headwinds from volatile feedstock prices, although DCM successfully maintained tight control over its value chain from production to distribution.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DCM’s capital structure in FY2025 remained prudent and stable, with a low debt-to-equity (D/E) ratio of 17.07%. Equity accounts for the overwhelming majority of funding, establishing a robust financial buffer. The company maintained short-term borrowings to fund working capital requirements (total short-term debt stood at approximately VND 2,282 billion at year-end). Major financial partners, including Vietcombank and VietinBank, provided credit lines at competitive interest rates supported by the company’s strong corporate standing. Despite periodic fluctuations in market interest rates, DCM’s healthy financial architecture minimized cash flow pressures and interest expense risks, thereby safeguarding shareholder returns in a less-than-favorable interest rate environment.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
DCM maintains a conservative provisioning policy. As of December 31, 2025, the company had set aside provisions for inventory write-downs and doubtful debts based on rigorous risk assessments of customer credit profiles and prevailing market conditions. Contingent liabilities primarily relate to payment commitments or guarantee obligations arising from transactions with related parties. No material legal disputes impairing the company’s financial standing were recorded. Regarding post-balance-sheet events, DCM reported no extraordinary occurrences that would materially impact the integrity of the audited financial statements. DCM’s Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) system effectively identified and proactively mitigated operational risks.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DCM remains firmly committed to its Net-Zero roadmap by 2050. In 2025, the company rolled out energy-saving initiatives at its manufacturing plants, achieving its target of a 3% reduction in energy consumption for the 2021–2025 period. Investments in emission-treatment technologies and waste-heat recovery further contributed to cutting Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions.
- **Social (S):** The company maintained a safe working environment with an average monthly salary of VND 45.12 million per employee. Total training hours reached 37,297 hours, focusing on leadership and technical skill development for 1,202 employees. Strict adherence to gender equality and occupational health and safety policies was maintained, with zero serious labor accidents reported.
- **Governance (G):** DCM’s corporate governance aligns with regional standards, backed by a highly independent Board of Directors. DCM ranks among the Top 5 listed enterprises with the highest governance scores on the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard. Independent audits conducted by Deloitte Vietnam and the adoption of reporting standards compliant with GRI/SDGs reinforce the company's commitment to transparency, bolstering confidence among both domestic and international investors.
