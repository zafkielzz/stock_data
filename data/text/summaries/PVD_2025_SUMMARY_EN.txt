### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, PV Drilling delivered exceptional financial growth, underpinned by a robust recovery in both regional and international rig markets. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 11,553 billion (up 20.8% YoY), while consolidated net profit after tax rose to VND 1,052 billion (up 50.7% YoY). The drilling services segment served as the core growth engine, with rig utilization rates maintaining an impressive high of 99.5% to 99.8%. All owned and chartered rigs (including PV DRILLING I, II, III, VI, VIII, V, etc.) secured continuous employment, with average day rates improving to approximately USD 89k/day. In international markets, PVD successfully defended its market share in key jurisdictions such as Malaysia, Indonesia, and Brunei through long-term drilling campaigns for major counterparties including Petronas, Pertamina, and Shell, thereby contributing a substantial share to consolidated top and bottom lines.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PVD’s capital structure remains conservative, with a Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio of 18.63%, supported by a solid expansion in total assets and equity. Total financial debt as of year-end 2025 comprised short-term borrowings (VND 5,619 billion) and long-term debt allocated for strategic investment projects, such as the acquisition of the new jack-up rig PV DRILLING VIII and preparatory expenditures for the upcoming PV DRILLING IX project. Debt obligations are predominantly denominated in USD, bearing a mix of floating interest rates (tied to SOFR or LIBOR) and fixed rates extended by major commercial banks in Vietnam, including Vietcombank (VCB), BIDV (BID), MB Bank (MBB), and SeABank. The company implements rigorous foreign exchange and interest rate risk management by utilizing USD cash inflows from service contracts to service principal and interest obligations, thereby minimizing exposure to currency volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONING, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Provisioning is executed conservatively by PVD in strict compliance with prevailing regulations to accurately reflect the true carrying value of assets. Specifically, inventory write-down provisions are derived from assessments of current conditions and the turnover capacity of spare parts and supplies earmarked for drilling campaigns. Regarding receivables, the company enforces a stringent credit policy, conducts periodic debt classifications, and maintains adequate provisions for non-performing loans associated with drilling clients. On contingent liabilities, off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments primarily relate to bid bonds and performance bonds for domestic and international drilling projects. No material legal disputes or extraordinary financial events were recorded post-December 31, 2025, that would adversely impact the corporation's operational standing.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** PV Drilling actively mitigates its environmental footprint across all drilling operations. The Drilling Management Enterprise (DME) and affiliated units continue to monitor and curtail greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3), optimize energy consumption, transition to Bio-Diesel (B30) fuel usage across rigs, and deploy circular economy initiatives such as the recycling of domestic and industrial wastewater.
- **Social (S):** The company upholds stringent occupational safety standards, achieving a Zero LTI (Lost Time Injury) track record across multiple rigs. The average employee compensation reached VND 29.5 million/person/month, complemented by comprehensive welfare policies, regular technical training programs to upskill personnel, and the safeguarding of employee rights.
- **Governance (G):** PVD adopts an advanced corporate governance framework aligned with international best practices and the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) standards. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence, supported by specialized sub-committees (ESG Strategy and Sustainable Development Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Audit Committee, and Risk Management Committee), ensuring transparency, independent audits, and strict compliance with GRI standards and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
