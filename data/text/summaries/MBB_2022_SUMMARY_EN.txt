### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2022, against a macroeconomic backdrop characterized by heightened global uncertainty, surging inflation, and aggressive monetary policy tightening, MB Group demonstrated robust performance, steadfastly executing its 2022–2026 strategic roadmap built upon four core pillars: "Digital Acceleration," "Customer Attractiveness," "Group Synergy," and "Sustainable Safety." Key scale metrics—including Total Assets, Charter Capital, and Profit Before Tax (PBT)—recorded impressive growth rates ranging from 20% to 38% YoY. Specifically, Group Total Assets reached VND 728.5 trillion, Charter Capital stood at VND 45.3 trillion (following the successful execution of a 20% stock dividend payout), and PBT reached VND 22.7 trillion (with the Bank accounting for VND 20.3 trillion). The primary growth driver stemmed from a comprehensive digital transformation strategy anchored by two flagship platforms: the APP MBBank (for retail clients) and BIZ MBBank (for corporate clients). These tools enabled MB to onboard nearly 7 million new clients in 2022, expanding its cumulative client base to nearly 20 million. Digital channel revenues doubled YoY, contributing 17% to total Bank revenue, with digital transaction penetration reaching 95%. Furthermore, subsidiaries delivered a solid performance, generating a combined total revenue of VND 23.7 trillion (+30% YoY) and PBT of VND 2.7 trillion (+18% YoY), representing 12% of the Group's consolidated PBT.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
During the 2022 financial year, MB’s equity base and charter capital were significantly strengthened through the successful completion of a capital hike, bringing charter capital to VND 45,339,861 million as of December 31, 2022 (compared to VND 37,783,218 million as of December 31, 2021). To secure medium- and long-term funding sources and comply with the State Bank of Vietnam’s (SBV) capital adequacy regulations, the Board of Directors approved and executed Tier 2 subordinated bond issuances alongside medium-term foreign borrowing plans. Capital mobilization, private placement bond issuances, and principal/interest repayment schedules were executed in strict compliance with periodic disclosure regulations. Nevertheless, amidst extreme liquidity pressures and soaring interest rates in the financial and domestic corporate bond markets during Q4 2022, MB deployed agile Asset-Liability Management (ALCO) policies to effectively mitigate interest rate risk and ensure systemic cash flow equilibrium.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
MB’s risk management and provisioning framework in 2022 was rigorously administered in accordance with prevailing regulations, most notably Circular No. 11/2021/TT-NHNN regarding loan classification and risk provisioning. Credit risk was tightly contained, with the consolidated non-performing loan (NPL) ratio remaining low at 1.09% (the Bank standalone maintained an NPL ratio of 0.83%) and the NPL coverage ratio reaching a robust 295%, establishing a substantial provisioning buffer against macroeconomic volatility. Concurrently, the Bank flexibly applied regulatory provisions concerning loan repayment restructuring and debt classification retention to support clients adversely impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and credit policies targeting agricultural and rural development. Regarding post-balance sheet events, MB established a 100% foreign-owned subsidiary bank in Cambodia, which officially commenced operations on January 2, 2023. Focused on digital banking and retail segments, this milestone marks a strategic advancement in MB’s safe and efficient international footprint expansion.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** MB integrates sustainable development goals into its core business operations, leveraging technology as a key enabler for emission reduction and environmental preservation. By promoting digital platforms such as APP MBBank, BIZ MBBank, and the automated MB Smartbank self-service model, MB enables 24/7 remote client transactions, thereby minimizing paper consumption and curtailing carbon emissions associated with physical commuting. Notably, MB partnered with the Sống Foundation to launch the "Together with MB Greening Vietnam" campaign to raise funds for afforestation, aiming to reforest 14 hectares in Ninh Thuận province; the initiative garnered nearly 100,000 supporters and over 1 million participations.
- **Social (S):** MB prioritizes the cultivation of a secure, cohesive corporate culture, implementing comprehensive welfare policies to safeguard the physical and mental well-being of its workforce. Furthermore, the Bank champions community engagement by spearheading digital transformation in humanitarian initiatives. It developed and oversees the "Thiện Nguyện" (Charity) app—a technological platform designed to bring transparency to philanthropic sponsorship and disaster relief operations—which currently boasts over 1 million users and serves as a core pillar of the Bank's social welfare framework.
- **Governance (G):** MB’s corporate governance strictly adheres to legal frameworks, its corporate charter, and advanced governance best practices under the rigorous oversight of the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Board, and specialized committees. MB implements state-of-the-art governance standards advised by major international partners (including IBM, McKinsey, and Gartner), thereby optimizing internal workflows, enhancing operational transparency, and safeguarding equitable rights for shareholders and stakeholders.
