**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
FY2022 marked a robust operational breakthrough for the Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV), with consolidated profit before tax (PBT) reaching VND 23,009 billion—representing a 69.8% surge year-on-year and outperforming the Annual General Meeting (AGM) targets. Net interest and similar income (NII) stood at VND 47,025 billion. The core growth driver stemmed from asset scale expansion, which surpassed the VND 2.12 quadrillion threshold (+20.4% YoY), cementing BIDV's position as the commercial bank with the largest asset base in Vietnam. Consolidated gross credit extension reached VND 1.53 quadrillion, up 12.1% YoY, capturing a 12.87% credit market share of the entire economy.

Regarding business segment mix, BIDV aggressively accelerated its strategic pivot toward the retail and Small and Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) segments. The retail loan portfolio proportion expanded by 3.6 percentage points to account for 43.4% of total outstanding credit, while SME loans represented 22%. In the wholesale banking segment, growth was driven by digitized trade finance solutions on BIDV iBank and supply chain financing products (developed in conjunction with the Asian Development Bank - ADB) targeting key sectors such as animal feed, building materials, telecommunications, petroleum, and automotive. International operations remained stable across the Laos and Cambodia markets. On the equity bourse, BIDV's share price traded within a range of VND 18,790 - 33.520 per share, closing the year at VND 25,910 per share (+2.65% YoY), with average liquidity exceeding 2.04 million shares per session.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
BIDV maintained a diversified funding structure and high liquidity buffers. Total mobilized funds reached VND 1.97 quadrillion, of which deposits from economic organizations and residential sectors (Market 1) accounted for VND 1.64 trillion, up 8.4% YoY and representing approximately 14% of system-wide deposit market share. Aside from customer deposits, the liability structure comprised interbank deposits/borrowings from other credit institutions (CIs), borrowings from the Government/State Bank of Vietnam (SBV), issued valuable papers, and syndicated/entrusted funding sources.

Financial capacity and equity leverage were reinforced, with charter capital reaching VND 50,585 billion and market capitalization touching VND 195.3 trillion (~USD 8.34 billion). Driven by optimized capital efficiency, profitability metrics improved markedly, with Return on Equity (ROE) reaching 19.34% and Return on Assets (ROA) hitting 0.95%. The participation of strategic partner Hana Securities (a subsidiary of Hana Financial Group) alongside Moody's upgrade of BIDV’s issuer and deposit ratings from Ba3 to Ba2 underscores the bank’s sound liquidity risk management framework and robust capital structure.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
Asset quality was tightly controlled, with the on-balance-sheet non-performing loan (NPL) ratio (under Circular 11/2021/TT-NHNN) declining to 0.96%. The NPL coverage ratio remained exceptionally high at 226%. BIDV fully provisioned for credit risk per regulatory mandates, including 100% specific provisioning for debt restructured due to the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic (pursuant to SBV Circulars 01, 03, and 14, alongside equivalent regulations in the Cambodian and Lao markets). The bank also strictly complied with debt classification based on Credit Information Center (CIC) data queries to ensure accurate recognition of systemic risks.

Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, BIDV incurred customary contingent obligations arising from bank guarantees, credit commitments, letters of credit (L/C), and derivative financial instruments deployed for client foreign exchange and interest rate hedging. There were no reports of abnormal legal disputes or major financial fluctuations occurring after the accounting balance sheet date (December 31, 2022) that would materially impair the reliability of the audited financial statements.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** BIDV is strategically positioning itself as a "Green Bank," leading the market in sustainable finance sponsorship with a green credit portfolio focused on renewable energy projects. BIDV is the pioneering domestic financial institution to partner with the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment in executing climate change adaptation solutions and green finance development.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes high-quality human capital development via the "BIDV 30-Year-Old Leadership Development Program" and the standardization of professional title regulations. Concurrently, BIDV fulfills its corporate social responsibility through community welfare initiatives, educational and healthcare sponsorships, national security and defense support, and the implementation of the National Financial Inclusion Strategy.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance has been refined in alignment with international best practices, executing a synchronized ESG governance framework comprising 07 component strategies for the 2021–2025 period. The consolidated financial statements are audited independently and transparently, complying with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) applicable to credit institutions and progressively converging with international sustainability reporting standards (GRI Standards).
