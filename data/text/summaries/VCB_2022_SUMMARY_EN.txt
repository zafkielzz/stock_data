### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, against a macroeconomic backdrop characterized by escalating global inflation and monetary tightening, Vietnam’s economy posted a robust recovery with GDP growth of 8.02% and average CPI inflation of 3.15%. Total import-export turnover reached USD 732.5 billion (up 9.5%), comprising export growth of 10.6%, import growth of 8.4%, and a trade surplus of USD 11.2 billion. Capitalizing on this momentum, Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - VCB) successfully fulfilled its operational targets, underpinned by core drivers of credit expansion and effective portfolio restructuring. Specifically, total gross credit outstandings officially surpassed the VND 1 trillion threshold, expanding by 19% YoY (representing an absolute credit increase of approximately VND 200 trillion), marking the highest growth rate across the domestic banking sector. Regarding segment configuration, VCB strictly controlled the proportion of medium- and long-term credit at 45% while prudently adhering to regulatory limits on short-term funding for medium- and long-term loans. The bank directed liquidity toward essential sectors and the Government's five priority areas, posting notable growth across segments: supporting industries grew by 27%, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) by 22.1%, and agriculture and rural development by 20%. Profitability dynamics were further reinforced by VCB's market-leading positioning, propelling FY2022 profit before tax (PBT) to over VND 37.3 trillion, up 36% YoY and cementing its status as the most profitable bank in the market.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Throughout 2022, Vietcombank dynamically managed its funding mobilization in alignment with asset deployment and market developments, lifting total assets to VND 1.8 trillion (a 28.2% expansion YoY). The bank successfully maintained a structurally positive migration of its funding and credit composition in line with strategic directives. In credit extension and capital allocation, VCB acted as the lead arranger and strategic partner for large-scale funding of national key projects and major conglomerates. Notable transactions included the signing of credit facilities totaling nearly VND 20 trillion for Vietnam National Petroleum Group (Petrolimex); serving as the lead mandated lead arranger for a USD 2.5 billion financing package for the Long Thanh Airport Project (part of ACV’s total investment exceeding VND 99 trillion); and underwriting VND 35 billion in credit agreements (out of a total investment capital of VND 70 trillion) for the Hoa Phat Dung Quat 2 Iron and Steel Complex Project. Concerning interest rate pressures and cash flow dynamics, to assist clients in post-pandemic operational recovery against adverse headwinds, VCB pioneered lending rate cuts of up to 150 bps (1.0% p.a.) during the final two months of 2022 for over 175,000 customers representing more than VND 500 trillion in outstanding loans (accounting for nearly 50% of total performing loans). Concurrently, VCB waived 100% of transfer fees and maintenance charges on its VCB Digibank digital channel effective January 1, 2022.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Vietcombank’s credit portfolio asset quality was rigorously managed, ranking among the top tier within the domestic banking system. The NPL ratio (on-balance sheet) was kept low at 0.68%, significantly outperforming assigned internal targets. Crucially, VCB maintained the highest loan-loss coverage ratio (LLCR) in the banking sector at 317% (reaching up to 457% according to select specialized valuation reports), thereby establishing a robust provisioning buffer against latent credit risks. The bank strictly complied with the State Bank of Vietnam’s (SBV) credit growth caps while maintaining a conservative and selective underwriting stance toward high-risk sectors—such as real estate, securities, and BOT/BT transport infrastructure projects—with investment and lending exposures in these sectors accounting for a negligible 0.5% to 2% of total credit outstandings. Internal audit and off-site surveillance were comprehensively maintained, with 22 audits successfully completed during the year across branches, subsidiaries, and head office departments.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** Vietcombank emphasizes the integration of environmental factors into its credit underwriting strategy, steering capital toward clean energy, renewable energy, and sustainable development projects to contribute to national environmental protection objectives.
- **Social (S):** The bank upholds high corporate social responsibility standards as a premier financial institution supported by a workforce of over 22,000 employees system-wide. In 2022, VCB intensified its community-focused social welfare programs with committed sponsorships totaling approximately VND 490 billion. Furthermore, the bank proactively alleviated the financial burdens of individuals and enterprises via extensive fee-waiver packages and broad-based lending rate reductions.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance at VCB operates with high transparency under the stringent oversight of the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board (comprising 4 members as of year-end 2022). The bank strictly complies with statutory regulations and the Law on Credit Institutions, early-adopts advanced risk management standards (such as Basel II under Circular 41 one year ahead of schedule), and maintains top-tier systemic ratings under the SBV’s Circular 52/2018 credit institution rating framework.
