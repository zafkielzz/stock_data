### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In FY2021, FPT Corporation reported consolidated revenue of VND 35,657 billion (+19.5% YoY) and profit before tax (PBT) of VND 6,337 billion (+20.4% YoY), achieving 103% and 102% of their respective full-year targets. This earnings outperformance translated directly into equity price appreciation, with the stock delivering an impressive return of +83.13% during the year. Core growth momentum was driven by three primary pillars:

*   **Technology Segment (44% of total PBT):** Serving as the primary growth catalyst, Global IT Services exhibited robust expansion, led by the US market (+52% revenue growth) and Asia-Pacific (+27%). Digital Transformation (DX) revenue surged by +72%, with Cloud computing accounting for over 50% of this figure. The conglomerate secured 19 large-scale contracts valued at over USD 5 million each (double the volume of FY2020). Domestic IT rebounded swiftly, underpinned by the FPT eCovax solution suite and strategic synergies from the Base.vn M&A transaction, driving revenue and PBT growth of 29% and 33.9%, respectively, in this vertical.
*   **Telecom Segment (38% of total PBT):** Effectively capitalized on the surge in remote work, online learning, and in-home entertainment demand amid the pandemic. Broadband subscriber counts grew by nearly 16%, while PayTV subscribers increased by approximately 21%. The segment PBT margin expanded from 16.7% to 17.5% driven by operating expenditure (opex) optimization.
*   **Education & Other Segments (18% of total PBT):** FPT Education recorded a 43% increase in total converted learners, reaching 74,313 students, thereby cementing its market position as a premier private educational institution.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Amid macroeconomic volatility induced by the pandemic, FPT maintained a proactive and flexible liquidity management strategy. Short-term debt issuances and repayments were executed dynamically to optimize working capital costs supporting telecom infrastructure and data center projects.

Detailed reporting indicates gross debt drawdowns of VND 16,765 billion alongside total debt repayments of VND 16,412 billion. Internal credit and lending activities totaled VND 394 billion, of which VND 185 billion has been successfully recovered. These financial transactions were executed in strict compliance with regulations, safeguarding solvency and maintaining a conservative capital structure. The Supervisory Board noted no instances of cash flow mismatches or anomalies regarding the management and servicing of the Group’s debt obligations.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Financial risk management was rigorously executed in accordance with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) and conservative accounting principles. Revenue deductions, cost-of-goods-sold provisions, allowances for doubtful accounts, and inventory write-downs were all recognized in their proper accounting periods and comprehensively assessed prior to the finalization of the consolidated financial statements.

During FY2021, the Supervisory Board registered no legal disputes, litigations, or contingent liabilities arising from off-balance-sheet guarantees that could materially impair the Group's financial position. Related-party transactions (including commercial trade, software services, and shared-cost allocations valued at VND 782 billion) were conducted strictly on arm's length commercial terms. Subsequent to the balance sheet date of December 31, 2021, no extraordinary financial events occurred that would fundamentally alter or materially impact the reported accounting figures.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
FPT comprehensively integrated sustainability criteria into its long-term corporate development strategy:

*   **Environmental (E):** Advanced green operations via internal digital transformation. Through 43 internal DX initiatives, FPT transitioned toward a paperless and cashless office model, generating operational cost savings of VND 98 billion. The completion and commissioning of the FPT Complex Phase 2 facility in Da Nang optimized energy efficiency and minimized operational carbon emissions.
*   **Social (S):** Placed human capital at the core of operations. The Group safeguarded the health, safety, and employment of its 37,180 workforce amidst the pandemic; and executed internal rotations of 500 managerial personnel to cultivate the next generation of leadership. FPT allocated VND 183.6 billion toward more than 100 Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives, benefiting 134,494 individuals. Notably, the Group initiated the establishment of Hope School in FPT City Da Nang to nurture and educate children orphaned by COVID-19.
*   **Governance (G):** FPT maintained its ranking in the Top 5 Best Corporate Governance Companies in Vietnam for 2021. Management demonstrated operational agility and shared risk mitigation via a 20% compensation reduction for senior executives during peak pandemic periods. A real-time data-driven management framework (FPT Data Lake) combined with OKR methodologies ensures operational transparency, rigorous regulatory compliance, and the maximization of shareholder value.
