### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In fiscal year 2023, despite a challenging macroeconomic backdrop domestically and globally, Vietnam demonstrated robust macroeconomic resilience, posting a GDP growth rate of 5.0%. Registered Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) reached nearly USD 36.61 billion (up 32.1% YoY), while disbursed FDI hit an all-time high of USD 23.18 billion (up 3.5% YoY). Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation's (KBC) core growth momentum is anchored in capturing the secular wave of FDI inflows into industrial parks (IPs) boasting strategic locations, synchronized infrastructure, and alignment with capital expenditure cycles of global technology conglomerates, including Canon, Foxconn, LG, JA Solar, Goertek, Fuyu, Luxshare, Jufeng, and Ingrasys. Alongside its core IP infrastructure leasing segment, KBC has actively diversified its revenue streams by concurrently developing ready-built factories and warehouses, integrated urban-industrial townships (a strategy pursued since 2017), and notably, a portfolio of Social Housing Projects (SHPs) addressing housing deficits for experts and industrial workforce (such as projects in Nenh town - Bac Giang, Bau Tram Lakeside Urban Area in Da Nang, and Trang Due SHP in Hai Phong). FY2023 marked several pivotal regulatory and legal milestones serving as catalysts for future top-line growth, encompassing the successful auction of the TMDV-05 commercial-services land plot in the North Song Cam New Urban Area (Hai Phong), the master plan adjustment for the Trang Cat urban and service area (584.9 ha), the regulatory approval of the Trang Due IP Expansion (Phase 3, 687 ha), and the proposed feasibility study for a 350-ha high-tech IP-urban complex in Can Tho.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Regarding capital structure and liquidity management, KBC has maintained a conservative posture to preclude the recurrence of operational risks associated with its conglomerate diversification phase during 2009–2010. The management proactively balances debt issuance and capital deployment to optimize return on capital while safeguarding solvency. A key financial highlight in FY2023 was KBC’s prioritization of meeting all debt obligations on schedule, culminating in the early full redemption of VND 3,900 billion in corporate bonds alongside VND 161.99 billion in accrued interest. This proactive deleveraging underscores KBC's robust financial profile, successfully eliminates near-term bond maturity walls, safeguards bondholder interests, and significantly enhances cash flow risk management and overall balance sheet resilience. *(Note: Granular data regarding short-term versus long-term debt breakdowns, floating interest rate exposures, or primary banking credit partners were not explicitly disclosed in the provided data set).*

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
Within its risk management framework, KBC has identified several material risk factors directly impacting its operational profile: (i) Regulatory and administrative risks pertaining to IP policy shifts (prolonged approval pipelines, modifications in tax incentive frameworks, land rental fee regimes, and site-clearance compensation pricing), which KBC actively mitigates through constructive regulatory engagement and policy advocacy; (ii) Competitive pressures for FDI attraction from India and Indonesia, compounded by the implementation of the Global Minimum Tax effective January 1, 2024, which compresses the efficacy of short-term tax incentives; and (iii) Operational risks within IP assets, including environmental contamination, natural disasters, industrial accidents, labor strikes, or global supply chain disruptions affecting FDI tenants. To counteract these exposures, KBC maintains stringent maintenance protocols, safety equipment provisioning, and ongoing occupational health, safety, and fire prevention training. *(Note: Specific metrics regarding inventory write-down provisions, allowance for doubtful accounts, off-balance-sheet guarantees, or active legal disputes were not recorded within the scope of the provided financial data).*

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** KBC has yet to adopt standardized Sustainability Reporting frameworks, attributable to the absence of a consulting partner aligned with its listed corporate model. Furthermore, KBC’s business operations do not fall under mandatory direct/indirect greenhouse gas (GHG) emission auditing mandates (exhibiting negligible carbon intensity). In its daily operations, KBC strictly complies with environmental statutes; its tenant base predominantly comprises "smokeless" high-tech enterprises. All industrial wastewater is treated at centralized wastewater treatment plants to meet National Technical Regulations on Industrial Wastewater (QCVN 40:2011/BTNMT, Column A) prior to discharge. Regarding energy and resource conservation initiatives, KBC integrates eco-friendly infrastructure design (LED street lighting, solar-oriented street illumination pathways, and utilization of local building materials). Additionally, administrative offices enforce power-saving protocols, paper usage reduction, and a strong preference for digitized documentation.
- **Social (S):** KBC places significant emphasis on human capital development through a flexible recruitment policy balancing experienced hires and fresh graduates, supplemented by rigorous internal training programs to insulate the firm against talent attrition. Crucially, KBC has committed to upholding collective labor agreements, maintaining a strict zero-layoff and zero-pay-cut policy even amid challenging macroeconomic headwinds. The corporation effectively couples IP development with corporate social responsibility by deploying large-scale social housing developments across Bac Giang, Hai Phong, and Da Nang to elevate the living standards of low-income workers and industrial laborers.
- **Governance (G):** KBC’s Board of Directors (BOD) effectively executes its oversight function over the Executive Board, ensuring strict regulatory compliance across all operational verticals, upholding corporate transparency, and championing sustainable development principles. The management apparatus is lean, featuring clear segregation of duties and authorities among the BOD, the Supervisory Board, and the Executive Board. Furthermore, strong emphasis is placed on cultivating a robust pipeline of middle and senior management successors to guarantee the Corporation’s long-term operational continuity and strategic stability.
