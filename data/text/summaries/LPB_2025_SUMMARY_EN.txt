*Below is a standardized qualitative analysis summary based on the 2025 Annual Report and verified data of LPBank:*

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, LPBank posted positive business growth with profit before tax reaching VND 14,269 billion, up 17% YoY, marking its highest level since inception. Core drivers stemmed from a robust shift in the business model toward in-depth growth, focusing on the retail and small and medium-sized enterprise (SME) segments, which helped optimize the credit portfolio and improve the net interest margin (NIM). Net interest income reached over VND 16 trillion (up 4%), while non-interest income broke out strongly to reach VND 5,847 billion (up 28.83%), making a positive contribution to total operating income (VND 21,881 billion, up 9.78%). The bank continued to maintain its cost-to-income ratio (CIR) at an optimal level of 28.3% (down 1% YoY) thanks to accelerated automation, digitization of operational processes, and management cost optimization. Regarding operational markets, LPBank focused on solidifying its domestic footprint, expanding network coverage, and optimizing its digital ecosystem (such as LPBank Biz, LCPhat Pay) to sustain market share and elevate its core competitive edge.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
LPBank’s financial structure in 2025 was firmly consolidated, with total assets reaching VND 605,585 billion (up 19% YoY). Equity stood at VND 47,192 billion, and charter capital reached VND 29,873 billion. Total capital mobilization reached VND 401,680 billion (up 18.62%), of which customer deposits accounted for the majority (VND 337,583 billion, up 19.22%), indicating a highly stable funding base from retail depositors. Customer loans reached VND 391,746 billion (up 18.1%). The bank proactively balanced short-term funding for medium- and long-term lending in compliance with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) safety ratios, while diversifying funding channels through the issuance of valuable papers (reaching VND 64,097 billion). Cash flow pressures were tightly managed via proactive liquidity governance models, maintaining a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 11.87% and controlling the Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio at 1.68%, ensuring the flexibility to meet financial obligations and liquidity demands amid any market volatility.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
LPBank’s risk management and provisioning practices were executed prudently, strictly adhering to SBV regulations and Basel III standards orientation. In 2025, credit risk provisioning expenses were recorded at VND 1,422 billion (down 27.21% YoY), reflecting strengthened asset quality and effective debt recovery capabilities. The on-balance-sheet NPL ratio was safely maintained at 1.68%, ranking among the Top 10 banks with the lowest NPL ratios industry-wide. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, LPBank maintained a safe portfolio of guarantees, credit commitments, and trade finance backed by a real-time limit-based risk control system (KRI, VaR, Stress testing). No material contingent liabilities or emerging legal disputes were recorded that could significantly and adversely impact financial operations. Financial events occurring after the accounting period-end date (December 31, 2025) were primarily related to charter capital adjustments and the approval of license amendments from the SBV under normal approval schedules, with no abnormal incidents arising that would negatively affect the Bank's operations.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
* **Environmental (E):** LPBank strongly integrated ESG criteria into its long-term development strategy, particularly focusing on the "Green Bank" orientation. The bank prioritized allocating credit capital to renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, and clean technology projects, while strictly controlling environmental risks in credit appraisal under the ESMF framework. Paperless office initiatives and the optimization of energy and resource consumption significantly reduced greenhouse gas emissions (Scopes 1, 2, and 3).
* **Social (S):** The bank focused on developing high-quality human resources through continuous training programs, ensuring a fair working environment, occupational health and safety (OHS), and absolute compliance with labor laws. Comprehensive welfare policies, regular health check-ups, and health insurance were well-maintained for employees. Additionally, LPBank actively fulfilled its social security responsibilities with hundreds of billions of dong allocated to education, healthcare, and the nationwide program for the eradication of temporary and dilapidated houses.
* **Governance (G):** The corporate governance system strictly complied with international best practices and Vietnamese legal regulations. The Board of Directors maintained a balanced structure with independent members participating in in-depth oversight. Independent audits were conducted transparently by leading auditing firms. LPBank applied information disclosure standards aligned with GRI and SDG frameworks, ensuring maximum rights for shareholders and stakeholders.
