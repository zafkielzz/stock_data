Below is the standard qualitative equity research summary based on the actual documents and dataset of Thành Thành Công - Biên Hòa Joint Stock Company (SBT) for the 2022–2023 financial year:

**1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In the 2022–2023 financial year, SBT's business operations experienced fluctuations driven by the global agricultural and sugar markets. The company's core growth drivers continued to center around the sugarcane value chain, spanning agricultural research, refined sugar production, and by-products (such as molasses, biomass electricity, and fertilizers). The company's export markets maintained their strategic positioning across Asia, Europe, and North America, effectively leveraging Free Trade Agreements. Strategic products such as organic sugar and high-grade refined sugar continued to play a pivotal role in sustaining profit margins, offsetting volatility in raw material input prices and global logistics costs throughout the fiscal year.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
SBT’s capital structure reflects the inherent characteristics of the sugar industry, featuring a high proportion of working capital dedicated to seasonal sugarcane procurement. The company maintains strategic credit relationships with major domestic and international commercial banks to secure short-term, medium-term, and long-term funding for raw material zone expansion projects and factory technology upgrades. Borrowings comprise both fixed and floating interest rates depending on the currency of the loan (VND and USD). Cash flow management from operating activities is closely monitored by the Board of Directors and the Board of Management to optimize interest expenses and tightly control financial leverage ratios amid a volatile market interest rate environment.

**3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS**
SBT maintains a conservative risk management policy regarding receivables and inventories. The provisioning for inventory write-downs and allowance for doubtful debts is executed in strict compliance with prevailing accounting standards, accurately reflecting the true value of assets. Off-balance-sheet guarantee commitments and contingent financial obligations are rigorously monitored through internal control systems. Regarding equity performance during the year, SBT stock opened at VND 11,080 and closed at VND 11,310, representing a modest annual growth of 2.08%, with a trading range between a low of VND 10,460 and a high of VND 15,210, alongside an average matched liquidity of 2,689,749 shares/session, reflecting a cautious yet stable market sentiment toward the company's medium-term prospects.

**4. ESG EXECUTION**
- **Environmental (E):** SBT remains steadfast in its agricultural circular economy model (Zero Waste), thoroughly repurposing bagasse as feedstock for biomass power generation, boiler ash for organic fertilizers, and recycling wastewater for irrigation. The company is progressively conducting inventories and optimizing greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) across the entire value chain to pursue long-term sustainable development goals.
- **Social (S):** The company focuses on building a safe working environment, strictly complying with Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards across its factories and farms. Human resource policies ensure gender equality, combined with professional capability-building training programs and comprehensive employee welfare, particularly for contracted farming communities within the raw material zones.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance adheres to international best practices and current securities regulations. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence in strategic orientation, working closely with independent auditors to ensure transparency and accuracy in information disclosure while applying sustainability reporting standards aligned with GRI frameworks and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
