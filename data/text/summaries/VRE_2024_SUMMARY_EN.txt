**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Vincom Retail (VRE) marks its 20-year development trajectory with an impressive compound annual growth rate (CAGR) averaging 14% in total gross floor area (GFA) and 15% in leasing revenue over the past decade, reinforcing its market leadership in scale and operational efficiency. In 2024, VRE's share price experienced a downward volatility of -27.05%, opening the year at VND 22,700 and closing at VND 16,560 (trading within a 52-week range of VND 16,130 to VND 27,760, with an average matched trading volume of 8,749,501 shares/session). Core growth drivers continue to stem from portfolio expansion—as of year-end 2024, the Company operates 88 shopping malls across 48 of 63 provinces and municipalities, encompassing nearly 1.9 million square meters of retail GFA—coupled with the rollout of next-generation Vincom Mega Mall formats (such as the 2024 launch of Vincom Mega Mall Grand Park) anchored on a "one-stop shoppertainment destination" strategy. Alongside retail leasing, the commercial townhouse (shophouse) business and leasing operations across major urban areas and large-scale destination complexes (such as Grand World Phu Quoc, Grand World Ocean City, Vu Yen Walking Street - Park, K-Town, Little Hong Kong, and Vinpearl Harbour) continue to generate supplemental revenue and capital for reinvestment.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** Vincom Retail actively supports the Government’s net-zero emissions commitment by 2050 through research and execution of initiatives focused on green energy, green mobility, and green consumption. The Company targets specific operational metrics, aiming to optimize electricity consumption by 8% to 10% across HVAC systems in its shopping malls, while targeting the publication of a standalone Sustainability Report starting in 2025 in alignment with Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standards.
- **Social (S):** The Company prioritizes customer and employee experience, underpinned by transparent and publicly disclosed policies regarding Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) and staff welfare. VRE emphasizes its "Service from the Heart" corporate culture while actively leveraging local workforces across its operating footprints, generating employment, and fostering engagement to bolster local community economies.
- **Governance (G):** Vincom Retail demonstrates high compliance with corporate governance standards based on the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS). The Company maintains transparency through detailed disclosures of major shareholders holding 5% or more, direct and indirect ownership stakes of Board and Management members, and individual executive compensation details. Anti-bribery and anti-corruption regulations are rigorously enforced via an "Anti-Bribery Commitment" applicable to contracting partners alongside transparency protocols. The Board of Directors strictly adheres to conflict-of-interest principles, with interested members abstaining from voting on related-party transactions, complemented by annual transparent independent audits of the financial statements.
