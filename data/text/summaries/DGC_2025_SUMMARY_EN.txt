Below is the qualitative analysis summary of the operational performance of Duc Giang Chemicals Group Joint Stock Company (DGC) for the fiscal year 2025:

### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, the Group's consolidated total revenue reached VND 11,262 billion (+14% YoY), while consolidated profit after tax came in at VND 3,188 billion (+2.6% YoY), achieving 106% of the full-year target. Core growth was driven by the export market, which accounted for up to 69% of total consolidated revenue at VND 7,769 billion (+15% YoY), while the domestic market contributed 31% at VND 3,493 billion (+12% YoY). By product mix, the yellow phosphorus segment remained the primary growth engine, generating VND 5,499 billion (+29.3% YoY) and contributing over half of total revenue, underpinned by stable international demand. Fertilizer products exhibited clear divergence: DAP fertilizers surged 48.7% YoY to VND 958.2 billion, and NPK fertilizers experienced an exponential spike of 216% YoY to VND 107.6 billion; conversely, WPA acid dropped near-absent (-99.9% YoY) and 85% H3PO4 acid declined 15.8% YoY. Detergents and byproduct segments recorded stable positive growth ranging from 20% to 24%, while the alcohol segment witnessed a massive scale breakout with revenue soaring 1,316% YoY to VND 225.9 billion. This expansion reflects a flexible product portfolio shifting strategy and the optimization of competitive advantages in key target markets, including Japan, India, South Korea, and Europe.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
DGC’s financial structure maintains a high degree of soundness and safety, with a consolidated Total Liabilities-to-Total Assets ratio of 21% (26.90% of Equity, translating to a D/E ratio of 7.54%). As of December 31, 2025, consolidated total assets stood at VND 19,550.1 billion (+23.6% YTD), heavily dominated by current assets at VND 16,123.5 billion (representing 82.5% of total assets). Regarding debt composition, consolidated short-term debt was recorded at VND 4,080.5 billion (+98.7% YTD due to expanded working capital requirements for production scaling), whereas long-term debt was tightly controlled at a low level of VND 63.6 billion (-4.9% YTD). The company maintains robust current and quick liquidity ratios at very safe levels of 4.0x and 3.5x, respectively. Although free from material capital structure risks or debt-service pressures thanks to a substantial cash buffer and high profit margins (ROE of 5.78%, ROA of 5.01%), short-term operating cash flows faced moderate pressure from an inventory buildup to VND 1,681.4 billion (+70.7% YTD) to support scale-expansion projects.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
The company faces legal risks and material adverse events occurring immediately prior to the release of its financial statements. Specifically, on March 17, 2026, the Investigation Police Agency of the Ministry of Public Security announced a decision to initiate criminal proceedings regarding charges of "Environmental Pollution," "Violations of Regulations on Mineral Research, Prospecting, and Exploitation," and "Violations of Accounting Regulations Causing Serious Consequences" occurring at Duc Giang Chemicals Group and related entities at Mining Site 25 (Khai trường 25). This incident resulted in the sealing and seizure of multiple accounting records and documents of the Group by competent authorities, rendering the company unable to complete the audit of its FY2025 financial statements within the statutory deadline. The Board of Management has committed to immediately updating and disclosing official audited figures as soon as the investigation concludes and auditing activities are permitted to resume. Provisions for inventory obsolescence and bad debts are currently under rigorous review, factoring in input material price volatility and the legal developments of related litigation cases.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DGC prioritizes emissions reduction by investing in proprietary technology to capture 100% of CO2 emissions from its phosphorus plants for power generation and direct coal consumption, yielding hundreds of billions of dong in annual cost savings while safeguarding the environment. The Group maintains advanced wastewater treatment systems across its facilities with a water recycling rate of 90–95%, achieving near-zero wastewater discharge into the environment. Nevertheless, long-term Net-Zero targets are currently being impacted by the ongoing environmental investigation at Mining Site 25.
- **Social (S):** As of year-end 2025, the Group’s total headcount stood at 2,674 employees with an average monthly salary of VND 22 million/person. The company strictly complies with Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards, organizes periodic health checkups, and provides full social, health, and unemployment insurance coverage. DGC fosters corporate engagement through sports initiatives (such as sponsoring the women's volleyball team) and fulfills its corporate social responsibility, allocating nearly VND 19.34 billion toward charitable activities, flood relief, and the eradication of makeshift housing during the year.
- **Governance (G):** DGC’s governance framework complies with the Law on Enterprises and the company's Charter, overseen closely by the Supervisory Board (which held 2 core meetings during the year). However, governance faces challenges regarding independence and C-suite compliance, as Chairman of the Board of Directors Dao Huu Huyen concurrently holds positions across multiple other enterprises. This necessitates a corporate governance restructuring and the election of supplementary Board members at the 2026 Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders to comply with new regulatory requirements. Related-party transactions are stringently controlled and approved via Board Resolutions with 100% consensus.
