**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
Throughout FY2024, VPBank’s (VPB) share price appreciated by 5.33%, opening at VND 17,440 and closing at VND 18,370, trading within a 52-week range of VND 16,300 to VND 20,140 with an average matching trading volume of 18,369,519 shares per session. VPBank's core growth momentum continues to stem from its comprehensive and differentiated ecosystem strategy, serving a client base exceeding 30 million customers. The bank has accelerated the scale and growth trajectory of its retail banking segment by deeply penetrating the middle-to-high-income segments, while concurrently scaling up comprehensive financial solutions and initiatives to capture market share in the SME and corporate segments. Furthermore, pioneering technological integration—leveraging AI, GenAI, and Big Data into operations—has enabled VPBank to optimize workflows, elevate operational efficiency, and enrich the customer experience, thereby catalyzing momentum across its core business lines.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Regarding its capital structure, VPBank achieved a significant milestone by successfully executing landmark M&A and capital-raising transactions, most notably the completion of a 15% equity stake transfer to its strategic shareholder, Japan’s Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC). This transaction expanded total equity to nearly VND 140 trillion—ranking second system-wide—while preserving its position as the commercial bank with the largest charter capital in Vietnam. This robust capital foundation significantly mitigates capital structure risk, furnishing abundant liquidity to extend credit to the real economy without inducing excessive short-term financial leverage pressures. While granular disclosures regarding fixed-to-floating interest rate breakdowns or the specific identities of individual debt-financing banking partners were not explicitly detailed in the aggregate FY2024 dataset, the backing of a robust international strategic shareholder underpins a resilient and flexible balance sheet structure.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
In credit risk and asset portfolio management, VPBank has operated an Environmental and Social Risk Management System (ESMS) since 2016, periodically upgrading it to IFC standards to evaluate credit risk exposures for corporate clients and business households. For off-balance-sheet commitments, the bank enforces rigorous risk limits and sophisticated measurement tools to monitor latent risks. Regarding independent auditing, VPBank publicly disclosed the execution of its audit contract, although specific audit and non-audit service fees were not itemized in the aggregated documentation. Current data reports no extraordinary legal disputes, litigations, or adverse financial events materializing post-December 31, 2024.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** VPBank is a pioneering financial institution in establishing a comprehensive ESG Risk Management Framework, integrating climate change considerations into its risk appetite when setting credit limits for carbon-intensive sectors such as coal-fired power. The bank is strategically implementing a Transition Plan targeting Net Zero by 2050, standing as the sole financial institution in Vietnam to publish TCFD-aligned climate-related financial disclosures for three consecutive years.
- **Social (S):** The bank prioritizes strengthening Organizational Health Index (OHI) metrics to cultivate a work environment that attracts and retains top-tier talent, safeguards labor rights, and strictly enforces human rights policies—including whistleblower protection safeguards against retaliation for reporting unethical conduct.
- **Governance (G):** VPBank maintains a transparent corporate governance structure, disclosing information in compliance with international standards such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Board independence is reinforced by explicit regulations concerning executive compensation, alongside robust review and approval policies for material related-party transactions, as codified in the bank's Charter and publicly disclosed on its Investor Relations portal.
