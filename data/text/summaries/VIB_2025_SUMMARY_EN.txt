Below is the qualitative and financial summary based on the 2025 Annual Report and actual operational data of Vietnam International Commercial Joint Stock Bank (VIB):

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2025, VIB's business operations maintained stability against a volatile macroeconomic backdrop. Profit before tax (PBT) reached VND 9,105 billion, representing a 1% increase compared to 2024. The bank's core growth driver continued to be the Retail Banking (RB) division, accounting for approximately 70% of total system-wide outstanding loans, with over 90% of the portfolio concentrated in the high-quality collateralized segment (mortgages and auto loans). Net interest income (NII) remained the primary contributor, while non-interest income (Non-II) reached over VND 3,500 billion, accounting for 24% of total operating income (TOI). The Corporate Banking (CB) division recorded a strong breakthrough with outstanding loans surging 58% year-on-year, driven by a selective strategy targeting customers with solid financial foundations and an increase in the Current Account Savings Account (CASA) ratio to 20%. Subsidiary VIB AMC recorded net revenue of VND 288.978 million (up 29.86%) and PBT of VND 84.993 million (up 41.49%), making a positive contribution to overall operational efficiency through valuation, trading, and collateral exploitation services.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, BORROWINGS & INTEREST RATES
VIB maintains a safe capital and liquidity structure anchored by a diversified funding platform comprising customer deposits, valuable paper issuances, and credit lines from domestic and international financial institutions. Total mobilized capital reached VND 329,649 billion, up 10% compared to 2024. Amid persistently high USD interest rates, the bank proactively adjusted its short-term mobilization structuring strategy to optimize the cost of funds (COF) and ensure liquidity. The Basel III Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) reached 12.4% as of December 31, 2025, significantly exceeding the minimum requirement, supported by a Tier 1 capital-dominated equity structure. Liquidity risk management is executed proactively by maintaining a high buffer of liquid assets and asset-liability maturity matching aligned with various market scenarios to mitigate short-term cash flow pressures.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISK, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
VIB maintains strict risk management discipline with a Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio of 2.2% (down 0.2% from the beginning of the year – the lowest level in the 2023–2025 period). Asset quality is rigorously controlled through debt classification and credit risk provisioning strictly in accordance with State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) regulations. Provision expenses decreased by 23% year-on-year, driven by improvements in the loan portfolio quality and a strategic preference for transparent collateral. Guarantees, letters of credit (LC), and off-balance-sheet obligations were closely controlled within safe limits. No abnormal legal events or material financial incidents occurred after the end of the financial year on December 31, 2025, that would negatively impact the bank's operations.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** VIB focuses on promoting green finance, integrating environmental factors into credit appraisal processes, and prioritizing renewable energy and emission-reduction manufacturing projects. The bank standardizes energy, water, and waste consumption management across its offices while accelerating operational digitization to minimize paper and ink consumption.
- **Social (S):** The bank maintains a professional, transparent, and equitable working environment, with female personnel accounting for over 56% of its total 10,278 employees. Competitive compensation, benefits, and ESOP programs help drive workforce retention and engagement. VIB also actively participates in key social security initiatives focusing on education, healthcare, and community support.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to international standards and legal regulations, featuring an independent "three lines of defense" model. The Board of Directors (BOD) maintains a high degree of independence, including independent members deeply engaged in specialized committees (Personnel Committee, Risk Management Committee). Information disclosure is transparent through bilingual (Vietnamese-English) reporting, and financial statements are independently audited by Ernst & Young Vietnam, ensuring compliance with advanced governance practices.
