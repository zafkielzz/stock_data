**1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING**
In FY2025, Vietnam’s macroeconomic environment demonstrated resilience, registering GDP growth of approximately 8% amidst global uncertainties and providing a robust catalyst for the domestic equity market. The VN-Index surged by nearly 41% year-on-year, accompanied by an expansion in market liquidity, with average daily trading value exceeding VND 29.5 trillion (up over 40% compared to 2024). Vietcap’s core growth momentum was driven by its proactive opportunity capture, flexible business execution, and revenue diversification. Full-year operating revenue reached VND 4,980 billion, while profit before tax (PBT) touched VND 1,629 billion, outperforming the annual target by approximately 15% (115% completion rate). Core business segments—brokerage, margin lending, and proprietary trading—all recorded marked improvements in both operational scale and efficiency. Vietcap further solidified its market leadership in the institutional client segment while progressively capturing market share in retail brokerage. Margin loan demand remained robust, with the total market value of collateral assets standing at VND 62,733,458,200,000 as of December 31, 2025, reflecting strong investor confidence in the firm's brand equity and operational capabilities.

**2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES**
Regarding the capital structure, Vietcap’s Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio stood at 81.96% at the close of FY2025, indicating that financial leverage is maintained within a prudent threshold to support core operations, notably margin lending and proprietary investments. The collateral pool securing short-term bank borrowings comprises term deposits, unlisted corporate bonds (such as the BID12504 issuance by the Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam), and selected equities classified under available-for-sale (AFS) financial assets. The treasury management system maintained sufficient flexibility to accommodate the disbursement velocity of margin loans; however, short-term liquidity dynamics continue to necessitate stringent monitoring of bank loan maturities and cash flows generated from securities trading activities.

**3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS**
The company maintained a conservative provisioning policy for financial assets and receivables to mitigate market volatility risks. Specifically, the margin lending portfolio and securities service receivables were closely monitored, with impairment allowances recognized and reversed dynamically based on actual developments as of the reporting date. For its covered warrant issuance operations, Vietcap strictly complied with regulatory requirements to maintain segregated collateral assets equivalent to a minimum of 50% of the total issued warrant value at the custodian bank, thereby ensuring settlement obligations. The records indicate no material legal disputes or extraordinary financial events occurring subsequent to the December 31, 2025 reporting date that would adversely affect the company's business continuity.

**4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION**
- **Environmental (E):** Vietcap prioritized operational efficiency through comprehensive digital transformation, minimizing paper usage across business workflows, and fostering a digital financial ecosystem alongside efficient energy resource management within its corporate offices.
- **Social (S):** The firm remained steadfast in its human-centric development strategy, ensuring a professional working environment, comprehensive welfare benefits, workplace safety, and the promotion of gender equality. The company emphasized long-term investments in human capital via extensive professional training programs and the cultivation of innovative thinking across its workforce.
- **Governance (G):** The corporate governance framework operated under high standards of transparency, ensuring the independence of the Board of Directors and rigorous oversight by the Supervisory Board. Independent audits were conducted rigorously, adhering strictly to information disclosure standards aimed at generating long-term, sustainable value for shareholders and the broader market.
