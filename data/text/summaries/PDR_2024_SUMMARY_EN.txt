### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
During fiscal year 2024, Phat Dat Real Estate Development Corp (HOSE: PDR) experienced an 18.01% share price contraction, opening at VND 23,370 and closing at VND 19,160 (with an intraday trading range of VND 15,460 to VND 28,010 and an average daily trading volume of 9,254,492 shares). Operationally, PDR characterized FY2024 as a preparatory, transitional, and restructuring year designed to navigate lingering real estate headwinds and subdued purchasing power ahead of a new industry cycle. Although failing to meet aggressive top-line growth targets set at the beginning of the year, PDR demonstrated operational agility, accepting strategic trade-offs to safeguard the enterprise and establish a foundation for recovery. Beginning in Q4 2024, the company officially accelerated sales execution, leading to a gradual recovery in top-line generation and setting the stage for a pronounced rebound in FY2025. PDR’s core structural growth driver stems from its expansive land bank of nearly 6,200 hectares situated in prime locations, including Binh Duong, Binh Dinh, Da Nang, and Ba Ria - Vung Tau. The intrinsic value of this land bank has been significantly enhanced by regional infrastructure development initiatives and administrative boundary re-allocations. The company is actively deploying a project pipeline comprising at least six major ready-to-launch developments (namely Thuan An 1 & 2, Quy Nhon Iconic, Q1 Tower, Nhu Nguyet Da Nang, Serenity Phuoc Hai, and Con Dao) with an aggregate projected gross development value (GDV) of VND 50 trillion, aimed at optimizing cash flow velocity.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
PDR prioritized financial risk mitigation, the minimization of short-term debt pressures, and liquidity preservation as core mandates throughout FY2024. To finance ongoing capital expenditures and project development, PDR capitalized borrowing costs amounting to VND 134,530,553,307 during the year, marking a substantial increase compared to VND 41,785,602,740 in FY2023. Regarding credit enhancements and debt obligations, the company maintains material borrowings and financial commitments secured by asset collateral held with credit institutions. Specifically, property rights arising from the Bac Ha Thanh Urban Area and Residential Project in Binh Dinh have been pledged as collateral for credit facilities extended by Military Commercial Joint Stock Bank (MBBank) - Saigon Branch. Additionally, The EverRich 2 and The EverRich 3 projects, along with all associated derivative property rights, have been mortgaged at Vietnam Prosperity Joint Stock Commercial Bank (VPBank) to guarantee loans extended to affiliated business partners within the group. PDR’s capital structure remains constrained by short-term advances from customers and receivables tied to project divestments, necessitating an accelerated sales pace and aggressive receivables collection to rebalance liquidity.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS
The company maintains a rigorous framework of reviews and provisioning to manage credit risk arising from trade and other receivables. Notably, regarding the receivable stemming from a personal loan extended under Loan Agreement No. 01/2022/TTMT dated June 7, 2022, to Mr. Hoang Hiep Dung, PDR has provisioned VND 17,500,000,000 as an allowance for doubtful debts. Regarding short-term receivables linked to project and equity divestments: the receivable from Zone 4 (High-Rise) of the Nhon Hoi Project in Binh Dinh is secured by property rights and beneficial interests originating from the Marriott Hoi An Resort & Spa Project in Quang Nam; receivables from the divestment of Saigon - KL shares and associated loan agreements are backed by land use rights of related individuals; and receivables from the divestment of BIDICI shares have seen a cash recovery of VND 175,000,000,000, with the residual balance maturing on September 30, 2025. Concerning advance payments and off-balance-sheet commitments, PDR records material prepayments tied to the Build-Transfer (BT) Orthopedic Hospital Project (secured by insider pledges of PDR shares) and the Ba Sang Islet infrastructure project in Thu Duc City. Large inventory assets, such as The EverRich 2 and The EverRich 3, are undergoing statutory administrative procedures to effectuate the transfer of remaining portions pursuant to executed Investment Cooperation Agreements with Big Gain Co., Ltd. and Dynamic Innovation Co., Ltd.

### 4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION
- **Environmental (E):** PDR integrates its Sustainability Report directly into its Annual Report, referencing the latest Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards while aligning with guidelines issued by the International Finance Corporation (IFC), the State Securities Commission (SSC), and Circular 155/2015/TT-BTC. The company focuses on identifying material environmental and social topics, utilizing the GRI 2021 framework as a self-assessment tool to remediate deficiencies and work toward full compliance with international sustainability standards over the long term.
- **Social (S):** PDR fully honors its commitments and obligations to its workforce, guaranteeing robust employee welfare benefits and strict adherence to labor regulations. Commemorating its 20th anniversary in September 2024, executive management instituted organizational enhancements focused on cultivating accountability, optimizing operational efficiency, and elevating individual competencies to forge an elite, multi-functional talent pool capable of proactively adapting to market dynamics.
- **Governance (G):** The company maintains a corporate governance framework anchored in strict compliance with statutory regulations, internal operational charters, and core values. Executive leadership is structured around a Chief Executive Officer supported by 4 to 5 Deputy CEOs, each overseeing specialized functional areas to ensure high-velocity execution. PDR emphasizes legal compliance, streamlining organizational and operational workflows to sharpen performance monitoring and risk management, thereby building a foundation for deep-seated, sustainable growth.
