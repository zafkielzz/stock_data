1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING:
In FY2024, Dat Xanh Group posted a net profit after tax of VND 453 billion amidst a complex macroeconomic and real estate market backdrop. Core growth momentum was driven by the continued handover of residential units at the Opal Skyline project (Thuan An, Binh Duong) and accelerated construction progress at the Gem Sky World mega commercial and entertainment urban area (Long Thanh, Dong Nai). Revenue and margin profiles were underpinned by two core operating pillars: Residential Real Estate Development and Real Estate Services. The Group maintains its strategic focus on the mid-to-high-end primary housing segments catering to genuine end-user demand across densely populated urban centers in Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong, and Dong Nai. Due to sector-specific characteristics, DXG generates no export revenues, with its target market being exclusively domestic. Under its 2025–2035 long-term strategic roadmap, the Group is scaling its ecosystem into 5 core pillars: Real Estate Development, Real Estate Services, Construction, Investment, and Technology, operated through a corporate architecture comprising 8 direct subsidiaries and 74 indirect subsidiaries. On the equity market, DXG shares registered a 19.57% correction over the year (closing at VND 11,220/share), reflecting prevailing market conservatism, though liquidity remained robust with average trading volume exceeding 12.66 million shares per session.

2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES:
Dat Xanh’s capital structure continues to reflect a blend of bank borrowings, corporate bond issuances, and strategic investment cooperation capital. The Group's short-term and long-term debt obligations carry floating interest rates predominantly ranging from 5.0% to 10.8% per annum. Capitalized borrowing costs for FY2024 reached VND 128.91 billion (compared to VND 115.58 billion in FY2023), reflecting the accumulation of financial expenses into the construction-in-progress values of key flagship projects, including Gem Sky World, Gem Riverside, Home Park City, Bao Ninh 1, and Tam Nhin Xanh. DXG maintains a diversified credit-provider network comprising major commercial banks such as VietinBank, VPBank, Agribank, BIDV, Vietcombank, HDBank, TPBank, and Vietnam-Russia Joint Venture Bank (VRB). Collateral structures supporting these borrowings are highly flexible, encompassing certificates of deposit, bank bonds (DXG utilized VND 25 billion of VietinBank/Agribank bonds to secure long-term debt), and land use rights across the Tien Hai, Long Chau, Lotus Bac Giang, La Maison Premium, and Cara River Park projects. To mitigate cash flow pressures and optimize liquidity, the enterprise proactively exercised rights in the rights offering to existing shareholders toward year-end, while securing business cooperation deposits from strategic partners (notably the capital contribution for the exclusive distribution of 45,000 sqm at the Dat Xanh Home Park City project via Ha An SG).

3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE SHEET EVENTS:
To enforce prudent balance sheet risk management, Dat Xanh has provisioned for doubtful receivables, prepayments to suppliers, and trade receivables originating from brokerage and marketing operations. The inventory portfolio is predominantly concentrated in real estate development costs, site clearance expenses, and construction outlays across core projects. Regarding off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities, DXG maintains commitments stemming from Business Cooperation Contracts (BCCs), escrow deposits, third-party payment guarantees for marketing and distribution contracts, and customer deposit receipts for product acquisitions from both individual and institutional buyers. Regarding post-balance-sheet events, on January 30, 2025, the Group made comprehensive public disclosures to the State Securities Commission (SSC) and the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE) regarding insider transactions (including the execution of rights in the late-2024 rights offering to existing shareholders) and related-party transactions in strict compliance with statutory regulations. The enterprise recorded no legal disputes or extraordinary financial anomalies that would materially impair the going-concern assumption subsequent to December 31, 2024.

4. ESG IMPLEMENTATION:
- Environmental (E): Dat Xanh integrates the United Nations’ 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) into its corporate strategy, operationalized through 115 localized national targets. The Group implements green urban development methodologies, emphasizing sustainable water resource management, energy efficiency, and clean energy applications in residential design and construction to minimize ecological footprints.
- Social (S): The Group focuses on cultivating a secure working environment, upholding gender equality policies, enhancing employee welfare, and accelerating elite workforce development to facilitate digital transformation (via the deployment of an Enterprise Resource Planning - ERP system). DXG also aligns corporate interests with local communities through urban-rural development initiatives and local livelihood enhancement programs.
- Governance (G): The corporate governance framework has been reinforced in alignment with the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard. In July 2024, the Board of Directors announced a senior leadership transition, with Mr. Luong Ngoc Huy assuming the role of Chairman of the Board, succeeding Founder Luong Tri Thin (who transitioned to Chairman of the Strategy Council). Independence and internal control oversight are safeguarded by the active role of an Independent Board Member serving concurrently as Audit Committee Chair. Financial statements are subject to transparent independent audits, ensuring regulatory compliance and maximizing long-term shareholder value.
