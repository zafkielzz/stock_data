Below is the institutional-grade equity research translation of the financial report summary for Vietnam National Petroleum Group (PLX):

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2022, Petrolimex’s business operations contended with extreme volatility in the global energy markets driven by geopolitical conflicts, which resulted in supply chain disruptions and surging international oil prices. However, underpinned by a robust recovery in domestic consumption following the COVID-19 pandemic, the Group’s consolidated net revenue recorded exceptional growth. The core growth driver continued to be its primary business segment—the import, export, and distribution of petroleum products—which accounted for the overwhelming majority of the revenue mix. Additionally, auxiliary segments such as petrochemicals, liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), jet fuel, maritime transportation, and terminal services served as key pillars for expanding gross profit margins, effectively cushioning periods when domestic retail prices failed to keep pace with rising input costs. Petrolimex’s primary consumer market remains the domestic market, supported by a nationwide distribution network, complemented by export activities and international jet fuel supply services at major seaports and airports.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
Petrolimex’s financial structure in 2022 maintained a balanced profile to ensure adequate working capital for strategic petroleum imports. Short-term funding requirements for inventory procurement faced substantial pressure driven by fluctuations in inventory valuation and foreign exchange rates. The Group maintained flexible credit facilities, primarily sourced through major domestic and international commercial banks, to finance short-term working capital. The majority of these borrowings carried floating interest rates benchmarked against domestic and international money market movements. Capital structure risks primarily centered around rising interest expense pressures within an overarching high-interest-rate environment, necessitating that the Group optimize its cash conversion cycle and strictly manage operating cash flow to mitigate reliance on short-term financial leverage.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
Provisioning in 2022 was executed conservatively by Petrolimex to accurately reflect the fair value of its assets—notably inventory write-down provisions stemming from the sharp reversal of global oil prices toward the end of the year, alongside provisions for doubtful trade receivables. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments and contingent liabilities, the Group maintained bank guarantees to support the opening of letters of credit (L/Cs) for petroleum imports and the execution of major commercial contracts. During the year and up to the reporting date, no material legal disputes were recorded that could severely threaten the financial standing of the enterprise. PLX’s stock price performance on the stock market during the year experienced a sharp decline from an opening price of VND 46,100 to a closing price of VND 28,000 (-39.26%), trading within a range of VND 20,270 to VND 55,840 with an average matched trading volume of 1,434,975 shares/session, reflecting investor caution amid industry-wide energy sector pressures.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** Petrolimex actively pursued green energy transition strategies, progressively controlling and reducing greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, Scope 2, and working toward Scope 3 inventories) in alignment with the Vietnamese Government’s Net-Zero commitments. Technical solutions aimed at optimizing energy efficiency and implementing circular economy models across storage terminals, petrochemical plants, and service station networks were accelerated to minimize adverse ecological impacts.
- **Social (S):** The Group strictly adheres to occupational health, safety, and fire prevention (OHS) standards due to the inherently high-risk nature of its operations. Human resource policies prioritize gender equality, comprehensive welfare benefits, comprehensive insurance, and professional development training programs, ensuring a high-quality workforce for sustainable growth.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly complies with regulatory standards applicable to large-scale public companies. The Board of Directors maintains relative independence to ensure transparency in strategic planning. Independent audits are rigorously conducted, while sustainability reporting progressively aligns with international GRI (Global Reporting Initiative) standards tied to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
