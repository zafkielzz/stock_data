### 1. BUSINESS DRIVERS & SEGMENT REPORTING
Fiscal year 2021 proved to be a challenging period for the financial and insurance markets due to the complex disruptions of the COVID-19 pandemic. Nevertheless, Bao Viet Holdings maintained operational stability through a flexible business strategy anchored by the corporate theme of "Proactive Adaptation." Core growth drivers continued to stem from two primary pillars—non-life insurance and life insurance—alongside steady contributions from financial investments and financial services (securities brokerage and fund management).

However, the price performance of BVH shares during the year reflected broader market and sector headwinds, opening at VND 57,920 and closing the year at VND 49,100, registering a -15.23% decline. The stock traded within an annual high of VND 61,550 and a low of VND 40,630, with average matched liquidity of 1,135,709 shares per session, indicating consistent investor interest and steady capital inflows from both institutional and retail participants.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As a specialized financial and insurance conglomerate, Bao Viet’s capital structure prioritizes liquidity safety and strict compliance with regulatory capital adequacy ratios mandated by state authorities. Capital mobilization and the structure of short- and long-term borrowings are prudently managed to ensure the capacity for sudden insurance claim payouts and to maintain adequate liquidity for short-term financial investment activities.

The group maintains flexible credit relationships with major systemic banking partners to optimize its cost of capital. Cash flow pressures are largely mitigated by steady net cash inflows from collected insurance premiums and investment yields from government bond portfolios and term deposits, enabling the group to minimize exposure to floating interest rate volatility in the broader market.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
Bao Viet’s provisioning for insurance contract liabilities and financial risk reserves is executed in strict accordance with prevailing Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) and Ministry of Finance regulations to ensure solvency against potential losses. Non-performing loans, doubtful debts, and investment impairment losses are reviewed periodically to accurately reflect the group’s financial standing.

During the period, off-balance sheet guarantee commitments and contingent liabilities remained under the rigorous oversight of the risk management division. No material legal disputes arose that could exert a significant impact on the group’s cash flows or corporate reputation. Financial events occurring after the December 31, 2021 annual accounting closing date were transparently disclosed in compliance with securities regulations, notably the publication of the 2021 Annual Report in April 2022.

This reduces indirect greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 2) and promotes circular economy awareness system-wide.
- **Social (S):** The human capital development strategy focuses on ensuring occupational health and safety (OHS) for employees, enforcing equitable gender equality policies, and maintaining professional capacity-building training programs alongside comprehensive welfare and insurance regimes.
- **Governance (G):** Corporate governance strictly adheres to international standards and regulations outlined in circulars governing information disclosure on the stock market. The Board of Directors maintains a high degree of independence and collaborates closely with independent auditors to ensure the transparency and accuracy of financial statements, while progressing toward the synchronized adoption of GRI / SDG sustainability reporting standards.
