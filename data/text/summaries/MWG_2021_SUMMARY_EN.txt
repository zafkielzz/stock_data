Below is the institutional-grade translation of the 2021 qualitative financial summary for MWG:

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2021, despite unprecedented disruptions from the 4th Covid-19 wave—which forced the temporary closure or operational restriction of approximately 2,000 stores in Q3—MWG delivered robust financial performance. Consolidated net revenue reached VND 122,958 billion, representing a 13% increase year-over-year (YoY) and achieving 98% of the full-year target. Profit after tax (PAT) rose 25% YoY to VND 4,901 billion, exceeding the annual guidance by 3%.

Core growth drivers stemmed from the agility of the company's Omni-channel model and superior procurement capabilities, which maintained a stable supply chain. The Mobile Phone and Consumer Electronics segment (TGDD/ĐMX) contributed VND 94,742 billion (accounting for 77% of total revenue, up 9% YoY), propelled by the breakthrough performance of new formats such as ĐMX Supermini (contributing VND 6,800 billion) and the Topzone chain specializing in high-end Apple products. Meanwhile, the Bach Hoa Xanh (BHX) grocery chain recorded a stellar performance with revenue exceeding VND 28,200 billion, up 33% YoY, officially surpassing the USD 1 billion milestone and entering the Top 3 largest food retailers in Vietnam. Total group online revenue grew by 53% to VND 14,370 billion, accounting for over 12% of total revenue. Internationally, Bluetronics in Cambodia generated VND 450 billion in revenue, marking a 135% growth.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As of December 31, 2021, MWG’s consolidated total assets stood at VND 62,971 billion, up 36.8% from the beginning of the year. The capital structure comprised VND 42,593 billion in total liabilities (representing approximately 67.6% of total capital) and VND 20,378 billion in equity. Notably, short-term borrowings surged by 57.7% to VND 24,647 billion, while long-term debt was fully settled to zero (bonds maturing in November 2022 were reclassified as short-term debt).

Short-term loans primarily consisted of unsecured working capital financing denominated in VND and USD from major financial institutions—including BNP Paribas, HSBC, Standard Chartered, Vietcombank, and Techcombank—carrying floating interest rates closely pegged to market fluctuations. Despite increasing short-term debt pressure to finance inventory accumulation and network expansion, operating cash flow remained positive at VND 171.39 billion, ensuring liquidity and a stable solvency profile for the entire system.

### 3. PROVISIONING RISKS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & SUBSEQUENT EVENTS
MWG proactively executed conservative provisioning policies to mitigate financial risks. Specifically, year-end inventory write-down provisions were recorded at VND 682.84 billion (compared to VND 504.19 billion at the beginning of the year), reflecting full compliance with regulatory provisioning standards and a prudent approach toward slow-moving inventory affected by the pandemic. Short-term trade receivables stood at VND 3,162 billion, assessed as having negligible collection risk.

Regarding off-balance-sheet obligations and financial commitments, the company maintained long-term operating lease commitments with future values scaling alongside the expansion of the retail network. According to the independent audit report by Ernst & Young Vietnam, there were no material legal disputes or off-balance-sheet contingent liabilities. Furthermore, no extraordinary financial events occurred subsequent to the fiscal year-end of December 31, 2021, that would require retrospective adjustments to the financial statements.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** MWG focused on optimizing energy and resource efficiency across its store network and distribution centers. The company rolled out rooftop solar power systems on a broad scale (installed across 68 supermarkets in 2021, generating VND 2.2 billion in electricity savings), optimized air-conditioning temperature settings, transitioned 100% of packaging at TGDD/ĐMX to eco-friendly PE plastic, and promoted biodegradable packaging models at BHX. Electronic and municipal waste was segregated and treated in strict compliance with legal regulations through licensed contractors.
- **Social (S):** With a workforce of over 71,000 employees, MWG maintained a professional working environment, strictly adhering to occupational health and safety (OHS) and insurance regulations. Female employees accounted for 38.9% of the total headcount, demonstrating workforce diversity and gender equality. Amidst severe pandemic conditions, the company deployed the "An Tâm Chống Dịch" (Anti-Epidemic Peace of Mind) insurance package for over 51,658 individuals (including employees and their families), while maintaining competitive compensation and executing 4,183 internal promotions.
- **Governance (G):** MWG’s governance structure complies with the Law on Enterprises, featuring the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Directors (BOD—including independent members), the Board of Management, and the Audit Committee operating directly under the BOD. Independent audits were conducted transparently by Ernst & Young Vietnam. The company strictly complied with information disclosure regulations for listed enterprises while progressively refining sustainable development standards in alignment with the integration of ESG practices into its operations.
