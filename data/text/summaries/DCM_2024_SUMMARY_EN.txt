Below is the qualitative and financial research summary for PetroVietnam Ca Mau Fertilizer Joint Stock Company (DCM), based on the 2024 Annual Report and verified data:

### 1. BUSINESS DYNAMICS & SEGMENT REPORTING
In 2024, DCM reported consolidated revenue of VND 14,037 billion (up 7% YoY) and consolidated profit before tax of VND 1,522 billion (up 21% YoY). This growth was driven by the optimized operation of its manufacturing complex—comprising 3 plants with a total capacity of approximately 1.5 million tons of fertilizer per annum. Notably, the Ca Mau Fertilizer Plant operated stably at 110%–115% of its design capacity, reaching the milestone of 11 million tons of Urea produced on December 25, 2024. Total sales volume reached 1,325.3 thousand tons (up 5% YoY). Core growth drivers included the expansion of high-end product portfolios (such as NPK, N.HIMATE+TE, and UREA BIO) and the successful M&A strategy to acquire Korean-Vietnamese Fertilizer Company Limited (KVF), lifting total NPK capacity to 660,000 tons/year. While maintaining its domestic market share (at approximately 10.62%), the export segment remained a key highlight with a sales volume of 319 thousand tons (accounting for 24% of total sales volume) and export value reaching nearly VND 2,940 billion. This expanded the Ca Mau Fertilizer brand presence to nearly 20 countries and territories, most notably through successful penetration into demanding markets such as Australia and New Zealand.

### 2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE, DEBT & INTEREST RATES
As of December 31, 2024, DCM’s total assets stood at VND 15,729 billion, representing a 3% increase YTD. The capital structure maintained a high level of safety, with a total liabilities-to-total assets ratio of 35% and a total liabilities-to-equity ratio of 55%. Short-term bank borrowings stood at VND 1,229.23 billion (with no long-term debt at the parent company level, and only long-term finance leases at its subsidiary, Petrovietnam Packaging Joint Stock Company - PPC). Short-term borrowing interest rates fluctuated flexibly from 2.6% to 3.5% p.a. for USD-denominated loans, and from 5.8% to 7.2% p.a. for VND-denominated loans. Major credit institutions granting significant credit lines to DCM include prominent commercial banks such as Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of Vietnam (Vietcombank - Thu Thiem Branch), Vietnam Joint Stock Commercial Bank for Industry and Trade (VietinBank), and BNP Paribas. A healthy capital structure supported by cash and short-term deposits totaling up to VND 7,039 billion ensures that DCM maintains full autonomy over its working capital, with no major debt-repayment pressure or liquidity risk.

### 3. PROVISIONS, CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & POST-BALANCE-SHEET EVENTS
The company maintains a prudent risk management policy with adequate provisioning. Specifically, year-end inventory write-down provisions were recorded at VND 42.06 billion (against a total inventory cost basis of VND 2,950.34 billion) to accurately reflect the net realizable value of slow-moving or defective items. Provisions for short-term doubtful receivables stood at VND 112.41 billion (including trade receivable provisions of VND 111.49 billion) based on actual recoverability assessments. Regarding off-balance-sheet commitments, DCM maintains standard guarantees, letters of credit (L/Cs), and operating lease contracts to support ordinary business operations. The company reported no material legal disputes affecting its financial standing, nor any abnormal financial events occurring after December 31, 2024, that would adversely affect business performance.

### 4. ESG EXECUTION
- **Environmental (E):** DCM remains steadfast in its green growth strategy. In 2024, greenhouse gas emission intensity (Scope 1 and Scope 2) decreased by 3.75% YoY, while energy intensity declined by 4.83% compared to the 2022 baseline year, driven by energy optimization initiatives and CO2 recovery at the plant. The company continued implementing the "300,000 Trees" initiative for the 2022–2025 period, alongside a used-battery collection program for environmental protection.
- **Social (S):** With a parent company headcount of 1,155 employees and an average income of VND 42.58 million/person/month, DCM maintained an absolute safe working environment (recording 0 lost-time injuries and 0 occupational diseases). The company strictly enforced welfare and insurance policies (contributions to social, health, and unemployment insurance exceeded VND 83 billion) and allocated nearly VND 17.5 billion toward community social security, education, and public healthcare programs.
- **Governance (G):** The company strictly adheres to best corporate governance practices, featuring a 7-member Board of Directors, including 2 independent members (ensuring objectivity and a balance of power). Specialized committees under the Board of Directors (Audit & Risk Management Committee, Nomination & Remuneration Committee, and ESG Committee) operate effectively. Financial statements are independently audited by Deloitte Vietnam, fully complying with Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), aligned with the GRI framework, and supporting the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
